# Foundations source review and dispositions

8 September 2026 · revision 1

## 1. Decision, scope and method

Jim's current decision is the authority for our implementation-origin policy: author graph and non-graph algorithms and data structures as SMALL Reliable Atoms and meaningful layers of composition, using the best openly licensed references as inspiration. The [governing document](algorithms-and-data-structures-governance-2026-09-08.md) states that policy. Earlier reports supply requirements, observations, counterexamples and design questions; they do not supply or ratify this new decision.

This review covered **41 distinct source files**: 36 Markdown narratives read in full and five JSON records inspected to the depths stated below. The corpus comprises the 17-file Typescript Graphs collection, the queue and two worked-example documents, the build/layering analysis and original-three review, ten August Graphify/Practice records, and nine relevant Castr/API/Interopet records. The current bundle has 17 files: fourteen revised existing documents and three new documents, as mapped by the [bundle index](foundations-bundle-index-2026-09-08.md).

Discovery used the graph collection and targeted searches for Reliable Atoms, data structures, algorithms, API decomposition, Castr and signal/time-series requirements. Unrelated project material, duplicate transfer archives and files merely mentioned by an index were outside the inspected corpus. This is a bounded review of relevant sources, not a claim that every file in the owner's wider collection was read. Existing local copies of attachments were used directly.

OCE's startup and planning guidance established the task and repository baseline; reason and metacognition separated owner decisions from source claims and challenged the scope inference; proportionality bounded research by unresolved questions; concept exploration and Parallax-style crosswalks compared semantic, computational, lifecycle and composition perspectives. Separate agent readings improved coverage and challenged interpretation. They share source lineage and infrastructure and do not constitute independent empirical replication. The retired clean-room workflow was not used.

Repository integration was grounded at `EngraphCode/open-curriculum-ecosystem`, branch `engraph`, commit `270b8ec6fb82dc6881c10ca101cb8d3c7a242d6e`. Repository directives were read as current integration instructions; August and early-September source claims retain their own revisions and dates. This review did not rerun historical probes, refresh external licence or package claims, benchmark implementations, or qualify newly authored algorithms. A targeted integration check of the W3C [RDFC-1.0 Recommendation of 21 May 2024](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-rdf-canon-20240521/) confirmed the canonicalisation domain and its distinction from persistent source identity; it was not a fresh upstream implementation survey. Prospective reference investigations verify their exact inspected material and licensing evidence under the governing policy.

## 6. Conserved cross-source cases

These compact cases preserve useful distinctions for future contracts and worked implementations. They are requirements and reasoned examples, not new executions. A case applies only at a boundary that owns the relevant responsibility.

1. **Independent qualification and conditional inheritance.** Name the inherited law, discharged premises, locally added guarantee, residual consumer proof and invalidating changes. Reference-derived tests retain their lineage. An atom's correct order does not establish a composition's domain interpretation, effect or lifecycle contract.

2. **Complete adverse-branch composition.** API×OCE §12/E-7 reports a synthetic local capability returning `NO_MATCH`/400 while the copied channel registry omitted 400. The resulting `UNDOCUMENTED_STATUS` made the evaluated server expression yield 502. This was not an HTTP execution. Matching successful selections had concealed the missing adverse branch. Test every public outcome arm and its translation at the composition boundary, including admitted input, processing, output and persisted meaning.

3. **Contextual occurrences.** The API boundary report's §5 example contains two lesson entities but three contextual occurrences. An entity index can intentionally have two entries while an occurrence collection retains three under its declared compound key. The reusable mechanism owns equality, key and cardinality laws; the domain composition owns the validity of the contextual key. Universal deduplication or universal duplicate rejection would be incorrect.

4. **Cold/reference and maintained views.** For the same admitted source and semantic configuration, an incremental result must satisfy the same observable contract as a full rebuild, after normalising only irrelevant differences. Exercise deletion, unchanged dependencies, retries, interrupted publication and configuration changes. Publish coupled indexes/manifests/views as one coherent generation. A source timestamp alone cannot represent every semantic invalidation condition.

5. **Query shape and coverage.** A traversal tree records discovery edges; an induced subgraph includes all admitted edges among the selected vertices. Neither output can silently stand for the other. Bounds, direction, selection, execution completeness and input coverage constrain what absence and continuation mean.

6. **Restored structure and future operations.** A reconstruction can reproduce current membership yet leave its future ID allocator able to collide with retained handles. Check the next valid mutations as well as the restored snapshot. Removal/build transitions must keep counts, membership and incidence coherent. The catalogue owns these graph acceptance obligations.

7. **Exact numerical conventions.** A count window's integer laws do not prove floating-point statistical stability, event-time semantics or sensor accuracy. Numerical compositions name weighting, missingness, variance divisor, quantile/lag/normalisation conventions and absolute/relative tolerances where applicable. Independent forward expectations are needed when paired operations, such as a transform and its inverse, could share compensating errors.

8. **Time, custody and authority.** Sample time, callback arrival, generation and expiry differ. Stop followed by a delayed worker result must not revive stopped output. Transfer changes buffer ownership. Restoring internally coherent old state must not silently restore revoked authority. These belong to the relevant effect/domain composition, not every pure atom.

9. **Full ordering through selection.** The keyword journey orders by count and keyword ID. A scalar numeric-priority queue with FIFO ties has a different contract. Any composing adapter must preserve the complete declared ordering and cutoff semantics and account for its cost; the independent full-sort model remains the oracle for that journey.

10. **Whole-life economy counted once.** Compare specification, qualification, integration, operation and meaningful subsequent changes at equivalent quality. Two pairwise integration reports can both claim to remove the same interpretation work. Shared proof or saved work is counted once, with residual consumer obligations explicit. Package count, code volume and repeated test totals do not establish saved effort.

## 8. Result and remaining evidence

The review supports a responsibility-based programme across graph and non-graph foundations, with exact contracts and complete composition journeys. It supplied requirements and failure cases worth conserving; it did not prove that one proposed decomposition is minimal, that a package is qualified, or that the selected origin policy already saves net effort.

Future reference surveys need exact material/licence records and design-specific applicability checks. Implementations need the comprehensive tests, mutation assurance, documentation, public-consumption checks and cost evidence required by their contracts. Numerical thresholds and operational budgets belong to the capability that promises them. None is fabricated from a research report's illustrative workload.

The next delivery decisions can therefore be made against the current governing policy and visible requirements, without repeating the full historical survey or demanding a pre-existing production caller. New evidence can change a mechanism or boundary through its owning design record; changes to the owner-established acquisition policy remain owner decisions.
