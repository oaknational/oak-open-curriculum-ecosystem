# Curriculum rationale template

A rationale explains _why_ a curriculum, unit, or lesson is designed the way it is, against each principle. It is what lets others adapt the work with confidence and what keeps quality from drifting. Keep it brief — a few sentences per principle is plenty.

Fill in the bracketed prompts and delete this intro line when done.

---

## [Title] — curriculum rationale

**What this is:** [curriculum sequence / unit / lesson / resource]
**Subject & phase:** [e.g. Year 5 maths]
**Place in the curriculum:** [which thread(s) it belongs to; what comes immediately before and after]
**Mapped to:** [national curriculum statements / statutory framework references]

### 1. Knowledge and vocabulary rich

[What substantive knowledge will pupils hold afterwards (key learning points)? Which vocabulary is explicitly taught and how is it defined and revisited?]

### 2. Sequenced and coherent

[Why this order? What prior knowledge does it build on, and how is it retrieved? Which thread does it advance, and how?]

### 3. Evidence-informed

[What evidence or principles of learning informed the key design choices? Which misconceptions are anticipated, and how are they addressed?]

### 4. Flexible

[What is core and what is adaptable? How can a teacher localise or adjust this without breaking the sequence? Where is the mapping to the national curriculum statements that supports safe adaptation?]

### 5. Diverse

[What selections of texts, examples, sources or contexts were made, and why? How does it offer both windows and mirrors? How is ambition maintained?]

### 6. Accessible

[How can all pupils, including those with SEND, access the same ambitious content? What scaffolds are used, and how do they fade? How do the materials meet accessibility needs?]

### Tensions and trade-offs

[Where did two principles pull against each other, and how was it resolved? (e.g. how flexibility was enabled without losing coherence.)]

### Known limitations / adaptation notes

[What this deliberately does _not_ do; what a teacher should consider for their own pupils and context.]
