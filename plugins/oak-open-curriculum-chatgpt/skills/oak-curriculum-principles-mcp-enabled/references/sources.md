# Sources and attribution

## The principles

The six curriculum principles and the subject guiding principles for 15 subjects are Oak National Academy's. Their full statement, evidence base, and meaning live in the companion **`oak-curriculum-principles`** skill, synthesised from:

- Emma McCrea, "Our 6 principles guiding our approach to curriculum", Oak National Academy, 5 March 2023 — <https://www.thenational.academy/blog/our-approach-to-curriculum>
- Oak National Academy curriculum pages — <https://www.thenational.academy/curriculum>

## The data

This skill connects to the **Oak Curriculum MCP server**, which exposes Oak's open curriculum API:

- API docs: <https://open-api.thenational.academy/docs>
- Data glossary: <https://open-api.thenational.academy/docs/about-oaks-data/glossary>
- Web application (for linking): <https://www.thenational.academy>

The MCP provides the domain model (`get-curriculum-model`), discovery (`search`, `explore-topic`, `browse-curriculum`), content (`get-lessons-summary`, `get-units-summary`, transcripts, quizzes, assets), progression (`get-threads`, `get-thread-progressions`, `get-prior-knowledge-graph`), and the misconception graph (`get-misconception-graph`).

## Using and attributing

Oak's curriculum data is published under the **[Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)** (OGL v3.0), which permits reuse but **requires attribution**. When you reproduce Oak content or data, credit **Oak National Academy**, link to the relevant lesson/unit on thenational.academy, link to the OGL, and carry through any content guidance attached to the material. Re-fetch live data for formal or published work rather than relying on cached slugs.

A ready-to-use attribution line:

> Contains data from Oak National Academy, licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

The OGL covers the curriculum **data and content**. It does **not** cover Oak's trademarks, logos, or brand assets — those remain © Oak National Academy and are used only per the [brand guidelines](https://support.thenational.academy/using-the-oak-brand).
