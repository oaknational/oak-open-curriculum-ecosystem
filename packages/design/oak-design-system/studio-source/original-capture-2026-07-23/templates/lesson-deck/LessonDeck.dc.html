Where does a puddle go?

Learning cycle 1 of 2 · Explanation, check, practice, feedback

The sun warms the puddle

  • Water at the surface gains energy from the sun.
  • Tiny particles escape into the air as water vapour — this is evaporation.
  • Warmer, windier days make it happen faster.

Misconception: the puddle doesn't soak away or vanish — the water changes state.

diagram: puddle drying in sun

Check for understanding

Which process turns water vapour back into liquid water?

aEvaporation
bCondensation
cMelting

Cold call, then reveal — the answer is b.

Practice task

Explain what happens to the water in wet washing on a warm day.

Write 2–3 sentences. Use the keywords evaporation and water vapour.

Success criteria I name the process. I say where the water goes. I use both keywords correctly.

Summary

  • Evaporation: liquid water gains energy and becomes water vapour.
  • Condensation: water vapour cools and becomes liquid again.
  • Both happen all around us — puddles, washing lines, cold windows.
