# Glossary

**Admission**  
The post-discovery decision about whether a Parallax capability adds enough expected value to justify its cost and, if so, at what depth.

**Alternative Register**  
A versioned set of live explanations, hypotheses, designs, nulls, and retirement or reopening conditions.

**Artifact entry**  
Direct invocation based on an existing typed artifact rather than an end-to-end user request.

**Basis / decomposition**  
A conceptual coordinate system: primitives, constructs, units, boundaries, relationships, and standpoints used to organise an inquiry.

**Bridge Claim**  
A first-class claim connecting scale regions through an aggregation, disaggregation, transport, mechanism, or transformation rule with evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, validity, and defeaters.

**Capability graph**  
The enduring guarded, typed, cyclic network of skills and admissible relationships from which bounded runs are selected.

**Composite execution**  
Methods intentionally integrated into one design, usually with declared sequencing or mutual constraint.

**Conflict/Dependence/Defeater Ledger**  
A synthesis artifact preserving disagreements, shared evidence dependencies, challenges, resolutions, and live threats to conclusions.

**Crosswalk Claim**  
A mapping between conceptual bases that records coverage, direction, cardinality, information loss, unmatched residuals, evidence, and limitations.

**Defeater**  
Evidence, condition, argument, or outcome that would materially weaken, invalidate, qualify, or reopen a claim or decision.

**Depth**  
The proportionate inquiry budget: screening, core, standard, or deep. Depth reflects metareasoning, not prestige.

**Domain profile**  
A stackable overlay that refines evidence criteria, methods, scales, external boundaries, and risks for investigation, science, software engineering, or digital product/service work.

**Epistemic Profile**  
A multidimensional assessment of support, validity, robustness, dependence, scale/basis stability, applicability, conflicts, and decision relevance.

**Estimand**  
The precisely defined target quantity an experiment or causal analysis intends to learn, distinct from the estimator and observed estimate.

**Evidence Record**  
A claim-relevant observation with provenance, transformation, scale, basis, relevance, quality, uncertainty, and dependence.

**Guard**  
An observable or assessable condition controlling whether a graph transition or skill hand-off is eligible.

**Inquiry Charter**  
The inquiry's purpose, intended impact, questions, claim types, stakeholders, authority, constraints, scales, budget, alternatives, stopping, defeaters, and reopening conditions.

**Inquiry revision**  
An immutable version of the inquiry state. Reopening creates a new revision rather than overwriting causal history.

**Invocation grammar**  
The collection-wide routing surface formed by skill names, descriptions, admission criteria, exclusions, composition rules, and hand-offs.

**Learning Signal**  
A bounded, evidence-linked observation about an outcome, surprise, failure, success, routing choice, or method performance emitted for host Practice handling. It is not yet policy.

**Lifecycle state**  
Operational progression such as draft, ready-to-run, running, paused, completed, blocked, or cancelled. It is distinct from epistemic status.

**L0 / L1 / L2 learning**  
Object-level learning about the inquiry; learning about methods/routing; and learning about the policy for changing methods/routing.

**Method pass**  
One identifiable execution of a method or protected perspective with recorded provenance and dependencies.

**Operational differentiation**  
Evidence that conceptual bases or method passes differ in primitives, boundaries, mechanisms, evidence demands, blind spots, standpoint, or incremental contribution—used instead of asserting literal orthogonality.

**Practice**  
The embedding environment that owns durable memory, continuity, consolidation, governance, canonical versioning, and generated adapters.

**Protected parallel execution**  
Multiple method or perspective passes kept sufficiently separate before synthesis to reduce anchoring and preserve useful difference.

**Product Experiment Overlay**  
A typed specialisation that references a general Experimental Design and adds digital product/service exposure, telemetry, guardrail, rollout, and decision semantics without duplicating the base design.

**Provenance DAG**  
The append-only ancestry graph of artifact revisions, transformations, outcomes, reopenings, and supersession.

**Reopening**  
A transition triggered by a defeater, threshold, surprise, harm, new evidence, invalid bridge, or changed context that creates a new inquiry revision.

**Run DAG**  
A bounded acyclic execution plan materialised for one inquiry revision from the cyclic capability network and current constraints.

**Same-scale pluralism**  
Use of differentiated composite, parallel, competitive, triangulating, or nested methods at a declared scale and basis.

**Scale region**  
A task-specific multidimensional coordinate/range covering such dimensions as time, system extent, population, abstraction, organisation, intervention, consequence, and monitoring.

**Sparse inquiry state**  
The selected subset of scale × basis × method × revision cells justified by relevance, risk, information value, cost, and coverage—not an exhaustive Cartesian execution.

**Status**  
One of `validated`, `provisional`, `inconclusive`, `insufficient-evidence`, `declined`, `reopened`, or `superseded`, with explicit implications.

**World-Return Contract**  
A commitment connecting a conclusion, decision, or intervention to predicted and adverse outcomes, observation scales, timing, thresholds, ownership, rollback, and reopening.
