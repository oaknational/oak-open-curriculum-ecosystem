# External skills-library review framework

The owner's direction (2026-08-02, in-session): "I am going to want to
review ALL of the skills libraries, and any other top ranking skills
libraries. So it will be a good idea to [put] a review framework in
place, and to go over our latest methods and topologies for
multi-agent, multi-level analysis." The review itself is owner-held;
this plan builds the instrument.

## Why now, and why this is distillation rather than invention

The estate has run three genuine external-library studies and never
written down the method:

- the mattpocock/skills substrate study (2026-05-14), which deferred an
  "external substrate review template" until a second study repeated
  the shape;
- the ponytail substrate study (2026-06-22), which recorded that the
  second-study trigger had now fired — and the template was still
  never written;
- the addyosmani/agent-skills arc (2026-08-02): breadth survey, then a
  commissioned six-leg tiered scan with a frame-challenger leg and an
  adversarial security leg.

The framework is owed by the estate's own recorded trigger, twice over.
The raw material is complete: the two substrate studies' Practice-lens
sections and fit-gate tables, the survey/scan pair's D1 rubric and leg
topology, the now-live `third-party-skills-require-security-review`
rule, PDR-115's attribution discipline, and the fit-gate + candidate
register design in the external-skills-substrate-learning plan (whose
register was never created — the ponytail promote-candidate is still
waiting on it).

## Workstreams

### WS-A — The framework document

Distill the three worked instances into one staged instrument. Draft
stage spine (the distillation refines it against the instances, not
the other way round):

1. **Intake** — identity, licence (PDR-115: unclear licence = treat as
   proprietary), provenance, pin SHA, scale. Cheap, always first.
2. **Security gate** — the live rule applied: enumerate the executable
   surface; review before anything lands; no default script execution.
   The addyosmani leg C report is the worked exemplar.
3. **Breadth survey** — one agent, structure/taxonomy/validation/
   governance at breadth; enumerate its own gaps (the addyosmani
   survey's closing section is the form to copy).
4. **Tiered deep scan** — legs sized to the corpus, tiered by stance
   (mechanical rubric legs cheap; adversarial/judgement legs
   heavyweight); ALWAYS one frame-challenger leg tasked to falsify the
   survey's headline claims before anything downstream absorbs them.
5. **Estate fit-gate** — the dedup matrix (read OUR surface before
   judging) + per-candidate verdicts (promote / defer / reject /
   observe) into the candidate register.
6. **Disposition** — with the lens set by tier purpose (owner ruling,
   2026-08-02): tier-1/2 libraries are **for learning best practice
   from, not for replicating** — "we are much better off installing
   them and letting their own maintainers keep them up to date" — so
   their dispositions are *learn* (best-practice observations folded
   into our own practice, never copied content) and *install*
   (upstream-maintained, security-gated: content-payload packs can
   track upstream cleanly; packs whose install path runs code need the
   pinned form with bump-reviews, per the security rule — each review
   returns which form fits). Tier-3 libraries (already studied)
   **integrate advantageous shapes or elements case by case** through
   the candidate register. Every disposition carries recorded re-open
   conditions; nothing is vendored-as-replica from tiers 1/2.

Every stage names its evidence obligations (quoted lines, pinned SHAs,
first-hand verification) and its cost shape, so the owner can price a
review per library before firing it.

### WS-B — Methods and topologies inventory

The owner asked to "go over our latest methods and topologies for
multi-agent, multi-level analysis." One authored survey of the
patterns in live use — tiered fan-out with model-tier-per-leg,
frame-challenger legs, adversarial verify, cricket panels (the
effort-inversion quartet), judge panels, loop-until-dry, pipeline vs
barrier orchestration, workflow journal/resume — each with when-to-use
and a worked instance from the estate's own record. This becomes the
framework's stage-4 menu rather than a separate doctrine surface.

### WS-C — The roster

The reviewable universe, from the 2026-08-02 repo-wide sweep: fully
analysed (mattpocock/skills, DietrichGebert/ponytail), partially
analysed (addyosmani/agent-skills, obra/superpowers — four planning
skills only, clerk/skills — inventory only, modelcontextprotocol/
ext-apps — inventory only), named-but-never-analysed (anthropics/
skills, openai/skills, mcpjam/inspector, MCP Apps skills,
K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills — resolved at the owner's pointer),
plus a discovery method for "other top ranking" libraries. The roster
is priced by the owner; nothing on it fires without his word. Tier
purpose is part of the roster (owner ruling, 2026-08-02): tiers 1–2
are learn-and-install (upstream maintains; no replication), tier 3 is
case-by-case element integration.

### WS-D — The candidate register

Finally create the register the substrate-learning plan designed
(candidate, failure mode, natural home, proof signal — "candidate
count is not progress"), and seat the two waiting entries: the
complexity-debt ledger (ponytail promote, converging with mattpocock
C8) and doubt-driven-development's mechanisms (addyosmani). Both come
from tier-3 libraries, which is the register's whole jurisdiction
under the tier-purpose ruling: case-by-case element integration, each
entry carrying its failure mode, natural home, and proof signal.

## Explicit non-goals

- **Not running any review.** The owner's hold binds every stage.
- **Not adopting anything.** Dispositions are the framework's output,
  each returning to the owner.
- **Not the authoring roster.** "The skills only Oak can write"
  (curriculum-author and teacher skill sets, per the 2026-08-02
  audience-partition ruling) is a separate, mission-strand decision —
  seeded here by name only.

## Verification

- The framework document reproduces the three worked instances'
  verdicts when walked backward against them (the distillation test).
- The register exists with its two seed entries and their proof
  signals.
- A dry-run pricing of one roster library (stages, legs, cost shape)
  reads cleanly enough for the owner to say go or no-go from it alone.
