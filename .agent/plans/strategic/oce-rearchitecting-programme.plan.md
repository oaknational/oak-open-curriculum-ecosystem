## Outcome

OCE retains its complete capability innovation framework, with potentially
reusable mechanisms, experiments and evidence conserved in one maintained source
home. Supported public packages and reusable execution facilities allow external
instances to vary ordinary configuration without OCE changes in most cases.
Construction, assurance, curriculum semantics, publication and receiving
responsibility remain connected rather than becoming separate completion claims.

## User groups and value

Capability builders receive complete reusable contracts and an incremental CF
construction path. Instance builders receive supported configuration and a complete
published closure. Curriculum/domain owners retain meaning and authority. Teachers
and learners may benefit from future services, but this programme does not claim
educational outcomes from architectural conformance. The curriculum infrastructure consumer, the exploratory pedagogy project, other consumers and
new innovation work retain distinct scopes, evidence and decision authority.

## The bet

CF-first construction and small complete vertical capabilities can strengthen
reliability without an all-CF gate. Retaining mechanisms while separating source
custody, qualification, distribution and operation makes independent instances
possible without fragmenting the framework. Layered models and Castr integration
can reduce semantic drift only when every boundary has independently checked
contracts and explicit failure. Configuration is one requirement among these.

## Success looks like

- A complete supported capability is constructed and consumed with exact reliance,
  identity, provenance, compatibility, correction and recovery evidence.
- The selected external instance builds and operates from published artifacts and
  data-only configuration; its ordinary-change scenarios require no OCE edit/release.
- All retained mechanisms have maintained responsibility homes, including tooling,
  lifecycle, search, experimentation and framework facilities beyond current apps.
- Publication and receiving acceptance are separately proved; no source-only pass
  is represented as registry availability or service readiness.
- CF qualification, compiler correctness, software conformance, operational
  readiness and educational benefit remain distinct claims. No team measurements
  or studies serve as programme acceptance.
