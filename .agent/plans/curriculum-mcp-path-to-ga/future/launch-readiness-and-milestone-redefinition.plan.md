# Launch-Readiness & Milestone Redefinition

**Status**: 📋 FUTURE — strategic stub, not executable until promoted.

## Intent

Act on the 2026-06-15 launch-readiness assessment: redraft the Curriculum MCP
milestone ladder from first principles (impact-first), and drive the
launch-readiness gates toward GA as defined by the owner-ratified keystones.

This is a **stub**. It carries no execution detail. Its job is to keep the
assessment findings reachable and to hold the named promotion trigger so the
redraft pass is picked up deliberately, not by drift.

## Inputs (read first)

- [Session assessment report](../../../reports/mcp-app-live-product-readiness-assessment-2026-06-15.md)
  — the full findings: first-hand verification, right/wrong/missing, the
  keystone decisions, and the milestone re-assessment verdict (§8).
- [Launch-Readiness Framework](../launch-readiness-framework.md) — the living
  gate structure (Groups A–D, dimension C4 whole-estate readiness).
- [Path-to-GA Programme](../roadmap.md) — the engineering/compliance sequencing
  index whose §6 A4 this work satisfies.

## Decided keystones (from the assessment, owner 2026-06-15)

- **K1** — "Live" = full GA with real users (teachers and curriculum leaders)
  and *observed* positive impact. Value-proof is a GA precondition.
- **K2** — Primary audience: teachers and curriculum leaders.
- **K3** — Scope: the MCP app in ChatGPT and Claude, which requires the whole
  estate beneath to be GA-ready.

## What promotion will produce

When promoted to `current/`, this plan will be expanded to:

1. Rewrite `milestones/m2`/`m3` and add an M4/GA definition to the impact-first
   ladder (Stages 1–4 in the assessment §8.3), keeping M0/M1 as completed
   history; update the Programme milestone matrix and the State Progression
   table accordingly.
2. Turn each framework candidate gate into a GA exit criterion routed to its
   owning collection.
3. Name the missing accountable owners (editorial, safeguarding, DPO,
   accessibility, product, go/no-go) and the Stage-3 impact bar.

## Promotion trigger

Owner direction to schedule the milestone-redraft pass. Until then this plan
stays in `future/` and the milestone files are unchanged.

## Non-goals

- Making product decisions — the keystones are owner-decided; any further forks
  (impact bar, minors handling) are named for the owner, not resolved here.
- Rewriting milestone files before promotion.
- Duplicating the framework or the report — this stub points to them.
