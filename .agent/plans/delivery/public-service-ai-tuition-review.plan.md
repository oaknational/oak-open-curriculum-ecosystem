# Independent review of the public-service AI tuition import (PR 66)

## Goal

PR 66 (branch `docs/public-service-ai-tuition-2026-09-06`; collection at SHA:5f04e4d0f, PR head
SHA:2db74f5bb) can be released or refused by the owner on first-hand evidence from a review
independent of the collection's author lineage: every import-fidelity property that the PR's own
validation surface cannot see has a verdict, every finding of the published author-lineage review
is mapped as accepted, partially accepted or refuted, and the decisions that are the owner's reach
the owner as one packet with the factors attached.

## User groups and value

- **The owner**: a hold decision that can be made from one card, with the two owner-only choices
  (the runner's cure; the external-repository visibility) and the settled-direction question
  carrying their factors rather than a recommendation.
- **The collection's author session**: the defects that can only be fixed at source (references
  by the fork's former repository name, a flat "settled" the collection's own chapter 12 grades
  as relayed, the seven content corrections the author-lineage review names, a planning chapter
  that never routes to the inclusion and rights chapters) returned with file and line, never
  edited in the byte-faithful files.
- **Future readers of the research surface** (agents and people): an OCE-side statement of what the
  collection's location does and does not confer, named as a factor with its home, so authority is
  not acquired by directory placement.
- **The estate**: the first review record for an ADR-226 import, with reproduction commands, and
  the two blind spots both reviews shared (external links; executables no gate reaches) named for
  the import procedure. Value offered, not need-proven: the procedure gains two lines only if the
  owner adopts them.

## Mechanism

A seat opened directly by the owner, under the estate's start-right-team foundation, with a
machine-local plan reviewed by a four-leg fleet-design review before pricing. The review's object
is the PR plus the author-lineage review that entered it; its instruments are seat-side
deterministic checks over the PR branch (an external-URL census, the internal-link count, the
runner's syntax, the workbook's zip integrity and formula census, the scanner reproduced in git
mode with token- and path-scoping controls, the tooling-coverage probes), a protected four-leg
frame gate adjudicated before any further spend, one audit leg per finding of the published
review, seat-inline checks of the engineering defect statements at the pinned revision and at one
pinned engraph tip, a correlated audit leg over the draft, and the seat's synthesis under the
Parallax contract (charter, frame set, conflict ledger, epistemic profile, world-return). Findings
route by type: cure-at-source to the author, decision-with-factors to the owner, already-homed to
the OCE record that owns it, disposition-as-intended with the control that proved it.
