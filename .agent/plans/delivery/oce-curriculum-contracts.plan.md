# Bind the first layered curriculum preservation contract

## Authority and pickup

Born sketch under the 10 October 2026 programme design. The owner commissioned
this design and documentary incorporation; implementation awaits this node's
ratification and the specific approvals its acceptance requires. Accepted direction
is [ADR-233](../../../docs/architecture/architectural-decisions/233-retained-framework-and-configurable-instances.md).
Read the [architecture](../../../docs/architecture/oce-architecture.md) and
[programme route](../../reports/repo-architecture/oce-rearchitecting/README.md)
for source scope and decision homes. No team measurement is commissioned.

## Goal

One public or synthetic curriculum entity, its revision, repeated occurrence and relationship assertions have complete definitions and explicit mappings from source through domain and app projections.

## User groups and value

Domain builders receive a complete small contract; the integration owners of the exploratory pedagogy project and the curriculum infrastructure consumer can assess their own required preservation without inheriting each other’s storage or product scope.

## Mechanism

Use the integrity seam matrix. Distinguish canonical entity, revision, occurrence, relation and assertion identity. Define authority, provenance, eligibility, missing/unknown values, ordering and multiplicity, correction/deletion, target constraints and intentional projection loss. Allocate enforcement to the selected public-source fixture, admitted store and wire contract. Identify source limitations honestly; do not mint a universal curriculum or learner model.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** — repo-safe — Independently expected fixtures preserve a repeated occurrence and two assertions; wrong identity, lost qualifier and unrepresentable target obligation reject explicitly.

- **AC2** — repo-safe — Every introduced representation has versioned complete definitions, meaning, provenance and an enforcement owner; loss/reversibility claims and correction paths are explicit.

- **AC3** — owner-held — The domain/profile owner records unresolved source eligibility, identity namespace or selected storage decisions before claims that rely on them. Public/synthetic design can proceed with bounded provisional identifiers.

## Todos

1. Author the independent semantic fixture and source-to-domain mapping.
2. Bind target representations and failure/correction behaviour; select only the contracts required by the first integrity slice.
3. Review the exploratory pedagogy project's C-DATA/PRIV obligations and the curriculum infrastructure consumer's boundary separately; return the approved profile to the Castr integration and integrity implementation nodes.

Each implementation PR is a bounded story with the repository’s default two-review-round
budget; this is a changeset control, not a study of teams or an output metric.

## Out of scope

No private records, learner modelling implementation, production migration, full ontology or host-context assurance.
