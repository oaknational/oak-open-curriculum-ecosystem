# Oak identity recognisability — a basis-driven transformation

slice pickup):
`.agent/reports/design/design-arc-plan-readiness-reviews-2026-08-13.md`.

## Goal

When this lands, the showcase's Oak identity is instantly recognisable
as Oak — judged against the canonical origin surfaces the owner named
(2026-08-13, in-session): the teacher and pupil experiences at
<www.thenational.academy> and Aila at labs.thenational.academy/aila — and
that recognisability is held by structure: no owner vigilance in the
operating loop. The named owner-card moments are: this plan's
ratification; threshold ratification at the S2 boundary; any DDR-008
exception card if invoked; and final sign-off.

Scope narrowing, named as a decision: the showcase is the measured
surface for this plan, with the capture corpus and every judgement
pinned to `identity=oak` explicitly (the default-face decision rides
the showcase-information-architecture plan and does not alter this
plan's measurements). The owner's criterion binds the repo-wide Oak
identity; the same identity-parametric instruments apply per-surface
(the hub, later demos) in follow-on arcs — the instruments are built
once here.

## Mechanism — a basis-driven transformation

The owner's frame (2026-08-13, in-session): defining a basis is the way
to define what we want to achieve, and to plan and measure the
transformation of our system to support the expression of the desired
parts of the origin system. Two dual bases:

**The expression basis** — the independent dimensions the framework can
render. First-hand delta against the kit (readiness review, pass B):
the kit ALREADY expresses most measured carriers — the double focus
ring, zero-blur offset shadows, the border-weight vocabulary, the
decorative-1..6 ramp across all five themes, the `[data-page]`
composition contract, and icon-role asset indirection — so most of the
visible drift is S3 VALUE re-truing, not new mechanism. The true gaps
are four, and only these are candidate S4 dimensions, each requiring a
recorded first-hand expressibility-gap demonstration plus consumer
evidence before it is built:

1. an illustration/geometry asset family with an identity-pack
   manifest (a zod-validated sibling manifest that EMITS custom
   properties, generalising the existing icon-role layer; DTCG
   `$extensions` only — the 2025.10 type set is closed and has no
   asset type);
2. a surface-class cascade axis (a scoped data-attribute axis
   generalising the existing `[data-page]` contract) — the carrier of
   band-rhythm and saturation policy, and the precondition for this
   plan's idiom probes;
3. a multi-tier masthead/chrome component with a general tier-role
   vocabulary;
4. ramp-usage and rotation policy (composition policy — distinct from
   the ramp VALUES, which are S3).

Mechanism is named in general parameters (ring count/thickness/offset,
tier roles, class→intensity policy); Oak's values live in the identity
pack. Each identity pack is its own package with its own licensing
surface — the S4 prerequisite that keeps reserved-marks content out of
the MIT kit root and makes the identity-№N test mechanical. New
mechanism lands in the kit's public-role model only (the second token
estate follows via its own semantic-merge thread, never a third
pattern). Composite-type adoption (shadow/border/typography) happens
inside the existing three tiers — an export upgrade, not a new
dimension.

**The measurement basis** — four recognition metrics on declared
orthogonal axes, gate CONJUNCTIVE, plus two guard conjuncts that sit
OUTSIDE the orthogonality count: the standing quality gates
(accessibility, contrast, token hygiene — a floor, not a likeness
axis) and the generality guard (below). Each metric declares its axis
and its distinct gaming vector (the admission rule's discharge):

| Layer | Axis | Gaming vector | Caught by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Binding conformance | declared structure (role→slot bindings, scale/weight relationships, recipe definitions) | conform on paper, render wrong | 2, 3 |
| 2 Idiom probes | rendered structure (presence AND prohibition, computed-style, per identity × theme) | idiom present but mangled | 3, 4 |
| 3 Motif fidelity | rendered geometry (aligned structural similarity at DDR-009 widths) | overfit to the chosen crops | 4, 1 |
| 4 Recognition trials | holistic perception (mark-masked lineups, fresh pinned judges) | keying on one cheap cue | 1, 2, 3 |

**Layer 1, doctrine-conformant.** The manifest declares role→role
relationships and shapes — which role binds where, the
decorative-order sequence, primary-action darkness, type-atom
relationships, ring and shadow RECIPES — never literal expressive
values. Expressive values are governed by DDR-007: conformance is a
perceptual band (each derived value differs from its reference under
inspection — lower bound — while remaining invisible in use — upper
bound), with derivability the proof and attribution rows for the
functional exact-match class. Provenance pin (DDR-006 forbids the
dependency, so no lockfile entry exists by design): the reference
repo URL + commit SHA + extraction date from a read-only capture,
committed with the manifest; refresh is a runbook step, never CI.

**Layer 2, both polarities.** Several carriers are ABSENCES (zero
blur, no display face in use, border-weight instead of elevation), so
the probe estate carries presence probes AND prohibition probes plus
usage-distribution probes (the type-ramp collapse is a usage property,
not a token value), evaluated against `getComputedStyle` on rendered
surfaces across the identity × theme matrix — theme-aware, since the
kit's own shadow policy legitimately differs per theme — never against
CSS source.

**Layer 3, witness-producing.** The fidelity pipeline is ratified
triage-only (diff magnitude never affects an exit code), and a
changed-pixel ratio would penalise exactly the DDR-007 perturbation we
are required to keep. So layer 3 GATES NOTHING in CI: it produces
register witnesses — structural similarity on per-region-aligned
luminance plus relationship conformance (contrast ratios, ramp
ordering) — adjudicated at round boundaries. Thresholds are method
constants recorded at S2 calibration; ratchet rows carry provenance
and are re-baselined, recorded, at each basis-version extension ("no
regression" binds within a basis version).

**Layer 4, register-shaped.** Trials execute out of band at a named
cadence (once per re-truing round); the repo-safe artefact is the
schema-validated trial register with the tally RECOMPUTED from rows.
Lineups mask marks (logo, wordmark, product names) — the declared
gaming-vector control — and distractors are licence-safe by
construction: our other identities (pds, emc2) plus single-carrier
ABLATIONS of the Oak face, which double as sensitivity probes of the
trial and of the carrier inventory. Judge model version is pinned;
trial n and its confidence bound are set at S2 calibration (a bare 4/5
at n=5 flaps and is not acceptable as a ratchet). Every trial record
and motif baseline is digest-bound to the artefact and judge it
witnessed; a stale witness reads as layer RED, never as layer absent.
The layer starts NON-BLOCKING and earns gate authority by measured
miss-rate against owner verdicts (the wow-verdict-register precedent).

**The generality guard (admission-rule row, outside the recognition
basis).** Axis: mechanism/identity ratio and the strategic node's own
falsifier. Gaming vector: "make the pack huge and call it config".
Control: every new S4 dimension must be exercised by a counter-brand
with a DIFFERENT strategy (the existing counter-identities are the
instrument), and a deliberately PARTIAL pack must render via declared
role-level fallbacks — the identity-№N regression, run whenever a
dimension lands.

**Gate dynamics (loop-exit-criteria).** Layers 1–2 run per-commit in
CI; layers 3–4 are register-adjudicated at round boundaries; the
conjunction is evaluated at adjudication moments. A layer disagreement
blocks, takes ONE bounded investigation cycle ending in a register row
(which metric mis-measured, cure applied); a recurring disagreement on
the same pair raises an owner card. S5 carries a round budget of four;
a persistently red layer at budget routes to the owner as evidence
about the METRIC, with the register trail attached. If S2's
calibration does not land a calibrated significance for layer 3, AC3
degrades to register-adjudicated evidence only and the recognition
basis is three metrics, recorded as such.

**Desired parts, not all parts.** The target is the origin's
target-state idioms (its current hand-drawn primitive generation), not
its legacy mid-migration surfaces nor its delivery accidents (remote
CDN asset dependence, hard-coded font family). We span where the
origin is going.

**Orthogonality admission rule (owner-ratified 2026-08-13,
in-session).** Every metric admitted to the set declares its axis and
its distinct gaming vector; a candidate sharing an axis with an
existing metric is rejected or explicitly replaces it; no metric
outranks another — the gate is conjunction. The owner's rationale,
near-verbatim: one proxy can be gamed; two high-quality orthogonal
proxies are very hard to game simultaneously; three are near
impossible. Canonical home: a design decision record authored in S1;
this plan cites it once landed.

**Relationship to design-system-completion.plan.md.** That ratified
plan owns the showcase page set (W1.4), identity asset delivery
(W2.8), identity authorship under the DDR-007 protocol (W2.9), and the
token-reference page (W4.4). This plan adds what it does not own: the
origin-referenced measurement basis and the Oak-face re-truing against
it. S4 mechanism work coordinates with W2.8/W2.9 — where a story
belongs there, it executes there; any re-homing is one owner card at
ratification, with dated amendments to the completion plan at his
word, never a silent fork.

**Sequencing (slice-level, not plan-wide).** S1 and S2 touch no
surface PR #846 changes and start immediately at ratification. S3–S5
re-true the switchboard estate and wait for #846 to merge (an event no
plan node represents; the review fleet — sanctioned and executing,
MCP-591 — precedes it). Citations to DDR-009/DDR-010 resolve on the
PR #846 branch and land with it; the measured variance-ratio true-up is
Director-ruled 2026-08-12 (comms event 4b5afe31; thread record
design-system-integration.next-session.md, S2b dispositions).

## Acceptance criteria (each with a proof — required)

1. Binding conformance green in CI against the committed manifest —
   structural rows exact; expressive rows inside their DDR-007 band
   with derivability proven. Exceptions only via DDR-008's closed
   predicate (owner card at invocation, registry row, expiry, re-card
   — there is no second conformance regime). Proof: `repo-safe` — the
   conformance validator this plan lands.
2. Idiom probes green — presence, prohibition, and usage-distribution
   probes for the ratified recognition set, per declared surface
   class, across the identity × theme matrix. Proof: `repo-safe` —
   the computed-style probe estate this plan lands.
3. Motif-fidelity witnesses at or above the method-constant thresholds
   recorded at S2 calibration within the current basis version (or the
   recorded three-metric degradation if calibration fails),
   adjudicated at round boundaries. Proof: `repo-safe` — the register
   rows with digest-bound provenance; the pipeline itself never gates.
4. Recognition-trial register at or above the ratified threshold with
   its stated confidence bound; binding only after control calibration
   and earned authority per §Mechanism. Proof: `repo-safe` — the
   schema-validated register with recomputed tally.
5. Final sign-off. Proof: `owner-held` — Jim, in Chrome, on the running
   showcase; recorded in the design-lane thread record with date.

## Out of scope

- Cloning origin pages — the showcase demonstrates the system; it is
  not a rebuild of the origin application.
- The origin's legacy idioms and delivery accidents (its superseded
  brush-border generation, remote-CDN asset dependence, hard-coded
  font family) — desired parts only.
- Expressive-value equality with the origin — DDR-007 forbids it; the
  band conformance above is the deliberate replacement.
- Making Oak the showcase's default face — that decision rides the
  showcase-information-architecture plan; this plan's measurements pin
  `identity=oak` regardless of the default.
- Whole-page cross-site similarity scores — structurally different
  pages make them noise; motif-level comparison only.
- Re-truing the hub and other demo surfaces — same instruments,
  follow-on arcs, named in §Goal.
- Changes to the DTCG token model itself (custom `$type`s) — the
  2025.10 type set is closed; assets ride the pack manifest.
