# Integrate and construct Castr C-1, then replace the qualified generator boundary

## Authority and pickup

Born sketch under the 10 October 2026 programme design. The owner commissioned
this design and documentary incorporation; implementation awaits this node's
ratification and the specific approvals its acceptance requires. Accepted direction
is [ADR-233](../../../docs/architecture/architectural-decisions/233-retained-framework-and-configurable-instances.md).
Read the [architecture](../../../docs/architecture/oce-architecture.md) and
[programme route](../../reports/repo-architecture/oce-rearchitecting/README.md)
for source scope and decision homes. No team measurement is commissioned.

## Goal

The admitted C-1 profile is generated by one qualified Castr path, with all affected OCE consumers and production correspondence verified and superseded generator execution retired.

## User groups and value

SDK and application builders receive coherent generated contracts with located failure rather than divergent generators or silent widening.

## Mechanism

After the profile decision and source-owned approval, follow MEASURE→MOVE→PLOT: integrate the agreed workspace group using code/history only where the measurement justifies it, otherwise carry the approved specification into fresh workspaces. Under Castr source authority, design the complete model and author finite construction legs, implement the admitted C-1 compiler path, and qualify it against independent source/profile fixtures. Actual capability readiness gates consumer reliance, not the earlier integration or construction. Then migrate only approved outputs and exercise emitted TypeScript/Zod/endpoint contracts and consuming clients. Preserve independent source-semantic oracles. Keep OCE MCP generation and openapi-fetch. Remove obsolete adapter/rewrite paths only when their responsibilities and consumers are accounted for.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** — repo-safe — Independent profile fixtures pass through actual generated/imported/used artifacts; status/media/default and invalid-input semantics agree with the approved source contract.

- **AC2** — repo-safe — All affected SDK/codegen/MCP consumers pass their relevant evidence; output has source/profile/compiler identity and deterministic reproducibility.

- **AC3** — repo-safe — Old generator/rewrite authority is removed for migrated outputs, retained outputs are explicit, and Castr’s agreed standalone boundary is mechanically enforced.

## Todos

1. Verify source-owned approvals and construct-outcome measurement; choose the authorised source/specification move and finite model/construction plan.
2. Integrate the agreed isolated group, construct and qualify the admitted C-1 compiler capability, then exercise actual consumer behaviour before replacing its generator.
3. Retire replaced paths and publish the supported generated-contract statement.

Each implementation PR is a bounded story with the repository’s default two-review-round
budget; this is a changeset control, not a study of teams or an output metric.

## Out of scope

No universal IR, unqualified later formats, broad MCP compiler transfer or external package publish. The Castr owner governs source ratification and model decisions; this node owns the OCE receiving integration/construction/replacement path. Source-repository archiving requires separate explicit authority.
