# Bind the first CF construction and assurance profile

## Authority and pickup

Born sketch under the 10 October 2026 programme design. The owner commissioned
this design and documentary incorporation; implementation awaits this node's
ratification and the specific approvals its acceptance requires. Accepted direction
is [ADR-233](../../../docs/architecture/architectural-decisions/233-retained-framework-and-configurable-instances.md).
Read the [architecture](../../../docs/architecture/oce-architecture.md) and
[programme route](../../reports/repo-architecture/oce-rearchitecting/README.md)
for source scope and decision homes. No team measurement is commissioned.

## Goal

A finite geometry responsibility has an executable contract and an agreed route from its laws to production source and consumed forms; every qualification obligation has an instrument or an explicit blocking design choice.

## User groups and value

Foundation builders receive a startable bounded construction; downstream capability authors receive an honest reliance contract without waiting for the whole foundation estate.

## Mechanism

Use the retained CF reconstruction specification and the geometry-to-heap contract. Bind the inherited exact finite domain (length zero through `2^32 - 1`, with no 32-bit narrowing); do not reopen it without explicit authority. Select the outcome algebra, grade and substrate; map every operation and rejection to laws. Decide proof/verification method, trusted transformations, native-source and packed execution profiles, and exhaustive dependency/exposure enforcement. Classify the unratified definite-architecture proposals separately. The decision owns this first unit, not a universal proof platform.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** — repo-safe — A reviewed contract fixture specifies finite domains, arithmetic bounds, successes and every invalid input, independently of candidate implementation; review checks totality and a minimal counterexample.

- **AC2** — repo-safe — An assurance allocation names proof/model technique, production correspondence, exact source/toolchain identity, source and emitted closure, boundary-negative probes, consumed forms and invalidation triggers. No sampled test is counted as universal proof.

- **AC3** — owner-held — The foundation and repository contract owners record the binding and any ratification needed for the first unit in the plan review; unaccepted N01–N12 choices remain explicitly unadopted.
