# Bind four wider domain-service contract records

## Goal

The four concrete records in [wider capability continuity](../../../docs/architecture/oce-wider-capabilities.md)
have exact selected scopes, owning authorities, public/synthetic examples and
finite construction interfaces. The B01–B16 obligations remain distinct and
actionable without treating a lifecycle framework as all domain implementation.

## User groups and value

Editorial, educational, research and standards owners receive explicit boundaries
for future capabilities. Builders receive complete small contracts; public MCP
and CF authors are not blocked by unrelated protected-service choices.

## Mechanism

Reuse the first curriculum fixture for retrieval/receipt. Bind one synthetic
authoring change→review→edition, one teacher-authorised activity→qualified return,
and one public/synthetic evidence→decision→correction record. Each names model,
identity/definitions, immutable versions, authority/purpose, permitted loss,
configuration versus new mechanism, consumed forms, compatible profiles, failure,
withdrawal and remedy. Preserve adopted teacher-led mastery/Bayesian direction;
choose estimator and educational policies only with their legitimate owner.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — repo-safe:** all four records include independent valid and adverse
  fixtures from the permanent specification, with complete definitions and
  accountable producer, validator, receiver and correction responsibilities.
- **AC2 — repo-safe:** the B map routes every material gap to a maintained contract
  or finite successor construction interface. No graph, lifecycle, compiler or
  general evidence mechanism claims to supply editorial/educational authority.
- **AC3 — owner-held:** the relevant domain/service owners record exact approved
  scope and blocked protected/educational decisions. Missing approval blocks the
  corresponding stronger service; public/synthetic specification stays possible.

## Todos

1. Bind the selected operation/profile for each of the four already specified
   records; reuse existing curriculum semantics rather than invent a universal IR.
2. Work its adverse trace through actual representation/authority boundaries and
   record exact unsupported outcomes, receipt, correction and remedy endings.
3. Return finite construction interfaces and acceptance cases to their domain
   owners and reusable facility owners; author the selected successor slice at pickup.

Use bounded story PRs and the default two-round review rule. No participant or
team study is commissioned, and no timings or staffing commitments are inferred.

## Out of scope

No full tuition/authoring service implementation, protected-data admission,
participant research, national learner store, universal workflow/evidence engine
or whole-catalogue gate. This node remains a proposed design binding, not ratification
of those products. The curriculum infrastructure consumer and the exploratory pedagogy project keep their independently governed scope.
