## Why this exists (diagnosis)

`practice-lineage.md` is **hard** (not critical): 849 content lines / hard 830 (critical 1245);
50,085 chars / hard 48,500. It holds 19 axioms + 28 active principles. The over-limit is a
**missing-graduation symptom**, not raw growth: §Active Principles is a buffer whose upward drains
(active→axiom when internalised; active→PDR/rule where the substance belongs) almost never run, so
full-narrative principles accumulate to the ceiling and stall. The 'culture as transmission of
disposition' principle added 2026-06-21 (a legitimate graduation) was the tipping addition.

## The plan (conservation-first)

1. **Compress internalised actives to axioms** (t2) — the file's own Growth Governance mechanism.
   Substance kept; narrative retired because absorbed. Honest 3+-repo bar caveat: this lever may be
   smaller than 28 suggests.
2. **Graduate decision/rule-shaped actives OUT** (t3) — PDR-046 §Move 3 (graduate upward, don't
   compress). Route by INTENT (the #4 correction below). This drains the list AND fixes homes.
3. **Split** (t6) is the OWNER-GATED fallback if still over — a Core-contract change (PDR + owner
   approval), never a unilateral edit.

## Governing constraints — the loss-prone part (DO NOT re-make this session's mistakes)

These are the owner's four corrections from 2026-06-21 and the standing doctrine that governs the
pass. They are the most likely things to be lost in compaction:

- **Promote and assess, never gate on approval** (correction 1; PDR-104, PDR-101). Author the
  graduations best-effort; the review quorum is the quality gate, not owner pre-approval. Do NOT
  register settled doctrine as owner-gated decision-debt, and do NOT excuse an over-target count as
  "by design" (correction 3).
- **Route by intent, not by menu** (correction 4). Each substance has ONE correct home by its
  intent: directives = how agents operate here; host ADRs = this repo's product architecture; PDRs
  = portable Practice-governance; governance docs (development-practice.md) = host dev discipline;
  axioms = internalised one-line principles. Determine the home by understanding; do not offer the
  owner a choice of doc-types.
- **No tombstones** (correction 2; no-tombstones-for-removed-ideas; PDR-091 §tombstone note). When
  an active principle leaves the list (compressed to axiom or graduated out), the destination IS
  the record — do not leave a "moved to X" memorial or an "X — not Y" negation-contrast. A removed
  incorrect concept is removed, not enshrined.
- **Conservation over fitness** (learning-before-fitness axiom). Compress a narrative to an axiom
  ONLY when it is genuinely internalised; NEVER trim/summarise narrative to hit the line/char
  budget. If honest graduation does not bring it under hard, that is the split decision (t6), not a
  licence to compress.
- **fitness_line_limit is owner-only.** Raising the hard limit re-opens the anti-gaming channel the
  triad closed; the role-cure for a registry overage is graduate/split, not raise. Not a first move.
- **PDR-003**: the MAIN agent authors Core (practice-lineage, PDRs) — never a sub-agent. Sub-agents
  ASSESS (read-only).
- **PDR-106 is reserved** for the Closure & Role-Routing doctrine (unauthored); if t3 mints a PDR,
  use the next free number, not 106.
