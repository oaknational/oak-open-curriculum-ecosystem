# Free Play

**Governance**: the divergent partner to
[`concept-exploration`](../concept-exploration/SKILL-CANONICAL.md) —
exploration converges raw observations toward a problem frame and warranted
proposals; play diverges through material with no frame at all and sees what
appears. The pair forms a border: anything play grows a shape for crosses
into concept-exploration; anything exploration cannot yet name may belong in
play. Imported and adapted 2026-07-20 from the Resonance estate (a private
sibling Practice repo — no public upstream URL exists; source repo-relative
path `.agent/skills/free-play/SKILL-CANONICAL.md`; AIP-142;
PDR-125 exchange); this estate's founding worked instance is the free-play
pass inside the 2026-07-20 merge-drive retrospective (three kept seeds, one
visible discard). Grounded in the variation half of
variation–selection–retention (an estate rich in selection instruments needs
a deliberate source of variation) and in the sensemaking caution that
premature formalisation forecloses what the vocabulary cannot yet say.

## Use When

- The owner hands over material and asks for play, wandering, or "see what
  you notice".
- A body of material (research, a codebase area, transcripts, a day's
  events) deserves an unpremeditated encounter before anyone frames it.
- There is no honest intent to converge yet — concept-exploration
  presupposes observations you mean to shape into a problem frame; play is
  where material lives before anyone means anything by it.

Not for formed problems, live incidents, or anything with a deadline in the
current window: play under pressure is neither play nor work.

## The Contract (inverted, deliberately)

- **Entry**: material + a time-box + a capture surface. That is all. No
  problem statement, no target, no hypothesis — writing one before exit is
  breaking the skill.
- **During**: wander. The moves are openers, not stages, in any order, none
  required:
  - **Juxtapose** — hold two unrelated pieces side by side; what happens?
  - **Invert** — read a thing backwards: what if the fix is the defect, the
    exception the rule, the failure the feature?
  - **Analogise** — "this is shaped like…" from any domain, the further the
    better.
  - **Remind** — "what does this remind me of?", followed honestly.
  - **Follow surprise** — whatever raised an eyebrow gets the next minute.
  - **Sit still** — reread something slowly for no reason.

## The Confabulation Guard

Seeds are **associations, never findings**. The harvest marks each item as
"this reminded me of…" / "these look shaped alike" — a report of an
association event, which is always true — never as "this is" / "these are
connected", which is a claim requiring evidence it does not have. At the
harvest, give each seed one second look: keep what still hums, and
**discard what was forced — visibly**, in the harvest itself. A discarded
association is proof the guard ran; a harvest with no discards, ever, is
suspicious. The second look is metacognition's fluency tripwire applied to
associations — the smoother a connection arrived, the harder the look (the
metacognition directive §Fluency Is a Warning); the harvest is the one
moment play borrows the foundational pair, and only there.

## Routing the Harvest

- A seed with a growing shape → [`concept-exploration`](../concept-exploration/SKILL-CANONICAL.md)
  (it can now name its contribution — the border is crossed honestly).
- A small sharp observation → the napkin, marked as a play seed.
- Most seeds → nowhere, and that is correct.
- **"Nothing appeared" is a valid, complete outcome.** Say it plainly and
  stop. This outcome must actually occur sometimes: a play practice that
  always returns insights is confabulating, and the practice — not the
  player — is then broken.

## The Success Test (also inverted)

Play succeeds over a SEASON, not a session: it has paid its way if an
occasional seed, honestly marked and routed, later matures somewhere that
matters — and if its empty-handed exits were reported as plainly as its
fruitful ones. The failure mode is forced profundity: profound-sounding
connections manufactured because a harvest felt obligatory. (Falsifier for
the skill itself: if after a season of use no play seed has matured
anywhere AND sessions never exit empty, the skill is producing theatre —
retire it.)
