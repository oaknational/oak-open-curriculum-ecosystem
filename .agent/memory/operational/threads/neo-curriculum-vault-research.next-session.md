## Recursive Metaloss Scan

Preserving the conclusions alone would still lose the semantics that keep them
safe:

- **Intent provenance:** broad audit findings were deliberately excluded after
  an owner reframe; they were not overlooked or refuted.
- **Evidence altitude:** “actual value” means publicly demonstrated artefact and
  operating-model value, not independently proven learner or commissioner
  outcomes.
- **Negative authority:** the opportunity list is exploratory and reversible;
  it carries no authority to contact NEO, exchange learner data, ingest content,
  endorse mappings, create a plan, or promise support.
- **Omission semantics:** absence of a report claim may mean non-goal, unknown,
  or negative evidence. A reviewer must identify which before treating silence
  as a defect.
- **Snapshot semantics:** commit pins make the analysis reproducible, not
  current forever. A later upstream change does not retroactively falsify a
  correctly described snapshot.
- **Review semantics:** “have another agent review it” means falsify the report
  against its declared contract. It does not mean invent a new brief and grade
  the report against that.
- **Compression risk:** removing warrants, falsifiers, counterinterpretations,
  or the reasoning trace would make the report shorter while increasing the
  chance that a future reader crystallises a possibility into a decision.
- **Handoff self-decay:** a handoff without a byte hash and write-time evidence
  can become a stale confidence signal. The hash above is a checkpoint, not a
  promise that later bytes are identical.

The recursion stops at irreducible decision, evidence, authority, and
continuation semantics. Discarded draft wording, superseded status snapshots,
temporary build artefacts, and private reasoning texture are intentionally
ephemeral; conserving them would bury the information a successor needs.
