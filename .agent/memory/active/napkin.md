## Audit-script output is input-to-verify, even your own (2026-06-19, Finch binds Halo)

Running the 7c.6 retired-banner check, a quick awk "first body line after frontmatter" reported 2 of 3
retired records as missing their retirement banner. First-hand reading showed ALL THREE had banners —
the awk grabbed the `# Heading` line and stopped, two lines above the `> **RETIRED…**` blockquote. Had I

nearest-content heuristic is the tell. Sibling: [[fidelity-audit-is-not-currency-audit]].
