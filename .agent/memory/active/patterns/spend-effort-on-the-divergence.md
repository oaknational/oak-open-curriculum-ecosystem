> **POLARITY: PATTERN.** When authoritative external research agrees with
> what you built, the agreement is the LEAST informative output. It feels
> good and teaches nothing.

## The shape

When external research, a benchmark, or a best-practice source aligns
with the repo, spend the effort on the **divergence**:

- Where the Practice is genuinely ahead (state it, but briefly).
- **More important**: the gaps the research names that the repo has *not*
  closed. State those as plainly as the wins.

## The cure

Treat agreement as a prompt to go looking for what the source knows that
you don't, not as validation to bank. The convenient half of the
comparison is the half to distrust. Sibling:
[`feedback_ground_convenient_claims`].
