# Adversarially Verify Your Own Synthesis in Long Analytical Work

Long analytical work drifts under its own momentum: the newest or narrowest
input silently captures the frame, and a fluent self-synthesis over-states.
The author cannot feel this happening — fluency reads as confirmation.

*(Graduated single-instance per [PDR-100](../../../practice-core/decision-records/PDR-100-decision-debt-as-a-first-class-pillar.md): synthesis-drift is a named family with multiple homed siblings, and the lenses give a clear answer, so this does not wait for a second occurrence.)*

## Pattern

Two habits, self-applied **on a cadence**, not when the owner intervenes:

1. **Re-ask the altitude question periodically:** *Am I still answering the
   BROAD question at the right altitude? Has the newest / most-concrete input
   captured the frame? Am I over-claiming?*
2. **Run an independent, adversarial pass over your OWN conclusions before
   presenting them as settled, and report what it DENTS.** Default the refuter
   to "refute this"; a synthesis that survives a genuine refutation attempt is
   worth more than one that was never challenged.

A validated map is valuable even when it re-derives a known verdict — **do not
sell re-derivation as discovery.**

## Anti-pattern

Presenting accumulated synthesis as settled because it arrived fluently and
internally cohered; treating the absence of a challenge as the presence of
correctness.
