> **POLARITY: PATTERN.** Suffering the exact gap you are curing, while
> curing it, is confirmation — and free requirements capture.

## The interpretive move

When the system building a capability hits the capability's absence in real
time, read it two ways at once:

1. **Directional confirmation.** The work is aimed at a real, live gap — the
   builders are the users (FRAME-1: the Practice as a meta-learning loop; the
   proof is in the dogfooding — `docs/strategy/stream-agentic-framework.md`).
2. **Requirements capture.** Every friction hit during construction IS the
   next backlog item, observed under real load rather than speculated.
   Route it to the frictions register / the capability's plan in the moment.

The corollary check: a protective mechanism that fails to fire on the very
session that installs it is the same signal inverted — evidence the mechanism
needs a structural firing moment, not more prose (PDR-098).
