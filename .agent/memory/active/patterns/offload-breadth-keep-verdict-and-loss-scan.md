> **POLARITY: PATTERN.** A deep-context session keeps landing high-value
> work by routing the breadth out and holding only what cannot leave its
> head.

## The shape

Route the heavy reading, drafting, mechanical edits, and gate runs to
fresh-context workflows and parallel sub-agents. Hold only the two
**non-offloadable possessions**:

- the **design verdict** (the call that needs the accumulated context),
- the **first-hand loss-scan** (only the context-holder can detect what
  durable substance is not yet homed — a fresh reader can verify a claim
  but cannot detect loss).

## The cure

Offload everything else **provided every returned output is critically
assessed first-hand** — that screen catches false "it's already tracked"
claims, false alarms, and cross-agent pointer mismatches. Caveat: a lane
needing security-sensitive refactors still needs a genuinely fresh seat;
re-spinning a spent session does not reset its budget, only breadth-work
parallelises. Sibling:
[`feedback_context_loss_probe_is_first_hand_only`], [`feedback_first_hand_means_me_not_subagents`].
