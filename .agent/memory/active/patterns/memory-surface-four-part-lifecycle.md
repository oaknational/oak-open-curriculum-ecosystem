> **POLARITY: PATTERN.** This entry names a *shape to repeat*, not a failure mode to avoid.
>
> See [`patterns/README.md` § Polarity](README.md#polarity-required-every-pattern) for the polarity discipline.

Concept stage (PDR-134): **candidate**. One estate, one consolidation; promoted to working
when a second surface class is curated by it without amendment.

## The pattern

A memory surface that accumulates entries is curated only when it has all four parts:

1. **A role** — what the surface is for, in one sentence, so a reader can tell live content
   from finished content.
2. **A disposition** for finished content — where its substance goes and what happens to
   the residue (here: graduate, then archive).
3. **A trigger** — the moment and the named workflow step that run the disposition. A
   runbook nobody invokes is not a trigger.
4. **A proof** that nothing was lost — a byte-identity check on the archive, a token check
   on the live surface, and a re-read of any passage that speaks about its neighbour.

A surface missing any one part fails in a recognisable way: no role, and live and finished
content cannot be told apart; no disposition, and curation is improvised each time; no
trigger, and the surface grows; no proof, and curation loses knowledge silently.

## Evidence

Read on 2026-09-17 during a dedicated consolidation: the napkin had all four parts and
stayed curated across more than a hundred rotations. `repo-continuity.md` and the thread
records had a role, a disposition and a runbook, and no skill or rule invoked the runbook;
they grew to critical on every fitness axis. The estate-coordination record, the
continuity-memory record and `frictions-register.md` had no declared disposition at all.
The runbook's token proof also missed a curated passage's statement about its neighbour,
which is why the proof has three legs.

The owner's decision on the disposition ("Graduate, then archive", 2026-09-17) and the
guard on the trigger ("archiving happens ONLY after full processing, not as a means to tick
a box", 2026-09-19) are recorded in the directive; the trigger is the `consolidate-docs`
skill's step 6b.

## Falsifier

After the trigger landed (2026-09-19), the large records still grow past their thresholds
across two consolidations without step 6b firing, or a surface curated under all four parts
still loses a fact. Either sends this pattern back for amendment.
