## The pattern

When a gate sitting or decision packet reaches the owner:

1. **Run every question through the Decision Lenses**
   (`principles.md` §Decision Lenses) and the corpus of prior owner rulings
   first.
2. **Record what they decisively resolve** as resolved, with the lens or
   ruling cited — these are presented as verdicts, not questions
   (`present-verdicts-not-menus`).
3. **Surface only the survivors** — questions the lenses genuinely cannot
   close — as `AskUserQuestion` items, each with a recommended verdict and
   its reasoning attached.

The owner's sitting then spends its minutes on real forks. The matrix filter
is the packet-scale application of `present-verdicts-not-menus`; it composes
with PDR-074's owner-attention economics.

Graduated at n=1 (PDR-100/README single-instance licence) on a recorded
distinguishing criterion: the shape is owner-directed and its recurrence is
guaranteed by named upcoming sittings (G2/G3/OG-2) — unlike
speculative-topology candidates, whose second instance may never arrive and
whose rejection costs nothing when their operational substance is homed
elsewhere.
