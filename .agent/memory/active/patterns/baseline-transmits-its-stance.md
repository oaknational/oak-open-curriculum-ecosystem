> **POLARITY: ANTI-PATTERN.** This entry names a *failure mode to avoid*,
> not a shape to repeat.
>
> See [`patterns/README.md` § Polarity](README.md#polarity-required-every-pattern)
> for the polarity discipline.

## The failure shape

A document handed to a judge or a fleet as its baseline carries two things:
its facts and its **stance** — the editorial choices, the framing, the
"keep as thin leaf" verdicts that were never separately ratified. Legs
inherit the stance as if it were a fact, because nothing in the brief
distinguishes the two. A ratification is the strongest carrier of all: a
brief that says "the ratified structure must be respected" turns every
reviewer into the incumbent's advocate, and the space is never searched.

Two instances named it in four days. Round-1 census judges given the
2026-04-28 matrix reproduced its stance in their target states; round 2
centred the question instead, and the difference was dramatic. Three days
later, fleet briefs seeded with deference to prior rulings produced panels
that graded the incumbent — the owner's diagnosis, verbatim:
"self-congratulatory theater" — while the anchor-free legs (a cold pass
forbidden the corpus; raw git measurement) were the only work that
survived, and they independently agreed with the owner's reopening word.
Deference contamination is anchor propagation one level up.

## The cure

- **Centre the question, never the predecessor.** The brief states the
  question and the evidence bar; a prior document enters as ONE candidate
  among others, never as the frame.
- **When the owner reopens a space, forbid deference inside it.** "The
  original target architecture was WRONG" strips authority from every
  prior ruling in the reopened space; briefs say so explicitly, admit only
  first-hand verified mechanism facts and measurements as evidence, and
  let prior decisions compete as ordinary candidates.
- **Protect only the owner's NUMBERED ratified decisions** from
  re-litigation (invoke-code-experts §Delegation Snapshot); plan-authored
  elaborations stay refutable.
- **Keep the owner's verbatim words as the governing text**, and mark the
  seat's own prose as challengeable — the anti-deference brief shape that
  held at the next panel.

## The tell

A fleet whose legs converge on the incumbent with fresh signatures and no
counter-proposal has probably been anchored. Convergence is not
corroboration when every leg read the same baseline first.
