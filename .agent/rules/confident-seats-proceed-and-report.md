# Confident seats proceed and report

Owner ruling (2026-07-27, during the review-loop untangling): a Director backup shape is to be
designed, *"but certainly if an agent is confident in their choice they could have kept going."*
The worked instance this cures: a seat froze for nine hours on an exact head awaiting a Director
ruling its own analysis already supported, re-pinging into a dead session.

## The rule

- **A seat confident in its choice PROCEEDS and reports** — it does not block on a Director
  ruling it has already asked for. The ask and the work continue in parallel; the Director's
  answer, when it lands, either confirms (no cost) or redirects (the cost of a redirect on a
  recorded, reasoned choice is bounded and acceptable — the cost of a frozen lane is not).
- **Confidence carries its record**: the proceeding seat states the choice, the reasoning, and
  the falsifier in comms at the moment of proceeding, so a later redirect has a clean seam to
  land on. An unrecorded confident choice is momentum, not confidence.
- **A wrong confident call is a capture, not a sin.** Blocking-by-default is the defect this
  rule removes; a reasoned proceed that gets redirected produced information. Capture it and
  move.

## The boundary (owner-confirmed 2026-07-27)

The grant covers in-lane decisions: scope calls, review triage under the pr-lifecycle triage
rule, base updates, sequencing within the seat's own claim, tooling choices. It does NOT cover:

- **freeze-bound surfaces** (served surface, auth path, landing page — these take the Director
  or owner word whoever executes);
- **merge execution** at settled follows the standing merge doctrine, not this rule's grant.
  That doctrine (owner verbatim, 2026-07-26/29, superseding the earlier word/key-turn
  topology this bullet once recorded): a green and clean PR — CI passing, no unresolved
  comments — merges without owner approval, and any resulting problem makes
  recurrence-prevention the immediate priority. Settled-state derivation and the bot-identity
  REST-merge mechanics live in the pr-lifecycle skill; freeze-bound surfaces above stay
  reserved regardless;
- anything the estate's rules already reserve to the owner (credentials, destructive
  operations, doctrine ratification, milestone placement).

Low confidence remains what it always was: route the question and hold ONLY what the answer
genuinely gates — never the whole lane.
