An agent taking the short orientation as authoritative could reject a legitimate architecture comparison or constrain a first activity unnecessarily. The recovered project directions establish the service/POC commitment, PaaS direction and preservation requirements; they did not establish an explicit owner ratification of this architecture. **Recommended treatment:** identify the kernel/profile architecture consistently as the source’s recommended design. Keep requirements with independent authority—educational integrity, access, human authority and controlled inference—distinct from this proposed way of satisfying them. An exact subsequent owner decision would resolve the status differently. No architecture redesign is required to correct the record.

### R2 — Align the LearnLM summary with the collection’s own evidence judgement

**Priority: medium. Confidence: high.**

The [LearnLM catalogue row](https://github.com/EngraphCode/open-curriculum-ecosystem/blob/5f04e4d0f717863857a2f681e6992263618709f4/.agent/research/public-service-ai-tuition/10-comparative-research-and-evidence.md#L189) says the supervised system was “at least comparable with human tutors”. Its [detailed account](https://github.com/EngraphCode/open-curriculum-ecosystem/blob/5f04e4d0f717863857a2f681e6992263618709f4/.agent/research/public-service-ai-tuition/10-comparative-research-and-evidence.md#L887) correctly declines equivalence.

The exploratory Bayesian study estimates a positive 5.5 percentage-point difference in same-session next-unit performance, with a 95% credible interval from −1.4 to +12.4 points and a 93.6% posterior probability of a positive effect. Every model draft was reviewed by a human. Those are promising, bounded findings; they do not establish a non-inferiority or equivalence result. The paper’s abstract itself uses the optimistic framing, so this is an interpretation correction rather than newly discovered contrary trial data. [Primary LearnLM paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23633v1).

The summary table is a likely retrieval and comparison surface. A stronger shorthand can travel without its detailed qualification even when both are present in the same file.

**Recommended treatment:** state the positive estimate, interval, immediate outcome and fully supervised configuration directly. Preserve the promising result without converting it into an established comparable-or-better conclusion.

## Findings for later decision use and minor correction

### R5 — Define the economic estimand behind “retained-benefit learner”

**Priority: medium before economic decision use; research-design clarification.**

[Chapter 04, line 357](https://github.com/EngraphCode/open-curriculum-ecosystem/blob/5f04e4d0f717863857a2f681e6992263618709f4/.agent/research/public-service-ai-tuition/04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md#L357) requires cost per retained-benefit learner. The primary study proposal estimates a continuous mean intention-to-treat effect. That effect does not identify which individuals benefited causally or how many did. Requiring a denominator and uncertainty is sound, but the relevant quantity remains undefined.

This is an identification issue, not just a missing decimal place. Even binary treatment/control success rates generally identify a net response difference, not the number of individual causal beneficiaries, without further assumptions.

**Recommended treatment before economic analysis:** specify an identifiable measure, such as incremental cost per prespecified outcome-scale gain or a justified responder-outcome risk difference. If the denominator is observed threshold achievers, label it descriptive and retain the comparison. This does not block preservation of the research proposal or require redesigning the study during this PR review.

It includes durable knowledge and independence alongside epistemic judgement, agency, equity and wellbeing. Mathematics, English, practical/creative subjects, resits and lifelong contexts require different activities and evidence. The valuable AI possibilities include manipulating and comparing representations, investigating and revising explanations, creating and critiquing work, and practising with tools. Oak content is an established asset; the additional work concerns how that knowledge becomes useful activity, feedback and independent capability.

The documents distinguish delivered support from selected support, assisted correctness from learning, and immediate performance from delayed unaided outcomes. ### How the cognitive constructs changed the review

OCE’s original metacognition directive, reason, concept exploration, proportionality, free-play and Parallax audit/synthesis guidance informed the work. The review used source-dependent, parallel documentary scrutiny; the reviewers are correlated through shared instructions, corpus and model lineage. They are not independent professional or empirical assurance.

- **Metacognition changed the object being judged.** A faithful research import cannot be judged as a completed service. Conversely, faithful bytes alone cannot establish usefulness or correct authority. Both questions needed separate answers.
- **Reason exposed the consequential warrants.** The important checks were what a summary allows a reader to infer, what a mean treatment effect can identify, and whether preserved dependencies remain recoverable. Real reproduction retired probe uncertainty more decisively than further textual suspicion.
- **Concept exploration and proportionality prevented scope inflation.** The collection’s future ideas and detailed contracts were tested for status and use, not rejected for length or converted into implementation requirements.
- **Parallax preserved different scales of evidence.** Schema, source behaviour, delivered activity, learning, organisational uptake and public value were reviewed as different claims. Multiple reports did not count as independent support.
- **Divergent reading supplied associations, not findings.** Graph/practice learning loops and educational feedback have useful structural similarities. That does not justify one shared learner/agent-state model; domain meaning and authority remain separate.
- **Adversarial synthesis tested the favourable conclusion.** The most credible challenge was that historical preservation might reintroduce superseded guidance. R4 survives that test. Tempting critiques of every historical code limitation, the 197 ideas and missing implementation were rejected because their claimed authority did not support those critiques.

The reusable learning signal is that concept preservation can fail at navigation and authority boundaries even when all bytes and mechanical links are sound. Future reader checks should ask the successor to recover a dependency’s meaning, identify whether a recommendation is adopted, and find any known successor. This review records that signal; it does not amend OCE’s skills or doctrine.
