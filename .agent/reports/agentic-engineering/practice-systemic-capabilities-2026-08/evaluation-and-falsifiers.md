# Evaluation and falsifiers

## Purpose

The findings in this report are theories and proposals. They should not become doctrine merely
because they form a coherent narrative.

Evaluation must answer four different questions:

1. **Semantic adequacy** — do the concepts describe real work without distortion?
2. **Operational value** — do they reduce ambiguity, duplication, failure, or attention cost?
3. **System effect** — do they improve human control, learning, recovery, and useful outcomes?
4. **Evolutionary value** — do they enable old machinery to disappear, or only add another layer?

A proposal that improves internal consistency while increasing total work is not a success.

## Evaluation principles

### Measure before and after one real slice

Do not estimate the whole Practice from diagrams. Select one bounded, consequential episode and
compare the current method with the proposed semantics and tooling.

### Separate evidence timescales

- immediate safety and validity;
- one work episode;
- recurring Practice learning;
- slow product and mission effect.

A fast operational metric cannot prove long-term impact. Slow evidence cannot delay an immediate
safety decision.

### Preserve missing-data meaning

No event can mean:

- no demand;
- failed instrumentation;
- unavailable capability;
- work abandoned before emission;
- privacy suppression;
- projection delay.

Every measure must name which interpretations remain possible.

### Avoid individual performance monitoring

Practice evidence should assess systems, workflows, capabilities, and aggregate outcomes. It must
not become covert worker or agent-operator performance scoring. Any people-derived signal requires
separate approved purpose, minimisation, access, retention, transparency, and lawful-basis review.

### Include deletion as an outcome

Every additive experiment names the instructions, statuses, indexes, checks, or procedures expected
to disappear if it succeeds.

## Core evaluation dimensions

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Truth** | Can participants establish the current authoritative world and its provenance? |
| **Identity** | Can one logical obligation and its attempts be followed across surfaces? |
| **Authority** | Is it clear who may act, correct, cancel, supersede, and decide? |
| **Liveness** | Can absence and partial failure be classified without over-reading green signals? |
| **Recovery** | Can interrupted work be reconstructed truthfully from durable evidence? |
| **Corrigibility** | Can a human redirect or stop work quickly without corrupting history? |
| **Efficiency** | Does total human and agent attention per trustworthy outcome fall? |
| **Simplicity** | Which surfaces and instructions became unnecessary? |
| **Portability** | Does the concept remain usable across hosts and weaker harnesses? |
| **Trust** | Are provenance, permission, runtime authority, and availability explicit? |
| **Learning** | Does evidence cause a decision, structural cure, or explicit no-change outcome? |
| **Forgetting** | Does obsolete operational burden leave while warranted understanding remains? |

## Measures for projection convergence

### Candidate measures

- number of load-bearing projections without a declared canonical source;
- number without generation or validation proof;
- number of contradictions found by the substrate doctor;
- time from canonical change to complete projection convergence;
- cold-agent errors attributable to stale operational guidance;
- count of manually repeated semantic inventories;
- expected deletion set completed per transition;
- old active paths still reachable after migration closure.

### Strong success signal

One canonical change produces deterministic updates or precise failures across every live
projection, and at least one stale summary or manual index is removed.

### Falsifiers

- the projection checker costs more to maintain than the drift it prevents;
- cold agents understand the full authority more reliably from the current source than from the
  enacted projection;
- generated views hide meaningful interpretation or minority evidence;
- the new registry becomes a rival semantic authority;
- transitions still require manual discovery of affected projections.

## Measures for the obligation lifecycle

### Candidate measures

- percentage of sampled work episodes with one resolvable obligation identity;
- percentage of attempts linked to obligation, actor, plan/ticket, and terminal account;
- number of duplicate or ambiguous successor attempts;
- number of work episodes requiring manual transcript archaeology;
- interrupted episodes reconstructable without the original session;
- failed attempts retained after eventual success;
- duplicate external effects after retry or uncertain delivery;
- work registered before actor start;
- obligations left in ambiguous non-terminal state.

### Strong success signal

A reviewer can reconstruct one multi-session episode, including failures and corrections, from
structured identity and evidence with less time and fewer assumptions than from current artefacts.

### Falsifiers

- participants cannot agree what the obligation is without reintroducing plan or ticket duplication;
- the identity requires manual propagation at every step;
- most real work does not fit occurrence/attempt semantics;
- the lifecycle adds statuses without changing decisions or recovery;
- external surfaces cannot retain the needed joins and a new central store becomes mandatory;
- the model increases coordination cost at N=1 and N=2 without material benefit.

## Measures for claim succession and stale-result protection

### Candidate measures

- orphaned claims per multi-agent episode;
- mean time from actor loss to safe reclaim;
- late results from superseded actors;
- ambiguous current owner incidents;
- manual liveness enquiries per episode;
- stale claim cleanup actions;
- false stale classifications;
- cases where a live lower liveness class was over-read as full actor health.

### Strong success signal

A dead actor's work is safely reclaimed, its later result cannot redefine current truth, and no
manual cleanup or speculative communication is required.

### Falsifiers

- trusted local work becomes dominated by lease maintenance;
- succession prevents useful evidence from late actors being retained;
- liveness classes and claim authority remain duplicated and contradictory;
- state contention rises because claims become over-exclusive;
- recovery requires a permanently available coordinator.

## Measures for durable waiting

### Candidate measures

- waiting obligations that survive all sessions ending;
- obligations resumed exactly once after multiple triggers;
- time spent keeping sessions alive only for continuity;
- forgotten or stranded waits;
- expired conditions surfaced for decision;
- resumptions lacking sufficient context;
- manual reminders replaced;
- work incorrectly labelled active while waiting.

### Strong success signal

A named obligation waits across machine/session loss and resumes once, with sufficient context and
no human reconstruction.

### Falsifiers

- the waiting store becomes a backlog of speculative ideas;
- resumption conditions are mostly prose and cannot be evaluated;
- automatic resumption produces unsafe or context-free work;
- ordinary Linear or automation facilities already satisfy the need with lower cost;
- expiry creates notification noise without changing decisions.

## Measures for correction and cancellation

### Candidate measures

- time from owner correction to affected actor acknowledgement;
- child work continuing after parent cancellation;
- attempts restarted solely because correction could not be applied;
- cancelled work deleted without evidence;
- outcomes whose final account omits material redirection;
- ambiguity among cancellation, supersession, abandonment, and failure;
- human confidence that interruption leaves a trustworthy state.

### Strong success signal

A human can correct or stop consequential work rapidly, propagation is visible, and later review
shows both original intent and changed path.

### Falsifiers

- typed events make conversational steering cumbersome;
- agents wait for formal correction metadata rather than acting on clear direction;
- cancellation leaves more half-updated projections than current practice;
- the event vocabulary creates disputes without improving downstream decisions.

## Measures for agent operational facades

### Candidate measures

- number of mandatory arguments representing implementation mechanics;
- first-attempt success rate for cold agents;
- command-help/source inspection required;
- shell/path/quoting incidents;
- rebuilds during ordinary command use;
- live attention occupied by logs or polling;
- full forensic evidence retained after concise interaction;
- procedural instruction lines deleted;
- time and tokens per common operational intent.

### Strong success signal

Cold agents complete the intended operation safely with fewer inputs and less attention while
reviewers receive better structured and forensic evidence.

### Falsifiers

- the facade hides decisions or silently selects the wrong scope;
- debugging becomes harder because internal steps are inaccessible;
- failures collapse into generic messages;
- the facade becomes one large central CLI dependency graph;
- procedural prose remains because agents do not trust or discover the operation.

## Measures for communication simplification

### Candidate measures

- explicit channel-selection decisions per episode;
- duplicate messages across surfaces;
- time to detect a relevant event;
- missed messages despite healthy watcher process;
- message-authoring friction and invalid-event rate;
- consolidation effort per useful durable insight;
- identity/path arguments per communication;
- number of channel types differentiated only by storage mechanics;
- total coordination events by team size.

### Strong success signal

Teams exchange fewer coordination events and make fewer routing decisions while retaining or
improving awareness, urgency, decision evidence, and consolidation quality.

### Falsifiers

- simplified planes erase recipient, confidentiality, urgency, or decision authority;
- total event count falls but human clarification and missed work rise;
- one channel becomes a noisy universal stream;
- semantic routing is unpredictable or difficult to override;
- consolidation loses social context needed for interpretation.

## Measures for validation ecology

### Candidate measures

For each control:

- risk or invariant;
- prevention/detection/repair role;
- evidence independence;
- runtime and attention cost;
- failures caught in the last period;
- false positives;
- escaped failures;
- overlap with adjacent controls;
- removal condition;
- whether a lower-level structural cure now exists.

Portfolio measures:

- uncovered high-severity risks;
- controls with no named outcome or harm;
- several controls reading the same projection;
- checks moved later with no assurance loss;
- checks retired after invalid state became unrepresentable;
- time-to-green by work shape;
- defect escape rate after placement changes.

### Strong success signal

The Practice retains strict assurance while running fewer redundant controls, gains at least one
independent detector for a real blind spot, and can explain why each remaining gate exists.

### Falsifiers

- quality or defect escape worsens;
- cost data causes pressure to waive correctness;
- detector independence is inferred rather than demonstrated;
- removed controls leave no executable adversarial proof;
- the map becomes another manually stale inventory.

## Measures for topology scenarios

### Candidate measures

- historical multi-agent failures reproducible;
- portable invariants executable across several host implementations;
- scenario setup time;
- number of bespoke smoke tests replaced;
- failure classes found before live occurrence;
- realism gaps found by comparing scenarios with incidents;
- ability to inject time, duplicates, stale actors, malformed projections, and capability loss;
- scenario vocabulary reuse across PDR, diagnostic, and test.

### Strong success signal

One historical coordination failure is reproduced, the structural cure passes, and the same scenario
fails when the cure is removed.

### Falsifiers

- the harness needs production orchestration complexity;
- scenarios overfit implementation rather than portable contracts;
- live incidents repeatedly reveal failures the model cannot represent;
- maintenance exceeds the bespoke tests it replaces;
- scenario success becomes a substitute for real episode evidence.

## Measures for the capability membrane

### Candidate measures

- capabilities with stable identity and provenance;
- installed/active/available/authorised states distinguishable;
- silent host adapter drift;
- capability collisions or shadowing;
- time to suspend or withdraw a capability;
- consumers still depending on retired versions;
- unavailable capability discovered before dependent work starts;
- authority exceeding declared need;
- capabilities whose continued trust has no evidence review.

### Strong success signal

Three unlike capability families can explain their lifecycle and authority with the same semantic
contract while preserving different implementations and proportionate trust controls.

### Falsifiers

- the common contract is generic boilerplate with no operational effect;
- unlike capabilities are forced into misleading identical states;
- one registry becomes required for every host;
- trusted local workflows acquire disproportionate admission ceremony;
- platform-native strengths are lost.

## Measures for contract-impact detection

### Candidate measures

- affected live artefacts discovered automatically;
- false-positive and false-negative consumers;
- time from contract change to complete migration;
- old paths left active after closure;
- compatibility layers with no named consumer;
- changes whose impact rule cannot be expressed mechanically;
- migration dispositions completed;
- deletion sets proven absent.

### Strong success signal

A major contract change identifies its complete living impact, migrates all consumers, and removes
the old active shape with less uncertainty than current search-driven work.

### Falsifiers

- semantic consumers are routinely missed;
- detector maintenance duplicates the contract itself;
- teams trust the detector and skip necessary reasoning;
- old paths remain because the detector only reports, not closes transition obligations.

## Measures for retention and forgetting

### Candidate measures

- raw events archived without full extraction;
- archived material later mined or never used;
- generated projections retained as historical truth;
- operational bytes and context removed after terminal account;
- review/recovery tasks still answerable after compaction;
- repeated lessons already represented in doctrine;
- forgotten material reintroduced because provenance was insufficient;
- transitions whose obsolete active surface survives in archive-like locations.

### Strong success signal

The Practice retains a smaller causal account that supports review, recovery, and learning while
removing operational detail and active ambiguity.

### Falsifiers

- future reviewers cannot verify a material claim;
- raw archives repeatedly rescue incomplete extraction;
- compact accounts erase minority evidence or uncertainty;
- deletion decisions become irreversible before their value can be assessed;
- archive reduction increases rediscovery or repeated failure.

## Measures for external automation

### Candidate measures

- recurring manual coordination events removed;
- time and attention saved;
- automation failures detected;
- obligations recoverable without automation-private state;
- duplicate effects under retry;
- credential and data authority;
- maintenance and upgrade cost;
- remove-the-automation drill success;
- doctrine or semantic change required to replace implementation;
- hidden state discovered outside Practice evidence.

### Strong success signal

A bounded automation removes measured recurring work, can be deleted without loss of semantic truth,
and leaves local Practice operation intact during failure.

### Falsifiers

- the workflow becomes the only place that knows why or what happened;
- local teams cannot operate when it is unavailable;
- retries create duplicate external effects;
- maintenance exceeds the manual cost;
- credentials or data exceed the minimum needed;
- no existing procedure or surface can be removed.

## Portfolio stop conditions

Pause or reject the broader programme if any of these become true:

1. The obligation lifecycle requires a new central authority rather than joining existing surfaces.
2. New semantics increase ordinary coordination inputs and documents without measured reduction
   elsewhere.
3. Current-contract projections become new manually authored summaries.
4. TAU evidence cannot be collected without disproportionate privacy or workplace-monitoring risk.
5. Test scenarios cannot represent real incidents without mirroring the whole production substrate.
6. Capability-membrane semantics collapse unlike capabilities into misleading uniformity.
7. External automation becomes required for local Practice liveness.
8. Transition programmes keep old and new active paths indefinitely.
9. Strict assurance or human control weakens.
10. The proposals do not produce expected deletions after one complete worked slice.

## Minimum evidence before ratification

Before any broad Practice decision:

- one completed projection-convergence repair;
- one historical work episode modelled through the proposed lifecycle;
- one live episode trialled;
- one interruption/recovery scenario;
- one stale-actor/successor scenario;
- one measured operational facade comparison;
- one validation-ecology slice with an explicit retained/moved/removed disposition;
- one privacy-reviewed TAU operational question or an explicit decision not to collect;
- one list of actual deleted or retired complexity;
- owner review of whether the conceptual gain justifies the new vocabulary.

The desired final finding may be narrower than this report. Evidence-backed reduction of scope is a
successful research outcome.
