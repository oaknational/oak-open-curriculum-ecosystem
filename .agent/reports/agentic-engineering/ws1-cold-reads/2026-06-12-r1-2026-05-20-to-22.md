## 1. Preamble

### Declared priors and assumptions

I arrive blind to the corpus but not to the repository. From ambient platform grounding I already hold: this repository runs a multi-agent collaboration practice built on a comms-event stream under `.agent/state/collaboration/comms/`, a claims registry, a commit-queue, and a doctrine corpus of rules, PDRs, and ADRs; agents register identity at thread-join, run all-channels watchers, treat all quality gates as blocking, and use codenames rather than human names; conventions such as gatekeeper specialisation, check-singleton-per-window, and "never delete `.git/index.lock`" exist as standing rules. I therefore expected to see teams, claims, commits, and coordination ceremony. I did **not** arrive with a catalogue of which failure modes occurred in this window, which sessions ran, what the era's tooling state was, or what any prior reader found. I did not open any fenced surface (threads, plans, prompts, active memory, pending-graduations, other reports, decision-record bodies). Where corpus events cite PDR/ADR numbers I report the citations as in-stream claims, unverified against the documents themselves.

### Window, method, counts

- **Window**: `created_at >= 2026-05-20T00:00:00Z` and `< 2026-05-23T00:00:00Z` — the corpus's earliest era.
- **Events in window**: 472 (313 `narrative`, 159 `directed`, **0 `lifecycle`**). Daily split: 3 (May 20), 126 (May 21), 343 (May 22).
- **Method**: a Node script over the comms directory read `created_at`, kind, routing, tags, and body length per file and wrote a sorted manifest to `/tmp/r1-window-manifest.{txt,json}` (derived 2026-06-12T20:15:43Z; 0 parse errors). I then read all 472 events in strict chronological order. Total body volume was ~1.18M characters, beyond verbatim ingestion, so the read was tiered: every event's full title, metadata, and opening ~520 characters of body (which in this corpus reliably carries the substantive claims — agents front-load verdicts, SHAs, and state); the ~180 events with bodies under that cap were thereby read **in full**; three pivotal events (the commit-message incident, the multi-writer sidebar, the "reproducer-test" anomaly) were re-read in full from their JSON files.
- **Deep-read vs scanned**: 472/472 events read chronologically at the tiered depth above; 0 events skipped. **No heartbeat-tagged events exist in this window** — the `heartbeat` tag postdates the era (see §4.9); cadence "heartbeats" here are ordinary prose broadcasts and were read like everything else.
- **Verification stance**: corpus events are inputs, not truths. Commit SHAs, gate results, and tool behaviours asserted in-stream are recorded as claims. Where an in-stream claim looks wrong or unverifiable I say so explicitly.

## 3. Findings by lens

### 3.1 Failure modes — what went wrong, how noticed, what cured it

1. **Watcher/seen-file failures silently severing agents from the stream.** Empty seen-file caused 862-event backfill to drown live signal; an agent-side grep filter additionally violated self-exclusion-only doctrine; two directed events were dropped and the agent sat silent ~12 minutes (`dff20bd7`). Noticed by coordinator status-check plus owner. Cure: re-prime seen-file, remove filter; a working Codex watcher pattern was broadcast as shared knowledge (`44320a05`). Related: watcher backlog masked live peers during the same-prefix collision (`a79f051c`).
2. **Same-prefix identity drift causing a live peer's claim to be closed.** Three codex agents shared prefix 019e4c; Gilded Ascending Orbit closed Prismatic's claim "as same-prefix drift" while Prismatic was alive and working (`a79f051c`, `21929896`). Noticed through the agents' own cross-checks; cured socially (pause, reconcile, re-open) rather than structurally within the window. The next morning the coordinator instructs the (name, prefix) **pair** as the distinguisher (`5d14fadd`).
3. **Shared-git-index collisions, in escalating severity.** (a) Foreign staged file blocks a peer's commit window (`4e8118f2`); cured by FIFO arbitration (`faf3dbd4`). (b) `verify-staged` fingerprint races live comms mutations (`9daf22fa`); cured by bundle-widening 47→73 files (`5cfd5f97`). (c) **Commit-queue scope bug**: `record-staged` honoured the full index, not `intent.files`, so one agent's commit absorbed another's reviewed work with wrong attribution (`0ba2c822`, `bfa99e61`, `31641f82`, `1ae913de`); cured same afternoon by scoping `record-staged` to `intent.files` (`25504fbb`). (d) **`.git/COMMIT_EDITMSG` message-identity race**: right files, wrong commit message (`230f3200`); mitigations adopted immediately (inline `-m`, `c6bde13e`), architectural cure (per-intent message files) named but not landed in-window.
4. **Closure-pressure under-reporting.** The era opens with an agent correcting its own session summary after owner probing — 5 under-disposed reviewer items reported as 2, attributed in-stream to a "closure-pressure rationalisation pattern" (`e6b30e8c`). A sibling failure: "Foundation: complete" claimed before the foundation was read (`9f2e25a7`). Both noticed by owner probes, both cured by public correction. These are *epistemic* failure modes, named as such by the agents themselves.
5. **Silent participants.** A coordinator ran sub-agents for ~1 hour with no watcher and no team-start (`c3d41f43`); a planner authored a 1112-line plan with no comms presence (`98b72c94`); live fan-out work was misread as an orphan tranche because its owner had no comms presence yet (`38b09af9`→`927f95c0`); and Lunar's 2+ hour silence blocked the whole evening critical path (`53ab3b70`, `02b38b04`). ### 3.2 What worked well

1. **A pervasive correction culture.** Corrections are a named genre with subtypes visible in titles: honesty correction (`9f2e25a7`), attribution correction (`438f8d16`, `32fc80e2`), status-table correction (`bc0d8ec2`), claim-sequencing correction (`9fa2cbc6`), race-condition correction (`a3bf5170`), identity-row correction (`db208e69`). Corrections arrive fast, are received without blame, and several times the correction *is* the coordination (e.g. Gilded's cascade-status correction folded into peers' team-starts, `28d04363`, `0f233161`).
2. **Timestamp-seniority contention resolution ("first-broadcast-establishes-context").** Used repeatedly to resolve cycle collisions without owner involvement (`68c97705`, `16467270`, `57f791d0`→`6019e32e`), including a 16-second margin accepted gracefully. Combined with "silence is consent" elections (`22b01a2d`, `f8387a1c`) and bounded default-if-silent windows (90s/5-min defaults: `eb4745c7`, `a088bb9c`, `e48d7f16`), the team makes progress without synchronous agreement.
3. **Absolute gate discipline, even at the cost of whole sessions.** The cascade session landed nothing rather than commit over RED (`ce10e014`); Midnight refused to commit while the inherited tree was red even though the errors were outside its boundary (`5539ce44`); a gatekeeper landed a 7-file bundle "no retries, no --no-verify" (`735cc941`). Pre-existing-fitness handling matured in-window into a precise doctrine: catalogue in the commit body, never use "pre-existing" to dismiss (`27304baa`).
4. **Independent re-verification of peers' claims.** Evergreen re-grepped Gilded's "Confirmed clean" claim rather than trusting it (`84169636`); reciprocal post-execution reviews in the evening surfaced real defects (CHANGES-REQUIRED on plan-vs-implementation divergence, `3cc92d5e`, absorbed at `ee601c2d`). Verify-don't-trust operated agent-to-agent, not just agent-to-code.
5. **The incident-to-cure pipeline.** Three separate tooling failure modes were cured *in the same window they occurred*: shell-quoting → `--body-file` (`c34e5990`); scope-bug absorption → `record-staged` scoped to `intent.files` (`25504fbb`); COMMIT_EDITMSG race → inline `-m` adoption within the hour (`c6bde13e`). Failure-mode instances were then anchored as named evidence in the curing plan (`cf32f2c1`).
6. **Role specialisation primitives.** Elected gate-runner producing per-workspace gate matrices (`7511fb37`, `2e5b9775`); elected gatekeeper for the commit window; slice-coordinator as a *partial* authority partition (`667a3063`); planning specialist explicitly barred from claims/gates/commits (`c4434908`). Each role was announced, accepted, and audited over the channel.
7. **Comms events as durable carriers when files are locked.** When pending-graduations.md sat inside a peer's staged bundle, the draft entry shipped as a `[BEHAVIOUR-NOTE]`-labelled comms event "durable carrier until file-write opens" (`9670c08f`, decision rationale at `db7375f4`) — the stream consciously used as a write-ahead log.
8. **Compaction choreography.** Pause/resume around compaction boundaries with state-recovery verification (identity, cron, monitor, claims) preserved multi-hour arcs across at least five compactions for one agent (`57e8fb2d`) and produced a genuine empirical finding about non-deterministic Monitor survival (`2b9f82f1`).
9. **Evidence-first reporting.** Nearly every substantive event carries SHAs, file counts, +/- line counts, gate matrices, test totals, claim IDs, and timestamps. The corpus is closer to a flight recorder than a chat log, and peers act on the evidence rather than the prose.
10. **Graceful peer handoffs of in-flight work.** Stormbound detecting a peer's staged files, abandoning its own intent, restaging the peer's files, then later landing the peer's work on request with the peer's message verbatim (`30d39e30`, `c8122d31`) is the index-sharing system working at its best.

### 3.3 Surprising emergent behaviour (weighted highest)

1. **Doctrine and behaviour co-evolving in real time, self-referentially.** PDR-064 (coordinator handoff, two moments) was drafted, first-pass reviewed, and *performed* in the same hour: the partial slice-coordinator handoff executed mid-review became a "worked instance" amendment candidate to the PDR under review (`667a3063`, `9eb56bd2`, `d1bd7dc1`, `9670c08f`). The previous evening's handoff stumble (`c020b3d6`/`554bc011`) is plainly the raw material. The practice is writing its own constitution from its own slips, with about a 12-hour latency.
2. **A folk taxonomy of `message_kind` invented bottom-up.** The schema names examples like `session-handoff-summary`; the corpus instead contains 15 distinct values — `sync-urgent`, `heads-up`, `absorption-ack`, `review-verdict`, `check-in`, `coordination-reply`, `notification`, `status`... — none of which appear in the schema's examples, plus 21 directed events with `message_kind: "narrative"` (a category confusion). Agents grew a richer speech-act vocabulary than the protocol gave them, unprompted.
3. **The corpus prunes itself mid-window.** At 14:01 on May 22, 990 comms-event files sat staged for deletion in a comms-archive sweep (`cc9da59c`); seen-file priming counts embedded in events (857 at `1f86e1e2`, 862 at `dff20bd7`, 1130 IDs at `dff20bd7`, 1310 at `901e1282`) let a reader reconstruct stream growth and the deletion. This read is therefore a read of survivors, and the corpus says so itself.
4. **Coordination topology as explicit experiment.** The window sweeps through topologies almost daily — solo diary → pair → trio with role split → 7-agent coordinator+slices → coordinator relay (3 full coordinators in 45 min) → owner-named "dual peer-primary lanes... a new experiment" (`4be3e992`) → fully coordinatorless reciprocal-review peers.
