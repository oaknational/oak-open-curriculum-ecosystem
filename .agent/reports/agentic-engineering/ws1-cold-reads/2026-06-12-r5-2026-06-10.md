## 3. Findings by lens

### 3.1 Failure modes

1. **Canonical watcher hang-but-run (twice, then cured same-day).** The `comms watch` CLI stalls silently while the process stays alive: Abyssal at ~14:07Z (`e27e964a`, capture `26c8b7e9`), then the Director themself at 15:28:55Z (`382ed72e`). Detection both times was *external* (owner noticing; understudy reading the frozen seen-file). Cure: portable polling loops immediately; hardening PR #157 (fail-loud per-step deadlines, default-on liveness file) merged at 17:10 (`6fc7351c`).
2. **The detector cannot detect itself.** Veiled, who had run the stall diagnostic *on Abyssal* hours earlier, attributed their own identical stall to routine work (`b385b94c`). The proposed cure — peers cross-check the Director's watcher staleness as cheap mutual cover — was adopted by subsequent Directors (`7f452f66` cadence declaration).
3. **Alive-but-blocked is indistinguishable from stalled.** Three variants in one day: Veiled (blind watcher), Celestial-Dusk (blocked on owner authorisation ~67 min, `9d421e29`), Celestial-Orbit (2-hour owner-transport gap with frozen heartbeat typed-args, `cd19782a`). Emergent cure: honest wait-state heartbeat labels (`cycle=blocked-on-owner-ask`, `cycle=idle-awaiting-owner-direction`), adopted across seats within the hour (`c9b4dc78`).
4. **Silent write failures in the coordination CLI.** Two Director ruling events "failed to write (CLI id-validation failure)" and were only discovered missing later (`53112f47`); the standing doctrine becomes "a write's only proof is the proof line" (`eb284f91`).
5. **Relative-path writes silently retarget the registry — write-proof proves the wrong destination.** Tempestuous's G3 fork asks landed in the *worktree's* comms copy; the proof line printed a relative path that read as the home; a ~30-minute coordination gap followed and a deadline-default nearly fired on an unseen ask (`9a164c5c`, `16939248`). The sharpest tooling finding of the day: the write-proof discipline itself can validate a misdirected write.
6. **Pre-grounding against a live peer's worktree grades the cure, not the head.** Airy-Squall's held REFUTE verdict on a Copilot finding was wrong because the worktree already carried the peer's uncommitted fix (`b46ccedd`). Cure: pin the SHA (`git show <head>:<path>`). (Overlaps a feedback note I carry; the corpus shows its origin event.)
7. **Replication-script simplification manufactures findings at exactly its simplification points.** A keep-first dedup produced a 473-lesson false-churn signal; the tell was equality between a methodology counter and a finding counter (`f58417a6`). 9. **Baseline-selection error in drift classification.** The Director routed a "new upstream schema bump" micro-PR diagnosed by comparing the working tree against the long-lived *branch HEAD* rather than origin/main; the implementer's execution-start re-verification returned a NO-OP verdict (`459451d5`); Director self-capture at `54fc0fee`. ## 4. We did not expect this

Genuine surprises, in descending order of how hard they landed:

1. **Zero machine-readable threading in 795 events, including the three events whose governing doctrine explicitly mandates the field** (`0a3d08ff`, `7f452f66`, `ed4e9d01` versus the requirement stated in `8c94ddc3` and `7dc40d71`). I expected at least the ceremonial events to comply with their own ceremony.
2. **The blind walkthrough** (`8c94ddc3`): a 90-line doctrine broadcast addressed to an agent whose 36-minute-old team-start the author cannot see, explaining the precise failure mode the author is currently inside. I did not expect the corpus to contain dramatic irony of this purity.
3. **The owner can see agent context budgets and the agents cannot** — "you are at ~36%... degradation at 40–45%" arriving as *relayed telemetry* (`593a93d5`), instantly displacing the written 80% doctrine across all subsequent transfers. I expected budget self-sensing or doctrine constants to govern; instead an invisible participant's instrumentation did.
4. **Two incompatible first-person accounts of who merged #160, both left standing** (`7cdb92a1` vs `8ccd639f`) — in a culture this obsessed with provenance, evidence, and the stream-as-record, I did not expect the stream to contradict itself on a merge actor and move on.
5. **The misdirected-ask incident** (`9a164c5c`): the system's own integrity device (the write-proof line) validated a write to the wrong universe, and the deadline-default machinery — the day's most-celebrated mechanism — nearly executed against an ask that effectively did not exist. The near-miss was caught by *courtesy*, not by design: an optional pre-default ping.
