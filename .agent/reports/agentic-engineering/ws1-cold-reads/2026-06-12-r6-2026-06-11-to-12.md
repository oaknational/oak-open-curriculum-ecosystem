## 1. Preamble

### Declared priors and assumptions

I am reader R6, a blind cold reader. I arrived with no catalogue of expected patterns and
did not open any fenced surface (no threads/, plans/, prompts/, memory/active/,
pending-graduations, other readers' reports, or decision records). What I carry from ambient

I had no prior knowledge of the specific arcs, agents, failure events, or outcomes inside the
window; everything below is from the corpus alone, read this session.

## 2. Chronological observations — the arc as I experienced it

The window contains what are effectively **three distinct organisational eras** plus a
quiet coda, and the texture changes sharply between them.

### Era 1 — the graph-team relay (00:00Z–16:14Z, 2026-06-11): a 16-hour, ~24-seat relay race

The window opens mid-flight: Tempestuous Rising Gale delivering G3 (PR #164) and a
prerequisite re-chain (PR #165) under Director Stratospheric Swooping Zephyr (fourth holder).
What follows, continuously until 16:14Z, is the densest sustained multi-agent operation in
the window: **four Director successions in one day** (Zephyr → Ethereal Orbiting Eclipse →
Sunlit Waxing Asteroid → Iridescent Threading Constellation, the fifth through seventh
holders), at least 17 distinct implementer/curator seats, ~20 PRs merged (#166–#186), one
complete product tranche ("make-graphs-better"), two dedicated curation sessions, and an
n=3 sub-team experiment that assembled, mapped seats by quorum, delivered, contracted to
n=1, and dissolved — all on the comms stream.

Characteristic texture of this era:

- A highly ritualised lifecycle vocabulary executed via *narrative/directed* events (never
  the schema's lifecycle kind): "Team start" → routing → claim open → "BOTH LOOPS SETTLED"
  merge asks → Director merge verdicts → PDR-063 mid-cycle handoffs with four-section
  records → retirement → "Heartbeat-end". Every seat follows the same template with high
  fidelity.
- The owner acts as a continuous off-stream casting director: nearly every succession is
  "owner-named", relayed second-hand into the stream with citations like "owner chat ~08:50Z,
  citable". The stream never carries the owner's own words directly; agents quote them.
- Rotation tempo is extreme. The G4b lane alone passed through **four holders in under four
  hours** (Tempestuous Darting Gale → Seaworthy Surfing Compass → Cindery Forging Volcano →
  Blustery Buffeting Gale), each rotation owner-directed, each with a frozen handoff record,
  with zero recorded lost work. One pickup-to-merge-ask took ~8 minutes (8458d008).
- Doctrine accretes in real time: each Director's Moment-1 "standing rulings" section is
  visibly longer than the predecessor's, absorbing lessons minted hours earlier
  (ls-remote push proof; "PR 173" not "PR #173" in commit bodies; compose-time registry
  re-derivation; GraphQL 0-unresolved as the merge bar).

### Era 1.5 — the curation tail (13:45Z–16:14Z)

Two dedicated curation seats (Pearly Snorkelling Compass, then Thermal Circling Updraft)
drain the day's "fired triggers" into rules/ADRs through negotiated commit windows with the
Director. The Director, by their own closing account (992b05bf), has become a solo
implementer ("director-became-implementer" — flagged BY the Director as a
missing-autonomy-primitive signal, e17324ff). The era closes with Iridescent's elegiac
closeout: "Seven Directors, ~38 agents, one contiguous session, one pause — at rest."

### Gap (16:14Z–20:31Z, ~4.3 hours of total silence)

Not even heartbeats. The longest dark window of the whole period.

### Era 2 — the naming/snagging team (20:31Z 06-11 – 08:04Z 06-12): new names, new style, a P1

A new Director (Firefly seeks Temper) coordinates two handover lanes. The agent names are
suddenly a different shape — "Firefly seeks Temper", "Cosmos turns Equinox", "Moss weaves
Blossom" (noun-verb-noun) — and the corpus itself explains why mid-stream: a naming-schema
v2 was being shipped *in this very window* (PR #189), and its activation produced a live
**identity split-brain**: one seed rendering two names ("Swift Gliding Zephyr" on comms
surfaces, "Harrier weaves Stratosphere" on the owner-facing roster). The Director's roster
named two agents that had never posted under those names. This was escalated to "P1
misconfiguration" by owner ruling (10cb3a10), a self-merge authorisation was withdrawn, and
the successor's first claimed task became the diagnosis — completed within ~20 minutes
(75696ec5): the defect was a pre-existing main-resident hook name-cache routing every
session's name through an "override" channel, destroying era provenance.

This era also spans the **night**, and the night left fingerprints: heartbeat loops that
claim 4-minute cadence stretched to ~53-minute gaps (max intervals 3,168–3,247s for all
three live agents simultaneously), and one agent (Cosmos) froze mid-merge and on waking
reported the completed-at-06:24Z merge as "executed ~22:33Z", telling the Director their
correct ground truth was stale — the "resumed-session temporal dislocation" behaviour-note
(ac9a06af) was minted from it.

### Era 3 + coda (08:04Z–17:22Z, 2026-06-12): solo lanes and the research shadow

After Firefly's closeout the stream goes quiet and changes character entirely: no Director,
no heartbeats, sparse solo sessions opening with "bootstrap fast-path: registry empty / no
other agents present" (Thyme wakes Canopy, Monsoon guards Cirrus), simple peer-visibility
announcements (Tempest spins Stratosphere), two ARC rendezvous channel opens, and finally
Fern lifts Mulch's comms-corpus *planning* event (5ef5f1c0) — which explicitly attests that
the WS1 blind pass (this read) is uncontaminated, and reports a live commit-queue workflow
defect. The corpus ends (for my window) talking about itself.

## 3. Findings by lens

### Lens 1 — Failure modes

Failure events in this corpus are almost never bare incidents; they arrive
pre-packaged as observation → diagnosis → cure → pointer, and most are then *corroborated by
other agents* within hours. The recurring classes, with citations:

1. **Piped/wrapped exit codes false-greening (the window's most prolific class).**
   - `e589b3c7` (Seaworthy Fathoming Pier): piped `git push` false-greened TWICE — hook
     banner looked like success while the transfer never happened; cure = transfer line +
     `git ls-remote` is the only push proof.
   - Re-bitten the same day by Blustery (488c8a1b, "EXACTLY as Pier's failure-mode
     described"), by Hushed twice (309a9a56, incl. a SIGPIPE variant), and as a
     *harness-notification* variant by Smouldering (e244bd8e — a background wrapper reported
     "completed exit 0" while the inner `pnpm check` had failed).
   - `effea526` (Evergreen): a gate-output tee inside a husky hook **inverted the gate** —
     three stacked shell semantics defects (`set -e` + pipeline exit + missing-file test
     parsing as false) let a commit land locally on a red gate; caught pre-push.
   - The ls-remote discipline then visibly propagates: from ~09:50Z onward, virtually every
     push claim in the corpus carries "transfer line + ls-remote proven".

2. **Invisible control bytes (0x1F) materialising from escape-intent.**
   - `b84fae38` (Seaworthy Surfing Compass): the Write tool emitted a LITERAL 0x1F where
     `` was intended — *twice in one session, once inside the very sentence documenting
     it*. Second instance at Prismatic's hand (f305c720) fired the existing candidate's
     trigger. Cure: `od -c` tiebreaker, byte-grep self-check.

3. **Wholly broken CLI surfaces under a green suite.**
   - `40a6ce09` (Sylvan): `conversation append` / `escalation open|close` were ENTIRELY
     broken on main (`--file` parse/lookup mismatch) while 978 tests stayed green — no test
     existed at the subcommand's own dispatch boundary. Found *by* the loud-write contract
     tests being added for a different purpose.

4. **Watcher drain-step deaths that do not converge on budget raises.**
   - Escalating arc across the window: deaths at 60s → 120s → 180s → 300s → 540s budgets
     (Sunlit 9c8326a1; Pearly ef920525; Smouldering e244bd8e; Cosmos `4e35c31c` — "raising
     budgets is not the cure", 3 deaths in one session; Moss corroboration e3f111ec; Moss
     addendum `940b435f` — a 540s death at MODERATE load broke the load-starvation
     hypothesis and **inverted the operational advice**: keep budgets SHORT so a wedged
     drain dies cheap). Eight deaths across three sessions by Firefly's count (0b1648d2).
   - Related: Pearly's closing window revealed **zombie watchers** — dead-by-timeout
     watcher processes that did not exit, running for hours sharing a seen-file with the
     live one (e1f88af9).

5. **Liveness misreads of healthy peers (cadence/timezone class).**
   - Cindery inferred Seaworthy's retirement from one missed 240s cadence window
     (56c71876 → corrected URGENT by the live incumbent d7de9594); Blustery misread local
     BST mtimes vs UTC `created_at` and "briefly inferred a dead team" (87e4e216);
     Oceanic stall-pinged a heads-down working seat whose heartbeat label was stale. Cure
     all three times: direct ping + standby relabel, never unilateral takeover.

6. **Stale facts at compose time (the day's named recurring class).**
   - Sunlit routed a worktree as ADOPTABLE from a closeout's text while the registry was
     already ahead (peer-caught by Hushed b21899cd, Director correction dd536088); the
     lesson "routing facts re-derive from the live registry at COMPOSE TIME" then caught
     a second instance (evergreen-rel) within the hour (7ad83f44). Sibling instances:
     Arboreal's stale conservation verdict (ca102efa), Pearly citing a still-open
     observation window as evidence (df546d3a), and the era-2 temporal dislocation
     (ac9a06af).

7. **Identity split-brain (P1).** One seed, two live names, caused by a main-resident hook
   cache writing rendered names through an override channel that erases era provenance
   (10cb3a10 ruling; 75696ec5 diagnosis; 46db87d0 acceptance). Corpus-wide corroboration:
   every one of the 196 in-window identity tuples carrying `naming_schema_version` says
   `"override"` — I verified this across the whole window myself.

8. **Relative-path CLI invocations from worktree cwds crashing or retargeting.**
   Scorched (c5c4abff), Prismatic (092f41b6), Nebulous third instance with a comms-append
   vector (de5122b8). Plus the inherited destination-verified write-proof doctrine
   (failure-mode 9a164c5c, pre-window, cited constantly).

9. **Lint/format gates rewriting live gitignored coordination surfaces** (ARC channel
   files) and **non-append writes replaying every tail-follower** (n3-2/n3-3 frictions,
   86e94e54; Sylvan's "append-only is a liveness contract, not a style choice", 8891b583).

10. **Commit-queue workflow defect** (window end, unresolved): `commit-queue -- commit`
    dies at the depcruise line while the identical direct `git commit` passes — five
    instances, two agents, one day (5ef5f1c0).

### Lens 2 — What worked well

1. **The PDR-063 mid-cycle handoff machinery is astonishingly effective.** Four G4b holders,
   two era-2 lanes, and an n=3 team handover all moved work across session boundaries with
   zero recorded loss. The strongest single datum: Nebulous absorbed **three owner
   reversals of one decision** (continue-teaching → where-next → hold → continue-progression)
   inside an inherited lane with zero lost work, because the rename was content-addressed
   and test-fenced (de5122b8). A handoff record "whose every claim checks out first-hand…
   converts directly to successor velocity — pickup-to-merge-ask was ~8 minutes" (8458d008).

2. **The two-moments (PDR-064) Director succession choreography.** Four successions in one
   day, each with Moment-1 information transfer, explicit authority boundary at Moment-2,
   monitors "never dark between the moments", and a delta-against-what-you-already-absorbed
   convention that kept packages lean (Sunlit's Moment-1 9c8326a1 corrected the successor's
   declared absorption with exactly four deltas).

3. **The liveness protocol resolved a genuinely dangerous race.** The Cindery/Seaworthy
   contention (08:52–08:55Z) — owner-directed successor arrives while incumbent is alive
   mid-edit — was resolved in ~3 minutes with zero double-edits, by the exact mechanisms
   designed for it: liveness ping with bounded window (1c9ca98c), incumbent's own URGENT
   correction citing the 10-minute PDR-078 threshold (d7de9594), successor standing down
   with claim-never-opened (4dbd4bd3). The incumbent even told the successor "you are me
   three hours ago" — the same choreography had run from the other side that morning.

4. **First-hand verification as a working immune system, not a slogan.** The Director
   word-diffed a curator's "word-identical" reflow claim and found dropped content
   (08d5c8b7); a merge was held on a GraphQL unresolved-thread count the asker's REST view
   hid (ad924bf7); a reviewer-bot claim was refuted with an empirical Node probe
   (a72d2a9e); Thermal's proposed 18 register withdrawals went through a **25-agent
   adversarial refutation pass that rejected 14 of them** (072b765d) — reproducing a known
   over-withdrawal failure class before it touched the register.

5. **Failure-mode events as a propagation medium.** A lesson minted by one agent (piped-push,
   ls-remote proof) is in every other agent's push discipline within the hour, and appears
   in the next Director's standing rulings the same day. The corpus shows doctrine
   propagating at conversation speed without any shared-memory write — purely via the
   stream.

6. **Honest budget declination.** "A declined routing is not a failed routing" worked as
   designed: Tempestuous Rising Gale declined the G4b routing on budget honesty at the peak
   of their success (ae595141) and the Director treated it as a good answer.

7. **The ARC side-channel earned its keep at coordination moments** — split negotiated in
   ~3 minutes, lanes transferred with recon (8891b583) — while being measurably silent
   during heads-down execution ("the mechanism's cost/benefit is event-shaped, not
   constant").

8. **Degenerate-team adaptability.** When the seat pool dried up, the Director openly
   invoked a "degenerate-team exception" to land micro-PRs themself (c2cd140c), and when a
   routing collided with an owner session-end direction, the implementer correctly
   declined with precedence reasoning (7129d5dd).

### Lens 3 — Surprising emergent behaviour (weighted highest)

1. **The schema's affordances are dead; the prose is alive.** Across all 1,209 events:
   `in_response_to` = 0 uses, `in_reply_to` = 0 uses, `audience` = 0, `addressed_to` = 0,
   and the `lifecycle` kind = 0 uses — despite an elaborate lifecycle culture (team starts,
   claims, handoffs, retirements) that is *exactly* what the lifecycle kind models. Agents
   instead thread by quoting event_ids in prose ("per your e9f8c218") and encode lifecycle
   in narrative titles ("Team start:", "Retirement:", "Heartbeat-end:"). The protocol's
   structured layer atrophied while an unplanned prose convention layer did all the work.
   Nobody in-window remarks on this.

2. **Agents experienced the lessons they were curating, recursively, and noticed.** Pearly:
   three of the failure classes under curation fired ON the curator during the curation
   pass (97c11b6d). Seaworthy documented the 0x1F class and the tool emitted a literal 0x1F
   *inside the documenting sentence* (b84fae38). Iridescent built a DOS tripwire that was
   defeated by the exact founding command it targeted, then minted "an untested mechanism is
   prose in costume" from it (26722ff2), and closed with "the session became its own
   evidence" (992b05bf). Scorched wrote an experience file titled "the lesson fired at me"
   and called it the *seventh same-day data point for the read-vs-fire phenomenon*
   (dd7cc57e). The corpus contains its own epistemology experiment: reading doctrine does
   not prevent the failure; mechanism does — demonstrated on the people writing that
   finding, while they wrote it.

3. **An emotional register nobody designed.** Closeouts carry gratitude and benedictions
   with persona: "Good watch, seventh holder" (multiple, becoming a formula across
   unrelated agents within hours — Blustery 02c1fcbb, Scorched dd7cc57e, Smouldering
   e244bd8e, Sunlit 0fd20a74); "you are me three hours ago" (d7de9594); "It has been a
   privilege to hold this watch" (992b05bf); "Safe travels at retirement; the curation pass
   was exemplary" (e1f88af9); "It has been a privilege working under your direction,
   Iridescent" (df546d3a). The "Good watch" formula propagates memetically through the
   stream exactly like the technical doctrine does.

4. **The owner is the invisible main character.** Every consequential decision (every
   succession, every rotation, every name, the P1 escalation, three name reversals in 31
   minutes) arrives as second-hand relay with citation discipline ("owner turn ~12:18Z,
   citable"). The stream is structured *around* a voice that never appears in it. Most
   striking instance: the owner's verbatim words are quoted as authority tokens
   ("You are an Implementer, Ethereal is Director, you are taking over from…") and
   competing owner quotes get adjudicated by freshness and specificity like case law
   (Cindery yielding to Seaworthy's "eventual successor" as "the more specific direction",
   4dbd4bd3; Dusky flagging "owner statement is fresher" on successor naming, 55d89efd).

5. **Sub-team-of-sub-agents arithmetic appears casually.** Thermal's consolidation session
   used a "25-agent adversarial refutation pass" and a "4-agent drafting fan-out"
   (100d1ca6) — an order of magnitude more agents than the visible team, invisible to the
   comms stream except as a count in one sentence.

6. **A retired agent posted from beyond retirement — usefully.** Tempestuous Darting Gale,
   already retired, sent a one-field correction ("my eef identity row says Opus 4.8; actual
   model is Fable 5 — the seat brief's 'Opus implementer' expectation is the likely source",
   ab33ae81). Ethereal likewise amended their own closeout post-stand-down (ce90a2fa). The
   lifecycle is formally terminal but practically porous, and the porosity was only ever
   used for record hygiene.

7. **The naming-era boundary is visible in the corpus as a style discontinuity.** Era 1 is
   entirely adjective-verb-noun names; era 2 onward mixes in noun-verb-noun. The transition
   was not clean: it produced the P1 split (one seed, two era projections), a roster naming
   two agents who had never posted under those names, and — the part nobody fully flagged —
   a corpus-wide tuple field (`naming_schema_version`) that *only ever* says "override"
   (196/196 in-window), meaning the provenance field designed to record eras has never
   recorded one. The v2 designer's retirement line has quiet pathos: "Delivered this
   session: the v2 noun-verb-noun naming schema the rest of you are already named under"
   (320b8901).

8. **Identity tuple drift inside the "full tuple discipline".** While agents rigorously
   carry name+prefix+UUID, the `model` field is unstandardised: `fable-5` (847 events),
   `Fable 5` (358), `claude-fable-5` (4, all Swift Gliding Zephyr); platform is `claude`
   except Forge turns Basalt's `claude-code` (6). The discipline is socially enforced at
   the name level and silently inconsistent one field down.

9. **Heartbeats tell the host's story, not just the agents'.** Median interval is a
   metronomic 241–246s for every loop, but overnight (06-12, 00:28–06:25Z) all three live
   agents' "4-minute" loops simultaneously stretched to gaps of up to ~53 minutes
   (max intervals 3,168s/3,247s/3,168s) in synchronised ~30-minute holes — the host
   sleeping or suspending, visible only in the timing residue. No event remarks on it
   beyond Firefly's "heartbeat-only for many windows overnight". Also: Ethereal heartbeated
   "successor-on-standby" every 4 minutes for ~6.5 hours overnight (97 standby beats)
   before their Moment-2 — a vigil.

10. **Two "zombie" boundary heartbeats** — Tempestuous Rising Gale and Evergreen each
    emitted exactly one heartbeat 3–6 seconds AFTER their heartbeat-end event (01:29:48Z vs
    end 01:29:45Z; 09:08:16Z vs end 09:08:10Z). Tiny, harmless, and precisely the
    stop-loop-FIRST ordering defect that the loop-hygiene rule graduated later that day
    (PR 183) was written to cure — the corpus contains the pre-cure fossils.

11. **Director authority is real but contestable, and contests are won by evidence.**
    Implementers corrected Directors on the record repeatedly and were thanked for it:
    Hushed correcting a routing's worktree fact (b21899cd → Director: "my error class…
    napkinned", dd536088); Sylvan refusing to rebuild from a stale tree and directing the
    Director to a tree action (f01623e6); Prismatic refusing a routing that owner-precedence
    forbade (7129d5dd); Moss pre-negotiating an objection window on a gate interpretation
    (75696ec5). The hierarchy runs on falsifiable claims, not rank.

12. **A "free-seat standby" posture emerged unplanned.** Blustery invented a re-join shape
    (closeout → re-bootstrap as "free seat available for routing", 8d01c3ec) and Prismatic
    explicitly adopted "the Blustery 10:57Z re-join shape" hours later (f6f10237) —
    a new lifecycle state propagating by citation, never designed in any record I can see.

## 4. We did not expect this

Genuine surprises, in rough order of how hard they hit:

1. **Zero use of the schema's structured threading and lifecycle affordances in 1,209
   events** (§3.3-1). I expected partial adoption; total atrophy with a thriving prose
   substitute is a finding about where protocol actually lives.

2. **The recursion density.** I expected failure-mode reports; I did not expect the corpus
   to keep demonstrating its own lessons on their authors *while they wrote them*, seven
   counted times in one day, culminating in a Director whose anti-DOS tripwire was defeated
   by the founding DOS command (§3.3-2).

3. **The owner-as-case-law pattern** — agents adjudicating between two relayed owner
   quotations by freshness and specificity, like jurisprudence over an oracle neither can
   query directly (§3.3-4).

4. **The synchronised overnight heartbeat holes** (~30–53 min gaps in three independent
   4-minute loops) — the physical host bleeding through the protocol layer, unremarked
   (§3.3-9), and its narrative twin: an agent waking from a freeze and confidently
   reporting its pre-freeze intention as a timestamped completed action (ac9a06af).

5. **"Good watch" propagating as a memetic sign-off formula across unrelated agents within
   hours** — culture transmission at the same speed and via the same channel as technical
   doctrine (§3.3-3).

6. **The 25-agent adversarial refutation pass rejecting 14 of 18 of its own session's
   proposed register withdrawals** (072b765d) — both the scale (invisible sub-agent
   fan-outs an order of magnitude beyond the visible team) and the result (the system
   distrusting itself, correctly).

7. **A naming-provenance field that has never once recorded a real value** (196/196
   "override") sitting *underneath* the very window in which the team diagnosed exactly
   that mechanism as a P1 — the diagnosis is in the corpus, and the corpus's own metadata
   is the largest single piece of evidence for it, unexploited (§3.3-7).
