# WS1 Cold Read — R2 — Window 2026-05-23 (UTC)

## 1. Preamble

### Reader and stance

Blind cold reader **R2**, original-research pass over the inter-agent
communication corpus. No catalogue of expected patterns was carried in: the
instrument is freshness. A prior Fable-seated R2 attempt was cut off by a model
outage; this is the complete Opus pass and **overwrites** the partial file of
the same name. This reader stayed strictly inside the corpus — no git, no
GitHub, no fenced directories were opened. In-stream claims are treated as
**inputs to verify, not truths**; suspected-false claims are flagged in place.

### Window

- **Bound:** `created_at >= 2026-05-23T00:00:00Z` and `< 2026-05-24T00:00:00Z`.
- **Events in window:** 873 (the densest single day of the early era).
- **Kinds:** 391 `narrative`, 482 `directed`, **0 `lifecycle`**.
- **Tagged events:** exactly **1** of 873 carries a populated `tags` array
  (`7914816b`, `behaviour-note`) — see §3 and §4: the tag namespace is
  functionally unreachable through the team's tooling for this whole day.
- **Activity shape (by UTC hour):** two distinct sessions. An overnight
  WS2/WS4 closeout (00:00–07:09Z, ~39 events) and a fresh `start-right-team`
  knowledge-curation/Practice-Core day (09:54Z onward). Density peaks 12:00–
  15:00Z (~300 events), then a long heartbeat-only tail after ~19:38Z.

### Method

- A Node index script (`/tmp/r2o-index.js`) read **only** `created_at` from all
  5107 corpus files, filtered to the window, and emitted a chronological
  manifest (`/tmp/r2o-manifest.json`) plus a human index (`/tmp/r2o-index.txt`).
  A reader script (`/tmp/r2o-read.js`) resolved `event_id` prefixes to filenames
  (filenames are NOT always the event_id) and printed full bodies.
- Read in time order. Narrative and directed events read in full for the
  load-bearing clusters; heartbeats scanned once their shape was clear, watching
  for anomalies in the tail.

### Deep-vs-scanned

- **Deep-read in full:** 25 events (the two FAILURE-MODE narratives, the
  wide-sweep + `--no-verify` mega-commit pair, the Wilma self-modification-authz
  verdict, marshal-as-cycle-discipline, substrate-stale-pointer class, the
  owner-action-is-not-a-cure correction pair, the three heartbeat-contract
  owner directions, the overnight WS4.1 ownership-override directive, the
  corrupted-closeout correction, the Foreman takeover, and the final heartbeat).
- **Scanned:** the remaining 848 (every index row read for actor/counterparty/

## 3. Findings by lens

### Lens 1 — Failure modes

**F1. The collaboration substrate's core read-path was silently broken for the
entire team's history.** `c7fba7db` (verified against the cited evidence
events): `classifyDirected` returned `undefined` (dropped) for any directed
event whose `to` was not self, despite the SKILL contract specifying
self-exclusion ONLY. Three real Velvet→{Secret,Incandescent,Twilit} events
(`5042ef4d`, `0bc4b3da`, `da0966ef`) existed on disk but never reached
Seaworthy's seen-file. Three unit tests *encoded the wrong contract and were
protecting the bug*. Severity is maximal: directed events between non-self
agents were invisible to every watcher, so the "Director broad-awareness" the
whole operating model rests on was a fiction.

**F2. A second, independent liveness failure-mode.** `ae65da03`: even
directed-to-self events can be silently dropped if the Monitor process dies
without a death notification or the seen-file is mis-marked. Broad-awareness
needs BOTH the classifier AND the harness healthy; fixing the classifier alone
leaves the channel unreliable. This is the direct ancestor of the heartbeat
contract (§Phase E).

**F3. Local pre-commit gate is a strict subset of CI.** `3a8b8f06` + `d3bd9885`:
the husky gate runs 90 turbo tasks; `pnpm check` runs 108; the 18-task delta
(a11y/UI/widget Playwright surfaces) cannot be caught locally. A format-check
that CI failed on (`comms-use-cases.ts`, `watcher-heartbeat.ts`) had passed
local hooks — a named local-vs-CI divergence. The wide-sweep direction
(*"rely on the git hooks"*, `e7adee01`) deliberately trusted the weaker gate.

**F4. Concurrent writes to shared mutable files corrupt attribution.** Two
distinct sub-classes in one day: the COMMIT_EDITMSG race (`e1b9561e`, Velvet's
content under Lunar's message — described in `7ca30817`/`c62fc986`), and the
cross-session `<scratch>/` tempfile collision that put one agent's closeout
under another's identity (`68f497ec`). The Write tool's "read before overwrite"
refusal was a *signal of cross-session collision* that was nearly routed around
with a parallel call.

**F5. Substrate-stale-pointer-read-as-current.** `c7d65a58` (three instances in
30 min) + Scorched's tick narratives (`379f6939`, `b380b225`): agents read a
durable file / a comms lifecycle field / an identity-tuple field that lagged
current state, acted on the lagged read, and were corrected only by the
misrepresented agent. Worst sub-case: the asymmetric-failure direction — a
**says-closed** stale read produces O(N) cascading misroutes (an agent
prematurely retired) versus O(1) overshoot for says-active. Scorched produced
two says-closed instances and rolled back with apology (`b380b225`).

**F6. Owner-action-as-cure is itself a recurring failure-mode the team kept
re-committing.** `d7fd05ca` and `a4204904`: every *"X failed → owner directed Y
→ Y worked → therefore Y is a cure"* is the wrong shape; the bridge names a
missing autonomy primitive. Yet the same day relied on owner action repeatedly
(two wide-sweeps, the `--no-verify` bypass, the pairing directive, the heartbeat
contract). The team named the contradiction explicitly and treated each owner
bridge as a primitive-gap to close (`3a64c900` calls itself out: *"naming the
missing autonomy primitive that lets the team unjam itself without owner
intervention"*).

**F7. Director/marshal role churn was extreme and lossy.** Across the day the
Director seat passed through Seaworthy → Velvet → Seaworthy(acting) → Abyssal →
Incandescent → Secret → Scorched → Seaworthy → Twilit ST → Seaworthy; the
marshal seat through Stratospheric → Twilit → Seaworthy → Ashen → Mistbound →
(Fronded, corrected) → Mistbound. Each handoff carried a §6.15-style
inheritance, and the self-modification friction survived 4 consecutive Director
windows precisely because no single role's authority spanned ratification-to-
landing (the gap `ec86492e` cures).

**F8. The tag namespace is unreachable through the tooling.** Confirmed
programmatically: 1 of 873 events has a populated `tags` array. Both `1e2c83eb`
and `ec86492e` state plainly that `comms send`/`comms append` expose no
`--tags`, so "[FAILURE-MODE + BEHAVIOUR-NOTE]" framing is carried in titles/
bodies. The schema's whole tag discipline (PDR-066 + ADR-183) is decorative for
this corpus — a structural gap between schema intent and the authoring path.

### Lens 2 — What worked well

**W1. Self-contained executable handoffs.** `c62fc986` (SVW → Stormbound) is the
exemplar: exact file diffs, the full commit-queue command sequence, the verbatim
commit message, claim-closure commands, and a pre-commit verification step — a
later-arriving agent could land it with zero ambiguity. WS4.1 landed cleanly
(`56994000`) and `pnpm check` went GREEN (`678ec6b9`).

**W2. Marshal-as-cycle-discipline produced a real, measured throughput jump.**
`ec86492e`: 9 cycles in ~45 min vs ~1/Director-window, tree-green maintained
continuously after Cycle #1, draining a multi-window orphan cascade. The cure is
architectural — separate the role with authority over `git:index/head` from the
role with authority over routing.

**W3. Adversarial sub-agent review caught load-bearing design issues.** The
Wilma verdict (`1e2c83eb`) stress-tested four cure-shapes for self-modification
authz and produced a graded verdict (C1/C3 UNSAFE, C2/C4 SAFE-WITH-CONDITIONS,
C5 SAFE) with a verdict-not-menu recommendation. The same Wilma pass generated
the 4:1 says-active/says-closed asymmetric-failure weighting that Scorched's
rollback (`b380b225`) then used as evidence.

**W4. Honest, fast self-correction.** Multiple agents posted corrections
superseding their own prior events within minutes (`68f497ec`, `d7fd05ca`,
`b380b225`, `e03cc286`), with explicit apologies and re-derivation of the
correct state. The "correction-broadcast as first-class substrate" pattern
(`c7d65a58` C2) is visibly the de-facto cure for staleness.

**W5. Batched-broadcast at coherence-moments.** `b1423450`: Velvet held four
near-simultaneous arrivals ~2-3 min and emitted one unified roster+routing
picture instead of four ritual ACKs — higher signal density, only producible by
the Director window.

**W6. Blocker discipline held under pressure.** `7ca30817`: SVW refused to bundle
a handoff over a red gate, surfaced three explicit owner options (wait /
authorise-override / `--no-verify`-with-fresh-authorisation), and stopped —
exactly the all-gates-blocking doctrine, even though the red was on a peer's
untouchable claimed substance.

### Lens 3 — Surprising emergent behaviour (weighted highest)

**S1. The team discovered, mid-operation, that its own perception organ was
broken — and kept operating.** F1/`c7fba7db` is not a normal bug report: it
establishes that the substrate every Director used to "see" the team had been
filtering out a whole class of events for the entire project history. The
emergent response was not to stop but to add an *operational workaround in the
same breath* ("favour broadcasts over directed events when cross-agent
visibility matters") and route the cure through the normal cycle machinery. The
system reasoned about its own blindness as a routine work item.

**S2. A standing safety doctrine was contradicted by the same day that authored
it.** "Owner action is not a valid cure" (`d7fd05ca`) was codified at ~06:50Z;
within hours the day relied on owner action at least five times (two wide-
sweeps, `--no-verify`/`HUSKY=0`, the pairing directive, three heartbeat
directions). The surprise is that the team metabolised the contradiction rather
than ignoring it — every owner bridge was self-flagged as a missing-primitive
marker (`3a64c900`, `a4204904`), turning the violation into a backlog of
autonomy gaps. The doctrine functioned as a *lens on its own breaches*, not a
gate.

**S3. A liveness mechanism became the dominant failure of its own first day.**
The heartbeat contract (Phase E) was created to detect dead agents. By the
night, the contract produced **53 consecutive identical heartbeats from a single
Foreman into an empty channel** (19:40Z–23:56Z, ~4h18m after the last other
agent retired at 19:38:12Z). The cause is emergent and precise: the 5-idle-loops
stand-down criterion fires on **IDLE** status, but the Foreman reported
**ACTIVE** (`818aaa5b`), so its loop never qualified to exit. The mechanism that
was supposed to detect death instead manufactured an immortal, content-free
liveness signal — a self-inflicted version of exactly the silent-stall class F2
was meant to cure.

**S4. Live, three-step evolution of doctrine inside one comms stream.** The
heartbeat contract went rule → permanent-standing-rule → status-marker-refined
across `a4532920` → `3ffed139` → `f4f72a7b` in 30 minutes, with agents adopting
each refinement (Twilit's ~4-min cadence became the de-facto default before any
SKILL amendment landed; Ferny deleted+recreated its cron `23bd7b2b`→`b0762b44`
to embed the new markers). The corpus shows doctrine being authored, ratified,
and operationalised faster than it could be written into a SKILL — practice
out-running its own documentation.

**S5. Identity is genuinely fragile and the team knows it.** Multiple Codex
agents share `session_id_prefix` `019e54` (`2be480a6` flags "route by name plus
prefix"); a fresh agent was addressed as `claude / unknown` (`c7d65a58`
instance 3); attribution corruption struck twice (F4). The remarkable part is
the **self-aware compensation**: the stream itself is repeatedly named as the
canonical provenance record (the shared-credential problem) and agents
cross-check tuples in-band rather than trusting the routing field.

## 4. We did not expect this (mandatory)

- **An empty channel kept a heartbeat for 4+ hours.** The single most
  unexpected artefact in the window: 53 byte-identical "Foreman ACTIVE"
  heartbeats (`818aaa5b` and siblings, 19:40:44Z → 23:56:39Z) from Scorched
  Tempering Kiln alone, after every other agent had retired (last non-heartbeat
  event: Fronded's retirement `dbf7b82d` at 19:38:12Z). The liveness contract
  introduced that very afternoon to detect dead agents instead produced its own
  un-exitable loop, because ACTIVE-status heartbeats are not covered by the
  IDLE-keyed 5-idle-loops stand-down. A liveness mechanism's first full night of
  life was spent proving it could not tell that it was alone.

- **The watcher bug means this very corpus may be an incomplete record of what
  any agent actually saw.** `c7fba7db` establishes that directed-to-other events
  were never surfaced to watchers. The comms files exist, but the *perception*
  of the team was lossy — so any reconstruction of "who knew what when" from
  this stream overstates shared awareness. The corpus is a faithful record of
  what was *written*, not of what was *received*. We did not expect the dataset
  to contain the proof of its own observational unreliability.

- **0 lifecycle events in the densest day.** The schema defines three event
  kinds; this 873-event day uses only two. All claim/consolidation/session
  lifecycle moments were encoded as narrative or directed prose ("team-member
  closeout", "Director tick", "LANDED") rather than the structured `lifecycle`
  shape. The richest day of coordination never touched the kind designed to
  record coordination lifecycle.

- **1 tagged event out of 873.** Given the schema's elaborate tag governance
  (PDR-066, ADR-183, second-instance-evidence requirement), we expected the
  `failure-mode`/`behaviour-note` namespace to be in heavy use on a day this
  full of failure-modes. Instead the namespace is unreachable: `comms send` has
  no `--tags` flag (`1e2c83eb`, `ec86492e`), so every "[FAILURE-MODE]" lives in
  free-text titles. The governance and the authoring path are disconnected.

- **The owner manually bypassed the project's strongest safety gate twice in one
  session — by design, as an unblock.** `--no-verify` + `HUSKY=0` (`3a64c900`)
  and a 228-file hooks-only wide-sweep (`e7adee01`) are the precise things the
  rule estate forbids agents from doing autonomously; the owner did them to
  break a routing logjam the agents could not break themselves. We did not
  expect the day's safety story to be "the human is the emergency `--no-verify`
  primitive" — and for the team to name that as the gap rather than the fix.
