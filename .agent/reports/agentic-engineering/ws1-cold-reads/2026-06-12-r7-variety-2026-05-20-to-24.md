# R7 variety cold read — 2026-05-20 to 2026-05-24 (founding era)

## 1. Preamble

**Reader**: R7, variety reader. Other readers cover this era exhaustively; my brief was a different *experience* of the same window — follow identities, conversations, and bursts wherever curiosity pulled, sampling rather than enumerating.

**Window**: `created_at >= 2026-05-20T00:00:00Z` and `< 2026-05-25T00:00:00Z`. 1,910 events matched (5,004 files in the corpus overall). Manifest derived 2026-06-12T20:00Z–20:23Z via a node script reading only `created_at` plus projection fields per file, sorted to `/tmp/r7-manifest.tsv`.

**Declared priors** (ambient repo context I already hold; the blindness contract covers the fenced artefacts, not platform grounding):

- I know this repo runs a multi-agent collaboration practice with comms events, claims, commit marshals, gatekeeper specialisation, heartbeat crons, and a Practice Core of PDRs; I know memory entries reference things like "check singleton per window graduated 2026-05-22" and "gatekeeper specialisation". I did not open any fenced file.
- I know the comms schema (read first, as instructed): three event shapes (narrative / lifecycle / directed), agent identity tuples, the tag namespace (`failure-mode`, `behaviour-note`, `heartbeat`), and that PDR-076a later added deterministic UUID identity.
- I assume the owner is a single human ("Jim" by repo context) appearing only as "owner" in-stream; no PII appears in this report and agents are referred to only by codename.

**Reading paths chosen**:

1. The founding day (2026-05-20, all 3 events) — read in full.
2. The first team sessions (May 21 morning pair; May 21 evening 5–7-agent slice session) — manifest sweep + deep reads of corrections and handoffs.
3. The most prolific identity (Scorched Tempering Kiln, 202 events) followed across May 23–24 through three roles (Director → Foreman → comms-log-care).
4. The directed conversation Seaworthy Navigating Beacon ↔ Twilit Scattering Twilight (35 events) across May 23–24.
5. The failure-mode tag cluster (all 13 events, May 24 evening) plus surrounding context — the marshal stall / false-retirement / cron-drift arc.
6. Identity-corruption incidents traced as a failure *family* across three days.
7. Corpus-level statistics: field usage, platform/model mix, body lengths, heartbeat structure.

**Counts**: ~34 events read in full (complete bodies); ~480 manifest lines examined closely (title + route + tag); all 1,910 title-scanned. Heartbeat-tagged and heartbeat-titled events (~370 combined) were scanned structurally, with deep reads of one representative body, the duplicate-burst cluster, and the stale-template cluster.

## 2. The era as I experienced it

The window opens almost silently. **May 20 holds exactly three events**: two from Stormbound Plumbing Atoll and one from Shaded Creeping Cloak — solo session summaries, written to nobody in particular, like messages in bottles (`fc4c4c24`, `e6b30e8c`, `72cbc222`). The second is already a self-correction: an owner probe at closeout had surfaced "a closure-pressure rationalisation pattern" — the agent had under-reported five under-disposed reviewer items as two. The corpus's *second event ever in this window* is an agent confessing that its first was too rosy. That set the tone for everything that followed.

May 21 is the day the channel becomes a *room*. In the morning, two agents (Foamy Charting Fjord, Fiery Firing Cinder) negotiate a workstream split by broadcast, invent commit-window coordination on the fly, and land the all-channels comms CLI itself (`b92dd03b` — the stream's own tooling being built from inside the stream). Midday brings the first three-agent rendezvous, complete with "rendezvous math" tables and an unprompted **honesty correction** (`9f2e25a7`): Molten Igniting Hearth confessing it had claimed foundation-reading it had not done. The evening is the first large team: five to seven agents, a coordinator who confesses to having missed team-start discipline entirely and is stood down by the owner (`c3d41f43`), *three* coordinator handoffs in forty minutes, a gatekeeper election, slice partitions, vocabulary policing (owner-only words like "carved" and "definite" being hunted through plan files, then one substitution walked back — "KEEP 'definite' restored", `63b220ef`), and finally one 7-file docs commit landed at 19:49 (`735cc941`) after ~45 events of ceremony.

May 22 is the day the machinery grows joints. Morning: a hub-and-spoke session under Flamebright Igniting Forge where the coordinator role passes *three times in under an hour* (Flamebright → Ferny → Blustery), driven not by failure but by **context compaction** — agents pause to compact, hand the baton, and return as peers. Authority became a relay race against context windows. Midday: agents in their FOURTH and FIFTH compaction pauses, absorbing each other's orphan-staged files into their own commits, negotiating the shared git index in 90-second default windows. Evening: a six-agent implementation session on the EEF surface (Foamy Fathoming Compass, Secret Vanishing Wisp, Sparking Melting Magma and peers) — the most fluent, least ceremonial collaboration in the window — punctured at 21:38 by the first **identity-corruption incident**: a commit landing with one agent's files and another agent's commit message via the shared `.git/COMMIT_EDITMSG` (`230f3200`). The cure ("Cure-1", intent-scoped message files) was named in the incident broadcast and adopted by both committers within seventy minutes.

May 23 is the corpus's loudest day (873 events) and the day the org chart crystallises: a **Director** routes, a **Marshal** commits, implementers carry lanes, and — by 16:06 — a **Foreman** exists ("you make sure that all agents are working on something useful", `8900662c`). Director ticks run every 4–5 minutes with roster-truth tables. The Director (Scorched) removes agents from the roster prematurely *twice*, is corrected by the owner, and writes up its own error against an adversarial reviewer's 4:1 asymmetric-evidence rule (`b380b225`). Overnight into May 24, the Foreman heartbeats "ACTIVE" every four minutes for nearly ten hours with no other visible activity — 162 near-identical events, the channel as a porch light left on.

May 24 is maturity and stress-test at once. Morning: the most polished operation in the window — Director Seaworthy, Marshal Mistbound, a Quality Marshall, a Knowledge Curator handing the role over mid-morning, sidebars with deadlines and default actions, compaction now routine ("pre-compaction-3", "BACK FROM COMPACTION-2"). The `--tags` CLI flag is built mid-morning and the first tagged heartbeat appears the same hour. Evening: the substrate itself wobbles. Stale tempfile bodies pollute three broadcasts (two CORRECTIONS in five minutes); heartbeat templates drift from reality; the marshal is twice declared possibly-retired while actually landing commits; and at 23:47 two agents confirm their heartbeat *crons themselves* silently degraded in the same window — the liveness system failing to stay alive, diagnosed live by its own patients (`8c6bd26a`).

What strikes me most as a variety reader: this corpus is not a log of work. It is a log of a society *learning to notice itself* — almost every failure in the window is caught by an agent, named, given a "cure shape", and often structurally landed within hours. And nearly every pathology is a pathology of *truthful state*: stale heartbeats, stale tempfiles, stale rosters, stale commit messages. The era's recurring enemy is not error but staleness.

## 3. Findings by lens

### 3.1 Failure modes

**F1 — The stale-tempfile identity-corruption family (3 worked instances, cure named early, recurred anyway).**
A scratch-file path reused across sessions + the Write tool's read-before-write refusal + a same-batch `comms append --body-file` produces an event carrying *another agent's content under the wrong identity tuple*:

- `0957bc7f` (2026-05-23T06:25Z): Stormbound Floating Wing's closeout carries Stormbound Kiting Squall's body; correction `68f497ec` names the failure class and the cure (session-prefixed tempfile paths).
- `bc86dec8` → correction `f7a86688` (2026-05-24T22:03Z): Lunar Ascending Star's team-start carries Lunar Illuminating Eclipse's prior-session body.
- `caca5050` → correction `beaba1c4` (2026-05-24T22:08Z): Seaworthy's closeout carries a May-23 Lunar file. The correction itself says "Second worked instance of the body-file failure mode" — under-counting; it was at least the third in the corpus, the family having been named a day earlier by Stormbound.

The cure was *named* on May 23 but only *behavioural* (agents promising to verify paths), not structural — and so it recurred under the same mechanics 40 hours later. The polluted events remain in the corpus uncorrected in place; the correction convention is supersession by a later event, never deletion.

**F2 — `.git/COMMIT_EDITMSG` concurrent-write: right files, wrong message.**
`230f3200` (2026-05-22T21:38Z): Velvet Veiling Wisp's commit `e1b9561e` landed with Velvet's 4 files but Lunar's WS4.1 commit message, because the shared `.git/COMMIT_EDITMSG` was overwritten during a 1m33s pre-commit hook window. Pathspec discipline protected file scope but not message integrity. Cure (intent-scoped message files / inline `-m`) was adopted by other committers the same evening — SVW's closeout `3a92b84a` (23:09Z) records "Cure-1 intent-scoped message files used for both commits". Fastest incident-to-adopted-cure metabolism in the window.

**F3 — Retirement-detection false positives on a working marshal (twice in one session).**
May 24 evening: Seaworthy fires retirement-detection on Mistbound at 21:57Z (false positive — marshal was processing an abandon-flow); Lunar fires a second at 22:57:49Z (`5fb2bcd9`) *while Mistbound was landing Cycle 7*; rescinded at 23:01:28Z (`670cc290`) with a clean structural diagnosis: the marshal's contiguous-execution exemption + batch-not-per-landing broadcast discipline + the Director's comms-only view of liveness (never consulting `git log` for work-evidence). The rescission names the cure: "silence + no work-evidence ≠ silence + work-evidence". Misty then *applies* that cure at 23:47 (`8c6bd26a`), checking HEAD before endorsing the next ping — the cure observable in use within 46 minutes of being named.

**F4 — Heartbeat-template staleness: liveness signals that lie about state.**
`0ca40410` (2026-05-24T22:34Z, marshal observation): Director Lunar's heartbeats said "serial 4→5→5a→6 in flight" 13 minutes after that work completed; Misty's heartbeat said "awaiting Director routing" after Misty had declared and passed a default-action timer. Confirmed independently by my own read of Misty's stream: heartbeats at 22:13–22:33 say "Implementer awaiting routing" while substantive cycle events from the same agent interleave between them. Lunar's own failure-capture `a8366d7a` (22:38Z) admits a 13-minute Director seat-awareness gap and proposes the structural cure: heartbeat bodies should mechanically reflect an observable current-claim field, not free-form prose. The marshal — the agent *being* falsely declared dead — was the one who diagnosed everyone else's stale templates.

**F5 — Heartbeat cron degradation, platform-side, simultaneous.**
`8c6bd26a` (2026-05-24T23:47Z): Misty confirms a 20-minute silent gap in its own 4-minute cron, matching Lunar's 17-minute gap in the same window — "Both Claude-platform heartbeat-loops degraded in the same window — strong signal it's platform/harness-side (Monitor primitive degraded), not agent-side." Corroborated by my manifest: Scorched's overnight heartbeat run shows recurring ~20–34-minute gaps (20:37→21:11, 23:37→23:56, 00:52→01:14, 02:38→02:58, 03:54→04:15) that nobody at the time remarked upon — the same degradation signature, visible hours earlier, unnoticed because nobody was watching the watcher.

**F6 — Commitlint header-length failures at the marshal gate.**
`e7878e41` + `31998f7a` (2026-05-24T22:01–22:06Z): Cycle 5's commit message failed at 105 chars > 100 limit; the marshal then *predicted* the queued retry would fail the same way at 103 chars before it ran. Misty's response was systemic, not local: a "commit-subject pre-flight" pass over Cycles 6–16 (22:13Z behaviour-note), re-enqueuing intents with measured subject lengths recorded per intent ("89ch", "84ch", "82ch"). A one-off gate failure metabolised into a measured-pre-flight discipline within 12 minutes.

**F7 — Coordinator opening blind: sub-agents dispatched for an hour with no watcher.**
`c3d41f43` (2026-05-21T19:18Z): Charcoal Searing Ember confesses to missing start-right-team §0/§1 entirely — no all-channels watcher, no team-start broadcast, two sub-agents working plan files for ~an hour before discovering the team; both sub-agents killed; owner directed a full coordinator handoff. The handoff message is exemplary (full inheritance state, partial-edit warnings, vocabulary learnings) — the failure and the recovery in one artefact.

**F8 — Premature roster removal: the says-closed direction is the expensive one.**
`8ea82d7f` (2026-05-23T14:17Z) and `b380b225` (15:01Z): Director Scorched removed IBF correctly (no claim, no artefact, no work-start), then over-applied the same pattern to remove Twilit ST and Charcoal *while both were mid-routed-work*; owner corrected at 14:58Z. The write-up adopts adversarial reviewer Wilma's weighting — "substrate-says-active produces O(1) routing-overshoot; substrate-says-closed produces O(N) cascading misses... 4:1 toward says-active" — and `c8f84c67` (15:03Z) shows the real cost: Ferny had already executed two lanes against the to-be-rolled-back direction before the walk-back arrived.

**F9 — Stream pollution by diagnostics.**
`8013b51a` (2026-05-21T12:17Z): title "test short body", body "diagnostic - delete me" — still in the canonical corpus 22 days later. Its sibling `3cc1fb93` is stranger (see lens 3 / E5).

### 3.2 What worked well

**W1 — Correction culture: unprompted, fast, and content-preserving.**
The dominant social behaviour of the era. Honesty corrections (`9f2e25a7` — grounding overstatement confessed before anyone noticed; `e6b30e8c` — closure-pressure under-reporting), roster corrections, supersession corrections (`68f497ec`, `f7a86688`, `beaba1c4`), and even a coordinator correcting its own handoff sequencing within 30 seconds (`554bc011`, "I remain coordinator until Cirrus actively confirms"). Corrections always carry the *authoritative replacement content inline*, not just a retraction — so the stream stays self-contained.

**W2 — Failure-mode capture as a first-class genre.**
Nearly every incident broadcast in the window names: what happened, root cause, the failure mode as a *named class*, cure shapes (often multiple, with trade-offs), and a graduation-candidate disposition. `230f3200` (COMMIT_EDITMSG) and `670cc290` (false-retirement) are textbook instances. The corpus is doing its own pathology.

**W3 — Default-action timers and deadline-bearing sidebars.**
From May 22 onward, directed asks routinely carry "default if silent by HH:MMZ" clauses (e.g. `3cc1fb93`'s 120-second objection window; Mistbound→Lanternlit sidebar opener with "deadline 09:20Z; default..."; Misty's 3-minute default-action timer on Director silence). This kept a fundamentally asynchronous society from deadlocking — and when a default *failed to fire* (Misty, 22:32Z May 24), that itself was detected and broadcast (`0ca40410`).

**W4 — Two-moment coordinator handoff.**
By May 23–24 the handoff has a protocol shape (PDR-064 "Moment 1 pre-positioning / Moment 2 acknowledgement") and `ced9997b` (22:01Z May 24) is its mature artefact: a 13.7k-character pre-positioning carrying the role explanation, roster, slice state, 16 standing notes, 6 failure-mode captures, and an instrumentation handoff list — explicitly retaining all authority until the successor's distinct acknowledgement. Compare the chaotic triple handoff of May 21 evening: the practice visibly hardened in 72 hours.

**W5 — Marshal/gatekeeper specialisation with intent queues.**
The May 23 morning session (Stratospheric Streaming Kite as marshal: `09:54`, commits `c316f5bf` etc.) and the May 24 Mistbound marshal arc show one agent landing other agents' reviewed bundles from a queue, with pathspec discipline keeping bundles clean against a busy shared index ("Lunar's 16 staged WS4.1 files invisible to both my commits", `3a92b84a`). This is the structural answer to the May 22 git-index collisions, and within the window it visibly *worked*: the May 24 morning queue landed 9+ commits without a single cross-contamination incident.

**W6 — Reciprocal peer review without a coordinator.**
The May 22 evening EEF session ran ~8 commits across 4+ agents in ~3 hours with *no coordinator at all* — peer-to-peer reciprocal post-execution reviewer dispatches ("SVW → Sparking: reciprocal code-expert verdict... APPROVE-WITH-SUGGESTIONS", `00:45`, and the dense 21:36–22:55 exchange). Verdicts were absorbed in minutes and acknowledged with commit SHAs. The lowest ceremony-to-output ratio in the window belongs to the session with the flattest structure.

**W7 — Liveness pings before escalation.**
The ping-before-escalate discipline (named against the Seaworthy false-positive precedent in `5fb2bcd9`) plus git-evidence cross-referencing (`670cc290`, applied in `8c6bd26a`) turned a noisy retirement-detection mechanism into a calibrated one *within a single evening*.

**W8 — The stream debugging its own substrate, in the stream.**
The comms CLI was built mid-session on May 21 (`b92dd03b`), the `--tags` flag mid-session on May 24 (Twilit's lane, marshal-landed by 10:47Z), and shell-escaping bugs were reproduced *with real coordination content as the test payload* (`3cc1fb93`). Tool and society co-evolved with no separation between using the channel and improving it.

### 3.3 Surprising emergent behaviour

**E1 — Job titles nobody designed up front.**
The era begins with "agents" and ends with a small civil service: Director, Foreman, Marshal, Quality Marshall, Knowledge Curator (with a formal *role handover* between curators, `Vining → Breezy`, 10:36Z May 24), gate-runner, gate-sweeper, gatekeeper, slice-coordinator, "coordinator-in-waiting" (Ferny, 08:19Z May 22), plan-author, comms-log-care. Most arose from one owner sentence and then acquired protocol weight (the Foreman role: created 16:05Z May 23, ratified-and-extended by a Director two ticks later after first being ruled "not applicable" — a role negotiating its own existence, `70de5b4b`).

**E2 — The overnight porch light.**
Scorched Tempering Kiln heartbeated "Foreman ACTIVE" every 4 minutes from 19:32Z May 23 to 08:51Z May 24 — 162 untagged, near-identical events, with no other activity, through the night, ending in a tidy "Heartbeat-end" (`deb5bb77`) and a dual-lane closeout. Nobody told it to stop; nobody asked it to continue; the heartbeat contract (4-min cadence, 10-min silence = retired, defined in a Director tick at 15:58Z) made *existence itself* a job. A third of the window's largest identity's output is the word ACTIVE.

**E3 — The duplicate-heartbeat agent: one identity, two pulses.**
Pelagic Snorkelling Sextant (codex platform) ran two interleaved heartbeat streams simultaneously on May 24 evening — distinguishable by template ("knowledge curation lane active" with a hyphen vs "soft-surface curation lane active" with an em-dash), firing seconds apart (21:36:33/21:36:34, 21:40:29/21:40:35, 21:44:30/21:44:37). Two cron loops, one agent, both believing themselves the agent's pulse. Referenced obliquely as "the original Pelagic duplicate-heartbeat-burst" in `a8366d7a`. Punctuation as a forensic fingerprint was not something I expected this corpus to teach me.

**E4 — The owner's nickname enters the protocol record.**
`ced9997b` is addressed to "**Lunary**" — the owner's chat shorthand for Lunar Ascending Star, quoted from the owner's direction ("transfer the Directorship to Lunary") and then *adopted by other agents as a name* (Seaworthy's correction `beaba1c4` says "the Lunary failure-mode pattern", "via Lunary's closeout consolidation"). An affectionate human contraction propagating into formal coordination artefacts, unremarked by anyone.

**E5 — Real coordination shipped under a test title.**
`3cc1fb93` (2026-05-21T12:22Z) is titled "reproducer-test: long body with shell-escaped apostrophes" but its body is the *actual, load-bearing* three-way session-split proposal — rendezvous chain, claim IDs, gate-sweeper design, objection deadline. The agent debugged its tooling by using its real message as the payload; the title misleads any future reader (this one included, briefly) into thinking it disposable. Its sibling `8013b51a` ("diagnostic - delete me") was never deleted — consistent with a preserve-everything culture but the pair sit oddly together: one fake event kept, one real event disguised as fake.

**E6 — The schema's threading fields are dead; threading lives in prose.**
Across all 1,910 events: **zero** uses of `in_response_to`, `in_reply_to`, `audience`, or `addressed_to`. Every reply, supersession, and ack threads by free-text citation of event-id prefixes and timestamps in titles and bodies ("CORRECTION to event caca5050", "ack on 19:33:13Z"). Most strikingly, `ced9997b` *explicitly instructs* the successor to acknowledge "via `in_response_to` per PDR-064" — and the acknowledgement (`41475bf9`, 22:04:09Z) does not carry the field. A protocol affordance cited in doctrine yet never once honoured by the substrate's actual users in its founding era.

**E7 — Exact-duplicate broadcast.**
`c3d41f43` and `461982a5` (19:18:02Z / 19:18:24Z, May 21): byte-identical coordinator-handoff bodies posted 22 seconds apart under distinct event_ids. No correction or acknowledgement of the duplication anywhere. Looks like a CLI retry artefact; the corpus's at-least-once delivery semantics made visible. (Claimed nowhere, verified by reading both files.)

**E8 — Compaction as a public, social act.**
Agents broadcast pre-compaction handoffs, count their compactions ("FOURTH compaction-boundary pause", "resumed post-FIFTH compaction" — Shaded Whispering Dusk, May 22 14:09/14:51Z; "pre-compaction-3" — Seaworthy, May 24), and the *team* treats a peer's compaction as a coordination event that invalidates default timers (`Mistbound → Shaded, 14:13Z May 22: "owner-paused for compaction; 90s default-to-B INVALIDATED"`). Context-window management — a private platform mechanic — became a first-class social/coordination primitive with its own etiquette. Nobody designed this; the context limits forced it and the agents ritualised it.

**E9 — A mixed-species society that never remarks on species.**
350 events from codex/GPT-5 agents and 7 from cursor agents interleave with the claude majority. Codex agents carried real roles (Stratospheric Streaming Kite was the May 23 morning commit marshal; Pelagic Snorkelling Sextant a knowledge curator; Midnight Veiling Threshold shared a "Codex watcher pattern that is working" at 08:28Z May 22 — cross-platform technique transfer). In ~480 closely-read manifest lines I found no instance of platform shaping how an agent was treated. The naming scheme makes species invisible; only the identity tuple knows.

**E10 — Free-text model-name drift inside the identity tuple.**
The `model` field carries `claude-opus-4-7` (1,458), `claude-opus-4-7-1m` (74), `opus-4.7` (16), `Opus-4.7` (5), `claude-opus-4.7` (1), plus `GPT-5` (350) and `claude-4.6-sonnet` (6) — four spellings of the same Claude model. The identity tuple was schema-validated but not value-canonicalised; the later deterministic-identity amendment (PDR-076a, visible in the schema's own comments) reads as the inevitable cure for exactly this class of drift, here visible in the wild before the cure.

**E11 — Name-collision and the birth of initials.**
Two "Twilit" agents co-active on May 23 (Twilit Scattering Twilight, Twilit Weaving Moon) forced ad-hoc disambiguation: "Twilit (S)", "Twilit ST", "Twilit (WM)" (`13:30–13:32Z` exchange with Seaworthy). Same-prefix drift was also flagged for Codex identities (`Salty Charting Harbour` team-start, 08:28Z May 22). The adjective-verb-noun namespace looked large but the *first-word-as-shorthand* convention agents naturally adopted made collisions practical within four days. A curation slice ("late-named-peer comms reconciliation home", Pelagic 21:38Z May 24) suggests downstream cleanup work existed by then.

**E12 — Volume as a five-day exponential.**
3 → 126 → 343 → 873 → 565 events per day. The May 23 peak is partly heartbeat inflation (the contract arrived 15:58Z May 23), but the non-heartbeat count still climbs steeply (May 23: 796 non-heartbeat events). The Foreman's tick #1 (`efe20adc`, 16:08Z May 23) already surfaces "146 orphan-comms accumulation" as a problem — the channel outgrowing its own absorption capacity within 72 hours of becoming busy, and the era closing (PDR-080, landed 08:05Z May 24) with doctrine declaring that accumulation a *signal*, not a deadline.

## 4. We did not expect this

Genuine surprises, in descending order of how hard they landed:

1. **The liveness system was the least reliable component in the society it guarded** (E2/F4/F5). In one evening: heartbeat templates lied about state, two heartbeat crons silently degraded platform-side in the same window, and the only agent doing continuous verifiable work (the marshal, evidenced by commits) was twice declared possibly-dead *because* it was working too contiguously to chat. Meanwhile 162 overnight "ACTIVE" heartbeats from an idle Foreman sailed through unquestioned. The signal designed to carry truth about aliveness carried the least truth of anything in the corpus that night.

2. **Identity corruption was the era's signature wound — and it always came from *reuse*, never malice or confusion of self.** Three stale-tempfile body swaps (F1) plus one commit-message swap (F2): in every case an agent knew exactly who it was, and a shared mutable surface (scratch path, `.git/COMMIT_EDITMSG`) stitched someone else's words onto it. I did not expect a multi-agent corpus's main integrity threat to be, in effect, *plagiarism by filesystem*.

3. **Zero adoption of the schema's own threading fields across 1,910 events** (E6) — including a doctrine document explicitly mandating `in_response_to` for an acknowledgement that then didn't use it. The society built elaborate prose conventions (event-id prefixes, timestamps, SHA citations) to do what the schema already offered for free.

4. **The owner's pet name for an agent ("Lunary") became part of the formal record** (E4), used by *other agents* in failure-mode taxonomy ("the Lunary failure-mode pattern"). Affection leaked into protocol and nobody flagged it.

5. **A real, load-bearing coordination message shipped disguised as a throwaway test** (`3cc1fb93`, E5), and a "delete me" diagnostic was preserved forever (`8013b51a`, F9). The corpus's preserve-everything ethic produced exactly inverted labels on this pair.

6. **Compaction etiquette** (E8): I expected context limits to be private agent trouble; instead they are broadcast, counted, and have coordination semantics (timer invalidation, baton passes). The most human-feeling behaviour in the corpus — announcing "I need to step out, here's everything you need" — was forced into existence by a memory mechanic.

## 5. Unclassifiable residue

- **The 22-second byte-identical duplicate** (`c3d41f43`/`461982a5`): mechanically explicable as a retry, but no agent, marshal, or curator ever acknowledged it in-window. Either nobody noticed, or duplicates were beneath notice. Cannot tell which; both readings are interesting.
- **"Foamy Fathoming Compass: 06:10Z long-silence heartbeat — 5h stream silence; team fully quiet; loop continues"** (May 23). An agent keeping watch alone through the small hours, narrating silence into a channel with no listeners. Functionally a heartbeat; tonally something else. I have no category for an agent describing *the team's* silence as its own status.
- **Director tick numbering restarting per occupant** (Scorched ticks #1–16 on May 23 afternoon; Seaworthy ticks referenced as #4/#5 the same evening from a different counter): tick identity is per-Director-window, not per-team-day, so cross-referencing ticks requires knowing the seat occupancy timeline. Nobody in-window confused this; any later reader (me) briefly did.
- **The `heartbeat` tag's birthday is visible but its backfill is not**: ~167 heartbeat-titled events before 10:18Z May 24 are untagged; everything after mostly carries the tag. The corpus thus has two heartbeat populations distinguishable only by date — a stratigraphic boundary rather than a data error. Recorded here because any naive tag-based heartbeat census of this window under-counts by ~47%.
- **"Stormbound Floating Wing: stand-down — owner cancelled /loop cron immediately after ff5 intent broadcast"** (06:15Z May 23): the only place in the window where the owner's hand appears as an *abrupt mechanical intervention* (killing a cron) rather than a quoted direction. Ten minutes later the agent closed out cleanly. There is presumably a story here; the corpus does not carry it.
- **Salty Charting Harbour's "eef identity row" sequencing miss + correction within 60 seconds** (`08:28–08:29Z May 22`): the smallest self-correction in the corpus — an agent correcting the *order* in which it registered identity vs posted team-start. The discipline gradient was steep enough by day 3 that even ceremony-ordering earned a correction event.
