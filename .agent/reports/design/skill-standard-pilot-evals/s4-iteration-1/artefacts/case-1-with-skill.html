About this unit

Britain's landscape, work and daily life were transformed in little more than a century. This unit follows that change from rural workshops to steam-powered factories, asking pupils to weigh up who gained and who paid the price.

Pupils build on their knowledge of Georgian Britain from Year 7, and this unit sets up the trade and empire topics that follow later in Year 8.

What pupils will learn
  1. Why steam power and new machinery changed how goods were made
  2. How and why people moved from the countryside into industrial towns
  3. What working conditions were like in factories and mines
  4. How industrial towns created new problems — and new reforms
