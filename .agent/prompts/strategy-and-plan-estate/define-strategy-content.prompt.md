## What the strategy is (decided)

A cohesive, leadership-grade **system-strategy** at `docs/strategy/`. Conception = **choices +
measures**. Structure = **portfolio tier + per-stream sections**, cohesive **across and within**
the three streams (the framework builds the other two; the tools are the foundation; the app
proves it). It **must expose an enumerable, stable set of strategic-choice IDs** — these become
the traceability targets every plan later resolves to.

## Derivation (the flow)

Each **vision element → strategic choice(s) → traces up to an Oak goal**, under the four pillars.
Stream → goal: teachers ← MCP app; ecosystem ← engineering tools **and** the agentic framework;
schools = **deliberate non-goal**. We **align, not fulfil**.

Diagnosis (the experiment→product challenge); Oak-alignment (stream→goal map, schools non-goal,

what-we-will-NOT-do + measures**; K1–K3 inside the app section as its readiness keystones; every
release-readiness requirement as a **named hand-off with accountability**.

## Owner inputs already settled — build on these, do **not** re-open

- **Cost** matters but is **not** critical and must **not** dominate architectural excellence;
  **funding is out of scope** (project funded). Do not centre cost/sustainability in the diagnosis.
- Teachers **already use** AI hosts; we bring Oak's rigour as (1) source materials, (2) agent
  guidance, and (3, future) a response-moderation service the host vets against. Positive framing.
- The **teacher is the safety layer**; teachers are critically-aware experts, not conduits;
  **nothing in the app is aimed at students**.
- **Measures are an Oak input** — defined and measured **with** Oak's analytics and research
  experts; do not invent them. Surface an owner/expert checkpoint.
- The **release channel is not unilateral**: app-like packaging + promotion in ChatGPT/Claude
  need **vendor collaboration** (vendors aware and **agreed**). **Marketing is blocked until the
  TPC risk is sufficiently mitigated.** Carry both as named hand-offs.
- **Compliance production blockers** (mostly external; tracked in the compliance lane): **ATRS,
  detailed DPIA, ICO Children's Code, safeguarding, independent AI-output evals.** Named hand-offs.
- **ICO Children's Code ↔ target-audience** ("nothing aimed at students") is an **open
  discussion**, cross-linked launch-readiness K2 ↔ §B3 — keep it linked; it is an owner + legal
  decision.
