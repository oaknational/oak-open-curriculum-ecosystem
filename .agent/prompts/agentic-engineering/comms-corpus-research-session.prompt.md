## Why this research, in the owner's framing

The comms event corpus holds patterns nobody has recognised yet. This is original research,
and it is explicitly NOT a find-and-fix-problems pass: you are looking for failure modes,
for **things that worked well** (so they can be protected and propagated), and — most of all —
for **surprising emergent behaviour** that can be encouraged or discouraged by tuning
activation enthalpy: nudges in comms tool design, defaults, affordances, and ceremony cost,
in preference to mandates. Discovery outranks confirmation throughout.

**This is very important research. It is not to be rushed.** Multi-session execution is
expected; depth beats coverage-per-hour. If you approach context budget mid-workstream,
retire per PDR-063 with your survey index and surprises log as the handoff assets.
