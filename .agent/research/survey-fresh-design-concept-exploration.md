## Movement 2 — the problem space

**Kind of thing**: a measurement-system design problem — a reference
standard (the corpus) plus an instrument family (the survey) for an
estate that changes while being measured. Not a classification exercise
(the census is designed) and not a doctrine exercise (the corpus's
sources exist).

**The gap and its mechanism**: the estate holds three bodies of proven
thinking about foundational patterns with overlapping but unreconciled
vocabularies, batteries, and placement epistemologies — and no single
reference surface an estate measurement can be scored against. The
causal mechanism is named by Finding 1: the estate's method re-derives
the same laws because the generator is real, and each derivation lands
in its own home with its own vocabulary. **The corpus's purpose,
sharpened: make the generator citable, so independent re-derivation
becomes recorded corroboration instead of silent duplication.** The
corpus is the structural cure for the missed-stitch class.

**Who the gap harms**: the owner's estate-boundary decision (waiting on
the census's axis); tranche work needing corpus-grounded target states;
future doctrine, which risks accreting a fourth battery and a fourth
capability sense; every future seat that re-derives without citing.

**Constraints**: the four owner fixed points; the execution gate
(expiry 2026-09-02, discharge at Jim's word); XPLAT-2 forward-scoping;
Finding 6's staleness gradient; the maturity-gradient direction
(instruments over vigilance); PDR-132 round budgets at authoring; the
quota throttle for any fleet work.

**Success**: a design under which every corpus member carries
claim-class-scored claims with evidence, falsifiers, provenance dates,
and an independent-derivation count; estate measurement scores against
the corpus mechanically where detector facts reach, with judged
readings corroborated proportionately per scale; the three batteries
are one battery-family keyed by claim class with zero new batteries;
placement epistemology chosen visibly with the owner; and the design
names its own falsifiers and staleness model.

## Movement 3 — what re-opening the solution space changed

- **The corpus must be a structured artefact, not a document.** The
  fluent first answer (a prose corpus with good headings) would regress
  Finding 4's maturity gradient — the finding's own falsifier names
  exactly that regression. The corpus follows the opener nodes'
  discipline: structured row data, closed vocabularies, a committed
  validator, human prose rendered from or cross-checked against the
  data.
- **Membership and claims separate.** The three batteries answer three
  different questions about one candidate (survival, establishedness,
  promotability). Admission to the corpus is therefore cheap — a
  recorded candidacy with evidence — while *claims about* a member are
  expensive and battery-scored. A pattern can be established in the
  wild yet not promotable here, and the corpus records precisely that
  profile. This dissolves "which battery admits members" and mirrors
  the detector-facts / judged-readings separation.
- **My layered DECON-5 frame is a candidate choice, not the
  adjudication.** "Meaning proposes where a seam is; second-consumer
  economics disposes when to materialise it" is a genuine epistemology
  with content — but the routing demands the owner choose visibly among
  live options, and presenting only the layered frame would average the
  conflict away. The adjudication package below carries the
  alternatives and a recommendation.
- **A closed SCALE vocabulary is missing from my own ratified node.**
  The machinery-deconstruction ledger's `generalises-to <scale>` column
  requires a scale value on every surviving row, and the node closes
  every vocabulary at authoring time — except this one. Filling the
  ledger without it is mid-census taxonomy invention, the exact failure
  the census node forbids for itself. This is the exploration's
  strongest concrete yield: an amendment proposal, routed to the
  Director below, before ledger execution.

## Movement 4 — synthesis and proposals

**Holistic understanding**: the fresh survey design is the design of
the estate's measurement organ — a claim-scored pattern registry plus
proportionate per-scale measurement — whose purpose is to make the
estate's real-but-uncited shared generator citable. Its inputs are
reconciliation problems, not authorship problems: three batteries → one
battery-family keyed by claim class; three capability senses → a
three-term normative lexicon; two placement doctrines → one visibly
chosen epistemology. The design itself must sit at the top of the
maturity gradient: corpus-as-data with a validator, every vocabulary
closed at authoring time, falsifiers operational.

Proposals (all design-only; none executes gated work):

1. **Close the scale vocabulary before ledger execution** (amendment to
   the machinery node's instrument spec, for Director review then the
   owner's amendment trail). The `generalises-to` column takes values
   from a closed set declared at authoring; a row whose element resists
   every value takes a recorded `needs-scale-adjudication` and routes
   once at fill completion, mirroring the census falsifier. *Warrant*:
   the node's own closed-at-authoring discipline applied to its own
   column. *Falsifier*: if drafting the set shows surviving elements
   generalise predominantly to non-scale targets (method, governance),
   the column needs a different axis, and the amendment routes back to
   design instead of landing.
2. **Corpus shape: registry-of-patterns with claim-class-scored
   batteries.** Structured rows; membership cheap, claims scored;
   per-claim provenance dates with XPLAT-2 forward-scoping generalised
   (establishedness never backdated); an independent-derivation count
   as recorded corroboration strength; stitch pointers from each member
   to its should-be sources and is-proven events. *Warrant*: the
   reconciliation-not-authorship reading plus the maturity gradient
   plus the missed-stitch cure. *Falsifier*: the side-by-side battery
   read (§Unresolved evidence) failing to map the batteries' gates onto
   distinct claim classes — heavy cross-cutting breaks the claim-class
   key and demands a different key.
3. **DECON-5 adjudication package for the owner moment.** Recommend the
   layered epistemology (meaning answers WHERE a seam is; second-consumer
   economics answers WHEN to materialise it), presented with the two
   live alternatives — the current rulings-ledger scope verdict
   (estate rules govern; the charter binds only its research frame) and
   the inversion (charter ratified estate-wide) — and the
   recommendation's falsifier: one real placement decision in estate
   history where meaning and second-consumer economics conflicted as
   answers to the SAME question would collapse the layers and demand a
   priority rule instead. *Warrant*: DECON-5 routes the choice to the
   fresh design with the owner present; a recommendation with named
   alternatives and a falsifier is a verdict, not a menu.
4. **Hold the sequencing fixed points.** Census first; the code-scale
   run decision waits on the ledger's contract-coverage map. Restated
   to prevent drift; no new proposal.

## Disputes and cautions on the banked inputs (the Director's ask)

- **Banked input 1, mild interpretive divergence**: "reconciled into
  the corpus, not accreted as a fourth" should not be read as *merged
  into one battery*. The reconciliation target is one battery per claim
  class with overlapping gates unified by mapping — three batteries may
  survive as three, keyed and deduplicated. Anti-accretion holds;
  anti-plurality does not follow from it.
- **Banked input 4, proportionality caution**: the premise-record
  template is prose-discipline era (Finding 4's first stage). Carried
  forward wholesale it would regress the gradient; its value survives
  as the *structure of judged-reading rows inside the corpus artefact*,
  not as a standalone prose instrument.
- No dispute with banked inputs 2 and 3; both are load-bearing above.
