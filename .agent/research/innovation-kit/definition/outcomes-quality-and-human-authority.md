# Outcomes, quality and human authority

- **Status:** proposed definition
- **Owns:** the human, educational, public, service and quality obligations a Kit-enabled
  experience must make legible
- **Does not own:** curriculum or pedagogical truth, product-specific interaction design, legal
  decisions, current implementation evidence or provider selection

## Quality is a property of the whole promise

The Kit exists to make excellent experiences cheap to create, not merely valid software cheap to
assemble. Excellence is judged across the complete relationship between a product, the people it
serves or affects, the authorities it depends on and the service that sustains it.

Every proposition therefore begins with:

- intended beneficiaries, situated context and intended progress;
- affected and excluded people, including non-users;
- the real curriculum, public or technical capability made tangible;
- the educational, human, product or public claim being made;
- the authorities competent to define meaning, judge evidence and decide what happens next;
- difficult, denied, degraded, stale, interrupted and terminal states; and
- the limits beyond which the experience must refuse, qualify or hand off.

This is not a demand for maximal process. It is the minimum needed to avoid designing a technical
success around an undefined human promise.

## Governing quality dimensions

Applicability is contextual; silent omission is not. A profile records the competent basis for
any dimension judged inapplicable.

| Dimension | The product-creation system must make possible |
| --- | --- |
| **Human and public outcome** | Name who benefits, who may be burdened, what progress is sought, what competing outcomes exist and what evidence could establish or challenge the claim |
| **Educational validity** | Where relevant, preserve curriculum fidelity, pedagogical intent, construct validity, teacher professional judgment, pupil agency, cognitive and emotional load, harmful displacement and distribution of benefit or harm |
| **Product and service design** | Support the complete journey, understandable choices, continuity, backstage service, escalation, humane terminal states and appropriate human judgment |
| **Content and information design** | Preserve comprehensible language, hierarchy, status, provenance, attribution, version, rights, uncertainty and recovery meaning across channels |
| **Interaction and visual design** | Provide semantic interaction, perceptual clarity, responsive behaviour, coherent Oak expression and equivalent action and understanding across input and assistive modes |
| **Trust, safety and rights** | Make identity, authorisation, safeguarding, privacy, consent, security, abuse resistance, challenge, correction and accountable remedy explicit |
| **Software and operational quality** | Preserve correctness, state/effect truth, determinism where needed, validation, diagnostic clarity, reliability, performance within declared latency, device, network and resource budgets, capacity, recoverability, supportability and evolvability |
| **Framework-consumer quality** | Provide coherent concepts, types, errors, examples, diagnostics, compatibility, migration and governed product-specific substitution without hidden policy or repository archaeology |
| **Evidence quality** | Bind observations to the exact claim, population, configuration, method, comparator and limitations; preserve negative, ambiguous and delayed outcomes |
| **Stewardship and sustainability** | Account for ownership, maintenance, provider exit, cost and resource externalities, licensing, preservation, retirement and lawful disposal |

Conformance to a component, schema, lint rule, test suite, hosting platform or internal framework
can support one dimension. It never establishes the whole.

## Success is not one state

Technical systems often use “success” for several different events. A truthful experience names
the last stage it can assert and does not borrow confidence from later stages.

| Stage | What may truthfully be asserted |
| --- | --- |
| **Intent received** | A request or command was received with an identity |
| **Accepted** | The system took responsibility for attempting work under stated conditions |
| **Committed** | An authoritative state transition completed atomically or under an explicit consistency contract |
| **Reconciled** | Required projections and external effects reached a declared consistent state |
| **Delivered** | The intended representation or resource reached the stated delivery boundary with integrity evidence |
| **Intelligible capability** | A person could access, understand and use the capability under representative conditions |
| **Realised outcome** | The intended human, educational, service or public condition was observed using a valid method |
| **Impact** | The outcome caused a wider or longer-term change under an evidence design capable of supporting that inference |

A clicked download link is not a usable archive. A queued operation is not a committed change. A
rendered relationship view is not teacher benefit. A passed accessibility check is not equivalent
use. The Kit must make these distinctions easy to preserve in product language, telemetry,
evidence and support.

Failure is equally plural: invalid request, denied authority, absent capability, stale source,
partial projection, provider interruption, unsafe retry, unreconciled effect, unintelligible
experience, unsupported claim and failed human outcome need different responses.

## Complete journeys and difficult states

A scenario is incomplete if it covers only the golden path. For every consequential proposition,
consider the applicable members of this set:

- first use, return, resume and hand-off between people or channels;
- anonymous, authenticated, delegated, denied, expired and revoked access;
- missing, restricted, unpublished, stale, superseded and corrected source material;
- empty, partial, duplicated, delayed, reordered and conflicting state;
- dependency unavailability, rate/resource limits and degraded or offline operation;
- user cancellation, safe retry, reconciliation, rollback, repair and terminal failure;
- keyboard, touch, pointer, screen-reader, magnification, low-bandwidth and non-visual equivalents;
- language, locale, direction, content expansion and culturally situated interpretation;
- support, incident, safeguarding, rights or policy escalation to a competent human;
- challenge, correction, withdrawal, non-resurrection and effective remedy; and
- retirement, export, archival continuity and lawful deletion.

The [worked scenarios](../scenarios/README.md) preserve historical difficult cases and add unlike
counter-instances. They are coverage instruments, not requirements that every product activate
every state.

## Educational and professional authority

The Kit can help compose curriculum identity, teaching resources, learning activities,
assessment interactions, feedback and evidence. It cannot infer pedagogical authority from
technical reuse or engagement.

Where an educational claim is made, the proposition must distinguish at least:

- curriculum fidelity from presentation or retrieval quality;
- engagement and completion from learning;
- captured response from assessment evidence;
- correctness feedback from durable judgment about a learner;
- recommendation from authorised professional decision;
- a general evidence base from suitability in a situated classroom; and
- aggregate benefit from the rights and outcomes of an affected individual.

Competent curriculum, pedagogy, assessment, research and professional authorities decide the
applicable semantics and evidence. The Kit supplies composition questions, provenance,
representative scenarios and assurance plumbing. It does not replace those judgments.

## Authority, challenge and remedy

Human authority is not a manual fallback added after automation. It shapes what the product may
claim and do.

A consequential experience should make clear:

- who authored the source meaning and which version applies;
- who or what made the current decision;
- whether an automated result is advice, projection, draft, gate or effect;
- who may challenge, correct, override, withdraw or appeal;
- how a challenge reaches the competent duty-bearer;
- what temporary protection applies while the matter is unresolved;
- how an accepted correction propagates to projections and future use; and
- what evidence establishes that remedy occurred rather than merely that a ticket was created.

Observability and analytics are sensors in this loop. They create value only when a competent
interpretation, decision, response and re-observation follow. Product support, editorial work,
provider administration and incident response are part of the service even when they sit outside
the code repository.

## Control boundary

The product must state which parts of the experience it controls. An owned web application, an
embedded component, an MCP capability inside an external agent host, a generated document and a
third-party classroom integration can pursue a similar outcome while controlling different parts
of interaction, identity, telemetry, privacy, recovery and support.

The Kit must not promise quality it cannot observe or control. It should instead provide:

- explicit host and control-boundary profiles;
- minimum semantic and accessibility contracts for exported capability;
- truthful degradation when a host cannot support an obligation;
- correlation and provenance across the boundary;
- tests or specimens that an independent host can exercise; and
- a clear split between the Kit's evidence and the host or product owner's evidence.

## Always-on quality invariants

Every profile, including local exploration, retains:

- named meaning and authority;
- explicit scope, claim and source boundary;
- no staged success represented as real capability;
- honest absence, failure, uncertainty and information loss;
- an accessible path appropriate to the claimed interaction;
- secrets and personal/sensitive data handled consistently with the declared profile;
- evidence reachable from the claim it supports;
- provider bindings never acquiring semantic authority, with lock-in and exit consequences
  explicit; and
- a visible path to correction, withdrawal or retirement.

Other obligations activate with context. The [composition model](composition-profiles-and-elevation.md)
defines that activation without turning quality into a maturity ladder.

## Reopening conditions

This quality model should change when competent authorities identify missing or wrongly combined
dimensions; representative users or affected people show that its scenarios omit consequential
experience states; or applying it creates ceremony that does not change product choices, evidence,
failure behaviour or remedy. Simplification is welcome only when it preserves the whole promise.
