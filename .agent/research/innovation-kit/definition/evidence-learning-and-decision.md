# Evidence, learning and decision

- **Status:** proposed definition
- **Owns:** claim classes, epistemic labels, evidence records, interpretation limits,
  dispositions, return routing and evaluation of the fast-and-good thesis
- **Does not own:** product strategy, competent specialist judgment, current-estate evidence or
  implementation approval

## Demonstrations are evidence instruments

The unit of value is not a demonstration shipped. It is a real capability made accurately
legible, a decision improved before commitment, a consequential claim narrowed or stopped, or
recurring capability proven through unlike use. A demonstration may also be valuable because it
inspires informed possibility. It becomes evidence theatre when polish, staged behaviour or
attention is promoted beyond what the method can establish.

Evidence is consequential only when:

1. the claim is explicit;
2. observations are traceable to source, method, population and configuration;
3. limitations and rival explanations remain visible;
4. a competent authority interprets the evidence;
5. a legitimate decision owner records a disposition; and
6. learning returns to the layer that can act, correct or retire.

## Claim classes

| Claim class | Example of what may be established | What it does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Possibility** | A real capability can be made tangible under stated conditions | Utility, adoption, portability or human benefit |
| **Comprehension** | A named audience accurately understands the capability and its limits | Continued use or outcome improvement |
| **Generativity** | The experience provokes materially new questions, ideas or collaborations | That those ideas are good or will be acted on |
| **Technical capability** | A contract is implemented and exercised across representative normal/adverse states | Product value or organisational adoption |
| **Utility** | People can complete a named task or make a better decision under a valid method | Sustained product fit, educational impact or general reach |
| **Product/service** | A complete experience works for its declared audience and operational obligations | Causal impact or portability to unlike contexts |
| **Portability/commonality** | An unlike consumer or independent composition preserves the contract and obtains value | Universal applicability or ownership authority |
| **Adoption/collaboration** | A mutually agreed next action or sustained use occurs | Effectiveness, benefit or legitimate authority |
| **Stewardship/public capability** | Meaning, provenance, challenge, export, correction and future choice remain usable | Teacher/pupil benefit unless separately evidenced |
| **Human or educational outcome** | A valid method observes the intended situated outcome | Wider causal impact, equity or persistence beyond the method |
| **Impact** | A suitable design supports a causal claim over a defined population and time | Benefits to unobserved people or absence of harm |

The evidence contract is proportionate to the claim, never to visual polish or deployment tier.

## Epistemic vocabulary

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Observed** | Directly supported by a named source, runtime observation, measured result or recorded stakeholder statement within a stated boundary |
| **Inferred** | A reasoned explanation of observations, with alternatives and warrant explicit |
| **Unknown** | Not established within the inspected boundary; never silently converted into absence or support |
| **Hypothesis** | A falsifiable claim with predictions, invalidators and a decision it could affect |
| **Proposed** | A design or decision offered for review but not authorised as current architecture or policy |
| **Authorised** | Accepted by the legitimate authority within a named scope and effective interval |
| **Historical/superseded** | Retained for provenance but no longer eligible to govern current action |

Current implementation evidence uses a separate dated vocabulary—demonstrated, partial,
proposed, absent and unknown—in the [estate snapshot](../evidence/current-estate-2026-08-30.md).

## Evidence record and ledger

The evidence ledger is a versioned set of records, not a scorecard. Each record follows the
[core evidence interface](core-records-and-interfaces.md#7-evidence-record) and links to the exact
proposition, profile, scenario, release and run.

The ledger must retain:

- positive, negative and ambiguous observations;
- denominators, exposure and non-use;
- failed collection, exclusions, unknowns and information loss;
- source, method, tool/model and configuration identity;
- producer/session ancestry when needed to test evidential independence;
- alternative explanations and dissent;
- claim boundary and what cannot be inferred;
- interpretation and disposition authorities;
- decision, return target and next revalidation trigger; and
- supersession or correction without rewriting past observation.

Repeated agent output, several reports derived from one snapshot, high graph centrality and
multiple generated projections do not become independent corroboration by accumulation.

## Comparator and falsifier discipline

Every consequential claim should name:

- the current or plausible alternative, including no-build or service change;
- the dimensions held equal;
- the threshold that would change the decision;
- the observation that would materially weaken or falsify the claim;
- the cost and risk of false positive and false negative conclusions; and
- who has authority to interpret the result.

For “fast and good,” comparisons hold the proposition, quality floor and applicable obligations
equal. A bespoke alternative that omits accessibility, failure handling or lifecycle work is not
an honest baseline. Metrics include recurring versus novel effort, policy archaeology,
verification and rework, diagnostic success, lifecycle/maintenance cost, recovery and exit—not
only elapsed time.

## Decision and return routing

Evidence never authorises itself. A disposition may be:

- advance or confirm;
- narrow the audience, claim or profile;
- reshape the proposition or method;
- retain a mechanism locally;
- admit, split or reject a Kit capability;
- change an adapter or provider binding;
- correct, withdraw, deprecate or retire;
- defer; or
- preserve as unresolved.

Learning returns first to the legitimate layer:

| Finding | Primary return target |
| --- | --- |
| Human or educational outcome | Proposition, product, curriculum/pedagogy or research authority |
| Semantic defect or source correction | Domain/source authority and all derived projections |
| Interaction or accessibility failure | Product/design/accessibility authority and composed scenarios |
| Runtime incident or recovery weakness | Capability contract, product/service owner and adapter/binding stewardship |
| Consumer archaeology or invalid combinations | Developer/agent experience and composition model |
| Provider limitation or exit failure | Adapter/binding selection and provider-exit contract |
| Repeated proposition-local burden | Kit-placement inquiry; not automatic admission |
| Strategy or portfolio evidence | Legitimate strategic owner, bounded by the claim class |

Cross-layer learning remains possible through links, but a convenient ledger does not acquire the
right to define curriculum, product or strategy.

## Learning includes correction and forgetting

Learning is a validated change in later behaviour, capability or decision quality—not an increase
in stored documents. The system must be able to:

- promote supported findings without losing their evidence boundary;
- retain failed mechanisms and negative examples as non-active knowledge;
- supersede claims and decisions while preserving history;
- withdraw stale guidance from ordinary discovery and agent context;
- propagate correction to dependent profiles, releases, examples and projections;
- distinguish deletion, expiry, deprecation, withdrawal, quarantine, archive and lawful erasure;
- record ignorance and unresolved questions; and
- observe whether the correction actually changed later behaviour.

Relevance may propose context. Authority permits action.

## Evaluating the definition itself

The proposed system should be challenged across unlike scenarios and reader journeys. Useful
questions include:

- Can a proposition owner reach an honest evidence contract without choosing architecture first?
- Can a competent authority see where its meaning enters, changes and can be corrected?
- Can a cold consumer identify active obligations and legitimate omissions without archaeology?
- Do profiles predict real rights, state, operational and service duties better than labels such
  as demo or production?
- Can a product expose failure and last-truthful-success stage consistently across UI, telemetry
  and support?
- Can an unchanged semantic core survive a binding or exposure change?
- Do negative and ambiguous findings alter a decision, capability or future question?
- Can obsolete guidance lose operational influence without destroying history?

## Whole-model falsifiers

Narrow or reject the model if it:

- accelerates polished demonstrations but not trustworthy evidence or safe operation;
- lowers quality in early profiles and calls later repair elevation;
- cannot express legitimate absence, degraded modes or proposition-specific divergence;
- centralises semantic, policy, evidence or product authority in the Kit;
- hides state, projection, release, correction, restore or provider-exit failure;
- creates universal abstractions that erase useful provider or domain behaviour;
- increases consumer archaeology and maintenance against an equal-quality comparator;
- treats use, reuse, conformance or gate passage as human-outcome evidence; or
- accumulates evidence without disposition, correction, learning, exercised reuse or retirement.

## Unresolved research

The definition deliberately does not settle claim-specific thresholds, product/service objectives,
the first implementation proof, profile representation or provider choices. Competent
educational, accessibility, safeguarding, privacy, security, service and product authorities must
validate the relevant activation and evidence rules. Current source can establish repository
mechanisms; it cannot establish live use, institutional ownership or human outcomes.
