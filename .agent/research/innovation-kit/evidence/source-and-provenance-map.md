## Primary lineage

The proposed definition draws most strongly from:

- the [research charter](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/research-charter.md), which treats
  excellence as the sole governing priority, makes developer/framework-consumer quality a
  first-class outcome and places premise challenge before architecture;
- the [meta-analysis](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/synthesis/meta-analysis.md), which identifies
  warranted correspondence, local-versus-consequential correctness, compression as value and
  risk, the human/institutional system, closed evidence/correction, delivery through time and the
  seam rule;
- the [working product model](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/synthesis/working-model.md), which
  separates outcomes and obligations from current mechanisms and preserves profiles, difficult
  states and risk-shaped assurance;
- the [ecosystem enablement model](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/synthesis/ecosystem-enablement.md),
  which identifies the Kit's developer/product-team audience and premise-first framework
  principles;
- the [OWA/Components concept synthesis](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/current-state/owa-components-concept-lenses/synthesis.md),
  which preserves human, product, semantic, operational, institutional and custodial lenses;
- the [Database/API/OCE concept synthesis](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/current-state/database-tools/concept-lenses/synthesis.md),
  which sharpens authority, contracts, state/time, operations, rights, public value and provider
  boundaries;
- the historical [journey traces](../web-app-deconstruction/docs/current-state/journeys/), which
  retain awkward cross-boundary outcome and failure cases; and
- current OCE packages, apps, plans and decisions cited in the
  [estate snapshot](current-estate-2026-08-30.md).

The deconstruction's twelve hypotheses remain low-confidence research questions. They are useful
because they state predictions and invalidators; they do not select the target shape.

Their useful cross-cutting challenges include: product closure matters more than architectural
coverage; questions should drive schema and mechanisms; bounded completeness is different from
arbitrary truncation; declared, implemented, activated and effective are distinct; projections
remain subordinate to authority; loss and ignorance need explicit semantics; provider
independence needs exit evidence; distribution is not learning; and history must not remain active
by default.

Those ideas were retained only where owner direction, current OCE or the primary deconstruction
provided a warrant. Source-specific vocabulary, product recommendations and later external
mechanisms were not imported as OCE authority.

## Free-play associations and their disposition

The corpus supported several exploratory associations:

- **Retained:** the Kit behaves more like a generative product-creation grammar than a toolbox,
  because composition, authority, lifecycle and evidence determine valid expressions.
- **Retained:** production readiness is an evidence vector—specified, implemented, integrated,
  deployed, activated, exercised, observed, effective, supported and replaceable are separate.
- **Retained:** the Kit's reusable value is governed compression: hide solved recurring work while
  exposing any distinction that can change outcome, authority, truth, rights or stewardship.
- **Retained:** conventional infrastructure gaps diagnose incomplete loop closure; they are not the
  definition.
- **Discarded:** “Kubernetes for innovation.” It imports infrastructure orchestration as the
  primary ontology and sidelines educational outcomes, competent authority, rights and remedy.
- **Discarded:** “LEGO for products.” It conveys modularity but not time, evidence, correction,
  migration, operation or stewardship.

Associations are generative prompts, not source claims. The retained ideas were shaped through the
problem frame, warranted against primary material and given falsifiers in the definition.

## Limitations

- The OCE evidence is pinned source, not a live production observation.
- The deconstruction is historical, mostly static and sometimes inferred; removed runnable
  harnesses are preserved only through analysis, reported methods and provenance.
- Multiple reports derived from the same repositories are not independent evidence.
- The supplied analyses are not committed here and are not durable project authority.
- Current source cannot establish teacher or pupil outcomes, legal applicability, operational
  ownership, provider control-plane state or institutional intent not recorded in the repository.
- The proposed record, profile and contract models have not yet been applied by cold consumers or
  reviewed by every competent authority they identify.

These limits are reasons to keep evidence, definition, examples and decisions in separate homes.
