This is the local entry point for research that informs the Oak Innovation Kit. It separates
durable strategy, the proposed product-creation-system definition, dated current-estate evidence,
worked examples and historical source material so that none silently acquires another's authority.

## Authority and status

- [`docs/strategy/`](../../../docs/strategy/README.md) owns durable strategic purpose and choices.
- The strategic plan owns the node identity, proposed outcome, typed relationship and ratification
  state. It does not own the whole value stream or the product-creation model.
- The [definition corpus](definition/README.md) is a proposed, internally coherent specification.
  It uses normative language to make the proposal testable, but it is not ratified architecture.
- Dated evidence files own source-bounded current-state claims. They do not define the target.
- Scenarios illustrate and challenge the definition. They cannot create a rule.
- The deconstruction is pinned historical analysis and examples, not current production truth and
  not a runnable workspace.

## The organising correction

The Innovation Kit is not organised here as a catalogue of packages, providers or fashionable
capabilities. The central subject is the complete system by which people turn a consequential idea
into a truthful, excellent, operable experience; gather proportionate evidence; make an authorised
decision; and preserve, evolve or retire what was learned.

SQL, managed PostgreSQL, migrations, typed data access, vector operations, queues, object storage,
identity, observability and similar foundations matter whenever a proposition activates their
obligations. Their absence in the current estate is useful evidence that the earlier analysis had
not followed the whole product-creation loop. They are examples inside the definition, not its
centre.
