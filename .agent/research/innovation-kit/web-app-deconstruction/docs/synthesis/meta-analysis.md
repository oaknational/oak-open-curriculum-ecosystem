# Meta-analysis: a truer basis for understanding Oak systems

## Status and epistemic boundary

This began as a reduction of the
[53 OWA and Components Concept Explorer passes](../current-state/owa-components-concept-lenses/README.md),
their [cross-lens synthesis](../current-state/owa-components-concept-lenses/synthesis.md)
and the earlier [ecosystem enablement model](./ecosystem-enablement.md). It now
also reconciles the later
[59-lens Database, API and OCE portfolio](../current-state/database-tools/concept-lenses/README.md)
and its [multi-lens synthesis](../current-state/database-tools/concept-lenses/synthesis.md)
against the same candidate basis. It asks which distinctions remain important
after current repositories, frameworks, packages, routes, providers, database
projections and component taxonomies are removed from view.

The source records establish that OWA, Oak Components and the Database-Tools to
oak-openapi to OCE chain encode many identities, states, projections, controls,
claims and failure cases. They do not establish realised educational impact,
public value, consumer success, organisational intent, production control
effectiveness or institutional legitimacy. Unless a statement below is
explicitly source-observed, it is inferred from the portfolios and remains open
to the invalidators at the end. Reconciliation does not promote either
source-specific synthesis, candidate kernel or premise-collapse proposition into
an architecture decision.

## Executive conclusion

The current systems are most truthfully understood not as an application, a UI
library, a database, an API and a generated-consumer estate, nor as a catalogue
of capabilities, but as parts of a system that **makes, transforms, acts on and
evidences claims for people across authority, context and time**.

The common problem across the original 53 passes and the later 59 is maintaining
**warranted correspondence**:

- intended progress must correspond to a realised human outcome;
- a claim must correspond to competent authority and evidence;
- an identifier must correspond to the same referent across versions and
  projections;
- an acknowledgement must correspond to the transition actually committed;
- interaction language must correspond to what a person can do and reasonably
  believe;
- public availability must correspond to equitable capability without crossing
  an individual's rights floor; and
- continuity must correspond to authentic, intelligible custody rather than
  merely a surviving URL or file.

Most observed complexity clusters where this correspondence crosses a change of
meaning, authority, state, audience, representation, evidence regime or
lifetime. Many risks identified by the portfolio arise when those changes are
compressed into a noun, Boolean, callback, component, event or route which no
longer carries enough truth.

The better analytical coordinates are:

> **value, reference, authority, transition, projection, evidence and
> stewardship**.

The candidate core vocabulary is smaller than the lens set: **principal,
outcome, referent, claim, norm, operation and observation**, connected through
typed scoping relationships. State, projection, evidence, capability, contract,
remedy and stewardship may be defined from these concepts and relationships.
The Database/API/OCE kernel currently refines these concepts rather than proving
an eighth primitive, while exposing unresolved naming and composition tests
around relations, releases, commands, technical results and delivery grants.
The portfolios support testing this reduction; they do not yet prove minimality
or independence.

## Common threads

### Correspondence, not layering, is the central architectural problem

The same shape recurs in curriculum identity, search, save/share feedback,
assessment, publication, caching, accessibility, rights, kit contracts and
preservation:

```text
human purpose
  -> claim or intended action
  -> competent authority
  -> state transition or transformation
  -> projection for an observer
  -> belief, capability or consequence
  -> evidence, correction and continued stewardship
```

The important boundaries are the arrows. Directory and package boundaries are
useful only when they align with them.

### Local correctness does not compose automatically

The portfolio repeatedly finds correct types, components, files, validators,
events and requests which cannot by themselves establish a semantically,
temporally, pedagogically, legally or accessibly correct outcome. Correctness is
often a relation between independently valid parts and the history by which they
were combined. The unit of assurance is therefore the consequential claim or
capability travelling end to end, not normally an isolated artefact.

### Compression is both the main value mechanism and the main risk

Components, schemas, routes, labels, design tokens, events and APIs make rich
knowledge usable by hiding detail. This is valuable when the omitted detail
cannot change the outcome. It becomes false certainty when it collapses:

- accepted into committed;
- unavailable into nonexistent;
- concept into placement or URL;
- source version into current projection;
- conformance into equivalent human capability;
- aggregate benefit into an individual remedy; or
- present access into preservation.

The durable asset is the discrimination the system has learned, not the wrapper
which currently compresses it.

### Every technical system here is also human and institutional

Teacher memory, pupil agency, editorial judgement, support work, provider
administration, incident response, consumer diagnosis, affected-party challenge
and long-term custody participate in the outcome. Treating them as external only
makes responsibility invisible.

A public kit is also an institution. Defaults, extension points,
documentation, compatibility and release policy distribute semantic authority,
consumer agency, risk and future maintenance work.

### Excellence is a closed evidence-and-correction loop

A feature, check, event or dashboard is not evidence of excellence by itself.
The recurring complete shape is:

```text
precise claim -> valid observation -> interpretation -> competent decision
              -> correction or remedy -> re-observation
```

Pedagogical validity, accessibility, reliability, security, analytics,
observability, governance and human rights differ in what they protect, but all
require this closure. Some values are floors rather than optimisation targets;
the loop must not turn dignity, rights or legitimate finality into proxy metrics.

### Delivery and evolution are one design problem at different times

Versioning, migration, retirement, provider exit, reversibility, support,
preservation and disposal shape present meaning and responsibility. Evolution is
not future administration around an otherwise complete runtime design.

## The negative space

The repositories are richest in the middle of the causal chain and weakest at
both ends: **why and by whose authority before a mechanism, and whether it
produced the intended outcome after it**.

| Source-visible foreground                    | Material negative space                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| pages, journeys and product claims           | an evidenced hierarchy from human intent to educational and public outcome                      |
| identifiers, schemas and projections         | authoritative identity, semantic invariants and correspondence across representations           |
| callbacks, requests and success states       | proof of commit, convergence, user belief and terminal resolution under adverse interleavings   |
| accessible controls and safety affordances   | equivalent lived capability, comprehension, effectiveness and affected-person participation     |
| events, tests, logs and configured checks    | interpretation, decision authority, applied control, correction and re-observation              |
| public source and package exports            | actual independent-consumer tasks, success, extension needs, support burden and influence       |
| content publication, redirects and downloads | historical custody, fixity, significant properties, lawful retention and future intelligibility |
| code ownership and release machinery         | legitimate authority, incentives, maintenance capacity, contestability and remedy               |
| web and English-language projections         | locale, cultural, document, media, offline and machine-channel equivalence                      |
| current providers and adapters               | tested semantic portability, exit, restoration and retained options                             |

Modules alone cannot close these spaces. They require human, educational,
service, operational, legal, governance, consumer and historical evidence.

## What is genuinely distinctive

Most implementation problems resonate with established software and service
design knowledge. The distinctive Oak problem is the intersection of several
concerns, not a uniquely exotic technical architecture.

These concerns do not safely collapse into generic web or UI engineering:

1. **Educational validity.** A correct interaction or score does not establish a
   valid learning or assessment inference.
2. **Curriculum epistemic authority.** Curriculum is versioned, placed and
   projected knowledge whose claims must be governable and contestable, not just
   content attached to routes.
3. **Asymmetric human contexts.** Teacher continuity and professional agency
   differ from pupil agency, cognitive load, safeguarding and evolving
   capacities.
4. **Public infrastructure obligations.** Open reach, equitable capability,
   sustainable reuse and commons stewardship coexist with individual rights,
   restrictions and effective remedy.
5. **Semantic consumer enablement.** A kit must let independent consumers
   realise excellent outcomes without reconstructing hidden Oak policy or
   surrendering legitimate product authority.
6. **Educational continuity.** Meaning, rights, provenance and accessibility may
   need to survive channel change, provider change and long time horizons.

Foundations, interaction grammar and outcome recipes remain analytically
distinct contracts even if an eventual implementation colocates or releases
them together.

## Resonance with known patterns

The portfolio provides reasons to use existing bodies of knowledge, not reasons
to invent Oak-specific substitutes for them.

Oak-specific domain semantics deserve first-class models. Standard technical
problems deserve excellent idiomatic solutions after their premises are
established.

## Where the present systems provide real value

Static source cannot prove realised impact, but it reveals accumulated knowledge
capable of producing value. That knowledge has five durable forms:

1. **Domain discrimination:** distinctions among curriculum concept, placement,
   version, audience, restriction, activity, attempt, result and projection.
2. **Interaction knowledge:** semantic controls, accessible behaviours,
   warnings, feedback, recovery, responsive adaptation and coherent language.
3. **Service knowledge:** publication, retirement, hand-offs, provider
   differences, partial failure, retry, support and cross-channel delivery.
4. **Institutional knowledge:** public contracts, examples, compatibility,
   deprecation, release discipline and consumer-facing explanations.
5. **Negative knowledge:** migrations, duplication, defensive branches and
   workarounds which may record hard-won failure knowledge even when their
   mechanism should disappear.

The preservation rule is:

> Extract and revalidate the decision, invariant, difficult case and evidence;
> do not grant automatic survival to the mechanism which currently contains it.

The recurring value mechanisms are semantic compression, constraint
preservation, truthful mediation, continuity, capability multiplication,
recovery and institutionalisation. Each is valuable only when evidence connects
it to an outcome or obligation.

## A two-level candidate basis set

### Seven coordinates for analysis

| Coordinate                     | Governing question                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Subject and value**          | Who should gain what progress, capability, right or public benefit; who can be harmed or excluded? |
| **Referent and meaning**       | What exact thing or claim is involved, with what identity, version and invariants?                 |
| **Authority and duty**         | Who may assert, decide, act or change it, and who answers for failure?                             |
| **Action, state and time**     | What meaningful operation or transition occurred, with what history and lifetime?                  |
| **Projection and composition** | How is meaning or capability transformed for this observer, and what must survive?                 |
| **Evidence and correction**    | What warrants the claim, can falsify it and closes the loop?                                       |
| **Stewardship and option**     | Who carries the promise through change and what must remain changeable or become final?            |

These coordinates identify real seams. They are not a proposal for seven
services, packages or framework modules.

### Seven candidate primitives

| Primitive       | Meaning                                                                                                                             |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Principal**   | A person, community, organisation or delegated technical actor that benefits, acts, decides, bears duties or stewards something.    |
| **Outcome**     | An intended or realised consequence or condition for a principal or public, including benefit, harm and externality.                |
| **Referent**    | An identity-bearing thing such as a curriculum concept, authored version, placement, record, artefact, service or release.          |
| **Claim**       | A contestable assertion about any concept or typed relationship, scoped by authority, effective time and confidence.                |
| **Norm**        | A requirement, permission, prohibition, invariant, right, duty, guarantee or public commitment.                                     |
| **Operation**   | An identity-bearing attempt to observe, create, change, publish, decide, migrate, reverse or remedy.                                |
| **Observation** | A provenanced result of research, measurement, execution, test, incident or audit; it becomes evidence only in relation to a claim. |

These concepts are provisionally separate because merging them can destroy a
material distinction. Outcome prevents delivery from masquerading as value;
referent preserves identity and custody; claim preserves uncertainty and
contestability; norm preserves `must`, `may` and `must not`; operation preserves
history and concurrency; observation preserves the difference between a signal
and evidence; principal preserves agency, authority and duty.

`Context` is deliberately not a single primitive or service. It is a typed set
of relationships which scopes the other concepts:

- audience and consumer profile relate principals to outcomes, claims and norms;
- placement relates referents to other referents;
- locale and jurisdiction scope claims and norms;
- channel relates a derived projection to its principals and referents;
- environment supplies preconditions for operations and observations; and
- effective time scopes claims, norms, authority and relationships.

This keeps each contextual distinction visible instead of collecting unrelated
dimensions inside one universal context object.

### Derived concepts

- **Authority** is a relationship granting a principal competence to make a
  claim, establish a norm or perform an operation.
- **State** is a time-scoped claim about a referent; its authority class and
  warrant from operation history, observations or both must be explicit. It is
  not merely the current value of a record.
- **Projection** is a referent derived for scoped principals, channels and
  conditions with declared preserved, adapted and lost properties.
- **Evidence** is an observation interpreted against a claim with a valid
  warrant.
- **Capability** means a principal can achieve an outcome under the applicable
  norms and typed scoping relationships.
- **Contract** is a public set of norms, claims and authority hand-offs among
  principals.
- **Remedy** is a norm-grounded capability or outcome realised through one or
  more owed operations and supported by evidence of effectiveness.
- **Stewardship** is the authority, norms and operations by which principals
  maintain, change, retire or preserve referents and contracts through time.
- **Preservation** is governed identity, custody, fixity, representation and
  evidence across time; it is not synonymous with retention.

### Database/API/OCE kernel reconciliation

**Observed:** the later authority-chain portfolio proposes a candidate kernel of
15 concepts plus four cross-cutting relation families after tracing authored
database state through projections, a public API, contract generation and OCE
capabilities. The source-specific proposal and its discrimination contracts
remain in the
[Database, API and OCE synthesis](../current-state/database-tools/concept-lenses/synthesis.md#candidate-conceptual-kernel).

seven primitives and typed relationships, but several require stricter derived
contracts than the original OWA/Components corpus made visible. The mapping
below is a test of the global basis, not a decision to implement these concepts
or a claim that all should appear in a public kit API.

The four cross-cutting relation families also map without being erased:

- **authority assignment and delegation** specialise the derived `Authority`
  relationship among principals, claims, norms and operations;
- **provenance and derivation** are typed relationships among referents,
  transformation operations and observations, and are part of the derived
  `Projection` contract;
- **evidence** remains an `Observation` interpreted against a `Claim` with a
  valid warrant; and
- **lineage and supersession** are time-scoped relationships and claims among
  referents, not a mutable “current” flag.

The chain's four uses of “capability” therefore remain distinct compositions:
a semantic capability contract is the derived `Contract`; delegated security
capability is an authority relationship and permission `Norm`; a policy decision
is an authorised decision referent and claims; and a delivery grant is an
enforcement-ready scoped permission. Sharing a word does not make them one
primitive or one service.

**Inferred refinements which survive this reconciliation:** identity-bearing
relations must be possible; immutable release payload and mutable release status
must remain separate; transformation definition, execution and output must not
collapse; human outcome must remain distinct from technical transition result;
and resource description, permission and delivered bytes must remain separately
assertable.

**Basis invalidators exposed by the later corpus:** add or split a primitive if
a representative, predeclared case shows that:

- an independently changing relation or placement cannot be expressed as a
  typed relationship or referent without losing its invariants;
- a release's scope, authority, completeness, digest and correction semantics
  cannot be composed into one enforceable derived contract;
- command intent, execution operation, technical transition result and human
  outcome cannot remain separately identifiable in the seven-concept grammar;
- transformation loss and compatibility cannot be stated without an additional
  independently authoritative concept; or
- resource identity, descriptor, permission, credential and delivery cannot be
  separated without making rights or failure behaviour implicit.

No inspected source or experiment has yet met one of these invalidators. That is
an evidence status, not a claim that the seven primitives are finally minimal.

### The system grammar

The basis forms one testable sentence:

> **A principal seeks or is owed an outcome concerning a referent. Typed
> relationships scope the parties, placement, locale, jurisdiction, channel,
> environment and time. Claims and norms establish what is asserted, allowed and
> required. Authorised operations may change the referent. Observations warrant
> or refute claims and enable correction.**

A system description which cannot complete that sentence contains an explicit
unknown or a hidden premise. It does not earn a compensating abstraction merely
to make the diagram look complete.

### Success and failure

Success is not Boolean. A capability contract must name the last stage its
authority can truthfully assert:

```text
valid intent -> accepted operation -> authoritative commit -> reconciled projection
             -> intelligible capability -> realised outcome -> impact
```

Failure must preserve the stage, knowledge state, affected outcome or norm,
scope, responsible authority, recoverability, reversibility and remedy.
`Absent`, `unknown`, `pending`, `unsupported`, `invalid`, `forbidden`,
`conflicted`, `stale`, `degraded`, `rejected` and `failed` are materially
different. Partial success is first-class.

### The seam rule

A boundary is architecturally material when one or more analytical coordinates
or primitive relationships change. Colocation is plausible only while value,
referent, authority, state clock, projection invariants, evidence regime and
stewardship remain aligned.

This is a better seam test than repository, package, route, component category,
provider or team chart. It also prevents indiscriminate consolidation: different
authorities, lifetimes or rights must not be merged merely to reduce the number
of modules.

## What should survive

### First-class concepts

- human and educational outcomes, including non-negotiable guarantees and harms;
- curriculum and learning referents with explicit identity, placement, version
  and semantic invariants;
- claims, norms, competent authority, provenance, delegation, rights, duties,
  challenge and remedy;
- explicit operations, histories, negative states and terminal outcomes;
- typed scoping relationships and legitimate variation;
- transformations, channels, composition rules and declared information loss;
- observations, warrants, assurance, feedback and correction; and
- governance across compatibility, maintenance, migration, exit, retirement,
  preservation and disposal.

### Knowledge that should survive without dictating architecture

- accessibility and interaction semantics;
- pedagogical and assessment intent;
- restriction, safeguarding, privacy, legal and attribution decisions;
- difficult failure, retry, concurrency, degraded and recovery cases;
- consumer guidance, examples, diagnostics and compatibility knowledge;
- historical reasons for migrations and compensating mechanisms; and
- brand and design decisions whose semantic purpose can be stated.

### Concepts that do not earn survival as foundations

- current repositories, packages, routes, providers, stores, caches, hooks,
  contexts, wrappers, callbacks and deployment topology;
- current component, token, prop, event and file-format taxonomies;
- `frontend`, `backend`, `shared`, `component`, `published`, `success`,
  `accessible`, `reusable` and `single source of truth` without the distinctions
  they compress;
- code reuse, DRYness, export count, feature count, event volume or one common
  schema as goals;
- migration bridges, duplicated projections and provider-shaped policy after
  their retained knowledge and continuing necessity have been tested; and
- framework conformance, static types, telemetry or CI gates as substitutes for
  outcome evidence.

This does not establish that every listed mechanism is wrong. It establishes
that none is an ontological primitive or has an automatic claim on OCE.

Neither the original 53-lens matrix nor the later chain-kernel reconciliation
currently demonstrates the need for another primitive, but that is a portfolio
result rather than a minimality proof. New human, operational, legal, cultural
or consumer evidence may expose a missing distinction. Any new concept should
first be tested as a role, typed relationship, derived construct, policy,
evidence type or implementation before expanding the candidate basis.

## Survival test for a proposed OCE concept

A concept earns first-class status only if removing it would collapse a
distinction that can change at least one of:

1. human or educational outcome;
2. harm, right, duty or effective remedy;
3. referent identity or semantic truth;
4. competent decision or action authority;
5. valid transition, history or terminal state;
6. contextual applicability or legitimate variation;
7. projection equivalence, accessibility or compositional validity;
8. falsifiability, diagnosis or correction; or
9. long-term stewardship, finality or option value.

It must also name an invalidator and explain why changing the surrounding system
cannot remove the need. Otherwise it is probably a derived view, policy choice,
implementation technique, teaching aid or historical residue.

## Invalidators and limits

This meta-analysis should change if evidence shows that:

- defects, co-change and user misunderstanding do not cluster where the basis
  coordinates change;
- representative consumers realise and verify excellent outcomes from lower
  level materials without reconstructing hidden policy;
- another independent primitive is required to express a material obligation or
  failure;
- one proposed primitive can always be derived without loss from the others;
- existing organisational, service and production evidence already closes the
  apparent correspondence gaps outside the repositories; or
- the basis produces ceremony for inconsequential decisions rather than better
  discrimination. Proportionality remains necessary.

Not every projection must be lossless, every state reversible or every value
measurable. Loss can be intentional, lawful erasure and safeguarding action can
require finality, and rights can constrain optimisation. Excellence requires
that the consequence, authority and evidence be explicit.

## Result

The 53 OWA/Components lenses and 59 Database/API/OCE lenses should not become
112 framework modules or quality gates. They are different tests of whether a
much smaller set of relationships remains true.

The truer unit of Oak architecture is a **warranted capability or claim moving
through people, authority, state, representation and time**. The truer reusable
asset is the invariant, decision, difficult case and evidence which keeps that
movement honest. The truer goal for OCE is to make those relationships explicit,
composable, diagnosable and governable while using the least machinery the
evidence permits.

That is a basis for architecture. It is not yet an architecture.
