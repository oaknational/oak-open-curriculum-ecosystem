# H006: Premise-derived framework boundaries

## Claim

**Governing decision:** Premise-first investigation is required before selecting Innovation Kit capabilities or boundaries. That policy is not under test in this hypothesis.

**Hypothesis:** Applying that method will produce a more conceptually coherent framework when each proposed capability, boundary and abstraction is derived from an evidenced outcome or obligation after its premise and surrounding system have been challenged.

The kit is deliberately a framework for additional consumers. The hypothesis concerns what belongs inside it and how those responsibilities fit together, not whether a second consumer has appeared.

## Why it is plausible

**Observed:** OWA and Oak Components make valuable outcomes possible through mechanisms accumulated across product generations, framework changes, providers and migrations.

**Observed:** several current areas contain overlapping routes, stores, query layers, provider compositions, UI systems and failure compensation.

**Observed:** OCE already contains curriculum, search, graph, Result, design and operational capabilities, but their current separation does not itself prove the enduring framework boundaries.

**Inferred:** some current complexity may disappear if the kit changes the authority, data model, workflow or service design that created it, while other responsibilities may require one authoritative shared implementation from the outset.

## Predictions

If the hypothesis is useful:

1. Every kit capability can be traced from current mechanism through educational, product, framework-consumer or operational purpose, obligation and domain invariant to an explicit architectural decision.
2. Some OWA and Components mechanisms are eliminated because their underlying premise or compensating problem no longer exists.
3. Several current systems can collapse where they duplicate authority or compensate for the same system choice.
4. Responsibilities remain separate only where semantics, authority, isolation, lifecycle or independently verifiable invariants require it.
5. Shared and product-local ownership are chosen from the nature of the responsibility rather than a reuse-count rule.
6. The resulting framework can be explained from first principles without referring to OWA folders, packages or framework history.

## Invalidators and weakening evidence

Reject or materially narrow this hypothesis if:

- premise analysis removes or combines behaviour that proves essential to curriculum integrity, user outcomes, accessibility, safeguarding, rights, security or operations;
- apparently duplicated systems own genuinely distinct semantics, authorities, failure isolation or lifecycle;
- a proposed collapse merely moves synchronization, state or failure complexity behind a less visible boundary;
- a platform-native or idiomatic alternative cannot express the required behaviour without fragile exceptions;
- historical compatibility or external protocol obligations necessarily preserve a current distinction;
- the method repeatedly produces abstractions that are easier to explain but less correct, humane, diagnosable or evolvable.

## Most direct discriminating work

1. Select one substantial OWA or Components area and write a complete premise record.
2. Trace each mechanism to its outcome, obligation, invariant, chosen system decision and historical cause.
3. Produce preserve, remove, combine, invert, generate, platform-native and redesigned-system alternatives.
4. Test each alternative against normal, restricted, stale, failed, resumed and recovery behaviour.
5. Record which concepts and boundaries remain necessary and why.

Repeat the method across different kinds of problem, including curriculum discovery, pupil state, accessible interaction and operational composition, before treating it as generally useful.

## Decision affected

The internal capability model, boundaries and extension architecture of the Oak Innovation Kit.
