## Priority rule

Run a validation first when it combines plausible user harm, direct reproducibility and uncertainty that affects an architecture hypothesis. On that basis:

1. V001, V003 and V010 test acknowledgement, compensation or blocking behavior with small controlled adapters.
2. V009 is reproduced at the handler boundary; deployed CDN/browser impact is its remaining narrowing test.
3. V004, V005 and V006 test pupil/Classroom integrity and authorization.
4. V011 tests the delivered curriculum artifact rather than only its generator internals.
5. V002 belongs at the focused unit layer and does not justify a journey by itself.
6. V007 and V008 need product outcome definitions before implementation changes.
7. V012-V019 follow the dependency and information order of their registered corpora, not an assumed time or cost limit: establish competent authority and identities, test the concrete release/policy/command/contract/delivery propositions, then judge capability durability and kernel minimality across the combined evidence.
