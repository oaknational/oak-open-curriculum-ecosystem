## 1. Purpose — the denominator every finding is priced in

The directives install the owner's model of excellence in a fresh agent before its first act,
so the agent is a reasoning peer and the later gates (hooks, rules, validators) are understood
rather than routed around. The estate's own doctrine says passive guidance loses to artefact
gravity and that mechanical refusals get worked around; what it wants is honest judgement
informed by shared knowledge (agent-collaboration §Knowledge and Communication). So the tier's
value is shared understanding at acceptable reading cost. It is not compliance by volume, and
it is not the enforcement layer. Provenance (dated owner verbatim) is load-bearing because
precedence is not approval. Size is an owner-accepted dated state (2026-06-01 "do not chase the
fitness number"; 2026-09-09 principles.md frontmatter "no landing trims substance to fit").
The sanctioned relief is graduation downward in a curation lane, never trimming.

Five frames were held apart during the review and none was allowed to win: A constitution
(case law leaking upward is the defect), B system prompt (position is salience, repetition is
emphasis, size is budget), C externalised owner memory (the files are the owner's own trail),
D immune system (each instance is antigen memory; the cost is memory at the wrong layer),
E portable Practice product (host-local and portable stance are unmarked in one directory).
Falsifier for the whole framing: if the owner's intent is enforcement-by-reading, the
reading-cost findings weaken. The owner's own words point the other way ("structure over
vigilance"; "not mechanical refusals").

## 3. Six collective findings

1. **The tier's self-map is wrong four ways and there is no index.** AGENT.md reaches 12 of
   15; start-right step 1 names 7; orientation l.24 names 6; the context-budget rule names 10.
   No enumeration is complete and no file classifies core (read every session) against
   situational (read when the domain fires), though the rules tier has exactly that split.
   continuity-practice and operationalisation-contract have no inbound link on the agent path.
2. **Case law is welding itself to the rules.** Provenance sits inside the sentence carrying the
   rule, so the reader parses history to reach an instruction. Measured densities: roughly
   12–15% of principles, 18–22% of testing-strategy, 35–40% of user-collaboration
   (front-loaded); 139 sentences over forty words across the tier. The generative WHY is about a
   third of principles. Within-file and same-moment duplication is the defect; cross-file
   repetition at different firing moments is emphasis and is kept.
3. **Growth is structural, not neglect.** Admission is by read-trigger alone (orientation l.86:
   "if it governs behaviour on every session, directive"). The graduation pipeline names targets
   that exclude directives (continuity-practice l.239–240), yet 54 graduation commits landed in
   principles.md. Exit is gated behind a <30% context budget that the mandated grounding read
   itself consumes (the read is about 237,000 characters before the rule tier). Trimming is
   rightly forbidden. Every tested split_strategy has not run.
4. **Cheap self-standard failures.** Level-1 exclusions breached in principles on every count;
   eight permanent-to-ephemeral citations across six files, two to dead paths; one dead template
   path; one dangling pattern name; one stale freshness header; fitness frontmatter absent on four
   files under three schemas; the named binding mechanism runs only in `pnpm make`, never in
   `pnpm check`. All are verifiable by a grep or a resolver.
5. **Several tiers wear one directory name.** An index, four doctrines, two meta-doctrines,
   three reference manuals, two runbooks, one reflective prompt, one style guide that excludes
   the directory it lives in. Thirteen of fifteen files have no level under the contract that
   governs them. "Directive" is functioning as the compromise label principles l.296–305 warns
   about.
6. **The bridge from text to behaviour is weak by the estate's own account.** The
   passive-guidance pattern says firing cadence beats content; start-right calls the tier
   "background grounding, not an installed tripwire layer". The tier buys recognition speed and
   is priced as prevention. No instrument measures adherence to any clause. First-hand instance:
   the owner had to interrupt this reviewer to make it step back to purpose, minutes after the
   reviewer read metacognition.md. Downgraded, not adopted: the architecture lens's "inverted
   hierarchy" reading, which describes a deliberate layering choice.
