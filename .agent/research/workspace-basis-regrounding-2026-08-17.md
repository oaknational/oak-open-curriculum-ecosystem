## What we can honestly say about "good" (and no more)

From the independent record only: boundaries should follow measured
co-change and named enforcement needs, not asserted lifecycles; the
live repo already enforces more real structure than the target
acknowledged (strata, membership, licence quarantine, the ESLint design
fence); the tree's levels are scarce and expensive while manifest
fields and validators are cheap and revisable; an agent workforce is
best served by fences it cannot fail to see (glob-level, not
convention-level). What good looks like as a whole is NOT yet known —
that is the open question, and it needs instruments that search, not
panels that grade the incumbent.

survey (walkers forbidden the census/basis corpus by construction,
