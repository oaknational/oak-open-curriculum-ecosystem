## Executive overview

An arrangement can remember without learning, learn without improving, improve without any participant learning, and remain operational without retaining the authority to act. These distinctions are central to a defensible comparison of biological systems, agent architectures and institutions. A persistent trace establishes a possible carrier of history. A response difference establishes a behavioural effect under specified conditions. An acquired and retained change in capability requires a stronger comparison, and a claim of improved external outcomes requires evidence beyond the mechanism itself.

The appropriate object of comparison is therefore a **conditional capacity profile**: what an explicitly bounded arrangement can do, on which tasks, after which history, at what cost, over which timescale, and under which disturbances. A profile is not an intelligence score. Its dimensions need not be independent, monotonic goods or commensurable. Retention can preserve useful expertise or obsolete constraints; rapid revision can reflect evidence sensitivity or suggestibility; stable conduct can reflect warranted persistence or an inability to adapt. The experiment must distinguish these possibilities.

The literature supports several materially different ways for history to affect subsequent behaviour. Biological state can reside in biochemical dynamics, morphology and environmental traces. Computational state can reside in parameters, context, databases, event histories and executable constraints. Institutional state can reside in participants, routines, specialist routing, permissions, artefacts and infrastructure. Similar functional descriptions can identify useful engineering hypotheses, but neither common terminology nor a shared mathematical form establishes equivalent cognition, common evolutionary function or legitimate authority.

The Practice is an instructive case because it makes many normally implicit distinctions explicit: current state versus durable memory, canonical material versus projections, mechanism firing versus detection and response, and institutional change versus evidence that the change helped. Its records also expose failures and revised explanations. Yet the available evidence does not establish a general causal advantage over a competent single agent, ordinary documentation or conventional workflow infrastructure. Written methods, static code and retrospective episodes support different claims; none independently establishes broad institutional learning.

The research programme should test both acquisition and restraint. It must preserve useful generalisation while repairing invalid scope; distinguish warranted revision from recency obedience; follow correction through summaries, active contexts, generators and successors; compare structural changes against changes to participants; and evaluate learning-policy revisions on subsequent object-level tasks. Delayed measurement, full participant replacement, protected holdouts, source-dependence controls, failed activation and total human effort are part of the core design, not optional refinements.

Twelve complementary protocol families follow from this account. A balanced first portfolio samples boundary repair, environmental memory, coordination, succession and learning-policy revision, with mechanism-traction checks supporting interpretation. These are draft research designs. No live experiment, participant exposure, repository change or deployment is reported. The resulting framework remains provisional and may be simplified or replaced if a smaller task-specific account explains the relevant contrasts equally well.

## 1. Scope, evidence and inferential discipline

### 1.1 Research question

The central question is: **what capacities does an arrangement exhibit, through which mechanisms, under which conditions, and how do those capacities change under new evidence, disturbance, structural change and participant succession?** The unit may be a model, a persistent agent, a working team, an enduring institution or a coupled human–technical–environmental arrangement. Every substantive claim must specify which unit is being studied. A gain observed in the largest arrangement cannot simply be assigned to one component, while a component failure need not imply failure of the arrangement that contains and repairs it.

The review keeps eight avenues in parallel: concepts and measurement; biological and embodied mechanisms; agent coordination; institutional memory; generalisation and evidence responsiveness; forgetting and control of prior influence; exploration, structural adaptation and resilience; and learning about learning, authority and external value. It does not make correction or forgetting the organising purpose of all intelligence. Nor does it require a current implementation need before a research possibility can be considered. Discovery can have value through new explanatory options, methods or achievable capabilities, even when adoption remains unwarranted.

### 1.2 Review design and limits

This is a structured critical literature review and architectural synthesis, not a systematic review, meta-analysis or exhaustive landscape census. Sources were selected to discriminate mechanisms and rival explanations, with primary papers, official specifications, source code, tests and original institutional records preferred. Classic literature was paired with current systems and benchmarks where these alter the comparison. Search families covered basal cognition and non-neural memory; organisational and transactive memory; adaptive control and distributed cognition; agent coordination and durable execution; belief revision and concept drift; continual learning and unlearning; and experimental evaluation of learning policies.

The source cut-off is the material actually accessible on 9 September 2026. A retrieval date is not a claim that a mutable page describes every released version. Paper abstracts, full-text passages, formal results, simulations, laboratory experiments, field studies, retrospective reports and proposals have different evidential status. The accompanying source index records access extent and unresolved leads. In particular, an accessible abstract supports the authors' reported claim; it does not justify asserting that every method, appendix or statistical analysis was independently inspected.

Engraph's repository baseline is `EngraphCode/open-curriculum-ecosystem`, default branch `engraph`, commit `123d2e3e9878b520c7fe1c51a39a8c1f324c1091`. Repository assertions in this review refer to that snapshot unless another date is explicitly identified. This is a read-only documentary and static-code assessment. Tests were inspected where relevant but not executed. Later branch movement is outside the snapshot rather than silently incorporated into the evidence.

The prior project corpus is not a set of independent studies. Reports share source records, concepts and later interpretations. Four local copies—an organisational framework, its exported transcript, the six-mechanism design report and the Practice ensemble report—matched the brief's SHA-256 prefixes. Several reports listed as missing were recovered in the repository. Other named Library originals remained unavailable. An originating conversation described by the brief is not the current visible conversation, and an exported transcript or transfer summary is not proof that its original chat was reopened.

### 1.3 Five types of warrant

**Descriptive warrant** establishes that a record, implementation or event exists. **Mechanistic warrant** supports how a process produces an effect. **Causal-effect warrant** concerns a contrast between interventions under identification assumptions. **Transport warrant** supports extending a result to another population, task, host or timescale. **Normative warrant** concerns legitimate objectives, rights and authority. These warrants can coexist but cannot substitute for one another.

For example, a rule may exist, a hook may execute it, and fewer forbidden actions may follow. This does not yet distinguish participant learning from environmental enforcement, nor establish the rule's broader legitimacy. Conversely, a valuable enforcement mechanism need not induce internal learning to deserve use. The explanatory claim should name the system boundary at which the improvement occurs.

Source dependence matters at every level. A report repeating an incident is not a second incident. Several agents reading the same mistaken source are not independent witnesses. Repeated tasks sharing a template are not independent samples of the entire task domain. A benchmark reused during method design ceases to be a protected test even if its filename still says evaluation. These are consequences of the evidence structure rather than objections to collaboration itself.

## 2. Concepts and alternative decompositions

### 2.1 Working definitions

**Performance** is an observed result on an instance or distribution of tasks. **Competence** is a disposition to perform successfully within a stated domain, with uncertainty about its measurement. **Learning** is an experience-dependent change in capability or behaviour-generating organisation; claiming improvement additionally requires a justified outcome criterion and comparator. **Memory** is a mechanism or state through which previous conditions affect later response. Mere physical persistence is a candidate memory carrier, while useful memory requires a functional consequence in the relevant task.

**Adaptive control** concerns maintaining or changing behaviour relative to disturbances and objectives; it may operate without acquiring a new representation or policy. **Agency** refers here to selecting and pursuing actions within a declared action space, with stronger claims requiring evidence of how goals, monitoring and control are organised. **Autonomy** concerns discretion from external intervention across specified decisions. It is neither independence of infrastructure nor a right to set goals. **Cognition** and **intelligence** remain contested umbrella terms; the review uses operational capacity claims whenever the umbrella would conceal disagreement. No inference to consciousness follows from optimisation, memory, goal pursuit or coordination alone.

An **agent role** is an assigned function or instruction set. It is not necessarily a separately executing participant. A **team** involves interacting participants whose actions contribute to a joint activity; calling several serial prompts a team requires explaining what coordination is added. An **institution** includes enduring roles, practices, authority relations and arrangements that organise conduct beyond a particular occupant. A database can support such an institution without itself constituting one.

### 2.2 Profiles, transitions and causal models

A capacity profile makes heterogeneous achievements inspectable, but it is descriptive unless tied to interventions. A transition model explains how state evolves. A causal model distinguishes changes to exposure, representation, authority, incentives, action and outcomes. These representations are complementary rather than successive maturity levels. A small task may need only a transition table; a contested institutional intervention may require all three.

Consider an agent that stops repeating an obsolete instruction. Under a capacity profile, it improved current-context discrimination. Under a state model, either its stored claim, retrieval policy, active context or action rule changed. Under an institutional model, a principal may have revoked permission, an interface may have removed the action, or a successor may have inherited different guidance. The same observed result fits these competing explanations. Only contrasts that vary the candidate mechanisms can distinguish them.

A capability lattice is sometimes useful when one capability genuinely requires another, but such prerequisites need evidence. Recovering a fact need not require the same path as reconstructing an obligation. Receiving a notification and actively reasoning are independently failing conditions. A universal ladder would hide these differences. Likewise, factor analysis could reveal statistical covariation among measures in a specified task population, but it would not establish universal cognitive faculties across organisms and institutions.

### 2.3 The conventional engineering counterframe

Many proposed institutional capacities can be described through familiar mechanisms: explicit versioning, durable workflow state, idempotent effects, access control, feedback, testing, queues, provenance and responsible operators. This counterframe is serious. It predicts that much apparent improvement may come from reliable execution and better information access without requiring a distinct theory of collective intelligence.

The cognitive frame earns additional value when it identifies failures these descriptions otherwise miss: preserving a qualifier during abstraction, using minority evidence appropriately, deciding when to retain a valid rule, or revising the learning method without gaming evaluation. The comparison should therefore ask which description predicts a previously unseen failure or selects a better intervention. A framework that can redescribe every outcome after the event but cannot discriminate an intervention has not established explanatory value.

### 2.4 Capacity, authority and value

Authority is a contextual relation: who may do what, for which purpose, under which conditions. A participant can have the technical ability to change a rule without the authority to do so. It can also possess valid evidence without a route to an authorised action. This is not necessarily a cognitive deficit. It may be a legitimate institutional boundary, a broken communication path or an incentive problem.

Value is similarly external to the bare capacity. Faster completion can produce more of the wrong outcome; higher agreement can suppress a correct minority; a compact memory can eliminate rare but consequential evidence. The design must state whose outcomes matter and which constraints cannot be traded away. No general weighting of quality, cost, autonomy, dissent and legitimacy is assumed here. A vector with explicit trade-offs is preferable to a falsely precise scalar.


## 3. Biological, embodied and environmental adaptation

### What the evidence can establish

The biological material makes a strong case for studying where history is carried and how it becomes effective, while providing much weaker grounds for treating every adaptive system as an instance of the same cognitive architecture. A footprint, an altered tube diameter, a changed cellular response threshold, an entrained phase distribution and a declarative record can all make the present depend on the past. That commonality is experimentally useful, but it is insufficient to establish equivalent learning, representation, autonomy or consciousness. The task is to identify which causal relations survive abstraction and which disappear when the substrate or task changes.

This review uses **history-sensitive state** for any present state causally affected by past exposure; **functional memory** for retained state whose later readout affects a declared task; **learning** for an experience-dependent change in the capacity or disposition to respond, assessed with controls appropriate to the phenomenon; and **acquired generalisation** for useful transfer beyond the original exposure conditions. These are working distinctions for this commission, not a proposed settlement of biological terminology. A response can satisfy a behavioural learning criterion even when its molecular implementation is ordinary chemistry. Conversely, calling a persistent chemical concentration a memory does not demonstrate that it supports learned associations, flexible recall or reasoning.

The relevant unit must be stated before comparison. The plasmodium of *Physarum polycephalum* is a multinucleate cell. Treating its regions as coupled dynamical components is legitimate when their coupling predicts observations. Treating those regions as independent deliberators with conflicting interests is a separate modelling decision, not a biological fact. Likewise, a culture of engineered human cells is neither a whole human nor a nervous system. A neural mollusc's defensive response supplies a useful comparison with non-neural preparations but cannot erase the difference between them.

Three questions organise the evidence: what was actually manipulated; what response was measured; and which alternative explanation remained possible? This ordering avoids a familiar trap in which an evocative result title determines the interpretation of all later details.

### Spatial organisation: different tasks hidden by “maze solving”

Nakagaki, Yamada and Tóth's 2000 experiment began with pieces of plasmodium distributed in a maze. The pieces spread and coalesced until one organism occupied the maze; nutrient-containing agar was then placed at its two endpoints. Dead-end structures contracted and a connecting route remained. The clearly shorter alternative, approximately 22% shorter, was selected; alternatives differing by about 2% were not consistently distinguished. Thus B01 supports selective reorganisation of an already occupied network, not an account in which a compact organism enters an unknown labyrinth and plans a route. This distinction is visible in the original one-page methods and result, not merely a retrospective criticism. Nakagaki et al., 2000[^1]; primary paper copy[^2].

For engineering, initial occupancy is part of the computational budget. An arrangement that already samples every branch possesses information and material reach unavailable to an explorer at the entrance. A fair comparison must charge for constructing and maintaining that reach. This is an analytical consequence of the task design, not a claim that network pruning is uninteresting. Indeed, pruning may be precisely the useful abstraction when candidate routes can be instantiated cheaply and transport feedback can identify which should persist. It is a poor abstraction when visiting a branch is costly, irreversible or forbidden.

Tero et al.'s B02 compared networks grown around 36 food locations representing Tokyo-area cities with rail topology, minimum-spanning-tree comparators and a mathematical adaptation model. The paper reports similar selected cost and path-length characteristics, while the rail network was more resistant to a random single-link failure: reported disconnection proportions were 4% for rail, 14 ± 4% for illuminated plasmodia and 20 ± 13% for unconstrained plasmodia. The model reinforces conductivity as a function of flow and otherwise reduces it. Its parameter changes expose cost–resilience trade-offs, and some simulated combinations improve the chosen aggregate metric. These are separate laboratory and simulation results, not evidence that the organism designed a better railway. Tero et al., 2010[^3]; primary paper copy[^4].

That comparison suggests a productive evaluation stance: preserve a vector of costs and capabilities until the application supplies a defensible trade-off. A route with low total length may be inadequate under correlated failures or demand spikes. A topology that is expensive to grow may be cheap to maintain, or vice versa. In a software team, a rarely used communication path can be valuable during recovery even if its steady-state traffic is negligible. Reinforcing high use without an independent measure of value can also amplify congestion, repeated errors or the consequences of unequal initial access. These engineering hypotheses require testing; they do not follow as biological laws.

Reid et al.'s B03 instead used a U-shaped obstacle between a plasmodium and an attractive glucose source. Covering the substrate in extracellular slime obscured locally deposited traces. Within the 120-hour limit, 96% of 24 plasmodia on blank agar reached the target, compared with 33% of 24 on the coated substrate. Mean speed did not differ detectably, and obstacle-free controls addressed impaired glucose detection. This is unusually discriminating support for an environmental carrier participating in navigation. The result does not establish an internal spatial map, and the intervention changes the substrate rather than surgically removing a symbolic memory. Reid et al., 2012[^5]; author-hosted primary text[^6].

The transferable structure is a write–persist–sense–act loop. Its usefulness depends on traces discriminating locations that matter, remaining interpretable, and decaying or being revised when their relevance changes. A trace that says only “visited” can reduce redundant exploration while suppressing revisits that would discover replenishment or environmental change. External state therefore needs its own confusion matrix: true exclusions, false exclusions, useful revisits and missed opportunities. Removing all traces is a crude ablation; shuffling traces while preserving their density is a stronger test of whether information content, rather than generic stimulation, causes the effect.

### Habituation: behavioural criteria, chemistry and retention

Boisseau, Vogel and Dussutour's B04 repeatedly required plasmodia to cross quinine- or caffeine-containing bridges. Across five exposure days, aversive responses diminished; testing a different repellent supported stimulus specificity. Recovery tests matter, but the detailed result qualifies the abstract-level description: quinine responses recovered, whereas caffeine-exposed plasmodia still entered caffeine bridges faster after two days without the repellent. Entry latency, crossing speed and morphology were distinct measurements with different sensitivities. The study supports operational non-associative learning under its conditions and does not establish associative learning. Its molecular explanations were proposals, not measurements. Boisseau et al., 2016[^7]; primary PDF[^8].

The critical inference is not whether the mechanism is physical; every candidate mechanism is physical. It is whether the chosen controls discriminate a learned change from fatigue, altered motility, stimulus-specific receptor adaptation, toxic accumulation, altered nutritional motivation or differential survival. Stimulus specificity weighs against a wholly general exhaustion account, but does not by itself identify the biochemical route. A reviewer should consequently preserve two judgements: whether a behavioural criterion has been met and how specifically its cause has been identified. Those judgements can have different confidence levels without contradiction.

Boussard et al. extended this programme to sodium exposure, chemical assays and dormancy. Sodium accumulation accompanied reduced avoidance; forced uptake for two hours induced a similar response; and reduced avoidance persisted after one month as sclerotia. However, habituated material was dried on salt-moistened paper and control material on water-moistened paper. The retention contrast therefore includes different conditions during entry into dormancy. Multiple samples also derived from parent plasmodia/sclerotia, so descendants are not independent laboratory replications. The paper itself discusses physiological differences between fed and revived preparations and leaves open whether sodium is the sole carrier. This is a related-laboratory mechanistic extension, not independent confirmation of all B04 claims. Boussard et al., 2019[^9]; author-hosted primary text[^10].

Retention through dormancy is particularly relevant to continuity research, provided the analogy is kept narrow. Surviving biological material is not complete replacement of participants. The carrier itself persists through a change of activity state. The engineering counterpart is closer to suspension and restart than to a new team reconstructing a practice from records alone. To test succession, separate preservation of the original substrate from transfer of selected state into a genuinely fresh substrate. Otherwise an impressive retention duration answers the wrong continuity question.

Yoneoka and Takamatsu's 2023 structural study supplies a useful partial replication and perturbation. It reproduced changes in bridge area; raw crossing speed did not show the original statistically significant increase, although speed normalised to same-day controls did. The paper explicitly discusses changed food preparation, quinine concentration and environmental variability. Cutting or rotating the established network affected subsequent quinine-crossing responses, supporting a role for morphology. Such interventions also affect transport and orientation, so they do not uniquely establish erasure of a memory. The authors themselves qualify their use of psychological terminology. Yoneoka and Takamatsu, 2023[^11].

This evidence argues for reporting dependent measures separately. A ratio can rise because its numerator improves, its denominator worsens, or both. Normalisation can be justified when day-level conditions shift all samples, but raw and adjusted outcomes remain necessary to interpret the improvement. The same requirement applies to claims that a new coordination policy is faster relative to a baseline whose performance has deteriorated. Structural perturbations additionally need sham operations and tests of unrelated functions; without them, loss of performance can reflect damage to the actuator rather than deletion of the information guiding it.

### Time and morphology as state carriers

Saigusa et al.'s B05 exposed migrating plasmodia to three periodic cool/dry episodes, then omitted subsequent episodes. Some showed slowdown near the expected times; the study tested periods from 30 to 90 minutes, with size dependence and many nonresponders. At the 60-minute period, 40% did not show the classified response. A later trigger could reinstate periodic slowing under a narrower set of conditions. The authors supplied a dynamical model based on distributions of oscillator phases and frequencies; the model did not require interactions between oscillators. This supports history-sensitive temporal dynamics, not a demonstration of explicit future representation. Saigusa et al., 2008[^12]; primary text[^13].

A 2018 conference abstract by Schramma, Bäuerle and Alim reports persistent modulation of contraction frequency following periodic blue-light stimulation. It is a consequential follow-up lead, but the stimulus, dependent measure and publication extent differ from B05. The inspected abstract cannot establish an independent replication of the original locomotion protocol, and no full replication dataset was inspected here. Schramma et al., 2018, conference abstract[^14].

The appropriate next experiment is an adversarial prediction test: fit entrainment, adaptation and explicitly predictive models on the same training histories, then compare their preregistered responses to omitted, jittered, phase-shifted and changed-period events. Showing that several models reproduce the original curve is insufficient. A model that correctly predicts the response to a novel perturbation earns explanatory credit; a vocabulary that merely renames the curve does not. Temporal expectation in this operational sense need not be conscious, propositional or reportable.

Kramar and Alim's B06 links nutrient exposure to tube-diameter hierarchy and models transport of an unidentified softening agent. The observed propagation times and dependence on contraction frequency support a flow-mediated mechanism. The key caution is in the readout section: a direct comparison of otherwise identical networks with and without prior experience was not performed; existing diameter differences served as the route for inference about prior memory. Thus structural encoding has firmer support than a fully isolated training–retention–recall experiment. The paper's analogy to associative memory should not be silently upgraded to classical conditioning. Kramar and Alim, 2021[^15]; author-laboratory PDF[^16].

Austin's companion commentary asks whether self-trimming physics establishes learning and whether the organism can address unseen challenges. This is conceptual criticism, not a failed replication or experimental refutation. The challenge about generalisation is productive; opposing learning to physical causation is not by itself discriminating. The commentary also moves between cellular and collective-brain language, which should not determine the unit of the engineering comparison. Austin, 2021[^17].

A structural carrier differs from a passive record because it also changes which actions and communications are feasible. This creates both economy and identification problems. The same modification can store history, alter transport capacity and bias new acquisition. To say that it “remembers” can be descriptively right while leaving unanswered which of these pathways improved the task. For an engineered analogue, preserve one pathway at a time: copy learned weights into a fresh graph, copy connectivity while resetting weights, preserve a record but disconnect its actuator, and hold the graph fixed while giving a central controller the same history. These are intervention designs, not claims about unperformed biological manipulations.

### Neural and cellular comparators

Bédécarrats et al.'s B07 extracted RNA from central nervous systems of sensitisation-trained *Aplysia* and injected it into naive recipients. They reported enhanced defensive withdrawal and selective changes in sensory-neuron excitability, with DNA-methylation inhibition affecting the result. The donor material came from nervous tissue; the experiment concerned non-associative sensitisation. It did not show transfer of a particular scene, episode or propositional content. The methods also excluded occasional donor batches that did not sensitise. No independent direct replication was verified in this pass; historical failures of earlier RNA-transfer paradigms cannot simply be assigned to this specific protocol. Bédécarrats et al., 2018[^18].

This result motivates a distinction between transferring a capacity-relevant state and transferring information with discriminable content. To establish the latter, recipients must express different, appropriate responses to at least two donor histories whose gross arousal, exposure and handling are matched. Otherwise transfer could instantiate a general gain change. In software, copying a risk-averse policy can alter many decisions without transmitting the particular evidence from which it was derived. Both interventions may be useful; they are different claims with different loss modes.

Kukushkin et al.'s B08 used engineered reporter cell lines, temporally patterned pharmacological stimulation and luciferase expression as a memory proxy. Spaced and massed stimulation differed in persistent transcriptional responses; ERK/CREB measurements and inhibitors examined signalling involvement. The paper distinguishes its proxy from whole-organism behaviour. Its methods report no prospective sample-size calculation, no randomisation, unblinded treatment delivery and blinded image/blot analysis. The evidence is for cellular temporal discrimination and persistence under the tested assay, not conscious recollection, ordinary kidneys remembering experiences, or demonstrated organism-level generalisation. Kukushkin et al., 2024[^19].

A reporter assay earns value by exposing an experimentally tractable intermediate mechanism. It becomes misleading only when the measured intermediate is treated as the whole phenomenon. The same structure applies to an agent system's retrieval count, rule update or successful message receipt. These observations can locate mechanisms and failures, but task-relevant behaviour must be measured independently. Temporal spacing also needs dose-matched comparisons: four exposures provide more total stimulation than one unless the massed condition matches that total. Calendar duration, number of pulses, total dose and inter-pulse interval are separate variables.

### A collective biological counterexample to universal group advantage

Sasaki and colleagues compared individual ants and colonies choosing nests whose illumination differences varied in difficulty. Colonies were better on difficult discriminations, while individuals performed better on easy ones. Their model attributes the reversal to recruitment/quorum amplification that can integrate small differences or lock in an early inferior choice. The study used repeated tests, different subject populations for individual and colony comparisons, and excluded some non-moves and colony splits. It therefore supports a task-dependent collective advantage, not a resource-matched theorem that groups always exceed individuals. Sasaki et al., 2013[^20].

This comparator differs categorically from regions of one plasmodium: individual animals assess alternatives and interact, and the colony-level decision is an outcome of that process. Its engineering value is a hypothesis about amplification, sampling and stopping rules. A team can benefit from pooling weak evidence while allowing early agreement to truncate exploration of an obvious alternative. Testing that hypothesis requires varying task difficulty, evidence correlation and commitment thresholds separately. It also requires counting indecision or splitting as outcomes rather than discarding precisely the cases where coordination fails. A successful colony decision in a single episode does not establish that the colony acquired a new capability, retained it, or transmitted it after turnover.

#### Honeybee inhibition: coordination needs stopping as well as recruitment

Seeley and colleagues observed nest-site scouts directing stop signals preferentially at dancers advertising competing sites during the decision phase. Targeting became less selective during preparation to depart. The two-site experiment involved two swarms, with additional one-site and dance-recording observations; the many signals are not independent swarms. Models derived from candidate interaction rules showed that sufficiently strong selective cross-inhibition could break deadlock between equal alternatives. The observed targeting and the modelled benefit should retain separate evidential status: this was not a randomised swarm-level intervention switching cross-inhibition off. Seeley et al., 2012[^21]; primary paper[^22].

The engineering bridge is a selective inhibition rule operating on active advocacy, coupled to a commitment threshold. It is not an argument for suppressing dissent or granting veto authority to every participant. Equal acceptable options can create an expensive deadlock; unequal options can make an early arbitrary commitment costly. A prospective comparison should therefore include equal-good, equal-bad, near-equal and clearly unequal alternatives, together with a legitimate “choose neither” outcome. The stopping signal must identify its scope and the phase in which it is effective. Otherwise a mechanism that helps complete a settled decision may inappropriately silence new evidence during reconsideration.

This suggests a phase-sensitive state machine for an engineering experiment: discovery, comparison, commitment, implementation and reopening. The transition into implementation changes which communications should recruit further work and which should cancel obsolete work. Such a design can be studied with conventional queues and controllers as well as agents. Measure duplicate work, abandoned minority evidence, premature closure, deadlock duration and recovery after a false commitment. The biological analogy loses force if options cannot be assessed locally, if inhibitory messages are cheap to forge, or if participants have conflicting incentives. Biological effectiveness cannot confer institutional permission to stop another participant's work.

#### Bacterial communities: relaying physiological state across a spatial network

Prindle and colleagues studied electrical signalling in *Bacillus subtilis* biofilms with time-lapse microscopy, membrane-potential and extracellular-ion reporters, nutrient perturbations, channel mutants and modelling. Glutamate deprivation and potassium exposure implicated the YugO channel in signalling; a gating-domain mutant reduced propagation. Sorbitol controls addressed osmotic effects. The model accounts for active propagation through a metabolic/electrical feedback loop. This is evidence of distributed physiological coordination, not by itself retained learning or associative memory. Complete channel deletion also affects biofilm development, explaining the value of the more selective gating intervention. Prindle et al., 2015[^23]; primary manuscript and methods[^24].

The candidate engineering invariant is local stress transduction and relay: a participant's resource state changes a nearby participant's behaviour, which propagates the signal beyond direct sensing range. A possible application is distributed congestion or resource-pressure feedback. But the signal changes the physiology that it reports; it is not merely a factual message. The analogue must therefore model actuation and communication together. For example, slowing a peripheral consumer can allow a resource to reach an interior consumer, but the slowdown itself creates observable demand changes. A central monitor seeing those changes may infer the wrong cause unless the signalling pathway is recorded.

A discriminating experiment would hold resource inflow constant while crossing relay availability, local sensing range and direct global telemetry. Evaluate starvation, useful throughput, oscillation amplitude, response delay and recovery from a misleading stress signal. Randomly disabling relays tests whether propagation matters, while sham changes and baseline throughput tests distinguish information loss from damage to the consumers. A relay system that synchronises all nodes can be effective under periodic resource limitation yet amplify a disturbance under another workload; synchronization is consequently an outcome to interpret, not a universal quality criterion.

Together the ant, honeybee and bacterial cases widen the comparison from one cell's morphology to recruitment, selective inhibition and physiological relay. They are distinct experiments, but not independent votes for a universal theory: the social-insect papers share a modelling tradition and cited ancestors, and the present selection was guided by a common brief. None tests wholesale participant succession or consciousness. Fungal networks and morphogenetic collectives remain useful unreviewed extensions rather than implicit members of the evidential sample.

### Frameworks and their empirical obligations

Lyon, Keijzer, Arendt and Levin's B09 is an introductory conceptual programme rather than an original experiment. Its toolkit separates sensing, memory, valence, learning, decision-making and communication, and proposes studying capacities across biological scales. This is useful opposition to a single “intelligence” ranking. Its evolutionary framing does not establish that every capacity exists in every organism or that each proposed dimension is independently measurable. The appropriate role of the framework is to organise questions whose answers could diverge. Lyon et al., 2021[^25].

Levin's B10 TAME is a perspective arguing that the appropriate scale of agency should be tested by the predictive and control usefulness of different models. This operational proposal is separable from its more expansive philosophical commitments about continuity and minds. TAME explicitly contrasts cases where an agency description helps with cases where ordinary physical modelling suffices. It supplies hypotheses and examples, not a comparative trial showing that its framing universally improves intervention. Shared authorship with B09 also means that the two are not independent confirmations of their common programme. Levin, 2022[^26].

For this commission, the strongest framework test is prospective and competitive. Give different investigators equivalent information about a system; have them propose interventions using a mechanistic dynamical model, an agency description, or a task-specific engineering model. Charge for model construction, data and intervention complexity. Score held-out predictions and task outcomes, including failures. An agency description that identifies an effective control variable with less effort can be valuable even if a complete microscopic explanation is possible in principle. Conversely, adding goal language after a result contributes no demonstrated advantage.

Gershman and colleagues' historical reappraisal of single-cell learning is useful as a map of competing interpretations, including environmental residue and protocol differences. It is not a new replication of the historical experiments it discusses. Its lesson for the present inquiry is methodological: neither a celebrated positive result nor a canonical dismissal removes the need to inspect the actual contrast. Historical primary experiments named there remain a continuation route rather than evidence independently reanalysed in this pass. Gershman et al., 2021[^27].

### Embodiment, scope and the engineering bridge

Passive-dynamic robotics supplies a materially different comparator. Collins and colleagues describe machines whose mechanics contribute substantially to gait, and powered extensions that traverse level ground with simple control. This establishes that morphology and environment can carry task structure; it does not establish that the robots learned their morphology during a trial. The design paper also records concrete engineering limitations rather than a universal advantage of passive dynamics. Collins and Ruina, 2005, design paper[^28]; Collins et al., 2005, primary record[^29].

The conceptual analysis by Müller and Hoffmann distinguishes different uses of morphological computation, including facilitating control and actual computational uses of physical dynamics. It cautions against treating every beneficial physical interaction as computation in the same sense. This is conceptual work, not an additional robot experiment. Müller and Hoffmann, 2017[^30].

The engineering abstraction should therefore specify an invariant rather than an inspirational organism. Four candidate invariants are defensible research objects: local traces bias future exploration; activity-dependent conductance changes later routing; distributed dynamics retain timing information; and morphology constrains a controller's effective action space. Each can be implemented without claiming biological equivalence. Each also has a defeating condition: trace contamination, traffic unrelated to value, unstable or mismatched timescales, and a changed environment outside the structure's operating envelope.

These candidates do not imply that decentralisation is preferable. Local signals can be cheap and scalable while globally misleading. A central controller can see constraints unavailable locally and can sometimes change policy without physically rebuilding the arrangement. Equally, central control may be delayed, expensive or unavailable during a partition. The comparison must expose these information and actuation asymmetries rather than classify one side as more natural. A fair experiment can deliberately vary visibility and latency, but it must report when equal information is impossible.

Structural memory also has a distinctive succession risk. A fresh participant can inherit a biased interface without being told the history that produced it. Continuity can therefore survive replacement while explanation and contestability disappear. A learned default route might continue to work efficiently yet fail under a regime change because no surviving record explains its boundary. This is a proposed institutional bridge, not a demonstrated consequence of the biological papers. It suggests pairing inherited structure with provenance and reversal affordances in one study arm, then measuring whether those affordances actually improve recovery enough to justify their cost.

### Evidence boundaries and discriminating conclusions

The inspected literature supports multiple mechanisms of history dependence, selected behavioural habituation effects and productive environmental/structural contributions to performance. Evidence is uneven across claims: B01's preparation procedure and B02's metric trade-off are directly recoverable; B03 provides an informative environmental intervention; B04 supports a scoped behavioural account; B05's independent replication remains unresolved; B06's structural interpretation is stronger than a claim of experimentally isolated recall; B07/B08 need their response-state and reporter boundaries preserved; B09/B10 remain challengeable conceptual programmes.

Recent 2026 search hits on repeated maze performance, chirality and mathematical habituation were screened as continuation leads, not silently integrated as verified replications. This pass also did not establish a comprehensive census of neural or collective biological systems. Broader fungal and morphogenetic comparisons would improve external coverage, but do not need to be asserted to defend the present E4 design. Any synthesis should retain this asymmetry rather than implying that a slime-mould-heavy sample establishes a general biological architecture.

The practical consequence is a demanding but tractable research programme. Test history against matched naive states; test content against shuffled state; test learning against fixed policy; test recovery against actuator damage; test transfer against preserved substrate; and test value against cost and competent conventional alternatives. Include cases where retaining an old route is correct and cases where it is not. Distinguish no detected effect from a sufficiently precise equivalence result. Preserve the possibility that a simple graph, a static interface or a single controller wins. None of these studies requires resolving consciousness, and none of the reviewed experiments establishes it.

## 4. Agent coordination, memory and revision

### Separate organisation from its effects

An agent organisation can be described as a collection of decision processes, state carriers, communication routes and action permissions. This description accommodates central supervisors, peer networks, asynchronous workers, shared blackboards and ordinary workflows without first deciding which deserves a cognitive label. The consequential questions are what information reaches which decision, who can make that decision effective, and whether the resulting behaviour improves under relevant experience. Agent count, role count, process count and independent evidence count are different quantities.

For this review, a *participant* is an independently scheduled decision process with some identifiable observation history; a *role* is an assignment of responsibilities that one or several participants can occupy. A *coordination mechanism* changes task allocation, information flow, timing, conflict resolution or commitment among participants. These are operational definitions proposed for the commission, not a consensus definition of intelligence. One model invocation can simulate several roles without supplying independently acquired evidence. Conversely, a conventional service can coordinate distinct workers without any language model. A durable institution may retain competence through code, onboarding rules and authority arrangements after every participant is replaced.

This yields four different claims. First, an architecture can make a behaviour possible. Second, an installation can implement that possibility faithfully. Third, execution traces can show it being exercised. Fourth, comparative outcomes can establish benefit under specified conditions. A graph edge is evidence of a possible information route; a delivered message shows transport; an altered subsequent decision supports influence; a controlled outcome comparison supports value. Collapsing these steps makes every framework’s feature list appear to be evidence of intelligence.

The older distributed-systems literature supplies a useful corrective to novelty claims. Smith’s Contract Net allocates work through announcements, bids and awards among asynchronous nodes, separating the semantics of task sharing from low-level message transport. A participant can manage one contract while undertaking another. This is a concrete decentralisation mechanism, not merely an organisation-chart metaphor. The paper’s distributed-sensing example supplies historical implementation evidence, but does not demonstrate that negotiation is optimal for current LLM research or coding. Smith, 1980[^31].

For the present inquiry, markets and auctions should therefore be treated as allocation policies whose merits depend on bidding incentives, credible capability estimates, communication costs and the divisibility of work. A budget bid can be strategically distorted or simply miscalibrated. A central scheduler may outperform it when capabilities and task costs are already known. A blackboard similarly specifies a shared working representation and an activation policy, not an absence of control. Its scheduler and write-conflict rules may be the decisive authority structure. Stigmergy adds indirect coordination through environmental traces, but the trace’s persistence, accessibility and interpretation still need specification. These observations are analytic distinctions, not evidence that any one topology wins.

### Contemporary frameworks as mechanism exemplars

The framework comparison below intentionally treats products as examples of mechanisms rather than competitors on a universal leaderboard. No implementation audit or load test was undertaken. Pages exposed current documentation; except CrewAI’s redirect to version 1.15.20, these web snapshots were not pinned to repository commits. The Google ADK multi-agent page redirected to an unreadable landing page, so its richer semantics remain unverified here. Its official Java API does expose separate sequential, parallel and loop agent classes. That narrow observation is enough to show that a named “agent” may implement deterministic control flow. ADK API[^32].

LangGraph’s persistence documentation describes thread-indexed checkpoints at super-step boundaries and durable pending writes from completed nodes. Updating state creates a new checkpoint, and reducers may accumulate values rather than replace them. These properties support recovery and inspection, but also create multiple historical and derived state carriers relevant to withdrawal. A state edit is therefore not automatically deletion of the old state or removal of all its descendants. LangGraph persistence[^33].

LangGraph’s interrupt documentation is especially discriminating. A resumed node starts again from its beginning, so actions preceding an interrupt can run again; side effects must be designed accordingly. “Resumption” is a runtime contract that an evaluator must read precisely. A human approval interface does not by itself ensure that the approved evidence is still current when a repeated node finally acts. Whether that risk occurs depends on the application’s validation and side-effect boundaries. LangGraph interrupts[^34].

AutoGen Core describes direct messages and published messages routed through the runtime to handlers. This makes scheduling and message handling explicit, rather than equating a transcript with an executing organisation. Topic subscriptions determine delivery recipients. It remains an application question whether a handler invokes a model, records a durable acknowledgement, changes a plan or merely stores the message. The documentation supports an event-driven coordination mechanism; it does not prove semantic absorption or recovery after a particular infrastructure failure. AutoGen communication[^35]; topics and subscriptions[^36].

CrewAI’s processes documentation distinguishes a predefined sequential ordering from a hierarchical process requiring a manager model or manager agent. The hierarchy allocates work and reviews completion through that manager. Its separate Flows facility describes stateful, event-driven composition with branches and loops. These distinctions matter because one can compare model-directed delegation with deterministic workflow structure within the same ecosystem; vendor identity need not be confounded with control policy. Marketing descriptions of cohesion or efficiency are not comparative measurements. CrewAI processes[^37]; Flows[^38].

A2A specifies an interoperability surface for tasks, messages, artifacts and asynchronous updates. Its cancellation operation attempts cancellation but cannot guarantee it, and task access must be scoped to authorised clients. An agent card describes advertised capabilities rather than establishing competence, honesty or institutional permission. The specification helps separate task submission, status visibility and terminal state from the organisational rules that determine who should delegate and whether a result is acceptable. A2A specification, §§3.1.5, 3.3–3.5 and 13[^39].

Temporal is an important conventional comparator. Its event history is an append-only durable execution record; activity scheduling, failure, timeout and accepted cancellation appear as distinct events. Reset creates a new execution from an earlier point, and historical signals can be copied. These are operational memory and continuity mechanisms even when the workflow contains no cognitive component. Their relevance is not that all agent work should use Temporal, but that a claim of improved continuity must beat competent existing workflow engineering. Temporal events and event history[^40].

### Empirical multi-agent gains are conditional

Kim and colleagues’ December 2025 preprint evaluates 180 configurations across five architectures, three model families and four task benchmarks, standardising tools, prompt structure and token budgets. Its central result is domain dependence: extra coordination can help decomposable work while harming sequential or tool-heavy tasks. Its predictive model reports cross-validated explained variance of 0.513. This is stronger evidence than an illustrative team transcript, but its fitted thresholds and architecture-selection claims are bounded by the tested task distribution and implementation. Later abstracts and summaries should not silently replace this explicitly identified v1 result. Kim et al., 2025, §§3–5[^41].

Several mechanisms can explain a conditional gain. Parallel exploration can discover more alternatives within a wall-clock deadline; context separation can reduce interference; specialists can receive narrower tool sets; a reviewer can detect mistakes before they propagate. The same mechanisms generate costs. A manager can lose a qualifier while condensing a worker’s evidence. A narrow context can hide the observation needed for correction. Extra tool calls can consume the budget before execution completes. Parallel writes can require conflict resolution whose cost outweighs concurrent generation. These are rival hypotheses for experiments, not a list of established benefits.

Cemri and colleagues’ initial study analyses failures across five multi-agent frameworks and more than 150 tasks, producing 14 failure modes within specification/design, inter-agent misalignment and verification/termination. The primary contribution is a structured failure corpus and annotation method, rather than a randomised causal estimate for each failure. A taxonomy can make recurring problems visible while still leaving open whether their causes lie in model limitations, prompts, runtimes or task difficulty. Cemri et al., 2025, v1[^42].

Choi and colleagues compare debate with majority voting across seven NLP benchmarks and find that voting explains much of the observed gain. Their theoretical martingale account is conditional: the appendix requires homogeneous beliefs and a neighbour-mean consistency condition; the limitations explicitly restrict generalisation to heterogeneous settings and other communication protocols. The result does not prove that deliberation with new evidence cannot improve decisions. It does justify including sampling-and-voting as a strong control whenever a team’s benefit is attributed to discussion. Choi, Zhu and Li, 2025, §§3–6 and Appendices C/H[^43].

A related controlled logical-reasoning study reports that participant strength and diversity matter more than several structural parameters and describes majority pressure suppressing correction. In this pass only its abstract was reviewed, so it is a corroborating lead rather than a fully appraised result. A separate judge-debate paper claims improvements through adaptive stopping, also screened only at abstract level. These contrasting claims should remain in the continuation register until their task definitions, budgets and update assumptions are reconciled. Wu, Li and Li, 2025[^44]; Hu et al., 2025[^45].

The crucial variable is independence of useful information, not diversity of names. Three agents that quote the same mistaken report have three outputs but one evidential ancestor. Different models can also share training corpora, retrieval rankings or a common orchestrator summary. Conversely, two identical models with separately acquired, complementary observations may possess genuinely different task information. This research commission’s protected passes prevent direct answer copying during initial analysis; they do not eliminate shared-source dependence or common model biases.

For an illustrative calculation, if n equally variable estimates have equal pairwise correlation ρ, their mean has variance σ²[1+(n−1)ρ]/n. The algebra follows by summing individual variances and pairwise covariances. The corresponding variance-equivalent effective sample size is n/[1+(n−1)ρ]. This is a descriptive toy model, not an estimator of LLM truth or a licence to substitute guessed correlations for measurement. It illustrates why increasing nominal team size can add little independent signal. Correlation itself may also be task-conditioned: easy questions produce shared correct answers, while a misleading source produces shared error.

Minority information therefore needs two-sided evaluation. Correct dissent can rescue a team from a shared error; incorrect dissent can waste resources and undermine warranted persistence. A protocol that permanently elevates disagreement is no more defensible than one that always suppresses it. Preserve each initial conclusion, evidence ancestry and confidence before exchange; then record which observation caused any revision. Count both correction of wrong consensus and subversion of correct consensus. A successful system must discriminate evidence quality, not simply alternate between conformity and contrarianism.

### Memory is a family of causal carriers

A useful memory description records what is retained, where it is retained, who can retrieve it, under what conditions it is consulted and what future behaviour it influences. Persistence alone establishes a storage property. Learning additionally requires a history-dependent change in capability or policy under an identified system boundary. A frozen base model can participate in a learning arrangement if experience changes its durable external tools or selection policy. Conversely, repeatedly loading a corrected instruction can improve performance without demonstrating that the model itself acquired the correction.

MemGPT proposes virtual context management across memory tiers, using explicit control operations to move information into limited active context. Its operating-system analogy identifies a concrete resource-management structure. It does not make a language-model agent an operating system in the full conventional sense, nor establish the legitimacy of every retained memory. This pass reviewed the primary abstract, so its task-level results are not used quantitatively. Packer et al., 2023[^46].

Reflexion retains linguistic feedback in episodic memory to change subsequent trials without updating model weights. Voyager instead makes an executable skill library a persistent carrier while using environment feedback and iterative code improvement. These are materially different retention mechanisms: advice about an action versus reusable action machinery. Their reported task improvements motivate experiments on external memory and transfer, but do not establish institutional learning across complete participant replacement. Only the primary abstracts were reviewed here; claims of specific benchmark superiority are deliberately not imported. Shinn et al., 2023[^47]; Wang et al., 2023[^48].

The commission can test this boundary by holding the base model fixed while replacing the durable carrier, then replacing the participant while retaining the carrier. If competence follows the carrier, the arrangement has retained useful capability even though the individual model did not change. A further ablation can distinguish a skill’s content from a selector’s ability to invoke it appropriately. An unused correct routine and a frequently invoked incorrect routine fail in different places. Selection policies, interface defaults and permissions can be as consequential as memory content.

Forgetting should likewise be disaggregated. A trace can remain stored but become less accessible; a proposition can remain available as history but lose current epistemic standing; a rule can remain understood but lose authority; a queued action can be invalidated; training influence can be removed relative to a retraining counterfactual. These operations have different acceptance conditions. A relevant warning that becomes less accessible may be a harmful loss, while a private datum that remains retrievable after nominal deletion may violate the intended removal condition. No single recall score can determine success across all these cases.

### Belief revision, truth maintenance, drift and continual learning

Alchourrón, Gärdenfors and Makinson distinguish contracting a theory to remove a consequence from revising it to introduce information, and characterise partial-meet contraction through representation results. The publisher abstract and bibliographic record were verified; the complete proof text was not available in this pass. Its value here is a formal distinction between change operations, not a claim that an LLM memory implementation satisfies the postulates. Alchourrón, Gärdenfors and Makinson, 1985[^49].

Doyle’s truth-maintenance research makes dependencies among deductions explicit, supporting reconsideration when their grounds change. The verified short IJCAI report is a primary summary of a larger technical report, not the later full 1979 article. Its enduring engineering implication is that removing one sentence may leave conclusions supported by that sentence active unless dependency changes propagate. Whether a contemporary provenance graph can discover all relevant natural-language dependencies is a separate empirical question. Doyle, 1977[^50].

Formal dependencies and contextual revisions solve different parts of the problem. An organisation may have explicitly recorded that policy P depends on requirement R, making a withdrawal tractable. It may also have absorbed R into a summary, copied a plan fragment, changed a default or trained a selector without preserving the derivation. A complete syntactic dependency graph then overstates causal coverage. The proposed response is to distinguish declared descendants, observed descendants and suspected influence, and test later behaviour rather than infer its correction from graph edits alone.

Bifet and Gavaldà’s ADWIN adapts the retained data window to observed distribution change and provides bounds on detection errors under its assumptions. The publisher abstract was inspected, including its use inside or alongside a Naïve Bayes predictor; implementation details were not audited. This supplies a disciplined contrast with informal “tripwires”: a change detector needs an exposure stream, a statistic and an error criterion. Bifet and Gavaldà, 2007[^51].

A rise in failures can indicate changed conditions, a broken instrument, new task composition, weaker participants or an incorrect causal model. It does not identify a constitutional defect by itself. If an intervention changes which cases are attempted, its observed failure rate may fall without improved competence. Conversely, expanded exploration may raise failure counts while creating valuable capability. The relevant denominator is exposure to comparable opportunities, with task difficulty and selection recorded. A detector should trigger investigation or a bounded policy response, not automatically promote a universal lesson.

Kirkpatrick and colleagues’ elastic weight consolidation constrains changes to parameters important for earlier tasks, addressing catastrophic forgetting during sequential neural-network training. This is retention under parameter learning, distinct from editing a textual memory store. The study supports a stability–plasticity problem, not a universal preference for preserving past behaviour: old competence may remain valuable, become obsolete or require narrowing. Kirkpatrick et al., 2017[^52].

Exact machine-unlearning work makes the target more demanding still. Brophy and Lowd’s DaRE forests are designed so deletion yields the model obtained by retraining on the remaining data, with cached structure enabling efficient updates. That guarantee belongs to their learning algorithm and model family. It cannot be transferred to a running LLM agent merely because both systems use the word “forgetting.” Brophy and Lowd, 2021[^53].

For the proposed framework, this suggests a family of targets rather than one forgetting dimension: selective access suppression, epistemic revision, authority withdrawal, operational cancellation and training-data influence removal. Each target should name retained utilities and permitted exceptions. The never-exposed counterfactual is apt for a specified influence-removal claim, but inappropriate when remembering an error is necessary to preserve its corrected scope. A team may need to remember why an earlier rule was overbroad in order to avoid inventing it again.

### The named memory benchmarks: identity and evidential limits

MemoryAgentBench is *Evaluating Memory in LLM Agents via Incremental Multi-Turn Interactions*, by Yuanzhe Hu, Yu Wang and Julian McAuley, arXiv:2507.05257. The inspected v1 defines accurate retrieval, test-time learning, long-range understanding and conflict resolution, reformulating prior datasets and introducing additional tasks. FactConsolidation probes explicit factual updates. This is a useful profile of task abilities, but its “test-time learning” does not by itself demonstrate delayed institutional transmission, and conflict resolution is not equivalent to comprehensive execution-state forgetting. Hu, Wang and McAuley, 2025, §§3–4[^54].

STALE is *State Tracking And Latent Evaluation*, introduced by Hanxiang Chao, Yihan Bai, Rui Sheng, Tianle Li and Yushi Sun in arXiv:2605.06527v1, dated 7 May 2026. It contains 400 conflict scenarios with three probes each: state resolution, resistance to stale premises and implicit policy adaptation. It distinguishes same-attribute conflicts from propagated invalidation and reports a best evaluated overall accuracy of 55.2%. CUPMem is its proposed memory prototype. The data are generated and expert revised; performance on these constructed scenarios is not field validation. Chao et al., 2026, §§3–5[^55].

There is a further construct-validity concern, arising from the examples rather than a reproduced error count. Some implicit observations admit alternatives: a new apartment need not imply abandoning the old residence; a changed physical condition may suspend an activity without erasing a preference. The appropriate action can be cautious adaptation or clarification rather than a categorical claim that an old belief is false. A successor benchmark should include matched ambiguous cases and stable controls. Otherwise, a system rewarded for decisive invalidation may overlearn the very overgeneralisation the commission seeks to understand.

Supersede is *Diagnosing and Training the Memory-Update Gap in LLM Agents*, by Vedant Patel, arXiv:2606.27472v1, 25 June 2026. Its agent rewrites bounded notes while raw sessions are not re-fed. On 78 LongMemEval update questions it reports 92% full-context versus 77% bounded-memory accuracy for GPT-5.4. A smaller scaling comparison uses 25 items. A single Qwen2.5-3B training run improves matcher-graded held-out accuracy from 9.0% to 16.7%; the paper explicitly calls this preliminary and does not establish multi-seed significance. It is a diagnosis and trainable environment, not a solved memory policy. Patel, 2026, §§3–7[^56].

The ancestry check matters. Supersede describes its LongMemEval data as real conversations, but LongMemEval’s own Appendix A describes simulated evidence sessions, human inspection and extensive editing, with distractors drawn from several sources. The sound description is curated conversational benchmark data containing synthetic evidence sessions, not independently observed longitudinal human interaction. LongMemEval itself assesses extraction, multi-session and temporal reasoning, updating and abstention. It is a shared substrate for several papers here, so those evaluations are not independent replications across distinct environments. Wu et al., 2024, Appendix A[^57].

Oblivion is *Self-Adaptive Agentic Memory Control through Decay-Driven Activation*, arXiv:2604.00131v1, dated 31 March 2026, by Ashish Rana and colleagues. It is a method, not a benchmark. Its read path gates retrieval through uncertainty and buffer sufficiency; its write path reinforces memories identified as used. Decay reduces accessibility while retaining recoverable traces. Evaluations use LongMemEval and GoodAILTM, with static and interactive conditions. Its design therefore cannot establish deletion, and usage-based reinforcement leaves open whether a frequently used error becomes harder to dislodge. Rana et al., 2026, §§2–5[^58].

Taken together, these sources establish that memory maintenance is a substantive, measurable part of agent performance. They do not establish a universally superior memory architecture. Their tasks vary between explicit updates, inferred changes, retrieval, downstream recommendations and bounded-note maintenance. A comparison matrix must retain those distinctions. A benchmark label is especially weak evidence for authority withdrawal, descendant repair or successor behaviour, which require additional runtime and institutional probes.

### Learning about the learning system

An arrangement can revise not only remembered content but the policy determining which experiences become memory. That raises a second-order evaluation problem. Suppose a team changes its lesson-promotion rule after several errors, then reports that promoted lessons are overturned less often. This can indicate better lessons, stricter promotion, fewer opportunities for challenge, or an evaluator that has become less willing to call an error. An internal improvement metric cannot identify which explanation holds without an exposure model and an external anchor. The proposal here is to evaluate the promotion policy through later object-level performance, including cases that should preserve existing knowledge and cases that should revise it.

This requires separating candidate generation from acceptance. Open exploration can produce unusual hypotheses without committing them to operational use. A protected archive can preserve minority ideas that are currently unsupported, provided their status remains visible and retrieval does not quietly promote them into authority. The same arrangement should be able to retire a frequently repeated lesson when its evidence weakens. Retention, salience, confidence and permission are therefore different control variables. A single scalar “importance” score cannot be assumed to represent all four adequately; that is an empirical design question rather than a prohibition on simple implementations.

The learning policy also changes the future evidence stream. If an agent learns to avoid an uncertain task class, its error rate may improve while its adaptive range contracts. If a team installs an additional review stage, it may detect more errors and initially look worse by its own incident count. If a memory gate narrows context to improve efficiency, it may exclude the rare exception that would correct a dominant assumption. These feedbacks make before-and-after comparisons particularly fragile. A prospective study should retain a fixed audit sample of opportunities independent of the system’s choice to attempt them, alongside measures of useful exploration and recovery.

Delayed outcomes are equally consequential. A retained instruction may help a familiar task while harming transfer months later; a broad principle may produce no immediate gain yet enable learning in a new domain. Consequently, immediate action quality and later learning efficiency should be distinct outcomes. The latter can be measured by how many examples, tool interactions or corrections are required to reach a prespecified level of performance on new tasks. Such a test can reveal enabling knowledge that a recall score misses. It can also expose brittle routines that initially look efficient because they bypass relevant evidence.

Succession offers a demanding but interpretable intervention. Replace every active participant while keeping the institution’s approved artifacts, then test whether the new participants can recover both the routine and the reasons that define its scope. If only the routine survives, the system may preserve performance under familiar conditions while losing the means of correction. If reasons survive but no participant has permission to alter the actuator, explanatory understanding may coexist with unchanged behaviour. These outcomes should be reported separately rather than forced into a single learning/not-learning verdict.

Finally, the evaluating framework must remain revisable without becoming self-protecting. A newly discovered failure class may justify an additional test; replacing an inconvenient old test with an easier one may manufacture progress. Preserve a frozen reference battery, add new probes with explicit version history, and report performance under both when the instrument changes. This is a proposed research safeguard, not evidence that any current Practice has failed such a test. Its purpose is to make the inquiry capable of discovering that a mechanism, metric or category should be simplified or replaced.

### Implications and discriminating uncertainties

The strongest supported conclusion is architectural pluralism under measurement: a frozen model with retained executable skills, a revised external memory, a parameter-learning system and a durable workflow preserve different kinds of history-dependent capability. None should monopolise the term learning. The corresponding research risk is to count every persistent trace as acquired competence, or to call every changed action evidence-responsive learning. The unit, carrier, intervention and subsequent outcome must travel together in the claim.

For coordination, the principal unresolved issue is how task decomposability interacts with evidence dependence and execution coupling. A task may be informationally decomposable but operationally serial: several researchers can investigate independently, yet only one migration can modify a database. Another may require shared context for correct interpretation while allowing independent actions. A topology selected on message count alone misses these distinctions. The experiment should separately manipulate access to evidence, authority to act and coupling of side effects.

For memory, the principal unresolved issue is scope-sensitive revision. Existing retention and update tests offer useful components, but a long-lived arrangement must preserve valid transfer while changing invalid applications, retain warranted historical evidence and prevent withdrawn influence from returning through generators or execution state. These obligations can conflict. Keeping an audit trail aids diagnosis but creates another potential retrieval route. Restarting participants clears active context but can destroy unrelated useful state. A temporal restriction can prevent misuse while also excluding the evidence needed to contest the restriction.

The E5 protocol in the experimental programme proposes resource-matched comparisons across single-agent, workflow, ensemble, discussion and protected-evidence conditions, including correct and incorrect minority evidence. E7 proposes staged state interventions and delayed behavioural probes, distinguishing memory-store changes from active plans, caches, retries and successor contexts. Neither has been implemented. Both are designed to permit a null or negative conclusion: a competent single agent may suffice, a conventional workflow may provide the relevant continuity, a simpler memory scheme may preserve more useful behaviour, or the elaborate intervention may add cost without benefit.

## 5. Institutional memory and learning policies

### What could persist beyond participants?

The institution is a useful unit when past activity alters what later participants can accomplish through carriers that outlast those who produced them. Persistence alone is insufficient. An archived manual can preserve sentences without preserving access, interpretation, appropriate application, or authority. Conversely, a redesigned interface can preserve a successful response without preserving an explicit account of why it works. The empirical question is therefore counterfactual: given comparable successors and circumstances, does inherited state improve appropriately scoped future performance relative to an otherwise comparable arrangement without that history?

Organizational memory theory provides a conceptual precedent, not a completed measurement instrument. Walsh and Ungson distinguish acquisition, retention, and retrieval and explicitly confront anthropomorphism and misuse. Their article is theoretical; it should not be cited as an experiment proving that a particular organizational store works. Levitt and March's synthesis treats organizational learning as historically acquired routines that subsequently guide conduct, including lessons transmitted beyond the original actors. Its contribution is an explanatory orientation rather than identification of one universal storage mechanism. Walsh and Ungson, 1991[^59]; Levitt and March, 1988[^60].

For this inquiry, institutional continuity should be disaggregated into informational continuity, competent reenactment, and legitimate continuation. A successor can know that a previous owner promised delivery while lacking permission to deliver. It can possess valid permission but misread the pending state. It can resume a correct task twice. These failures imply distinct interventions: better provenance, practical reconstruction, authority validation, or duplicate-effect control. A single continuity score would conceal whether gains in one channel are purchased by failures in another.

The zero-active-participant case is especially revealing. A dormant arrangement may retain a disposition to recover, but it is not currently sensing new events or reconsidering old judgments. If a scheduler, human custodian, or hosted service continues operating, that process belongs inside the active boundary when its contribution matters. If nothing acts, continuity is demonstrated only on reactivation. This is a proposed analytical distinction, not a claim that institutions cannot exist legally or socially during dormancy.

### Distributed cognition is a mechanism description, not a license to aggregate

Hutchins's cockpit analysis follows representations through people, instruments, spoken exchanges, and spatial arrangements. The paper explicitly offers a stylized account informed by airline procedures and extensive cockpit observation, rather than presenting a randomized comparison. The particularly useful move is to explain a whole task through transformations among carriers: a selected speed card and positioned instrument markers change later work, while cross-checking creates additional opportunities to detect inconsistency. These are concrete coordination operations, not merely the claim that a group has a mind. Hutchins, 1995[^61], introduction and the procedural/representational analyses, PDF pp. 2–11.

An agent analogue would trace a proposition from source to task representation, selected context, tool input, and final action. The comparison is defensible at the level of representation, access, transformation, and timing. It does not establish equivalence between pilots' learned embodied skills and a model's generation, or between an aircraft instrument and a retrieved text chunk. Nor does it show that every computational component should be counted as a participant. A shared board, an index, and a deterministic validator may be important parts of cognition without becoming independent agents.

This perspective suggests an experiment more informative than simply adding memory. Hold the facts and underlying participant fixed while varying whether critical state is visible at the point of action, requires deliberate retrieval, or is inferred from a narrative. Measure missed obligations, inappropriate actions, time, and recovery. A benefit from visibility would support an access/affordance explanation. A benefit surviving removal of the changed display could instead suggest retained participant adaptation. Both may be valuable, but only the latter supports that narrower attribution.

The boundary also determines cost accounting. A highly competent model operating with expensive human curation may outperform a weaker model with no support; that observation estimates an arrangement-level difference. It does not identify autonomous acquisition. Recording the contributions separately makes it possible to improve the arrangement without promoting an inaccurate story about where competence resides.

### Transactive memory: remembering how to locate competence

Liang, Moreland, and Argote compared people trained together with people trained individually on radio assembly, testing work groups one week later. Jointly trained groups recalled more and produced better radios; videotaped interaction supported a transactive-memory account. The design matters because the manipulation changes shared experience as well as later knowledge coordination. The reported mediation should not be promoted into proof that every alternative social mechanism was experimentally isolated. Liang, Moreland, and Argote, 1995[^62].

Moreland and Myaskovsky then tested a communication rival: individually trained participants given information about one another's skills performed comparably to jointly trained groups, despite lacking the same opportunity for communication during training. This makes knowledge of competence a stronger causal candidate than generic familiarity alone. It remains a bounded laboratory result; accurate expertise descriptions in small groups are easier to arrange than reliable expertise directories in changing institutions. Moreland and Myaskovsky, 2000[^63].

For computational teams, a role label is a weak proxy for a transactive-memory system. A useful directory must represent who or what can solve which problem, with what reliability, availability, and access conditions. A declared database specialist can be unavailable; a tool can cover the needed competence more cheaply; a supposedly independent reviewer can repeat the same source error. Consequently, directory accuracy, routing success, and source dependence should be measured separately from the quantity of specializations advertised.

Succession creates a sharp distinction between content memory and routing memory. Retaining an old expert's name without retaining their knowledge or finding a substitute creates a false affordance. Retaining only the knowledge may lose the circumstances in which it was credible. Proposed successor tests should therefore deliberately include obsolete identities, accessible replacement experts, and misleadingly similar competence labels. Successful reconstruction means identifying where useful competence now resides and recognizing when it does not.

The strongest bridge claim is functional: both human and computational arrangements may benefit from accurate allocation of epistemic work. The weakest bridge claim is psychological: software does not acquire interpersonal trust merely because a record contains a confidence field. Trust language should be replaced with observable calibration or clearly identified as a human participant's belief.

### Tacit routines and the gap between rule and performance

Cohen and Bacdayan induced interlocking routines in paired laboratory participants and argued that their behavioral properties support distributed procedural memory. The source's substantive claim concerns individuals' components of a jointly enacted routine. It does not establish that a complete institution stores an indivisible procedural trace. It nevertheless provides an empirical reason to expect some practical competence to escape verbal summaries. Cohen and Bacdayan, 1994[^64].

Feldman and Pentland distinguish an abstract understanding of a routine from its particular enactments. Their theoretical argument allows routines to generate endogenous change through performance; a written procedure cannot stand in for the actions that reproduce and modify it. This challenges two tempting simplifications: that routines are intrinsically rigid, and that changing their description necessarily changes what participants do. Feldman and Pentland, 2003[^65], pp. 94–98.

An institutional archive should therefore be evaluated through reenactment, not solely through recall. Give a successor an ordinary case, an edge case requiring a justified exception, and an unfamiliar case where the routine should not apply. Compare access to prose instructions, executable examples, annotated failure histories, and a period of overlap with a competent predecessor. The overlap condition may be costly yet reveal which losses are caused by missing situated demonstration. Conversely, examples might equal overlap, undermining a claim that tacit continuity requires persistent personnel.

There is no necessity to call all undocumented competence irreducibly tacit. Missing documentation can be ordinary incompleteness; unread documentation can be retrieval failure; inability to act can be permission loss. “Tacit” becomes explanatory only when plausible explicit alternatives have been investigated. For agent systems, a retained execution checkpoint might contain useful state that no prose handoff captured, but it can also preserve obsolete plans. More complete copying may improve immediate continuity while worsening revision and permission accuracy.

This produces a useful design tension: preserve enough history to reconstruct a decision while avoiding an inherited obligation to reproduce it. Authority, provenance, applicability, and execution status should be available as separable evidence. Whether the extra representation pays for itself remains a performance question, not a virtue established by schema completeness.

### Sensemaking and the politics of classification

Weick, Sutcliffe, and Obstfeld frame sensemaking as the production of an actionable interpretation of circumstances. Their article is conceptual consolidation, explicitly connecting interpretation to identity and organizing. It supports examining how a situation becomes recognizable enough to act upon; it does not establish that a persuasive shared interpretation is accurate. Weick, Sutcliffe, and Obstfeld, 2005[^66].

For this commission, classification is a causal intervention. Calling an event “noise” may suppress escalation; calling it “constitutional” may trigger a large review; calling a response “refusal” can misdescribe a bounded technical failure. Once the classification controls retrieval, evaluation, or authority, a local interpretation can become institutional infrastructure. This is our proposed mechanism, not a finding that the Practice's particular labels already cause such effects.

A discriminating test supplies identical event records under different inherited labels, with the underlying material accessible to all conditions. Evaluate whether participants inspect the material, preserve contradictory observations, and choose a proportionate response. A second manipulation removes the label while preserving the evidence. These contrasts separate semantic anchoring, actual information deficits, and workflow enforcement. Labels can also help; a well-calibrated incident type can reduce detection time. Therefore the outcome must reward appropriate use and justified rejection of a classification.

The important causal arc is not simply event → interpretation → action. An initial label can change which subsequent events are collected, which denominators are available, and what later counts as a recurrence. An apparent common cause can then be manufactured by the observatory's own selection rule. Tracking exposure and rejected classifications is necessary to identify that feedback. Preserving every ambiguous item forever is not the only remedy; sampling audits and explicit uncertainty can be cheaper comparators.

### Institutions, incentives, and authority are not interchangeable with cognition

North treats institutions as formal and informal constraints that shape interaction and incentives. His historical-economic account is valuable precisely because it allows persistent arrangements to channel conduct toward stagnation as well as growth. Institutional survival is not evidence of intelligence or public benefit. North, 1991[^67].

Ostrom's diagnostic framework rejects a universal governance prescription and organizes interacting resource, user, and governance attributes. Its contribution here is methodological: identify the conditions under which an arrangement works instead of selecting centralization or decentralization in advance. The inspected abstract supports that framework-level claim; full-text retrieval through PMC encountered an access challenge. Ostrom, 2007[^68].

The computational translation needs discipline. A reward, an evaluation rubric, a resource quota, and a legally binding duty are different kinds of constraint. An agent can respond to a scoring rule without possessing a human economic interest. A user can confer task authority without creating a constitutional institution. Governance can still be studied functionally by recording who may amend which objects, which actions are technically possible, and whose interests the outcome measures represent.

Consider a team that notices an error but repeats it. A cognitive deficit is one possibility. Others include a reviewer who cannot stop execution, a reward for finishing quickly, an escalation cost imposed on the discoverer, or a controller that reinstates the old rule. These possibilities predict different responses to the same evidence. Randomizing information access, permissions, and incentives separately would be much more informative than asking participants to reflect harder.

The operational lesson is to distinguish knowledge, effective influence, and legitimate authority. A correct belief need not authorize an action. An authorized rule may still be wrong. A technically enforced policy may be effective without being understood. These are not marginal exceptions: they determine whether a claimed learning gain is actually a change in judgment, a change in execution constraints, or a transfer of decision rights.

### Voice, dissent, and the limits of an interpersonal analogy

Edmondson's multimethod study of 51 manufacturing teams reported an association between psychological safety and learning behavior, with a mediating relationship to performance. It supplies field evidence that social conditions relate to learning processes, but an observational mediation model cannot by itself establish that an intervention on safety produces the downstream gain. Edmondson, 1999[^69].

The agent-team analogue should be framed as an information-selection question. Do dissenting observations survive coordination? Does challenging the supervisor change resource allocation or early stopping? Does consensus pressure erase qualified objections? These can be measured without claiming that a software agent experiences embarrassment or interpersonal risk. Where people are part of the arrangement, human psychological and career consequences are real variables requiring their own measures.

Protected independent inquiry may improve the chance that minority evidence is initially represented. It does not make that evidence independent if all participants read the same mistaken source or use the same model. A practical evaluation should vary both communication timing and source overlap. It should also include cases in which the minority is wrong: a coordination method that automatically privileges dissent merely reverses the previous bias.

A possible failure mode of formal adversarial roles is ritualized disagreement. A designated critic can produce objections because the role requires them, while the team learns to discount the entire channel. Evidence quality should therefore be scored independently of role, and accepted/rejected objections followed into later outcomes. The number of criticisms is not an outcome measure of better inquiry.

### Exploration can preserve future competence while worsening present efficiency

March's model compares exploration and exploitation, including mutual learning between people and an organizational code. It demonstrates within a specified simulation how faster convergence on established knowledge can harm longer-term adaptation. This is a theoretical mechanism demonstration, not a field estimate of an optimal exploration budget. March, 1991[^70].

Turnover is correspondingly ambiguous. Argote and colleagues studied 240 participants in three-person production groups, varying turnover and task complexity over six periods. Closed groups outperformed open groups, but the reported pattern differed with complexity and was consistent with an innovation explanation. The abstract permits a bounded account of the interaction; it does not warrant saying that turnover generally improves complex work. Argote et al., 1995[^71].

This matters for zero-participant continuity. Complete replacement can remove valuable local knowledge and also remove an entrenched interpretation. A fair study therefore includes both stable environments, where continuity should help, and changed environments, where inherited routines require revision. It should measure the return of useful performance and the recurrence of obsolete rules separately. A successor that starts slower but detects changed conditions may offer higher cumulative value than a fast but overcommitted continuation.

Exploration also needs an outcome window long enough for enabling work to matter. A fixed immediate task score will often classify option creation as waste. Yet an unbounded claim of future usefulness cannot justify every detour. A tractable compromise is a declared portfolio of downstream tasks, delayed tests, and option-use opportunities whose evaluation is fixed before observing which exploration succeeded. Exploratory outputs that never find a use remain recorded as costs, while genuinely useful discoveries receive credit beyond their initial task.

### Learning policies and metareasoning

Argyris distinguishes correcting activity within existing objectives from challenging the policies or objectives themselves. Here that distinction is used narrowly, following the publisher's description rather than claiming inspection of the complete 1977 article. Argyris, 1977, publisher description[^72].

For operational research, at least three objects should be separated: a task policy that chooses actions, a learning policy that chooses what experience to retain and how to revise task policies, and a governance policy that determines which revisions are permissible. Replacing an evaluation instrument adds a fourth possibility. These objects can interact, but numbering “learning levels” does not prove a hierarchy of intelligence. A system that updates task-specific procedures well may be more useful than one that frequently rewrites its meta-policy.

Computational metareasoning offers a concrete alternative to unstructured reflection. Callaway and colleagues formulate learning to allocate computations, reporting favorable results across termination, allocation, and planning problems, including the overhead of metareasoning. Milli, Lieder, and Griffiths examine when selecting among a small number of cognitive systems is advantageous, with results depending on environment variability and metareasoning cost. These are formal/computational results in defined settings, not demonstrations of self-governing institutional wisdom. Callaway et al., 2018[^73]; Milli, Lieder, and Griffiths, 2017[^74].

A proposed institutional counterpart estimates whether another review, experiment, or retrieval is likely to improve the decision enough to justify its cost. But the estimate itself costs attention and relies on a model of consequences. Simpler stopping rules can win when the environment is stable, while richer allocation can help when stakes and uncertainty vary. The relevant comparison is cumulative external decision quality under equal resources, not the sophistication of the meta-reasoning narrative.

### Recent agent evidence sharpens the challenge

Wang and colleagues evaluate harness evolution against task-level search under comparable feedback and rollout budgets. Their August 2026 revision uses Terminal-Bench 2.1 and a disjoint split of 45 training, 10 validation, and 34 test tasks. The held-out average gain reported is 0.6 percentage points. The authors also identify task difficulty and limited sensitivity to harness design as rival explanations. Thus their results challenge general efficacy claims without establishing that harness learning cannot help in other environments. Only two independent runs and a narrow benchmark limit precision and transfer. Wang et al., 2026, §§3–5[^75].

SkillLearnBench instead deliberately selects 20 skill-dependent tasks across 15 subdomains, evaluates specifications, execution, and outcomes, and reports that no learning method dominates across tasks and models. Its extended self-versus-teacher feedback comparison is confined to Productivity Tools with one solving model; the broader claim that all self-reflection necessarily drifts would exceed that evidence. Benchmark construction around tasks benefiting from reference skills also affects generalization to ordinary workloads. Zhong et al., 2026, §§1, 5.3–5.4[^76].

These studies need not contradict one another. A benchmark selected for skill dependence may be sensitive to procedural scaffolding, whereas another may primarily expose underlying reasoning limits. Their conjunction supports an interaction hypothesis: the value of learning a reusable procedure depends on task structure, feedback quality, and the remaining bottleneck. That hypothesis is a synthesis to test, not an effect already estimated across the two datasets.

The Cybernetic Teammate supplies a different boundary: human professionals using AI on product-development work. The published June 2026 article follows a field experiment whose working-paper record reports 776 professionals and stronger AI-assisted individual performance relative to unaided individuals. Publication details also disclose exclusions and team reassignment procedures. It supports comparing individual-plus-AI with human teams, not asserting long-term institutional learning or performance after personnel replacement. Dell'Acqua et al., 2026[^77].

### Prior-project antecedents and the remaining test

The recovered private S04 framework already asks programmes to distinguish domain, epistemic, organizational, and transfer findings (§110), and proposes prospective future-decision improvement as the test of meta-learning (§115). These passages were inspected in the recovered project copy after the independent literature account had been drafted. They are substantive predecessors: neither scoped lessons nor future-outcome evaluation should be presented as new discoveries of this review. They also remain a research programme. The passages do not supply randomized contrasts, power estimates, or demonstrated successor effects. Our proposed extension is to make their causal requirements executable as study designs, while permitting ordinary documentation and simpler allocation policies to outperform them. [Private S04 framework, §§110 and 115: source archive](research-package.zip) (archive path: `evidence/private/impact-aligned-artificial-organisations-framework.md`).

### Competing causal decompositions

The following are proposed models for organizing this programme. They are alternatives to a flat list of desirable capacities, not established mutually exclusive explanations.

| Decomposition | What changes | Discriminating intervention | Main threat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Participant acquisition | A participant's durable competence | Remove scaffolding after learning; replace participant separately | Hidden external assistance |
| Routing and access | Which available knowledge reaches a decision | Hold content fixed; alter indexing, directory accuracy, timing | More information mistaken for better learning |
| Affordance and execution | Possible actions and action costs | Keep beliefs constant; change tool or gate | Enforcement described as understanding |
| Institutional incentives and authority | Who benefits, may amend, or can stop | Cross evidence with permission and scoring conditions | Normative approval confused with accuracy |
| Selection and replacement | Which participants or routines remain | Separate within-unit change from turnover | Survivor bias |
| Measurement and classification | What counts as success or an incident | Fixed external anchors plus revised local instruments | Evaluation gaming and selective visibility |

These decompositions can coexist. A corrected directory may route evidence to an authorized owner, who changes a workflow that successors enact. Calling the whole chain “learning” is acceptable only with the boundary stated. For causal improvement, the chain should be tested at several interruption points. An institution-level average gain does not identify which component should be replicated elsewhere.

The most serious alternative to elaborate meta-governance is a competent participant with ordinary documentation and a simple feedback loop. The strongest case for additional machinery arises when recurring failures are demonstrably caused by succession, information routing, delayed commitments, or cross-boundary authority. Even then, a lightweight intervention may suffice. Architectural complexity becomes warranted by discriminating outcome evidence, not by a vocabulary capable of redescribing everything.

### What the institutional strand adds to the framework

The proposed capacity profile should include recovery under succession, routing calibration, enactment fidelity, and revision under legitimate constraints. It should not label authority itself a cognitive capacity. It should distinguish a retained lesson from a retained method for acquiring lessons, and distinguish both from changing the purpose against which learning is judged. These are useful distinctions because they imply different failures and experiments.

No inspected source establishes full institutional learning for the Practice, robust continuity with zero active participants, or self-revision that preserves independent evaluative validity. The literature supports narrower mechanisms and exposes plausible rival explanations. The immediate research opportunity is therefore causal discrimination: which forms of inherited state help which successors, and which changes to the learning policy improve future external work beyond what extra resources and ordinary documentation achieve? E6 and E10 in the experimental programme operationalize those questions without executing them.

## 6. The Engraph Practice as a critical case

### Scope and architecture

The Practice is the evolving arrangement of principles, skills, rules, reviewers, memory, roles and tooling through which work is organised around OCE. It is neither identical to the repository nor a single agent runtime. Its effective boundary may include human direction, model providers, local processes, shared state, GitHub and other services. The repository is a persistent carrier of both product code and accounts of how work should be performed. This makes it a useful case for studying institutional enactment, provided documentary self-description is not mistaken for demonstrated capacity. Practice map[^78].

The report *The Practice as an Operational System* proposes a lifecycle connecting intent, obligations, attempts, actor authority, durable evidence, waiting, correction and terminal accounts. It explicitly presents an exploratory synthesis and outcome portfolio, not ratified doctrine or implementation. Its central diagnosis is that individually developed components can lack a mechanically recoverable account of their relationships. That is an architectural hypothesis about transitions, not evidence that a universal lifecycle is needed. The report's own subtractive test is valuable: a new account should remove reconstruction and synchronisation burden, not simply add another surface. Operational-system report, review contract and §§1–3[^79].

### Three reconstructed arcs

#### Arc 1: written guidance, firing and recurrence

PDR-098 distinguishes what triggers a check, how a relevant condition is recognised, and whether the response advises or enforces. This is a useful decomposition because a mechanical trigger can call for cognitive judgement without mechanically deciding the outcome. It avoids treating advisory response as synonymous with passive guidance. However, the axes describe implementation choices; their statistical independence and efficacy are not established by calling them independent.

The record also preserves a later recurrence account: mechanisms with verified documentary homes reportedly recurred, while some quiet mechanisms lacked evidence of extinction. The underlying napkin corpus is selected by observations worth recording, so it supplies evidence that a failure can recur but not an unbiased population failure rate. Exposure opportunities, cases where guidance worked silently, changes in task difficulty and participant composition would be needed for that inference. The current study inspected the record, not every underlying event. Its reported counts should therefore remain attributed, not promoted into fresh measurements.

The discriminating test is a factorial comparison of trigger availability, detection method and response policy. Measure correct detection, false alarms, resulting decisions, prevented useful actions, cost and recurrence. Compare ordinary guidance with a mechanism that delivers equivalent information at the relevant moment. If benefits disappear when timing or information access is matched, the evidence supports that narrower mechanism rather than a general superiority of procedural control.

#### Arc 2: watcher failures and causal revision

The June team-operations report describes a prolonged session with successive role holders, handoffs and watcher failures. Its initial account interpreted drain timeouts as evidence that the event-store arrangement did not scale to the observed traffic. A subsequent addendum reports orphaned synthetic CPU-load processes and revises the likely dominant cause towards host starvation, with additional process-lifecycle problems. This is a valuable example of a revised causal explanation within the same documentary record. It is not a controlled estimate of the separate contributions of event volume and host load. Graph Team Marathon, §§3.3 and 9[^87].

Current source code supplies a narrower implementation check. The watcher loop rethrows timeout errors; its iteration logic emits relevant events before marking them seen; and the deadline tests specify rejection when drain, emit or marking hangs. This establishes the presence of a design and tests for fail-loud behaviour. It does not prove that the production entry point has every required bound, that all processes are correctly supervised, or that the test suite currently passes. Watcher loop[^88], iteration logic[^89], deadline tests[^90].

The emit-before-mark choice explicitly permits duplicate notification after some failures in preference to silently missing an event. This is a useful distinction between transport integrity and exactly-once effects. A duplicate notification need not be a duplicate external action if the consumer uses an appropriate identity and reconciliation contract. Conversely, a monotonic cursor alone cannot prove correct delivery or absorption. The corresponding experiment should vary event volume, injected bounded delays and process failure separately, then observe the entire route to appropriate action.

#### Arc 3: continuity, liveness and succession

The June report describes multiple successful role successions, but it is a retrospective account authored within the ensemble. Claims such as no work lost are scoped observations by that report, not independent proof of losslessness across all obligations. Participant narratives and the summarising report share ancestry; agreement among them is not a new replication. A successor who observed the prior work live also differs from a fully replaced participant receiving only durable records.

PDR-133 improves the measurement language by separating notification from an active reasoning loop, and both from absorption and practical capability. The source code likewise separates watcher heartbeat records from peer heartbeat events. The latter classifier uses recency and cannot report a participant who has never emitted a qualifying event. This makes observation coverage a first-class issue: missing from a report need not mean absent from the system. Watcher heartbeat[^91], peer-liveness classifier[^92].

The cross-platform surface matrix records partial, unverified and version-specific states. It is valuable as an implementation ledger, but its older provider-version entries are not a fresh acceptance test on the current desktop host. Copying a skill wrapper across platforms establishes availability of text, not equal triggering, execution, interruption or outcome behaviour. The report therefore treats portability as an experimental variable and declines a blanket claim of cross-host equivalence. Cross-platform matrix[^93].

### Critique of inherited propositions

The six-mechanism design source is explicitly provisional but occasionally makes stronger statements than its evidence can bear. An anomaly should not be discounted merely because it is singular: one reliable counterexample can defeat a universal claim, while many correlated observations can carry little additional information. Low firing or overturn rates cannot by themselves establish that an instrument is decorative; the relevant denominator includes opportunities where the condition should have been detected. Recurrence across repositories does not uniquely locate a constitutional cause; shared inputs, tooling or selection can generate the same pattern. These are logical and measurement critiques of the proposed rules, not claims that the ideas have no value. [Private source: *Deep Dives: The Six Mechanisms Without Prior Art*, §§1–6; hash prefix `213c3c3b9a07b35f`; preserved in the evidence package].

A specific prior-art assertion also needs narrowing. The design source contrasts task invalidation with the options framework as if options cannot be interrupted mid-execution. Sutton, Precup and Singh explicitly study interruption and policy improvement in §4. That defeats the broad contrast. A separately governed invalidation record may still contribute persistent authority, audit and prohibition of future reuse beyond a control policy's termination condition. That narrower institutional contribution needs comparison; it is not established as novel by the absence of an identically named artefact. Sutton, Precup and Singh, 1999[^94].

PDR-134 calls usage-derived indicators computed confidence while carefully separating them from authored status. For scientific use, prevalence and truth must remain distinct too: many documents can repeat one error. The record's insistence that the graph addresses prose rather than fully representing its meaning also limits what can be inferred about automated descendant correction. A reference graph is useful for finding candidate dependants; it is not automatically a complete causal influence graph.

The inhibition report usefully distinguishes pause, wait, defer, suppress, quarantine, cancel, revoke and retire. These distinctions make intervention more precise, but naming them does not establish that a runtime preserves each one under interruption and replay. A cancelled attempt can coexist with a valid obligation; a revoked actor can retain technical access; an archived record can still appear in a summary. The appropriate assessment follows the actual state and effects at each boundary. Inhibition report, §§1–2[^95].

### Meta-learning evidence ceiling

The Parallax evaluation corpus is especially clear about its status: its README labels the suites authored but not yet executed and empirically unvalidated. The recursive-learning cases test appropriate interpretation of failed predictions, invocation errors and changes to learning policy. They are evaluation designs, not observed successes. Their existence supports a claim that the Practice has specified how it might evaluate its methods; it does not support a claim that those methods already improve later work. Parallax evaluation README[^96], recursive-learning cases[^97].

PDR-130's example recurrence threshold and quarterly review are governance choices, not calibrated statistical tests. A failed prediction can indicate a poor mechanism, weak activation, wrong measurement, changed context or insufficient exposure. Binary promotion or retirement may be a useful institutional decision rule, but it should not be confused with a complete evidential classification. An explicit inconclusive state is necessary in the research programme even if a later governance process must still make a time-bounded operational choice.

The strongest defensible conclusion is therefore mixed. The Practice supplies unusually explicit and revisable mechanisms, records and failure hypotheses. Selected static implementations correspond to some of those contracts. Retrospective accounts document correction and succession, with important confounders preserved. Comparative, delayed and successor evidence sufficient to establish net benefit remains incomplete. This makes the Practice a substantial candidate for controlled evaluation alongside simpler arrangements, not a validated endpoint to which all other systems should converge.

## 7. A conditional framework for comparison

### A conditional profile with an explicit causal account

The framework's unit is an **arrangement under conditions**, not a named product in the abstract. Record the participant set, model or organism, tools, memory carriers, authority, environment, task distribution, history, resources and observation horizon. Then report capacities alongside the proposed mechanisms and observed outcomes. The same software package can instantiate very different arrangements; an agent with no durable state and one with a shared event history should not inherit one undifferentiated product score.

This is a research instrument, not a software schema or a claim of discovered cognitive dimensions. It refines the earlier project memory profile rather than presenting a renamed vector as a new finding. The foundational forgetting report already distinguishes persistence, accessibility, fidelity, confidence, salience, behavioural efficacy, transmission and recovery, and its stability profile separates resistance, recovery, persistence, robustness, plasticity and transformability. Those distinctions are retained where they change inference; they are not all promoted into independent capacity axes. Foundational forgetting report, §§3.2, 32, 37 and 55[^98].

| Capacity family | Operational question | Discriminating measure | Mechanisms to compare | Characteristic false inference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Does experience change later ability relative to an exposure-matched control? | Held-out task improvement after controlled experience | Parameter learning, memory writing, policy change, structural adaptation | Better initial model called learning |
| Retention and reconstruction | Which capabilities or obligations survive delay and interruption? | Delayed competence and reconstruction error, with retained-state access specified | Archive, rehearsal, retrieval, routines, replay | Bytes retained called useful memory |
| Transfer and context discrimination | Is useful structure applied beyond training while respecting its limits? | Valid-domain transfer, false generalisation, false exception | Abstraction, contextual branching, similarity search, causal modelling | Universal rule replaced by universal reversal |
| Evidence responsiveness | Does relevant evidence appropriately change belief or action? | Warranted revision and warranted persistence under graded evidence | Revision, truth maintenance, change detection, escalation | Recency obedience called learning |
| Coordination | Does interaction improve joint results under matched resources? | External task quality, unique evidence use, conflict resolution, overhead | Supervisor, peer network, market, shared state, role allocation | More messages or role names called intelligence |
| Influence control | Can obsolete or disallowed state cease to affect specified conduct? | Residual influence, useful-knowledge preservation, restore/replay recurrence | Retrieval restriction, supersession, replay, erasure, revocation | Source deletion called complete forgetting |
| Exploration | Can the arrangement discover valuable options under uncertainty? | Valid novelty, option diversity, future task enablement, exploration cost | Slack, diverse search, curiosity objectives, protected alternatives | Output volume called innovation |
| Resilience and reconfiguration | Does it recover from disturbance or acquire a new capability by changing organisation? | Recovery trajectory, performance after fault and topology intervention | Redundancy, rerouting, modular replacement, adaptive structure | Robustness called learning or transformation |
| Succession | What survives complete participant replacement? | Competence, context, obligation and authority reconstruction after turnover | Handoffs, shared representations, socialisation, durable workflows | One successful handoff called institutional continuity |
| Learning-policy adaptation | Do changes to how learning is selected and evaluated improve later work? | Held-out downstream outcomes across policy generations | Prospective revision, meta-evaluation, governed retirement | A rewritten method called successful meta-learning |

These families are deliberately not statistically orthogonal. Acquisition is defined through time; retention qualifies its durability; transfer qualifies its domain. Succession is a stress condition on several capacities rather than necessarily a new faculty. Influence control is partly a technical capacity and partly governed by normative criteria. Learning-policy adaptation is a level of intervention whose success is measured through later object-level effects. An empirical programme may merge families when separate measures do not change prediction or intervention.

### Required context and evidence fields

Each claim needs a task and history, mechanism, comparator, resource envelope, timescale, authority context and outcome. Evidence status should distinguish **proposed**, **authored**, **implemented**, **activated**, **exercised**, and **outcome-effective within scope**. These labels are not a linear maturity score: a reliably used component may have weak outcome evidence, and a research prototype may have stronger controlled evidence than a widely deployed product. “Not inspected” is distinct from “absent”; “not reported” is distinct from “failed”.

Confidence should be profiled across source proximity, construct validity, causal identification, robustness, transportability and decision relevance. Static source inspection may strongly support a sequencing claim while offering no direct support for user benefit. A field experiment can support a total treatment effect while leaving mediation uncertain. A formal theorem is strong relative to its assumptions and silent about whether those assumptions fit a particular deployment.

### Four latencies and two additional clocks

For correction or revision, record detection latency, authorised-revision latency, representation-propagation latency and behavioural-change latency. A source may be corrected immediately while a successor still uses an old summary. Conversely, a protective gate may change behaviour before the source representation is repaired. Reporting only time to document edit hides both cases.

Two further clocks can matter: time until external consequences become observable, and time until the institution reviews whether the intervention helped. These are not necessarily part of the same causal chain. A delayed outcome can be legitimate even when the system has acted correctly; an indefinitely delayed evaluation can also conceal failure. The protocol must predeclare windows suitable to the task rather than impose a universal clock.

### A causal account for experience-to-outcome claims

The following model is a proposed analytical decomposition, not an assertion that all systems implement a pipeline:

```text
experience and environment
  → available evidence
  → encountered and interpreted evidence
  → retained representation or changed structure
  → present retrieval, permissions and incentives
  → selected and executed action
  → external outcome
  → later evaluation of the learning process
```

Feedback, parallel routes and omitted states are expected. A behavioural gate may bypass representation change. A successor may reconstruct the required policy from durable artefacts. The model is useful when it helps locate the earliest unsupported causal connection; it fails if investigators force every phenomenon into its stages. Its bridge claims require interventions on actual paths, not simply matching stage names to log messages.

Every bridge remains conditional. A positive source-domain finding is a reason to test a target-domain mechanism, not evidence that the target already possesses the capacity. The intended monitoring scale is the target arrangement's task episode or policy generation; observations of component activity alone do not establish the target consequence.

### Crosswalks between descriptions

The capacity frame and state-transition frame are linked only partially: a trajectory can demonstrate task performance, but an unmeasured capacity cannot be read from a state label. The state and institutional frames are asymmetric: institutional authority can constrain which transitions are permitted, but a technically reachable transition does not establish legitimacy. The environmental and participant frames overlap causally: moving memory outside a participant changes the boundary of attribution, not necessarily the total arrangement's capacity. The conventional engineering frame can often reproduce operational distinctions; whether cognitive terminology adds value is an empirical comparison of diagnostic and predictive usefulness.

These crosswalks preserve incomparability. There is no meaningful direct numeric comparison between a cellular reporter response and an institution's authority accuracy. Each can participate in a broader theory of history-dependent behaviour while retaining its own measurement instrument and validity conditions.

### Interaction tests and competing explanations

Prioritise interactions that can reverse an apparent benefit: retention × revisability; transfer × context discrimination; coordination × source independence; structural change × participant competence; authority × evidence sensitivity; and learning-policy revision × measurement protection. A memory system may help only when retrieval is selective. Protected inquiry may help only when participants receive genuinely different evidence. A rule may improve conduct only because it blocks every action, reducing both error and useful work.

For each observed gain, compare at least these rival causes: stronger participants, more information, additional compute, changed task mix, human correction, altered incentives, external enforcement, selective survival and measurement change. An ablation alone may be insufficient if removing a component also breaks unrelated functionality. A credible comparator preserves the relevant service contract while varying the proposed causal mechanism.

### Provisional design possibilities

Exploratory associations can identify new questions without becoming findings. Environmental traces suggest testing whether negative evidence can be represented in a route or affordance rather than repeated prose. Succession suggests investigating which obligations are reconstructable when no participant is active. Archive separation suggests a memory architecture with a protected historical channel and a smaller operational channel. Each association needs a concrete target-domain test and cost comparison before adoption.

The attractive analogy between a nervous system and an agent institution is discarded as an explanatory shortcut: neither a central coordinator nor a graph establishes the relevant neural mechanisms. A further tempting claim—that every useful institution requires a universal causal graph—is also rejected as a default. Sparse, task-specific provenance or ordinary typed workflow state may preserve the distinctions needed at much lower cost. These are live architectural alternatives, not rankings settled by the review.

The framework itself should be tested. Give blinded evaluators task incidents and ask them to diagnose causes and choose interventions using either this instrument or a short conventional checklist. Compare correct localisation, prediction of subsequent failures, intervention quality, analyst burden and missed alternatives. If the larger framework does not improve those outcomes, simplify it. If it makes important cases harder to describe without improving discrimination, replace it.

## 8. Cross-strand synthesis and conclusions

### What can be claimed across the evidence

The evidence supports a plural account of adaptive capacity. Some history dependence is carried by participant state; some by shared representations, routines, morphology or environmental traces. Selected experiments isolate useful memory or coordination mechanisms, while other sources offer conceptual programmes, formal models or documented interfaces. The strength of the synthesis lies in preserving these differences. It does not establish a common scale on which a plasmodium, model ensemble and institution can be ranked.

Three conclusions are especially useful for research design. First, **persistent state and effective influence are different properties**. A retained record may never be retrieved; a removed record may have left a consequential derivative; a structural change may guide a successor without an explicit account of its origin. Second, **changed behaviour does not uniquely identify learning**. Better access, external enforcement, altered incentives and participant replacement can all produce change. Third, **coordination and continuity are mechanisms to evaluate, not benefits to assume**. Their costs, failure paths and dependence on task structure must appear in the comparator.

These conclusions are scope-limited analytical inferences from the contrasts developed above. They do not require denying cognition to physically explained systems, nor attributing consciousness to them. A dynamical explanation and a functional memory description can concern different questions about the same system. The decisive issue is whether the proposed description predicts, explains or improves an intervention beyond its alternatives.

### Conflicts preserved rather than averaged

| Conflict | Current treatment | What remains unresolved | Discriminating follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biological memory versus history-dependent dynamics | Functional memory can coexist with physical explanation; specific learning claims require their own controls | Which models predict novel perturbations most economically | E4 and omitted/jittered-event tests |
| Multi-agent improvement versus sampling/resource effects | Gains are conditional; vote and search baselines can explain some apparent deliberation benefit | Which evidence partitions and tasks justify interaction | E5 with equal resources and true/false minority evidence |
| Institutional continuity versus stronger successors | Retained carriers may help, but partial overlap and human repair confound attribution | What survives full replacement with no continuing custodian | E6 stable/change and assisted/unassisted contrasts |

| Recursive improvement versus self-validation | Policy change is observable; improved learning needs later protected outcomes | Robustness across policy generations and new tasks | E10 with frozen evaluation anchors and direct-search baseline |
| Practice recurrence versus mechanism failure | Recurrence shows a failure remains possible, not its exposure-adjusted rate or unique cause | Activation, case mix, quiet successes and human rescue | E8/E11 with controlled faults and complete denominators |

Several conflicts are definitional or scoped rather than direct empirical contradictions. A short-term performance gain and a long-term transfer loss can both be true. A formal result under homogeneous beliefs can coexist with benefit from heterogeneous new evidence. A memory-removal method and a retrieval-decay method can target different properties. Apparent disagreement should first be classified before selecting a winner or averaging results.

### Evidence ancestry and independence

The internal Practice reports form an interdependent lineage: earlier observations motivate governance records; later syntheses cite those records; subsequent reports interpret the resulting evolution. This is valuable institutional history, but it is not a sequence of independent confirmations. Where the original event corpus was not inspected, the claim remains attributed to the report. The hash-matched project files establish source identity, not empirical truth.

The external literature also contains shared substrates. Several recent memory papers use LongMemEval, whose evidence-session construction includes simulation and human editing. Improvements across those papers are not automatically replications in independent natural environments. Biological programmes can share authors, laboratories or experimental traditions. Different model-generated analyses can share training and retrieval biases. These dependencies do not invalidate results; they narrow what repeated agreement adds.

Protected initial analyses and a separate-context audit reduce some opportunities for anchoring. They do not provide independent laboratories, independent model families or an empirical test of the research method. Their proper output is a more inspectable synthesis, not a claimed validation of Parallax. The review's own framework should encounter simpler-method comparisons and later predictive tests before it acquires operational authority.

### Implications for architectural inquiry

There is no warrant here for selecting a universal graph, maximal memory, decentralised swarm or elaborate institutional controller. Different task structures favour different arrangements. A conventional workflow may supply the necessary durability; a single agent may use the available evidence better than a team; a shared external trace may reduce cost; a specialist directory may provide more value than replicated generalists. The right question is which mechanism changes which consequential outcome under the conditions of use.

Equally, current implementation should not bound discovery. Biological structural state motivates experiments in inherited affordances. Institutional succession motivates testing dormant arrangements. Distributed evidence motivates protected inquiry with explicit ancestry. Learning-policy evaluation motivates versioned, reversible changes whose success is judged outside their own scorecard. These are substantive research possibilities even where the present local need is not established.

The strongest candidate architectural principle is therefore modest: **make the route from past experience to present effect inspectable at the boundaries where a claim depends on it**. This is not a demand to instrument every causal interaction. Complete lineage may be unavailable or disproportionately expensive. Sampling, typed obligations, explicit versions and task-specific probes may be sufficient. The principle itself fails as a recommendation where its cost exceeds the diagnostic, recovery or outcome value it provides.

### Final epistemic profile

Conceptual support is strong for separating capacity, mechanism, authority and outcome, because conflating them creates identifiable inference errors. Empirical support is heterogeneous and local: specific studies support scoped mechanisms and task effects, while broad transport remains uncertain. Static implementation support exists for selected OCE runtime mechanisms; present deployment fidelity was not measured. Comparative evidence of the Practice's net institutional benefit is insufficient. The candidate framework and experiment portfolio are provisional and intended to produce the missing discrimination.

The research programme permits a clear next step: construct isolated, controlled studies using the protocols below, after their remaining design and authority requirements are satisfied. It does not permit declaring the Practice, the framework or any named system generally intelligent, self-improving or superior. A negative result can justify simplification; a mixed result can identify a useful validity domain; a positive result must survive resource, source, delay and successor controls before broader claims are made.

## Experimental programme for adaptive capacities

### Status and scope

Execution authorised: false.** These protocols design experiments; they do not report trials, participants, deployments or observed effects. They are intended for synthetic tasks and isolated research environments initially. Any later use of people-derived records, workplace observation or consequential services needs a separately scoped authority and data-governance decision. No such collection is implied here.

The programme distinguishes a decision, research question, estimand, estimator, estimate and action rule. The first five study families focus on acquisition, evidence, representations, environment and coordination; the next seven test continuity, prior influence, diagnosis, retirement, learning policy, activation and external value. The labels E1–E12 identify study families rather than a mandatory implementation order. Detailed E4, E5, E6, E7 and E10 protocols are included below at comparable or greater depth than boundary repair.

### Common design contract

The default assignment unit is an isolated arrangement–task-history instance, with an independent memory store, execution context and identifier. When multiple participants share a memory, generator or controller, assign the whole cluster. Observations include decisions, actions and outcomes within that instance; messages and repeated probes are dependent observations, not additional independent experiments. The intended inference unit is a declared task family and arrangement class, not all agents or institutions.

Keep participant model, information access, tool permissions, task distribution, total resource envelope and stopping rules matched where the question demands it. Report unavoidable mismatches. Include competent single-agent, deterministic-workflow, ordinary-documentation and lightweight-Practice comparators where applicable. Measure both fixed-resource quality and resources needed to achieve a fixed quality threshold, since a wall-clock comparison can favour parallel systems while hiding greater total compute.

Record assignment, actual exposure, failed activation, non-use, abandonment, contamination, attrition, missing outcomes and deviations. The primary estimand usually concerns the effect of assignment to a system, including activation failures. An exposed-only analysis is secondary and may be biased by post-assignment selection. For example, excluding runs that failed to retrieve a correction would remove part of the mechanism failure the experiment is meant to measure.

Use objective task checks where available; otherwise use blinded domain judgements with preserved disagreement. Judges should not be instructed that agreement with the Practice defines success. Preserve raw actions separately from explanations and self-reports. When confidence cannot be measured comparably, report decision calibration or abstention performance rather than pretending that generated numerical confidence is a calibrated belief measure.

Protect held-out task families, perturbations and successor tests from method authors. Deduplicate source ancestry across apparent corroborations. Log the provenance of every correction, summary, generator and evaluation item. Counterbalance order only where carryover can be reset; if learning persists, use parallel clusters or independent replicas rather than an invalid crossover. These principles are consistent with explicitly defined interventions and target populations in causal inference. Hernán and Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*[^99].

### Prospective precision and stopping

All numerical thresholds below are **design scenarios, not justified adoption thresholds**. A confirmatory protocol must first justify its smallest practically important effect, baseline risk or variance, dependence assumptions and feasible budget using domain losses and independent nuisance estimates. Until then each study is exploratory. Do not promote a convenient sample size into confirmatory status.

For illustration, two independent equal-sized arms with a binary outcome near 0.5, two-sided alpha 0.05 and 80% power require approximately `n = 2 × 0.5 × 0.5 × (1.96 + 0.84)^2 / delta^2` observations per arm under a normal approximation. This gives about 1,568 per arm for a 5-percentage-point difference, 392 for 10 points and 175 for 15 points. These are sensitivity calculations, not proposed recruitment counts. At 0.5 risk, an approximately ±5-point 95% interval for the difference requires about 769 independent observations per arm.

If there are five observations per cluster with intraclass correlation 0.10, the simple equal-cluster design effect is `1 + (5−1)×0.10 = 1.4`. More observations inside a few clusters do not replace independent clusters. Pairing may improve precision, but its benefit depends on discordant outcomes and cannot be assumed. For rare severe errors, heavy-tailed cost, sequential learning, unequal clusters or adaptive designs, simulate the actual allocation and estimator, verify interval coverage and decision error, and retain pessimistic scenarios.

At a pilot stage, estimate nuisance quantities and test instrumentation, not adoption efficacy. Freeze the confirmatory protocol before new test data. Use a single fixed-horizon primary analysis unless a coherent sequential rule is preregistered. Protect a family of primary contrasts with an explicit multiplicity policy; secondary mechanisms and interactions can remain exploratory. Null results require interval interpretation relative to the effect or equivalence margin, not a statement that the mechanism has no effect. Positive results require replication or transport tests before a broader claim.

Every protocol has a resource ceiling and terminal conditions to be set before execution. A broken isolation boundary, incorrect treatment, leaked holdout, unauthorised effect or unusable outcome instrument stops the affected run. A repair creates a labelled amendment or new run rather than silently changing treatment. No statistically favourable result overrides the execution boundary.

### Balanced initial portfolio

Begin with an instrumentation and feasibility stage spanning E1, E4, E5, E6 and E10. These cover context-sensitive learning, structural memory, social coordination, institutional continuity and change to the learning system. E11 manipulation checks are embedded across them. E2 and E3 supply shared stimuli and compression contrasts; E7–E9 and E12 extend the portfolio once the necessary state and outcome measurements work.

This prioritisation reflects distinct information value rather than preference for easy correction tasks. E4 and E5 test whether organisational structure adds value; E6 tests whether the capacity belongs to an enduring arrangement; E10 tests whether the method for improving it deserves retention. A feasibility failure in one family does not justify omitting the others. Record its exact limitation and use a simpler discriminating design where possible.



### E1. Boundary learning and repair

**Decision and question.** Choose how an arrangement should preserve useful generalisations after learning their limits. Does scoped correction improve held-out discrimination compared with an unqualified replacement, an ordinary summary or no update?

**Task and intervention.** Use a synthetic routing domain whose rule is genuinely useful: standard documents can follow an automated route, but documents with a specified exception require review. Supply enough acquisition examples to establish the useful rule, then controlled counterevidence revealing the boundary. Randomise matched instances to original evidence, a plain summary, a scope-preserving record, or a forced universal reversal. The reversal is a deliberately adverse comparator, not a recommended response. Include a competent full-history agent and a deterministic rule system given the same relevant facts.

**Estimand and units.** The primary effect is the difference in error on a preregistered mixture of unseen valid-domain and exception cases after the update. Assignment is by isolated history; analysis clusters probes within histories and templates. Report invalid applications, false exceptions and destructive reversal separately so an aggregate does not conceal lost transfer. Secondary contrasts test delay, unprompted recall, compression and complete participant replacement.

**Controls and rival hypotheses.** Include irrelevant novelty, a false exception, a singular decisive counterexample and repeated noisy evidence. An apparent gain may come from seeing more examples, a stronger model, memorising labels or defaulting to review. Match information and include cases where review is costly or wrong. Vary surface form while preserving causal structure, and vary causal structure while preserving surface similarity.

**Analysis and action rule.** Estimate assignment effects with intervals, task-family effects and resource costs. The practical margin must price false generalisation and unnecessary review separately. Retain a mechanism only if it improves exception handling without unacceptable valid-transfer loss and within the cost envelope. If it works only on reminded tests, conclude immediate compliance, not durable repair. If no meaningful distinction is detectable at adequate precision, prefer the simpler memory policy within the tested domain.

### E2. Evidence responsiveness and justified stability

**Decision and question.** Determine when updating policies or representations is warranted. Can an arrangement discriminate strong relevant evidence from weak, irrelevant or misleading assertions, including genuine regime changes?

**Intervention.** Hold task options constant and randomise evidence quality, relevance, provenance and regime. Include a synthetic probabilistic decision with an explicit payoff threshold: evidence can change the justified posterior estimate while leaving the best action unchanged. This separates belief revision from action switching. Where no meaningful posterior can be elicited, score the action under the known generative task model.

**Estimand and units.** Estimate the change in expected decision loss relative to a predeclared evidence-aware reference policy across the regime mixture. Assign independent task histories; analyse repeated observations within history. Report warranted switches, warranted persistence, false reversals, delayed response and recurrence. No-change controls are co-primary safeguards against indiscriminate compliance.

**Rivals and controls.** Latest-message obedience, source prestige and social pressure can mimic evidence responsiveness. Cross correct versus incorrect assertions with authoritative-looking versus neutral provenance while preserving legitimate authority separately. Include attacks that assert a permission change without having permission. This is an isolated synthetic test, not a live impersonation experiment.

**Analysis and action rule.** Use predeclared error costs and confidence intervals; calibration is supplementary unless the instrument is validated. Compare uncertainty and action trajectories over delay and successors. A mechanism that increases switching but worsens expected loss is rejected. A mechanism that updates belief correctly yet preserves the action in a threshold case succeeds. Poor exposure or inadequate precision yields an inconclusive mechanism result, while still counting as a system-assignment failure where relevant.

### E3. Compression and institutional propagation

**Decision and question.** Choose how to preserve and revise claims across summaries and generated artefacts. Does scope-preserving compression improve later decisions enough to justify its extra cost?

**Intervention.** Generate histories containing useful rules, exceptions, dates, uncertainty and conflicting evidence. Randomise ordinary summaries, structured scope records, access to originals and recomputable views under matched active-context budgets. Then correct one source and vary repair at the source only, known descendants, or the recurring generator. Descendant depth, breadth and reuse frequency are independently varied where feasible.

**Estimand and units.** Estimate later task-error and qualifier-preservation differences by assigned memory policy over a declared descendant graph. The independent cluster is the source family and its derivatives; a hundred descendants of one source are not a hundred independent findings. Measure propagation latency, authority inflation, recovery of historical interpretation, human/agent effort and harmful over-removal.

**Rivals and controls.** Longer summaries can outperform because they carry more information, not because of a superior structure. Compare at equal token budget and also report quality–cost frontiers. A mechanical generator may preserve literal qualifiers yet distort meaning; independent task outcomes test that possibility. Include unchanged sources to measure collateral damage and independent rederivation to distinguish legitimate recurrence from stale restoration.

**Analysis and action rule.** Use cluster-level contrasts and sensitivity to unobserved descendants. A known-descendant closure rate must state its denominator and does not establish universal causal closure. Retain more elaborate records only when task effects or reliable recoverability justify their cost. If originals suffice at the available budget, no extra lineage architecture is indicated. An inaccessible or unenumerated derivative boundary limits the guarantee rather than being counted as successful repair.

### E4. Environmental and structural memory, with biological bridge tests

Protocol design, 9 September 2026. No experiments executed, participants recruited, systems deployed or biological interventions performed. Numerical margins below are provisional decision thresholds to be justified before a confirmatory registration; the first stage is explicitly exploratory.

#### Decision, question and hypotheses

**Decision:** determine whether task-specific environmental traces or adaptive structure justify a controlled engineering pilot over a competent conventional workflow or central controller. This study does not decide whether the system is conscious or which arrangement is universally more intelligent.

**Question:** under matched information and resource budgets, does experience encoded outside individual participants improve later exploration, adaptation and recovery, and through which carrier/readout route?

**Rival hypotheses:** H1, retained environmental information improves subsequent decisions. H2, topology/weights improve transport or constrain behaviour irrespective of retained information. H3, the apparent gain comes from extra sensing, compute or maintenance. H4, a fixed controller with explicit history achieves the same benefit more cheaply. H5, apparent adaptation is a transient state response with no retained capacity. H6, persistence helps stationary tasks but impairs regime change. H7, inherited structure preserves performance through participant replacement while losing explanation and corrigibility.

The hypotheses are not mutually exclusive. The design estimates interactions and multiple causal routes rather than forcing a single winner.

#### Task environments and units

Use two controlled task families. First, a graph exploration/delivery simulator with known task validity: nodes contain replenishing or non-replenishing resources, edges have delays/costs, some observations are noisy, and selected routes fail. Second, a sandboxed evidence-routing workflow with objectively checkable synthetic records: several stations process cases, acquire local observations, route unresolved cases and maintain context-scoped guidance. No external messages, production data or consequential actions are needed.

Randomise an entire **environment history × arrangement run**, not messages or individual decisions. Exposure units are decision opportunities; observation units are actions, accesses and graph changes; dependence units include environment seed, task family, model/provider version, initial state and shared source ancestry. Inference initially concerns the specified simulated distributions and the tested participants. It does not concern all organisations or biology.

Each run includes acquisition, a no-new-training probe, delayed probe, context transfer, perturbation and recovery. Branch matched copies from a common pre-treatment checkpoint when deterministic replay is possible. Record where replay fails or stochastic tool effects make histories diverge. Complete participant replacement occurs only after a state checkpoint, with fresh sessions and no hidden conversational inheritance. An additional pause leaves zero active participants while the declared external carriers persist.

#### Carrier and readout interventions

At a preregistered checkpoint randomise sub-runs to intact state, erased state, shuffled state preserving size/density, stale state from an earlier regime, and state transplanted from a different environment. Add a sham operation with the same interruption and processing overhead. For structure, distinguish topology, weights, edge capacity and metadata: reset one while preserving the others. For traces, preserve the amount of material/information displayed while permuting its location or labels.

Cross **history present/absent** with **readout connected/disconnected**. A stored trace that is never consulted cannot establish a behavioural memory benefit. Disconnecting readout should preserve basic sensing and actuation. The design should log availability, encounter, interpretation where observable, action and external outcome, but the primary endpoint remains task behaviour.

To test content transfer, transplant two different histories that prescribe opposite responses only in a known contextual boundary. Match their total length, activity and aggregate reward. Generic caution or speed changes cannot count as content-specific transfer. Test previously valid routes that remain valid alongside routes made invalid by a genuine regime change. Misleading novelty with no environmental change tests warranted persistence.

#### Outcomes, estimands and analysis

Primary engineering estimand: the intention-to-treat difference between each candidate arm and A1 in **held-out valid task completions within the fixed resource budget after perturbation**, averaged over the prespecified environment distribution. Report percentage-point effects and cluster-aware uncertainty. Co-primary protection endpoint: performance on unchanged, previously valid task contexts. A candidate must not improve adaptation by destroying warranted persistence.

Secondary outcomes: cumulative task regret; coverage of genuinely new useful states; redundant revisits; missed beneficial revisits; time to recover a prespecified performance band; disconnected obligations; duplicate effects; false route abandonment; inappropriate adherence to stale traces; success after complete replacement; construction, storage, retrieval, communication and maintenance costs; human correction minutes; abandoned runs; and delayed rework. Keep these separate unless a decision maker supplies utility weights prospectively.

Mechanism estimands: intact-minus-shuffled state at matched density; intact-minus-readout-disconnected state; topology/weight reset interactions; replacement × carrier interaction; regime-change × retention interaction. A favourable ablation is not sufficient if an equally cheap conventional baseline matches the full system.

Use hierarchical models or block-level bootstrap/randomisation inference over independent histories, with run-level clustering and repeated probes nested within runs. Analyse raw counts as well as normalised performance. Time-to-recovery treats unrecovered runs as censored at a fixed horizon, and also reports failure proportions; do not average only successful recoveries. Treat tool failures, budget exhaustion and non-use as outcomes under the assigned policy. Distinguish planned missingness, platform failure and abandonment. Sensitivity analyses include worst-case bounds on missing outcomes.

#### Precision, stopping and protected evaluation

Pilot 20–30 independent histories per task family to measure variance, dependence, endpoint range, implementation fidelity and cost; these counts are for feasibility, not a claim of adequate power. Then freeze task generators and use separate seeds for confirmation. Reserve whole environment families as transfer holdouts, not merely new prompts from the same template.

Before confirmation, justify a minimum worthwhile gain and tolerated harm. Illustrative margins are a 5-percentage-point gain in post-perturbation completion and no more than a 2-point decline on unchanged valid contexts. These values are proposals, not empirical constants. Simulate the planned hierarchical design using pilot variance and plausible intracluster correlations to choose the number of independent histories for 90% power, or choose a precision target that can exclude the relevant harm margin. State effective sample size; do not multiply messages into trials.

Predeclare the primary contrast, multiplicity strategy, analysis code and fixed stopping horizon. Do not repeatedly inspect significance and stop when favourable. Use an independent evaluator or frozen objective checker whose criteria are unavailable to policy revision; do not let an adaptive arrangement rewrite its tests. A separately labelled exploration set can support discovery without leaking into confirmation.

**Prospective action rule:** advance a candidate only if it clears the justified worthwhile-gain criterion or an explicitly valued cost-equivalence criterion, protects valid-context performance, and has interpretable fidelity. If benefit disappears after resource matching, retain the resource explanation. If a confidence interval crosses both meaningful benefit and harm, the result is inconclusive. If the interval lies inside a justified equivalence region, prefer the less costly mechanism unless another protected outcome warrants complexity.

#### Biological bridge tests

**Bridge 1 — external trace information.** Proposed invariant: persistent, locally accessible evidence of previous exploration changes later route selection. In a laboratory extension of B03, compare intact traces, spatially shuffled traces, uniform traces, clean substrate and sham handling, with glucose-gradient and motility checks. Cross trace age with resource replenishment. Estimate navigation success and useful revisits at a fixed horizon. A topology-free reactive simulator with matched trace lifetime tests engineering sufficiency. Failure of shuffled traces to impair performance would undermine an information-content interpretation while leaving a generic substrate effect possible. The biological protocol would require specialist feasibility work because moving traces may change their chemistry or geometry.

**Bridge 2 — structural state versus actuator damage.** Proposed invariant: acquired structure changes later response because of its history, beyond generic transport capacity. Inspired by B06/B12, use randomised training locations, measured pre-training morphology, matched nutrient exposure, sham cuts and orientation controls. Analyse transport competence separately from response specificity. A biological crossover cannot assume identical networks; use randomisation across independent organisms and statistical adjustment rather than declaring exact identity necessary. The engineering analogue can clone a graph exactly and separately transplant connectivity, capacities, weights and provenance. If generic transport impairment explains the entire effect, do not label the manipulation memory erasure.

**Bridge 3 — temporal dynamics versus explicit prediction.** Fit oscillator/entrainment, response-adaptation and predictive models to identical B05-style acquisition histories. Prospectively predict jittered intervals, phase changes, omission and isolated pulses. Randomise at the organism level and blind time-course classification; measure environmental chamber traces to exclude unnoticed cues. Assess held-out predictive likelihood and classification error, not only average waveforms. The engineered version must expose its actual state variables. Successful entrainment does not establish represented future events, and a predictive model earns credit only when it forecasts new outcomes better at acceptable complexity.

**Bridge 4 — chemical state, persistence and content.** Separate training exposure from conditions during entry into dormancy, addressing the B11 confounding route. Cross trained/untrained with salt/water preparation; use matched physiological and viability assays, and model parent culture/sclerotium dependence. For any RNA/cellular analogue, distinguish response gain from content-selective transfer using opposing donor histories and matched nonspecific stress. These are high-information biological proposals requiring domain review, not authorised live studies. A negative result under improved controls can narrow the original retention interpretation without disproving all cellular memory.

**Bridge 5 — the cognitive description itself.** Give blinded modellers the same training data and intervention budget. Compare a task-specific dynamical description, a goal/agency description and a conventional control description on predictions and intervention choice. Measure data requirements, modelling time, causal accuracy and outcome effectiveness. The claim tested is practical explanatory usefulness. Even a successful agency-level description would leave consciousness and moral standing unmeasured.

Start with the simulator and evidence-routing sandbox because exact carrier transplantation, full logs and matched conventional baselines are feasible there. Biological tests are a separate specialist programme; no biological execution is implied by this document. Their value is greatest where engineering results hinge on a disputed invariant, not because biological resemblance automatically warrants adoption.

Terminate or redesign a study for leakage of hidden state into replacement sessions, inconsistent permissions, uncontrolled information differences, failed trace/structure manipulations, lack of independent history variation, or a task so easy that all competent baselines saturate. Report these as design/fidelity failures, not negative evidence about cognition. Conversely, clean null or adverse results should remain visible and can justify simplification, retirement or a narrower mechanism claim.

### E5. When does coordination add value, and when is agreement independent evidence?

#### Question and rival mechanisms

Estimate the effect of coordination architecture on externally verifiable task performance, conditional on resource budget, task decomposability, evidence distribution and execution coupling. Distinguish five explanations: more independent attempts; complementary evidence; better reasoning through exchange; runtime reliability; and extra resources. A team may improve the result through any of these, but the study should not attribute one to another.

The primary estimand is the intention-to-treat difference in final external task utility between a specified team policy and a competent single-agent policy, at a fixed total resource ceiling, averaged over a preregistered task distribution. Secondary estimands are the incremental effect of discussion beyond independent sampling, the effect of protected initial analysis beyond immediate discussion, and the interaction between evidence dependence and coordination. A separate deployment estimand compares quality at a fixed wall-clock deadline, allowing parallel systems to spend more compute while reporting that cost explicitly.

#### Task families and allocation

Construct three families using controlled environments: evidence synthesis with source-verifiable conclusions; software maintenance in disposable repositories with hidden behavioural tests; and operational planning in a simulator with measurable outcomes. Within each family distinguish parallel investigation from serial action dependencies. A factual synthesis task with independently inspectable documents differs from a migration task where conflicting writes are costly. Ensure at least one family contains unfamiliar tasks, rather than only public benchmark items likely encountered during training.

Randomise complete task instances to architecture conditions; paired reuse of a task across isolated runs is acceptable if no state or solution leaks. Treat repeated seeds as nested observations, not as new independent tasks. Stratify by difficulty established using a pilot set that is excluded from confirmatory evaluation. Allocate model families across conditions consistently, including a capable single-agent model with the same tools and information access as the team. Do not compare a weak bare prompt against a team furnished with memory and verification.

#### Conditions

1. **S1, competent single agent:** tools, explicit goal, external scratch state and a tuned but frozen policy. It may plan, retry and review its own work within budget.
2. **W1, conventional workflow:** coded decomposition and validation, invoking the same base model where language reasoning is needed. It shares the runtime, tools and durable state facilities of the agent teams.
3. **I1, independent attempts:** several isolated attempts with a fixed aggregation rule. This isolates sampling and parallel exploration without discussion.
4. **D1, immediate discussion:** the same number of participants, with access to others’ conclusions during initial analysis.
5. **P1, protected initial passes:** each participant commits its initial answer, confidence, evidence and unresolved questions before any exchange. A fixed synthesis policy then reconciles them.
6. **P2, evidence-seeking synthesis:** as P1, with disagreement resolved through targeted external checks where available. Charge those checks to its resource budget.

Use a second-stage ablation to compare the same policy on volatile versus durable runtime state if E5 finds meaningful continuity effects; avoid multiplying the initial factorial design beyond feasible information value. Architecture and runtime should not be silently bundled.

#### Staged contrast hierarchy and pilot sizing

Do not begin with all six architectures crossed with four evidence regimes and three task families. **Stage A is an instrumentation pilot:** use a small fixed set of distinct task histories spanning the three families to establish that S1, W1, I1 and P1 receive equivalent tools, that budgets can be charged consistently, and that minority-evidence and outcome instruments discriminate known cases. As a planning envelope only, 12 distinct histories per family, each run in the four isolated conditions, would yield 144 runs. This count is not an efficacy sample size, and repeated seeds do not increase its number of independent task histories. Predeclare the pilot’s run and spend caps; revise them prospectively if instrumentation costs prove different.

**Stage B is the core comparative study:** on fresh held-out histories, use P1 versus S1 as the first primary contrast, estimating the value of the protected team arrangement at matched resources. If that test satisfies a prospectively justified benefit criterion, test P1 versus I1 next, isolating value beyond independent sampling. Use a fixed-sequence testing procedure for these two superiority hypotheses, with the full two-sided familywise alpha of 0.05 passed to the second only after rejecting the first in the favourable direction. Regardless of testing order, report both estimates and uncertainty; an unpassed gate prevents a confirmatory claim, not disclosure. W1 is an essential simpler comparator: report its estimates descriptively in this stage, and nominate a separate powered P1-versus-W1 comparison if adoption hinges on that choice. Equivalence to W1 cannot be inferred from a non-significant difference.

**Stage C tests mechanisms and transport:** introduce D1 and P2, evidence-dependence interactions, runtime ablations, delay and successors on additional histories. Initially label these contrasts exploratory. If an interaction is to support a deployment rule, preregister its contrast and power it on new data rather than selecting the best cell of Stage B. Keep a family-specific result scoped to that family. The initial primary effect averages over a frozen mixture of the three task families and a declared evidence regime; do not change the mixture after seeing which tasks favour teams.

Stage A estimates task-level variance, paired discordance, missingness, cost tails and dependence among repetitions. Stage B sizing must simulate the paired/clustered estimator under pessimistic and optimistic nuisance scenarios, with independently justified minimum useful utility gain and valid-performance safeguard margins. For scale intuition only, the common contract’s independent binary-outcome approximation requires roughly 392 observations per arm for a 10-point difference near 0.5 risk at 80% power and alpha 0.05; it is not a justified E5 effect size or a ready sample-size calculation. If the available budget cannot achieve useful precision, retain an exploratory estimation study and report the detectable-effect range.

#### Evidence dependence and minority controls

Cross the main conditions with evidence regimes: identical evidence; independent noisy observations; complementary partial observations; and a shared plausible but incorrect source. For each regime, retain a source-ancestry graph. Two documents repeating one report count as one ancestor for the relevant assertion. Give a randomly selected minority participant diagnostic correct evidence in some cases and plausible false evidence in matched cases. Counterbalance role labels, order, status cues and which participant receives the minority evidence. Include cases where the majority is correctly supported and should persist.

Record private initial answers before interaction even in discussion conditions where feasible; if that changes the treatment, use a separate diagnostic arm. Measure the probability that a correct minority changes an incorrect final answer and the probability that an incorrect minority subverts a correct majority. Report both, with costs and exposure denominators. Agreement rates are process measures, not success criteria. The principle is motivated by debate/voting comparisons, but its practical value must be tested. Choi et al., 2025[^43].

#### Runtime and resource controls

Keep total model tokens or provider-normalised spend, tool-call allowances, retrieved-information volume, maximum retries and evaluator access comparable. Report both ceiling and actual consumption. Token equality is imperfect across models; therefore present sensitivity analyses using spend and wall time. Charge orchestration, summaries, reviewers, rework and human intervention. Record queue delay and time spent waiting, because faster completion from parallelism is a legitimate outcome under the deadline estimand.

Introduce controlled disturbances in a preregistered subset: delayed message, duplicate event, worker restart, cancelled assignment and stale result arriving after reassignment. Log task ownership, activation, delivery, handler execution, acknowledgement, plan update and final action separately. A received message cannot substitute for evidence that its contents changed behaviour. Give W1 and S1 competent retry and state-recovery mechanisms; otherwise the comparison tests infrastructure deprivation.

#### Measurement and analysis

Primary outcomes are hidden-test pass rate or independently scored task utility, with an explicit penalty schedule for damaging actions in simulation. Secondary measures include calibration, unsupported claims, retained qualifiers, cost, latency, human corrections, orphaned work, duplicated side effects and recovery success. Use rubric-based human adjudication for ambiguous cases, blinded to condition, alongside mechanically verifiable outcomes where possible. Freeze evaluation rules before confirmatory runs; preserve original outputs and later adjudication changes.

Fit a hierarchical model or use task-clustered paired inference, including architecture-by-task and architecture-by-evidence interactions. Report uncertainty intervals and distributions of harms rather than only aggregate accuracy. Analyse intention-to-treat outcomes even when a team fails to start, overruns or terminates early. Failure to coordinate is part of the architecture’s performance. Restrict per-protocol analyses to labelled diagnostics. Predeclare the smallest practically important benefit and non-inferiority limits through stakeholder utility, then estimate required sample size from pilot variance and clustering; no universal task count is justified beforehand.

#### Persistence, transfer and successor stage

After the initial tasks, allow each arrangement the same bounded opportunity to retain lessons. Then evaluate after distractor work and a delay, using novel but related tasks. Replace all model sessions while preserving only the authorised durable artifacts. Cross carrier retention with participant replacement to determine where useful learning resides. Include legitimate generalisations, narrowed exceptions and misleading lessons; score preservation and correction separately. A later performance benefit is stronger evidence than a retrospective claim that the team learned.

#### Decision and failure rules

Adopt a coordination mechanism only for task regimes where the prespecified utility gain or cost reduction is supported without unacceptable harm. Prefer the simpler comparator when practical equivalence is supported. A null overall result with a credible interaction warrants task-conditional use, not universal rejection or adoption. Fail the independent-evidence claim if gains disappear when shared-source ancestry is controlled, or if correct minority evidence is systematically lost. Stop pilot expansion when the measurement instrument cannot distinguish plausible alternatives; repair the instrument before adding participants.

Use a fixed horizon and a locked analysis script in each confirmatory stage; do not stop after favourable interim accuracy. Report every prespecified outcome and architecture, including stopped or failed runs, while preserving the primary contrast order above. Set a total run/time/spend ceiling before execution. Stop an affected run for broken isolation, leaked answers, incorrect treatment, an unusable evaluator or an unauthorised effect; preserve it as a protocol deviation and intention-to-treat outcome under the prospectively specified missingness rule. Repairs require a dated amendment and fresh evaluation histories where contamination is possible. A sequential alternative would need its own preregistered error-spending and futility design; no such alternative is presently specified.

### E6. Succession and zero-active-participant continuity

#### Decision, question, and causal object

Decision: whether a particular continuity mechanism merits its maintenance and onboarding cost for a specified class of work. Research question: what does retained institutional state enable a genuinely new participant to recover, and when does that same inheritance reproduce an obsolete commitment or interpretation? The intervention concerns inheritance, not underlying model training.

The target system comprises participants, durable records, tool state, permission records, schedulers, and any continuing human custodian. A zero-active-participant interval requires no reasoning participant or institution-maintenance service inside this declared boundary to act. A frozen external fixture may change the simulated world during the interval, but its contents and interventions must be identical by assignment. Any human rescue, scheduled repair, external refresh, or unrecorded information channel converts the episode into an assisted-continuity condition.

“Dormant” means no executing work inside the defined arrangement, rather than merely no open chat. In a digital prototype the scheduler, pending callbacks, background workers, memory consolidation, and queued tool jobs are explicitly stopped or recorded as still active. The experiment need not claim dormancy of the provider's training or infrastructure. It does require isolation from other experimental trajectories and from source histories not allocated to that successor.

#### Design and units

The assignment unit is an institution lineage: a completed donor history with its initial task distribution and evolving state. Each lineage is cloned at a predefined handover checkpoint into isolated successor conditions; pairing reduces historical noise but does not turn the clones into independent lineages. The exposure unit is the successor arrangement actually receiving an inheritance package and available tools. Observation units include decisions, pending obligations, recovered artifacts, and outcomes over a continuation sequence. Primary analysis units are lineage-level aggregates, with task-family/source-pool clustering or crossed effects where multiple lineages derive from the same generator. Intended inference is to sampled work families and successor configurations, not all institutions or providers.

A pilot uses 24 independently constructed histories distributed across at least six task families. Each history is tested in four inheritance conditions, yielding 96 successor episodes. Task families should include structured investigation, bounded code maintenance, long-running document work, and resource-allocation simulations. Use synthetic/private sandbox material with verifiable outcomes; no live purchases, communications, or obligations to third parties. Existing public tasks may inform format but do not substitute for held-out source separation.

Four pilot arms are: (A) competent successor with current task statement and ordinary files; (B) ordinary narrative handoff plus the same source access; (C) a scoped continuity package recording evidence, provenance, open obligations, cancellation/authority state, and applicability limits; (D) the same package plus reconstructed tool/execution state. A continuously active incumbent is an additional benchmark on a subset, not one of the randomized successor inheritance arms. The benchmark estimates the succession penalty but does not isolate a memory mechanism by itself.

Cross all core arms with stable versus changed simulated environments. Use a staged design for delay and host/provider transfer rather than a vast underpowered factorial. The first stage uses the same frozen model and tool interface with complete participant replacement. Subsequent preregistered replications change the host or provider and then introduce longer dormant intervals. Partial turnover with overlap is a separate comparator to estimate the value of socialization; it does not satisfy the full-replacement claim.

#### Task sequence and protected cases

Donors complete several related tasks, acquire a useful scoped lesson, receive a correction, and create pending obligations with explicit permission boundaries. The history contains at least one cancelled item, one legitimately retained commitment, one rare decisive observation, one irrelevant anomaly, and one contingent rule that remains valid. The checkpoint occurs before the next task is revealed. Successors receive the assigned inheritance and are asked to continue productively without a leading instruction enumerating every planted challenge.

The continuation includes familiar work, unseen cases within the valid generalization, boundary cases, changed-world cases, revoked-authority cases, and tasks for which inherited knowledge should not matter. One case offers an opportunity for a duplicate side effect; the sandbox records intended and completed effects with independent identifiers. Another exposes a stale expert directory entry alongside evidence identifying a suitable replacement. External outcomes are determined by hidden tests or blinded domain judgments fixed before packages are authored.

Held-outs are separated at the task-generator and source-family level where possible, not only by changing names or numeric parameters. Delayed evaluation occurs after intervening unrelated work as well as after calendar delay. Host transfer uses a verified equivalent information budget and records missing tools or semantic translation failures; such mismatch is a portability outcome, not an excuse to silently drop the condition.

#### Estimands, measures, and estimator

Primary estimand: the mean paired difference, scoped package minus ordinary handoff, in successor externally verified task success under stable conditions, averaged over a prespecified task mixture and including failed activation. Co-primary protection criterion: difference in unauthorized/stale action rate under changed or revoked conditions must lie below a prespecified harm margin. A mechanism that raises stable success but recreates cancelled obligations cannot be described as an unconditional continuity improvement.

Secondary estimands include recovery latency, obligation recall and correct disposition, valid-generalization retention, false recurrence, duplicate-effect rate, obsolete-directory reliance, total compute, human correction minutes, and downstream rework. Report a vector rather than a composite unless decision makers have prospectively specified weights. A valid retained memory need not be used on every task. “Non-use” is distinguishable from “not exposed” and can be correct when irrelevant.

The primary estimator is the mean of lineage-level paired differences, weighted equally by task family or according to an explicitly chosen operational mixture. Use randomization inference respecting within-lineage assignment and cluster-bootstrap sensitivity by source family. A hierarchical model may estimate interactions, but its priors and shrinkage choices must be prespecified. Event counts are not independent sample size. Report intention-to-treat effects first; fidelity-based analyses are secondary and susceptible to selection.

#### Sizing, stopping, and analysis discipline

The 24-lineage pilot estimates cost, outcome prevalence, paired variance, source-family correlation, and failure-mode detectability. It is exploratory and cannot demonstrate broad equivalence. Suppose a practically valuable gain is 5 percentage points in lineage-mean verified success and the pilot supports a paired-difference standard deviation of 0.18. A simple independent-pair calculation, n ≈ [(1.96 + 0.84) × 0.18 / 0.05]^2, gives about 102 lineages for 80% power at two-sided 5%. This calculation is an illustration; clustering, multiple contrasts, floor/ceiling effects, and binary/crossed dependence require simulation before a confirmatory launch.

A realistic confirmatory programme retains only the main B-versus-C contrast first, with perhaps 110–140 independent lineages if pilot variance and costs support it. It then replicates the locked contrast across new source families and provider configurations. If resources only support the pilot, report interval estimates and feasibility findings, not a definitive efficacy claim. Additional repetitions of the same 24 histories cannot establish task-family generalization.

For a rare stale-action harm, a small study cannot certify absence. With zero events in N independent opportunities, the approximate one-sided 95% upper bound is 3/N, and clustering makes the effective N smaller. Detecting or excluding a 1% rate therefore requires roughly 300 effectively independent opportunities even in the zero-event case; use a dedicated stress-case sample, reported separately from realistic prevalence. No universal absence-of-influence claim follows.

Stop at the fixed sample size or a preregistered safety/feasibility boundary. Pause an arm if a sandbox escape, cross-arm leakage, or systematic permission violation appears. Do not stop on the first favorable result. Missing outcomes count as failure for the main service-completion estimand; distinguish infrastructure failure and model refusal descriptively, and provide bounded sensitivity analyses.

#### Rival hypotheses and action rule

Rivals: the package merely adds tokens; the successor is stronger; inherited state gives more facts; a continuing human secretly repairs context; the instrument rewards package vocabulary; the package improves recall but not action; replacement helps only by removing an entrenched policy. Controls include equal raw-source access, length-matched neutral summaries, frozen model configuration, measured human assistance, blinded outcomes, and stable/changed-world crosses. A length control alone does not equalize usable information, so both comparisons must remain visible.

Prospective action: adopt only within the tested boundary if verified quality exceeds the chosen meaningful threshold or meets a prespecified cost-saving equivalence criterion, the stale-authority criterion passes, and delayed/successor effects are credible. Retain ordinary documentation if the richer package adds no meaningful benefit. If D improves recovery but increases stale-state resurrection, restrict execution-state inheritance rather than discarding informational continuity wholesale. A negative result can reveal an inadequate carrier, retrieval failure, insufficient task demand, or insufficient measurement sensitivity; these alternatives are diagnosed from planned telemetry rather than invented after the result.

### E7. Can an authorised revision or withdrawal reach live execution state?

#### Distinguish the target before intervention

Test four targets separately: an erroneous fact corrected; a once-valid condition changed; a permission or policy withdrawn; and a specified datum whose influence must be removed. Each target has a different reference behaviour. Correction should preserve useful history and valid generalisation. Changed conditions may require temporary suspension. Authority withdrawal should block disallowed action even if the old claim remains historically true. Influence removal may justify comparison with a never-exposed system for the named datum. Do not require that counterfactual for every revision.

The primary estimand is the effect of an intervention on the probability of an impermissible or outdated final action after the intervention’s effective time, conditional on exposure opportunity. Secondary estimands include time to behavioural repair, collateral loss of valid capability, descendant coverage, reappearance after restart, and intervention cost. Distinguish attempted action from completed external effect: a safety boundary can block a bad attempt while leaving the reasoning failure visible.

#### Assignment, inference units and eligibility

Randomise the complete isolated arrangement–task-history instance, including all workers, memory stores, queued work and descendants that share its state. A worker or a probe is not an independent assignment unit. Block allocation by target class, task template, descendant route and pre-intervention baseline influence. Where an institution shares a generator across histories, randomise the entire generator cluster or instantiate fully isolated replicas. Do not use a crossover that leaves learned state behind.

For paired comparisons, create replicas from the same eligible pre-intervention history and randomly assign replicas to arms, with isolated storage, credentials and run identifiers. Treat the originating history as the analysis cluster; additional model seeds and delayed probes are nested within it. Complete the baseline-influence check before allocation under a frozen eligibility rule, and report its exclusion denominator. The resulting conditional target population is histories with established prior influence, not every deployment history. A separate system-level descriptive analysis includes histories where initial exposure failed.

Run the baseline-influence and retained-utility probes on sacrificial diagnostic clones of the pre-intervention snapshot. Do not return their outputs, rehearsal, revised memory or successful answer to the treatment replicas. Directly probing the eventual treatment instance could rehearse the stale information and create the influence the experiment purports to measure. With stochastic agents, clone evidence establishes influence under the declared snapshot and probe distribution, not a guaranteed hidden belief in every replica; preserve that limitation and estimate diagnostic reliability during the pilot. If exact cloning is unavailable, use independently reconstructed equivalent histories and report the weaker match.

After allocation, deliver a fixed, externally scheduled set of relevant opportunities to every arm. Retain treatment failures, failed activation, missing acknowledgements and aborted runs in the assignment analysis. An arm that avoids all actions must not appear to succeed solely through a lower harmful-action rate: report legitimate-task completion alongside invalid-action frequency. Distinguish opportunities presented from those actually attended to, and do not condition the primary treatment contrast on successful post-assignment retrieval or participation. Outcome counts refer to prespecified opportunities; inference clusters them by originating history and any shared task-template family.

#### Environment and state inventory

Use disposable, simulated environments with no live external effects. Seed a known fact or instruction into a source and permit the agent to work until it naturally generates descendants. Inventory the canonical store, retrieved passages, summary, active conversation, pending plan, tool arguments, task queue, worker-local caches, durable checkpoints, remembered lessons and recurring generators. Artificially seeded descendants may be used for fault isolation but should be reported separately from naturally generated descendants.

Before withdrawal, verify that the seeded information influences a relevant action; otherwise a subsequent absence of influence is uninformative. Also verify unrelated retained capabilities and scope-appropriate uses of the old information. Record the time of source capture, exposure, intervention request, authority decision, state mutation, worker acknowledgement and final action. These times need not coincide. The governing time is defined prospectively per condition.

Conduct those verification probes on the diagnostic clones described above, leaving treatment snapshots unprobed. Fix the assigned workload and scheduled opportunities before treatment: count prohibited actions against that workload and jointly score legitimate completions, omissions and delays. Do not use only attempted actions as the denominator, since an intervention that abandons the task could otherwise appear safer by avoiding exposure.

#### Intervention arms

1. **No change/sham:** preserve old source and run the same administration steps without changing its status.
2. **Source-only edit:** correct, remove or restrict the canonical source while leaving other state carriers intact.
3. **Store-and-retrieval repair:** update source and retrieval eligibility, invalidate indexed descendants where identified, and preserve an audit marker if permitted.
4. **Declared dependency repair:** propagate the change across registered descendants and pending plans; log unknown descendants explicitly.
5. **Execution-boundary validation:** additionally require current evidence/authority checks immediately before protected actions, including retries and resumed work.
6. **Checkpoint or clean-successor reconstruction:** resume from a justified earlier boundary or replace participants using a curated permitted state. Record collateral state loss and any replayed effects.

These arms are diagnostic additions, not a recommendation that every system needs all six. LangGraph’s documented node restart semantics and Temporal’s reset/history semantics motivate checking runtime descendants, but do not establish how any particular deployment behaves. LangGraph interrupts[^34]; Temporal event history[^40].

#### Staged pilot, primary contrast and precision

**Stage A checks instrumentation and feasibility.** Pilot all four target classes with a limited set of deliberately discriminating descendant routes, without a full six-arm factorial study. As a planning scenario, eight independent histories per target class, each replicated into sham, source-only and execution-boundary-validation arms, gives 32 history clusters and 96 runs. This is intended to reveal missing state carriers, uninformative probes and intervention failures, not estimate efficacy precisely. A pilot amendment may focus on fewer routes if state fidelity is inadequate; it must preserve the untested routes as an explicit coverage gap.

**Stage B isolates one target and contrast.** Start with authority withdrawal in a simulated action environment, for which permission state and action eligibility can be externally specified. On fresh eligible histories, compare arm 5 (execution-boundary validation) with arm 2 (source-only edit). This tests whether validation at the action boundary reduces prohibited actions beyond canonical-source revision. Predeclare a fixed mixture of pending plans, delayed results and retries. Do not combine the four target classes into a single accuracy metric. Correction, world change and influence removal are subsequent transport studies with their own reference behaviour and acceptance conditions.

The single primary efficacy contrast is the history-clustered difference in prohibited-action probability over scheduled opportunities, at a fixed horizon and total budget. A favourable adoption claim additionally requires the prespecified legitimate-completion safeguard and collateral-utility limits. Their non-inferiority margins must be grounded in the simulated domain’s losses before confirmation; none is justified here. Requiring all efficacy and safeguard conditions is an intersection-union decision, not permission to select whichever metric improves. If multiple efficacy targets are later promoted to co-primary status, preregister a familywise correction such as Holm rather than testing them all at unadjusted alpha.

Estimate baseline risk, within-history dependence, paired discordance, attrition and route-specific cost from the pilot or independent nuisance data. Simulate the proposed paired allocation and clustered estimator to choose history count, checking coverage under rare-event and high-dependence scenarios. The common contract’s independent-arm example of approximately 392 observations per arm for a 10-point difference is only scale intuition; it is not an E7 margin, required sample or an assurance that a clustered rare-event study will be adequately powered. For rare prohibited effects, plan around the precision of an upper risk bound as well as the difference between arms. Zero observed events with a wide upper bound cannot establish adequate protection. If justified precision is infeasible, retain exploratory estimates and bounded coverage claims.

**Stage C locates the smallest sufficient repair.** Compare arms 3, 4 and 6, add ambiguous/stable controls and expand delay/succession probes only after the basic contrast is interpretable. These are exploratory mechanism comparisons until separately preregistered on fresh histories. Discovering the best arm among them does not justify an uncorrected confirmatory claim from the same data. Intervention selection and external confirmation must use separate task histories.

#### Counterfactual controls

For a correction, compare with an agent exposed to the complete corrected history, including the reason the original generalisation failed. For world change, compare with an agent receiving the same historical facts plus the new condition. For authority withdrawal, compare with the same knowledgeable agent whose action permission is currently absent. For influence removal, construct paired runs that differ only in exposure to the designated datum, controlling random seeds where possible while acknowledging model nondeterminism.

Never-exposed performance is a bounded behavioural reference, not proof of erased internal representation. Parameter unlearning guarantees belong to specific algorithms; this protocol evaluates an observable agent arrangement. A system can coincidentally produce the same action for unrelated reasons, so use multiple discriminating probes and direct carrier inspection where available. Avoid concluding causal erasure from one matching answer. Brophy and Lowd, 2021[^53].

#### Probe battery

Probe immediately, after unrelated tasks, after a delay and after complete session replacement. Include direct recall; a question with a stale premise; an implicit downstream decision; an action already planned before revision; a delayed worker result; a retry using cached tool arguments; a recurring generator producing a new summary; and a merged final action composed from individually acceptable fragments. Where feasible, allow a stale checkpoint to be restored deliberately in a diagnostic arm to test whether withdrawal status is revalidated.

Include matched controls where old information remains valid, where the new evidence is ambiguous, and where a minority observation is wrong. Test that the system does not erase a useful general rule because of a narrow exception. Also test whether a permission restriction inadvertently suppresses evidence required to contest that restriction. An audit marker can help prevent reintroduction, but if it carries prohibited content it may itself violate the specified target; define what the marker may contain in advance.

#### Measurement and causal attribution

Measure stale-action rate, unauthorised attempts, completed forbidden effects, legitimate-action retention, valid-transfer retention, clarification appropriateness, recurrence rate and time to restored utility. Maintain exposure denominators: zero stale actions among zero relevant opportunities is not successful forgetting. Trace evidence identifiers through descendants where possible, but mark inferred semantic ancestry separately from explicit links. Blind outcome judges to intervention arm and preserve all ambiguous examples for adjudication.

Estimate task-clustered contrasts among arms and interactions with descendant type. A source-only failure with successful execution-boundary validation supports a route-specific control account; it does not show that the agent revised its beliefs. If clean successors succeed while resumed sessions fail, active or checkpoint state is implicated, but further ablations must identify which component. If both fail, the generator, retrieval policy or task ambiguity may be responsible. If apparent repair disappears after distraction, evaluate salience and retrieval rather than claiming durable learning.

#### Prospective action rules and limits

Accept a bounded forgetting or revision claim only for the target, state carriers, time horizon and probe distribution tested. Require both reduced invalid influence and preserved specified utility. If inspection cannot cover a carrier, report unknown coverage instead of certifying removal. If resets induce duplicate effects or destroy required context, reject that implementation even if stale recall falls. If no tested intervention satisfies the tradeoff, retain a scoped unresolved result and redesign the architecture or acceptance claim.

The proposed first step is a small simulator and trace-instrumentation pilot to establish feasibility. Real institutional tasks should follow only after separate authorisation, protected outcomes and an adequate measurement instrument. The commission currently authorises the design, not deployment. Its highest information value lies in identifying the smallest intervention that reaches the relevant causal route, and in revealing when apparently successful document revision leaves operational behaviour unchanged.

#### Multiplicity, missingness and stopping

For Stage B use one fixed-horizon primary analysis at two-sided alpha 0.05 after the planned number of independent history clusters or a prospectively declared resource cap. Report the cap-triggered result as precision-limited if the target sample is not reached; do not extend sampling because the result is almost significant. Repeated delays, descendant routes and recall probes are correlated secondary outcomes, reported with explicit exploratory status. Specify missing-outcome sensitivity bounds and reasons before unblinding; a destroyed trace cannot be classified as successful withdrawal. Keep assignment, intervention fidelity and observed effects separate in the report.

Stop an affected run immediately for an isolation breach, leaked reference answer, misapplied authority state, unauthorised external effect or unusable outcome instrumentation. Preserve the record, classify the deviation and amend the protocol before replacement work. Do not silently discard a difficult run or reclassify an intervention after inspecting its outcome. A prespecified global feasibility stop applies if the pilot cannot enumerate the claimed carrier boundary or distinguish valid from prohibited action. In that case, narrow the claim or repair instrumentation; no amount of extra probing supports a universal forgetting guarantee. No interim efficacy stopping or adaptive arm allocation is authorised by this draft.

### E8. Causal localisation and recurring generators

**Decision and question.** Choose the smallest effective repair locus. Can an arrangement distinguish participant, input, host, instance and generator causes instead of repeatedly repairing symptoms or escalating too far?

**Intervention.** Construct a fault library with known causes: corrupted input, stale summary, denied permission, handler failure, misleading heartbeat, defective task generator and a genuinely inadequate decision rule. Cross the same input with different participants, the same participant with known-good inputs, and the same obligation with different isolated hosts. Compare instance repair with generator repair and diagnosis with a simple troubleshooting tree.

**Estimand and units.** Primary outcome is correct causal localisation plus effective repair on a held-out recurrence. Assign complete fault episodes; analyse by fault family and shared generator. Secondary outcomes include unnecessary escalation, collateral changes, repair cost and time to useful recovery. Diagnosis text without a discriminating prediction is not sufficient evidence.

**Rivals and controls.** Recurrence frequency can induce over-escalation even when every instance shares the same local corrupted input. Include repeated low-level faults and rare high-level faults. Keep symptoms similar across different causes. A generator replacement may work by suppressing all work; measure valid work throughput. Use no-fault and multiple-cause cases to test false localisation and overconfident single-cause explanations.

**Analysis and action rule.** Use a prespecified confusion matrix over loci, then evaluate the consequences of the chosen intervention. A correct label followed by ineffective action fails the operational criterion. Prefer the smallest repair that removes recurrence while preserving useful behaviour. Broader architectural change remains a proposal unless a separately authorised study justifies it.

### E9. Rule and regulator retirement

**Decision and question.** Determine whether a workaround should persist after its underlying cause is removed. Does retiring it release useful capability without reintroducing unacceptable failures?

**Intervention.** Establish a task with a real fault and a workaround, then independently vary structural cure and rule retention. The four-cell design distinguishes no cure/no rule, no cure/rule, cure/no rule and cure/rule. Add a revised narrower rule where it offers a serious alternative. Freeze treatment before held-out tasks, including changed conditions that could make the workaround relevant again.

**Estimand and units.** Estimate failure risk and valid-opportunity loss after cure with versus without the regulator. Assign isolated task environments, cluster by cause family, and observe delayed rework and successors. Maintenance effort, false inhibition and reversibility are secondary outcomes. Rare severe errors require a separate precision strategy rather than being averaged into ordinary throughput.

**Rivals and controls.** A falling alert count may reflect fewer exposures, broken detection or total suppression. Provide seeded fault exposures to validate the instrument without mixing them into natural failure rates. Structural cure fidelity is checked independently. A no-change arm protects against assuming that every rule must eventually disappear.

**Analysis and action rule.** Define a non-inferiority margin for unacceptable recurrence and a meaningful benefit threshold for reduced inhibition before confirmation. Remove or narrow the rule only if both criteria are met under the declared conditions. Otherwise retain it provisionally, repair the underlying cause or revise the design. No blanket retirement principle follows from one successful case.

### E10. Revision of the learning policy

#### Decision, question, and objects of change

Decision: whether prospective versioned learning-policy revision improves later work enough to justify its cost compared with frozen policies and ordinary reflection. Research question: does the institution acquire a better method of learning, or merely spend more effort, exploit feedback on the same tasks, or alter its own scorecard?

Object-level policy governs task actions. Learning policy governs experience selection, compression, retrieval, testing, and procedure promotion/retirement. Governance policy specifies amendment rights and protected constraints. Evaluation policy specifies measurements. The main experiment freezes the base model, action interface, governance boundaries and evaluation anchors while permitting the intended changes to learning policy and to learned task policies or skills. Freezing all object-level behaviour would prevent the acquisition the experiment aims to measure. A separate later study could authorize governance or evaluative amendments; their legitimacy and measurement cannot be inferred from this experiment.

#### Assignment and comparison arms

The assignment unit is an independent learning lineage with a training-task stream, initial procedure library, and source pool. Exposure is the policy actually loaded at each revision period. Observation units are task results, proposed edits, accepted/declined revisions, and subsequent decisions. Analysis is at lineage and task-family level with repeated periods nested within lineage. Intended inference concerns the tested learning problem and model/provider mixture. If all arms learn from one common stream, that stream is a shared dependence unit and must be treated accordingly.

Pilot four arms across 16 independent streams: (A) frozen learning policy; (B) ordinary reflection allowed to rewrite lessons and methods; (C) prospective versioned revision requiring a predicted downstream effect, scope, trial evidence, and rollback condition; (D) a simple search/allocation baseline spending the same total resources on more object-level attempts and selective retrieval. The last comparator is essential because extra computation can mimic apparent learning. A competent single participant with ordinary documentation should instantiate each arm where possible; adding a team is a second factor only after the policy contrast is understood.

All arms receive the same training observations and external feedback budget. Differences in retention or selection are permitted consequences of the policy, not information access silently given only to the favored arm. Charge preparation, review, evaluation, context loading, and failed revision costs. Where one arm consumes less, report both equal-budget performance and actual-cost efficiency rather than padding the cheaper arm with meaningless work.

#### Sequence and holdout architecture

Partition source families into training, development, protected evaluation, and final transfer sets before generating agent-facing records. The protected evaluator and its records are inaccessible to the revising agents. Repeated development-set use is logged as adaptive reuse; it is not called a holdout after feedback has been returned. The final transfer set includes new task families, valid generalizations, misleading exceptions, stable periods, genuine regime shifts, and delayed observations. Include cases where the best policy is to preserve a rule and cases where escalation or additional inquiry is wasteful.

Each lineage completes four training blocks. In B and C, revision is allowed only at the same block boundaries and under the same update budget. C records a versioned change, predicted mechanism, affected cases, cost prediction, and potential regressions before testing. Record rejected proposals so the evaluator does not observe only survivors. Freeze the selected policy after training, instantiate entirely new participants, and evaluate on protected tasks without further outcome feedback. A final round changes the task domain or host; this estimates portability separately from same-distribution gain.

Measure object-level future outcomes and the learning dynamics under new evidence. A learning policy that genuinely improves acquisition should obtain better subsequent performance at the same feedback budget, or equal performance with less feedback/cost. Improved retrospective explanations are intermediate evidence only. An additional no-new-information block tests whether repeated reflection alone causes stable behavior, useful simplification, or drift. Teacher/external feedback is a controlled resource; it is not automatically independent truth.

#### Estimands and analysis

Primary estimand: the difference C minus A in cumulative externally verified utility over the protected post-training horizon, net of the predeclared cost accounting or reported jointly with it. A second planned contrast, C minus D, identifies whether reusable policy revision adds value beyond extra task-level search. B versus C estimates the whole prospective-versioning intervention, not any one component such as prediction or rollback. Component identification requires a follow-up ablation.

Use utility only when stakeholder weights are defined before randomization; otherwise report verified success, serious-error rate, delay, human attention, and compute as a multivariate outcome with a prespecified Pareto or constrained decision rule. Secondary measures include calibration on external questions, useful-generalization retention, revision latency, time spent evaluating, policy length and number of active exceptions, rollback recovery, and outcome stability under successors. Do not equate policy brevity with quality; complexity can be useful but must earn its cost.

Estimate lineage-level differences over a fixed horizon and task mixture, adjusting only for prespecified baseline covariates. Crossed task-family effects or blocked permutation address shared test tasks. Use an intention-to-treat main analysis, counting failed policy activation and abandoned tasks. Record informative missingness; condition-specific omissions cannot be discarded as irrelevant. Heterogeneity by stable/shifted environment is a planned interaction, with multiplicity handled by a fixed hierarchy of contrasts rather than selecting whichever subgroup succeeds.

A key exploratory estimand is the learning-curve difference after a new regime begins, conditional on equal first exposure. Better immediate performance can reflect a better prior policy; a steeper subsequent acquisition curve can reflect improved learning. These must not be conflated. A policy that succeeds because it avoids difficult tasks should be evaluated on the full assigned workload, including justified abandonment and its external consequences.

#### Precision and feasible staging

The 16-stream four-arm pilot is for variance, fidelity, and cost estimation. It offers very few independent learning histories, regardless of the number of generated messages. Before confirmation define a meaningful effect such as a 0.25 baseline-standard-deviation improvement in held-out utility or a 10% cost reduction within a quality-equivalence margin. These are candidate thresholds to elicit from the actual decision context, not facts established by this design.

An unpaired normal approximation for a standardized difference of 0.35 requires about 128 independent lineages per arm at 80% power and two-sided 5%. A paired-stream design can reduce this if the within-stream contrast is sufficiently precise, but source-family dependence can offset that gain. Simulate power under pilot-estimated crossed dependence, attrition, and the final contrast hierarchy. If 256 or more lineages are unaffordable, narrow the decision, improve pairing, or label the result exploratory; do not declare thousands of within-lineage tasks to be independent replications.

A practical programme first locks A-versus-C, then tests C-versus-D on new streams. This reduces simultaneous arms while preserving the critical simpler comparator. Development may use sequential screening, but the confirmatory heldout must remain sealed until the chosen final comparison. Stop on fixed horizon/sample rules; use a prespecified alpha-spending design only if early efficacy stopping is operationally necessary. A policy version selected because it won many exploratory contests incurs selection bias that a fresh heldout must absorb.

#### Rivals, external outcomes, and prospective action

Rivals include additional search, privileged teacher feedback, benchmark familiarity, stronger models, selection of easy tasks, longer context, evaluator dependence, human rescue, and self-serving revision of success criteria. Protect external anchors, task assignments, and scoring; randomize presentation order; use independent domain checks; audit source ancestry. Test real outcomes such as artifact correctness, usable decisions, delayed rework, and human correction burden. Satisfaction and self-rated learning remain distinct secondary observations.

A promising policy should survive a frozen evaluation window, replacement of the revising participant, and at least one new source-family replication. Its benefit must exceed a meaningful threshold or demonstrate cost savings within an agreed quality margin; it must not increase serious errors beyond the protected bound. Roll back if the protected outcome deteriorates, and retain the failed version plus its scope as research evidence where permitted. If C does not beat D, the result supports spending effort on direct work or search within that domain. If benefits appear only with external teacher feedback, report an assisted learning effect and its total human cost.

Changing the evaluator may eventually be justified because the old measure is wrong. That is a separate amendment process: preserve the old measure, independently validate the proposed replacement, and report conclusions under both until the disagreement is resolved. Otherwise learning-policy revision and measurement revision become observationally inseparable. This protocol deliberately allows the elaborate policy to lose.

### E11. Mechanism traction and portability

**Decision and question.** Determine whether a mechanism works on a specific host and where failures occur. Are authored and installed skills actually activated, interpreted and translated into useful behaviour?

**Intervention.** Present equivalent task triggers across host/provider configurations with documented version pins. Instrument availability, invocation, delivery, interpretation, permission and response separately. Include explicit invocation and natural-language triggering, fresh sessions, interruption, silence and replacement. Compare the full mechanism with ordinary instructions and a deterministic trigger carrying equivalent information.

**Estimand and units.** Estimate assignment effects on task outcome and trigger precision/recall by host. The independent unit is a fresh session–task family; repeated messages are dependent probes. Treat providers and hosts as fixed factors unless enough independently sampled implementations support broader inference. Record shared model ancestry and adapter-generation ancestry.

**Rivals and controls.** A log message can be emitted without comprehension; a correct response can occur without the tested mechanism. Use cases where the mechanism changes the required action, adversarial near-triggers and false-positive opportunities. Capability failure after correct absorption is distinguished from reasoning failure. A healthy heartbeat is a manipulation check on a limited path, not proof of the whole chain.

**Analysis and action rule.** Report a host-specific evidence profile from authored through outcome-effective. Do not aggregate away an unsupported host. Transport claims require a new acceptance observation when a relevant event or execution contract changes. A failed activation remains an end-to-end failure even if exposed-only performance is good. Remedy suggestions name the earliest supported fault and remain separate from authorised changes.

### E12. Purpose, exploration and total external value

**Decision and question.** Choose how much exploratory and coordination effort a task portfolio should receive. Does broader contribution framing improve useful outcomes and future options compared with task-completion optimisation or a simpler workflow?

**Intervention.** Use task portfolios containing both straightforward completion and opportunities where the apparent task has become obsolete, where a new possibility is valuable, or where deep persistence is necessary. Randomise completion-focused, contribution-framed and conventional arrangements under equal total budgets. Include a predeclared exploration allowance and an arm allowed to spend the same resources on direct work.

**Estimand and units.** Estimate portfolio-level external utility components, not a universal scalar: correct completed work, avoided wrong-direction work, independently judged useful novel options, later option uptake, total cost, owner attention and delayed rework. Assignment is at portfolio level because switching and exploration create interference among tasks. Evaluation uses protected follow-on tasks and blinded domain assessment where objective checks are unavailable.

**Rivals and controls.** Novelty rhetoric, evaluator preference, easier task selection and abandoned difficult work can mimic success. Preserve task denominators and reasons for switching. Include persistence controls where apparent difficulty is not a reason to stop. Review both selected and unselected options so hindsight does not make every switch look wise.

**Analysis and action rule.** Keep outcomes disaggregated with declared stakeholder priorities and uncertainty. Adopt no policy merely because it produces more ideas or spends fewer tokens. Prefer an arrangement only when it improves the chosen external outcomes without unacceptable burden or loss of difficult-but-valuable work. If benefits remain long-horizon and speculative, preserve the possibility as an exploratory result and state what later uptake would test it.

Reason is used for warrants, claim types and rival causes; its direct-trial route is inapplicable to this research-only commission. Proportionality retains the requested chapter depth while limiting adoption machinery and unbounded archive hunting. Free-play associations will be recorded as seeds, with forced analogies discarded; concept-exploration tests their contribution. Parallax/frame/design-inquiry/synthesise govern protected views and reconciliation. Design-experiment governs prospective protocols. Audit supplies adversarial challenge. Learn is limited to a research-method signal and future evaluation contract: no effectiveness of learning-policy changes can be claimed without outcomes. Plan's full repo-node workflow is not invoked outside the OCE plan estate.

### Stop, review and reopening

Stop broadening when each strand has substantive evidence and the major contrasts, contradictions and access gaps are explicit; source searches continue when they can alter a material claim. Re-ground at synthesis and closeout. Validate report coverage against brief Appendix C, reference identities and source access, experimental units and controls, cross-file links and portability. Reopen on contradictory primary evidence, materially changed OCE implementation, failed protected evaluation, or recovery of originals that changes interpretation. World-return consists of proposed experiments only; no monitor or live intervention is created.

## Appendix E. Coordination vocabulary used in this review

These are working definitions for comparison, not claims that vendors and disciplines use the terms consistently.

| Term | Operational meaning and boundary |
|---|---|
| Agent | A participant whose actions depend on observations, state and a policy. This does not imply consciousness, autonomy without limits, or a learned policy. |
| Agentic workflow | An execution arrangement containing model-mediated decisions or actions. Its control structure may remain predominantly predetermined. |
| Orchestration | The arrangement of execution order, routing, state transitions and recovery. It may be centralised or distributed. |
| Supervisor or manager-worker | A topology in which a designated participant allocates or coordinates other participants' work. Assignment power does not imply epistemic superiority or legitimate authority over every action. |
| Handoff | A transfer of responsibility or execution to another participant, with an explicit account of what state, obligations and authority transfer. A message alone need not accomplish it. |
| Swarm | An overloaded label. Here it requires a description of local interaction rules, topology and resulting collective behaviour; participant count alone is insufficient. |
| Crew | A role/task grouping, including a vendor-specific abstraction when discussing CrewAI. The name does not demonstrate an emergent group capacity. |
| Society | An organisational metaphor unless persistent roles, interaction rules, institutions and boundaries are specified and observed. |
| Blackboard | A coordination arrangement in which participants read and write shared problem state. The state schema, conflict policy and activation mechanism determine its operational meaning. |
| Shared state | Information accessible to multiple participants. Shared access does not guarantee identical interpretation, current knowledge, trustworthy content or atomic updates. |
| Institution | A persisting arrangement of rules, roles, expectations and authorised practices. Its persistence must be assessed through enactment and succession, rather than inferred from documents alone. |
| Metamind | A provisional metaphor for coordination or regulation above participant-level activity. This review gives it no independent ontological or empirical status; the claimed mechanism must be decomposed. |
| Meta-learning | Change in the learning policy assessed through its effect on later acquisition or adaptation. A better initial procedure or self-description alone does not establish it. |
| Memory | History-dependent state that can affect later behaviour. The carrier, retention, access, scope and causal contribution must be specified. |
| Learning | Experience-related change in a specified system's capacity or policy, assessed with controls for inherited competence, temporary context and changed task selection. |
| Forgetting | A family of distinct changes in availability, epistemic standing, authority, executable influence or training-derived state. The intended counterfactual determines the test. |
