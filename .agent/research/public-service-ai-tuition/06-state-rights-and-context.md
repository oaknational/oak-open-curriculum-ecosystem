# State, rights and governed context

**Purpose:** make the public AI-enabled tuition service useful while keeping the information that informs it limited, intelligible, correctable and under appropriate authority. Preserve the wider research on continuity, governed forgetting and replaceable providers as a source of testable possibilities for OCE.

**Standing and evidence date:** reconstruction on 6 September 2026 of the specialist learner-modelling report of 5 September, the revised national-commons report of 4 September, the 4 August governed-context and personal-graph syntheses, and the 2 August governed-forgetting research launch. Present project instructions govern the tuition design. Wider architectures, national legislation proposals, research programmes and acceptance scenarios remain proposals; none has been implemented or empirically validated by this document. Legal references below identify the dated source basis and review questions; they are not a fresh legal assessment.

The educational rationale and subject profiles are in [02](02-education-and-teaching-knowledge.md) and [03](03-learner-experience-and-profiles.md). [04](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md) owns E7's complete estimand, comparators, allocation, measures and release decisions. [05](05-inclusion-and-human-service.md) owns functional access and the effective human service. [07](07-capabilities-bridges-and-proof.md) owns the general proof obligations between capabilities. [09](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md) owns national operating models and standards. This document owns the state semantics and operational contracts those documents use.

## 1. The service begins with the present activity

The reference service uses the current goal and task, the learner's present work, help already shown, declared settings and explicit uncertainty about what the evidence means. Optional continuity can preserve selected work and necessary exposure history. A cross-session inferred hypothesis is a separate intervention whose additional value must be earned through E7.

A strong no-derived-state service remains an available learner choice, an operational fallback, a causal comparator and a rollback target. It can use the same curriculum, subject profile, content/task pool, model, tools, interface, safeguards, educator control and approved learning-support repertoire as a state-conditioned service. It can use current-session context, learner or educator declarations, and the same minimum exposure history where necessary to resume work or avoid accidental repetition. It cannot consult a retained cross-session probabilistic judgement about mastery, misconception, strategy or ability.

Persistence brings additional error, correction, security, rights and feedback-loop costs. Where it supplies no worthwhile educational advantage, the no-derived-state service is preferred. A good next-answer predictor does not establish a useful teaching decision, and neither establishes durable learning.

The canonical distinctions are:

| Object | What it represents | Who or what supplies its authority |
|---|---|---|
| Domain intent | What is to be learned, including disciplinary practices and boundaries | Curriculum/domain owner and the applicable learner-goal/profile authority |
| Pedagogical knowledge | Subject- and context-valid explanations, tasks, representations and possible misconception families | Educators, subject expertise and bounded research evidence |
| Model of learning | A falsifiable process hypothesis about how learning may change | Research synthesis with stated conditions |
| Observation | What was attempted, answered, revised or delivered under specified conditions | Attributable event evidence |
| Declaration | A goal, preference, access choice or context supplied by a person | The identified declaring person within their authority |
| Optional learner hypothesis | What may presently be understood about a named task or construct | A versioned estimator and evidence; uncertainty remains explicit |
| Policy | The approved rule selecting among permitted moves | Named educational and service change authority |
| Action and exposure | What was actually delivered, with help and tool exposure | Observable execution and its provenance |
| Independent outcome | What the learner can later do unaided | Independently governed, construct-valid assessment |
| Service evidence and release | Whether evidence warrants a new service version | Separate evaluation, access, rights, safety and release decisions |

An episode may update a reversible hypothesis and select a permitted move. Independent assessment measures capability outside that teaching path. Service learning may propose a separately reviewed candidate version. These are different loops. Their interfaces must preserve origin, meaning, scope, permissions and versions.

## 2. State classes and the complete hypothesis contract

### 2.1 Classes with different persistence and uses

| Class | Examples | Retention and use |
|---|---|---|
| Ephemeral episode context | Current task, recent turns, present attempt, help shown | Shortest operational duration supporting coherence and current-task scaffolding; separately justified incident records have another purpose |
| Declared context | Chosen goal, language, display setting, communication need, desired pace | Informed choice and specified purpose; attributable, easily changed or reset; a choice is not a diagnosis |
| Observable educational history | Named tasks, assistance exposure, revisions, authorised formative checks | Minimum events for a defined period and purpose such as resumption or educator judgement; research use requires its own authority |
| Derived educational hypothesis | Probability about a named knowledge component, a strategy alternative, possible misconception or expected recall at a specified time | Ephemeral by default; cross-session retention only within E7-approved coverage and with expiry/revalidation; consulted only by named reversible teaching decisions |
| Attested achievement | Issuer-signed qualification, badge or demonstrated achievement with criteria | Learner-controlled disclosure with issuer, evidence, validity and status; technical verifiability is not educational equivalence |
| Excluded operational inference | Emotion, personality, diagnosis, SEND status, vulnerability, motivation, intelligence or future potential | The tuition service does not create or use these as adaptation state |

Ephemeral content can still be personal or safeguarding information. Persistent content is not permanent. Every retained class needs a purpose, accountable owner, access rule, retention trigger and deletion, restriction or archive disposition.

### 2.2 Minimum schema if a material hypothesis is permitted

| Field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis identifier | Stable audit identity, without turning the hypothesis into a human trait label |
| Construct and scope | Named knowledge component/task family, subject/profile, phase and context |
| State proposition | Probability distribution or ranked alternatives; `unknown` remains representable |
| Supporting and contrary evidence | Source-event references with assisted and unaided evidence separated |
| Observation conditions | Task, modality, language, access setting, assistance, timing and relevant context |
| Uncertainty | Confidence/posterior together with calibration cohort, coverage and known distribution-shift limits |
| Provenance and versions | Estimator, features, curriculum/profile, policy and task versions |
| Time | Creation, last challenge, last independent check and last update |
| Expiry or revalidation | A construct-appropriate rule, not one universal interval |
| Permitted effects | The specific reversible teaching moves that may consult the hypothesis |
| Excluded effects | Grading, placement, discipline, eligibility, diagnosis, employment and unvalidated cross-context use |
| Contestation | Learner/educator explanation, correction, reset, use restriction and deletion status |

The service must be able to represent uncertainty and contrary evidence without generating an unwarranted number. A named “mastery” field cannot make latent knowledge directly observed.

### 2.3 Time, correction and state horizons

A vocabulary-recall estimate, procedural fluency hypothesis, conceptual interpretation, chosen goal and qualification have different temporal dynamics. Expiry, decay and revalidation therefore need joint subject, learning-science, psychometric and privacy work. Test proposed horizons against policy-independent anchors and test the decision value of refreshing the state. Age alone is a poor general validity rule; a stable high-consequence constraint and a volatile preference should not share a half-life.

Stale state that repeatedly sends a learner to inappropriate work, reduces challenge, delays access or imposes repeated correction is evidence to shorten or remove persistence. Correction of a source event, contest of an interpretation, reset of optional state and deletion under an applicable right are separate operations.

### 2.4 Purpose-separated custody

These are logical boundaries, not a prescription for eight databases:

| Boundary | Permitted contents and users | Boundary that must hold |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and authority | Minimum identity, age/authority and institutional relations for authentication and rights administration | Identity administration does not become a pedagogy or inferred-state store |
| Episode context | Minimum current interaction for tuition and necessary safety functions | No automatic conversion into a lasting profile |
| Educational event ledger | Minimized observations and help exposure for approved purposes | No covert trait derivation or inclusion of secure formal outcome instruments |
| Declared settings | Goals and access choices for presentation and support | No inferred diagnosis from preference or accommodation |
| Derived state | Only the state and reversible decisions accepted through E7 | No grading, placement, discipline, eligibility, employer access or foundation-model training |
| Independent outcome enclave | Instruments and outcomes for the evaluator and authorised researchers | Isolated from the live policy and state estimator |
| Service evidence | Versioned process/outcome/subgroup/incident evidence for accountable change decisions | No direct live prompt, reward, routing, safeguard or weight update |
| Portable work and achievement | Learner-selected work, declarations and attested evidence | No hidden export of inferred learner state |

## 3. Useful hypothesis testing and its evidential limits

### 3.1 A task-grounded mechanism

State the learning goal; preserve the actual response; form a small set of subject-valid alternative explanations; include insufficient evidence and plausible access/interface failure; choose a low-burden discriminating task; update rather than merely confirm; select the least revealing and least restrictive valid move; require learner action and later unaided evidence; return authority or escalate when uncertainty or risk matters.

| Observation | Competing explanations | Useful discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Incorrect cross-multiplication | Misunderstanding equivalence; arithmetic slip; notation difficulty; copied procedure without meaning | A structurally related, low-load representation or prediction task |
| Brief textual interpretation | Concise valid response; insufficient evidence; reading-access barrier; unclear expected form | Ask for one cited phrase and its effect on interpretation |
| Delayed response | Deliberate reasoning; assistive technology; retrieval difficulty; interruption; connectivity | Offer more time or another input mode and examine task evidence |
| Wrong answer after heavy assistance | Insufficient concept understanding; answer disclosure; imitation; access failure | A fresh unaided anchor with assistance history explicitly separated |

These alternatives are testable possibilities about the task. Response length, timing, face, voice or language does not authorise an inference about motivation, personality, emotion, diagnosis, SEND, vulnerability or potential. A question such as “Would read-aloud, more time, a different explanation or a break help?” permits a declaration for the present purpose.

### 3.2 What the model families supply

This evidence map preserves the specialist report's distinctions; it does not transfer effects into this service.

| Family and source | Useful contribution | Limits that affect use |
|---|---|---|
| IRT/Rasch — [Rasch, 1960](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED419814) | Relates item response, latent proficiency and difficulty; useful assessment calibration under its assumptions | Usually static; not a learning or teaching-change theory; dimensionality and item functioning need local validation |
| Cognitive diagnosis — [Junker & Sijtsma, 2001](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/01466210122032064) | Explicit attributes and task/Q-matrix mappings permit interpretable hypotheses | Invalid attributes or mappings invalidate interpretation; categorical mastery can overstate uncertain evidence |
| Bayesian Knowledge Tracing — [Corbett & Anderson, 1994, *Knowledge Tracing*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01099821) | Skill-local probability, learning/performance parameters and procedural sequencing | Original ACT programming context and restrictive assumptions; “probability known” is not psychological truth or a causal comparator result |
| Logistic/performance-factor models — [Pelánek, 2017, *Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, Logistic Models, and Beyond*](https://www.fi.muni.cz/~xpelanek/publications/umuai-overview.pdf) | Inspectable opportunity, success/failure, item and learner effects; valuable simple baselines | Correlation may stand in for learning; construct and feature specification determine meaning |
| Time-sensitive recall — [Settles & Meeder, 2016, *A Trainable Spaced Repetition Model*](https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174/) | Half-life regression reported over 45% lower recall-prediction error against tested baselines and 12% higher daily engagement | Operational vocabulary-learning scope; engagement is not learning; one decay model does not cover concepts, procedures and strategies |
| Deep sequence state — [Piech et al., 2015, *Deep Knowledge Tracing*](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bac9162b47c56fc8a4d2a519803d51b3-Abstract.html) | Improved next-response prediction on evaluated data | Opaque semantics; [Ding & Larson, 2019](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED599227.pdf) found apparent skill tracking could encode overall ability or irrelevant history in tested models |
| Prediction-target comparison — [Mao, Lin & Chi, 2018](https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/318) | On two tutoring datasets, Bayesian variants better predicted post-tests while LSTM variants better predicted gain categories | Ranking depends on target; neither contrast estimates the benefit of using a predictor to choose teaching |
| Bayesian plan recognition — [Conati, Gertner & VanLehn, 2002](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A%3A1021258506583), [Gertner et al., 1998](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~conati/my-papers/Gertner-AAAI98.pdf) | Alternative problem-solving paths, uncertainty and hint choice in bounded physics problems | Domain-engineered, without evidence of generic psychological perspective-taking |
| Open learner models — [Bull & Kay, 2007, SMILI](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/publications/student-models-that-invite-the-learner-in-the-smili-open-learner-/) | Inspection, negotiation and control frameworks | Transparency's educational effects depend on interface and use. [Long & Aleven, 2017](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11257-016-9186-6): two studies, 302 Year 7–8 pupils total; small-study effect, larger-study no main effect but shared-control interaction. [Hou et al., 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40593-021-00250-6): 159 pupils; no significant condition-level learning, enjoyment or engagement differences |

The [KLI framework of Koedinger, Corbett & Perfetti (2012)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1551-6709.2012.01245.x) separates knowledge components, learning processes and instructional events, including memory/fluency, induction/refinement and understanding/sense-making. A model of a learning process does not establish the state of this learner. A general spacing effect does not show that one pupil has forgotten one concept; a common misconception does not show that a particular response expresses it.

Narrow pedagogical perspective-taking can be formally modelled: [Shafto, Goodman & Griffiths (2014)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24607849/) predicted human behaviour across three laboratory paradigms involving rules, prototypes and causal concepts. Generic LLM “theory of mind” is not established: [Ullman (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08399) found brittle responses to principle-preserving alterations, and [Shapira et al. (2024)](https://aclanthology.org/2024.eacl-long.138/) reported substantial adversarial failures across six social-reasoning tasks. These findings are model-generation and benchmark specific, not tuition-effect tests.

### 3.3 Calibration, abstention and E7

Calibration is tested overall and by knowledge component, subject, phase, item/task form, difficulty, prior attainment, language, modality, accommodation, access need, elapsed time and relevant groups. Repeat it after material curriculum, item, estimator, model or policy change. Separate assisted and unaided evidence and use policy-independent anchors. Report probability bins and observed frequencies, Brier/log loss, discrimination, coverage, missingness, abstention and the costs of false mastery and false difficulty. Expected calibration error alone can hide local failure.

[Guo et al. (2017)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html) demonstrate neural miscalibration and useful held-out temperature scaling in classification benchmarks; [Ovadia et al. (2019)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/8558cb408c1d76621371888657d2eb1d-Abstract.html) show degradation under shift. Neither validates educational calibration. A 2025 [BKT reading-ability fairness study](https://educationaldatamining.org/EDM2025/proceedings/2025.EDM.long-papers.158/index.html) covered 8,549 mathematics learners: aggregate error appeared similar while skill-level reading-related bias emerged; reweighting helped little. [Gardner, Brooks & Baker (2019)](https://learninganalytics.upenn.edu/ryanbaker/LAK_PAPER97_CAMERA.pdf) examined 44 MOOCs and over four million learners, illustrating consequential subgroup differences and the limits of one fairness metric.

Unknown, disputed, stale or out-of-distribution state should cause current-task diagnosis, learner choice, appropriately broad practice or human review. Greater complexity must earn an increment in decision value with acceptable rights burden. E7 separates credible estimation from a frozen causal comparison of otherwise equivalent policies. It reports whether state changes actions before claiming that those changed actions improve independent outcomes. [04 §7](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md#7-e7-optional-learner-state-and-causal-decision-value) supplies the full study and stopping contract.

## 4. Authority, standing and admission

### 4.1 Several independent dimensions

The governed-context synthesis identifies eight dimensions. A single `active`, `available` or `owner` field cannot carry them.

| Dimension | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Existence | Does a record remain? | A superseded judgement remains as protected history |
| Epistemic status | What is warranted and in which scope? | A hypothesis is contested |
| Accessibility | Can this actor retrieve it? | An authorised reviewer can inspect source evidence |
| Disclosure | May it be shown to this audience for this purpose? | A safeguarding abstraction may cross while its narrative stays protected |
| Operational eligibility | May it inform the present reasoning or generate work? | An archived plan cannot generate new tasks |
| Action authority | May this actor cause this effect? | A reviewer may recommend but not release |
| Liveness | Which parts of the actor/service can presently function? | Receiving a heartbeat does not prove reasoning, permission or publication capability |
| Retention | In what form, for which purpose and until when does it remain? | A terminal account may outlive unnecessary raw interaction detail |

A claim can exist, be historically warranted and available to diagnostic reviewers while being excluded from ordinary tuition context and carrying no action authority. Provenance describes origin; it does not establish present truth, legitimacy or benefit.

### 4.2 Field-level admission

A storage capability or information plane does not authorise population. For each proposed field or derived feature, identify: precise educational/public purpose; actor and decision; why less identifying or aggregate information is insufficient; affected parties and conflict handling; source authority, accuracy and contest route; permitted derivations and excluded consequences; retention/review/retirement; recipients/processors and uncontrolled-disclosure risk; lower-data or human alternative; and evidence plus stopping rule for the claimed benefit. Failure of necessity excludes the field. Success for one purpose does not open another.

The wider national-commons proposal is deliberately conditional: authoritative public knowledge can be independently successful even if no general learner-context federation proceeds. [09](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md) owns that public-value case.

### 4.3 Children, adults and relational records

The learner has standing; a child's future adult self has an interest in avoiding unnecessary permanent labels; guardians hold scoped and changing duties; schools and public bodies hold particular lawful responsibilities; other affected people and evidence custodians may have rights in relational records; independent reviewers may have authority over defined disputes. These do not collapse into property ownership or one birthday switch.

An authority change is a service event:

| Change | Response |
|---|---|
| Capacity or guardian arrangement changes | Reassess the specific access/disclosure/delegation, preserve appropriate confidential support and accessible representation/review |
| Institution changes | End or reassess old relationship-based access; select justified transfer, verify the new authority and preserve outstanding duties; safeguarding has its own protected route |
| School study becomes adult, workplace or professional learning | Establish new goals, assessment and authority; revalidate selected evidence; do not turn an old school hypothesis into employment assessment |
| Learner, professional and institution disagree | Retain attributable statements, minimise disputed operational use, offer safe fallback and specify who can resolve which question |
| Record concerns third parties or a protective duty | Separate optional tuition use from independently justified retention or disclosure; protect third-party information and explain limits through a capable human route |

Factual correction, annotation of a disputed professional judgement and challenge to a decision are different. Assertions do not become true by majority. A reset of optional learner state need not erase an independently justified record; retaining that record does not license future tuition use.

The 4 September source proposes a statutory learner-data charter: child-readable notice; access subject to narrow reviewable protections; correction and durable disagreement; protected source/derivation/influence receipts; intelligible account of algorithm/policy/human contribution; human review and representation; advocacy; refusal of genuinely optional processing without losing core service; portable certified records; purpose-specific expiry and bounded descendant correction; and effective remedies. This is a proposed new settlement, not a list of already uniform or unqualified legal entitlements. Its current-law routes include [ICO pupil-information guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/schools/pupils-info/), [DPA 2018 section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/section/9) and [UNCRC General Comment 25](https://www.ohchr.org/en/documents/general-comments-and-recommendations/general-comment-no-25-2021-childrens-rights-relation). Implementation requires decision- and jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## 5. Context and receipts: what evidence can actually show

### 5.1 A governed context transform

Similarity, search ranking and recency generate candidates. A consequential context decision additionally resolves purpose, actor/role, obligation/attempt, source and transformation provenance, epistemic status, current eligibility and authority, sensitivity/disclosure, time and volatility, contradiction/supersession, decision value, harm from inclusion or omission, and the smallest sufficient abstraction.

The transform is: retrieve candidates; validate sources; resolve principal, purpose and scope; evaluate eligibility; minimise disclosure; handle conflicts; select within a proportionate context budget; render an inspectable result. Possible results include exact content, minimal abstraction, warning, competing assertions, diagnostic-only access, request for appropriate authority, deferral, quarantine or refusal.

A projection record identifies purpose; consumer identity/role; source coverage and omitted classes; generation time; policy/source versions; conflicts/uncertainty; expiry/invalidation; and descriptive, analytic or operational use. A protected receipt records exclusions, redactions, freshness and declared information loss without disclosing withheld content. It is not necessary to store every prompt to justify every modest action; consequential claims need proportionate reconstruction evidence.

### 5.2 Evidence ladder

| Claim | Supporting observation | What it cannot prove |
|---|---|---|
| Source lineage | Identified source versions, event/help/access conditions and transformation | Accuracy, current eligibility or educational usefulness |
| Eligible context | Present authority, purpose, operation, time, selected fields, constraints and expiry | Delivery, internal attention or effect |
| Prepared/delivered/consumed context | Request/execution identifiers, accepted boundary, completion/failure and known omission/truncation; report only the stage observable | Complete internal consumption or token-level causal influence |
| Observed action difference | Frozen-policy contrast or other justified discriminator plus actual action/exposure records | That every included item contributed, or that the change improved learning |
| Bounded causal benefit | E7's independently governed durable unaided outcome, fidelity, uncertainty and harms | Transfer beyond the tested state, decision, version, population and setting |

A declared read-aloud choice may justify presentation without creating inferred reading ability. A renderer receipt may show receipt and delivery of audio. Actual access and learning still require their respective evidence. An inferred misconception cannot borrow a declaration's authority because both are called context.

Source provenance records origin and transformation. Material-participation records identify which projection, policy, evidence and plan versions reached a consequential execution. They are not perfect causal explanations. Missing observability narrows the claim, and does not justify unlimited logs. Receipts, explanations and traces have their own purpose, access, retention, correction and security rules.

### 5.3 Contract for a consequential exchange

Use this contract when selected work, attestation, correction or support crosses an authority boundary. A task-local activity need not carry the whole contract.

| Part | Required meaning and observable check |
|---|---|
| Parties and standing | Affected principals, source/receiver roles and responsible people, delegation, other claimants; verify present receiver authority and ability |
| Purpose and contemplated effect | Why information crosses, requested support/decision, allowed consequence and excluded uses; a support preference must not become permission for grading or employment inference |
| Selected content | Minimum objects and exact versions; observation/declaration/hypothesis/attestation status; subject/context, assistance, uncertainty, dispute and declared losses |
| Operations and duties | Allowed storage, consultation, derivation and onward disclosure; retention/review, human handling, notice and return route |
| Time and change | Expiry, renewal basis, policy/source versions, correction/restriction channel and resumption conditions; retries and reminders cannot renew grants |
| Receiving disposition | Receipt by an appropriate recipient; local acceptance, qualified acceptance, refusal or pending review, with reasons |
| Effect and reconciliation | Attempt, acknowledgement, confirmed effect, open questions, owner and legitimate terminal state |
| Limits and remedy | Controlled descendants, unreachable/external recipients, residual disclosure limits, accessible challenge and recovery |

A provider receiving an adult learner's selected qualification can verify issuer, integrity and status, then decide recognition against its own criteria. The signature does not prove course equivalence or justify unrelated learning-history disclosure. The receiving domain makes a local, scoped disposition: sender acceptance is not inherited authority.

## 6. Changes require current authority and an actual effect

### 6.1 Candidate transitions

An observation, interpretation, correction, policy change, grant revocation or intention cancellation are different writes. A consequential transition identifies subject/kind, observed current state, proposed state, reasons/evidence, causal alternatives, affected projections and consumers, required authority, reversibility/blast radius, review/expiry, expected actuator, verification and rollback.

The sequence is: candidate; structural and expected-state validation; causal/proportional judgement; appropriate authority decision; deterministic represented-state change; bounded actuation; reconciliation. This is a semantic contract for material changes, not mandatory frontmatter or approval paperwork for every edit.

| Mechanical work | Bounded judgement | Person or constituted authority |
|---|---|---|
| Parse/schema/identity; expected state; expiry; permissions; deduplication; invalidate descendants; emit evidence and reconcile | Interpret ambiguity; locate likely cause; compare alternatives; recommend a discriminator, duration or review trigger | Set legitimate purpose, values and priorities; decide consequential disclosure/erasure and competing rights; accept significant risk; amend governing policy |

A machine-readable policy can enforce adopted decisions without becoming a source of legitimacy. Repeated agent behaviour must not silently ratify policy.

### 6.2 Action-time validation and effect receipts

Immediately before a consequential effect, revalidate actor identity, delegation, prohibitions, source and plan versions, affected-party constraints and external preconditions. Use minimum privilege and short-lived authority; use idempotency or another duplicate-safe mechanism where effects can recur.

A prior plan does not confer current permission. A late result from a former actor remains attributable candidate evidence and must be judged against authority both when produced and when received. It cannot reactivate a revoked grant or overwrite current state by arrival order.

An effect receipt identifies plan and inputs, current authority, attempted effect, observed change and confirming evidence. Distinguish prepared payload, API acknowledgement, actual external effect and completed service duty. Partial, ambiguous and failed effects remain open for reconciliation. No receipt alone proves learning or successful human support.

### 6.3 Locate the cause before widening the response

| Failure locus | Example | Relevant actuator |
|---|---|---|
| Record | Incorrect or misattributed assertion | Correct, contest, quarantine or supersede |
| Derivation | Summary distorts valid source | Invalidate/recompute |
| Actor | Processor repeatedly misuses valid context | Narrow/revoke authority or replace processor |
| Grant | Wrong scope, purpose, duration or operation | Amend, pause, expire or revoke |
| Obligation | Contradictory or obsolete work | Wait, reshape, cancel or supersede |
| Generator | Recurring rule creates unwanted duties | Suppress/revise generator |
| Provider/host | Environment does not honour separation or revocation | Isolate, replace or stop use |
| Governing policy | Adopted rule repeatedly creates harm | Accountable policy review and amendment |

Useful controlled contrasts are same record/different actor; same actor/known-good record; same obligation/different provider; different obligation/same provider; same source/different derivation; same context/narrower grant. Widen intervention only when failures plausibly share a cause, local inquiry has exhausted useful information, the wider level has an effective actuator, broader consequences are proportionate, evidence/uncertainty survive, reset conditions are explicit and the regulator's own failure behaviour is known. Failure count alone does not establish common cause.

## 7. Correction, revocation, retirement and erasure

### 7.1 Distinct operations

| Operation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pause | Stop an active attempt temporarily with context/history preserved |
| Wait | Keep an obligation alive without an active worker until a named condition |
| Defer | Preserve an option without creating current work |
| Suppress | Prevent ordinary retrieval or recurrence of an item/rule/generator |
| Quarantine | Retain disputed, untrusted or sensitive material while isolating its influence |
| Cancel | Intentionally end the specified attempt or obligation |
| Revoke | Remove an actor's/application's/provider's authority |
| Retire | Remove a policy, preference, routine or model from present operation |
| Refuse | Decline a use incompatible with the applicable authority or constraints |
| Supersede | Preserve a historical claim while withdrawing current epistemic or operational status |
| Operationally forget | Remove ordinary retrieval weight or action-authorising power while retaining justified evidence |
| Erase/unlearn | Remove a named representation or satisfy a specified learned-influence contract under an explicit boundary and test |

“Not now”, “not until”, “not for this purpose/actor/provider”, “not again from this generator”, “not anymore” and “not at all” have different effects. A review date reopens consideration; it cannot answer it. Reopening may require new instructions, repaired provenance, changed circumstances, new evidence, provider capability change or explicit reauthorisation.

### 7.2 Bounded descendant convergence

Correction at a source is incomplete if old summaries, embeddings, features, policies, caches, adapters, exports or queued work still act on it. First stop new participation at the relevant enforcement boundary. Then enumerate and disposition material descendants within declared controlled or contractually governed scope.

| Descendant condition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Impermissible exact/copied material | Delete or sanitise under the applicable purpose and exceptions |
| Regenerable view/summary/feature | Recompute without the removed/corrected contribution |
| Unsupported justification | Invalidate and stop decision use |
| Uncertain provenance or disputed status | Quarantine pending a scoped decision |
| Legitimately retained evidence | Restrict to its separate purpose; prevent ambient operational use |
| Independently supported proposition | Remove the targeted contribution while retaining independently warranted support; removing a record is not necessarily erasing a fact |
| Learned parameter influence | Evaluate exact, certified approximate, empirical or behavioural remedy against its explicit contract; retrain, withdraw or cease a feature where needed |
| External effect/copy beyond recall | Notify, seek acknowledged disposition, remedy or compensate where appropriate; register residual limits |
| Legitimate evidential hold | Record the authority, access restriction, review and eventual disposition |

A correction event should identify enough to find descendants without repeating sensitive content. Known lineage cannot discover every covert copy, unaudited human memory or diffuse statistical consequence. State the guarantee's systems, contracts, time horizon and residual risks.

### 7.3 Anti-resurrection and recovery

Restarts, backup restoration, replayed queues, cache/index rebuilds, repeated imports, delayed replicas and provider switching must consult current restriction/supersession/revocation state before making old content operational. Minimal records of non-permission may be needed for erased source identities, revoked grants, completed intentions, invalid imports and retired providers. Such records have their own retention purpose and expiry conditions; they must not retain the prohibited content as a hidden recovery copy.

Distinguish source denial time, time to stop new controlled use and time to finish descendant repair. Revocation should reach credentials, running/queued sessions, cached contexts, capability discovery, scheduled jobs, derived policies, waiting obligations and controlled exports. Where prior disclosure cannot be recalled, the status must say so.

### 7.4 Scoped deletion/unlearning procedure

Resolve requester, subject, purpose, jurisdictions and competing duties; enumerate primary records, replicas, backups, indexes, caches, exports, messages and model inputs; traverse source-to-derivative lineage; classify descendants; choose delete, cryptographic erase, recompute, invalidate, unlearn, restrict, contextualise, notify or hold; prevent resurrection; test ordinary and adversarial retrieval, inference, memorisation, membership, downstream policy and stale replicas; record untested/uncontrolled limits; recheck delayed systems after the propagation window.

A forgetting claim reports: object originally present; carrier/boundary; dimension changed; operation; ordinary/adversarial probes; residual implicit/derived/structural influence tested; reference condition; recoverability; copies/derivatives covered; authority and affected parties; applicable constraints; falsifier; untested or unremoved influence; and whether its conclusion concerns access, behaviour, representation or physical erasure. A missing response is not proof of removal.

## 8. Continuity across workers, providers and life contexts

### 8.1 Durable intent and obligations

Retain semantic identity separately from provider task IDs:

| Object | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Durable intent/rule | Goal, adopted policy, commitment or conditional routine |
| Obligation occurrence | One concrete thing now owed, due or requested |
| Attempt | One bounded effort to satisfy it |
| Actor authority | Who may presently act under which delegation |
| Evidence | Outputs, observed effects, confirmations, failures and questions |
| Terminal account | Legitimate completion, cancellation, expiry, invalidation, supersession, refusal, exhausted attempts or decision not to promote |
| Consolidated learning | Separately accepted changes to future practice, with evidence and history |

A failed attempt does not prove the underlying goal invalid; later success does not erase earlier failure. A recurring generator and one occurrence are different: cancelling an occurrence need not stop the generator, and repeated bad occurrences may require changing the generator.

`waiting` and `dormant` are continuing conditions, not synonyms for fulfilled or cancelled. A waiting record needs obligation, state, resumption condition, evidence source, earliest/latest review, authority needed to resume, successor context, expiry behaviour and duplicate-trigger handling. Time can trigger review, without restoring old authority.

### 8.2 Zero-worker continuity and succession

All agents and workflow products should be able to stop while a duty remains intelligible. After time and provider change, a new actor must recover what is owed, why, what has been attempted, what evidence is missing and who may authorise the next step. It must not revive completed, revoked or superseded work. Distinguish principal, actor, session, enduring episode/thread, attempt, provider and successor; succession transfers bounded work, not identity or all prior permissions.

Liveness has independently failing dimensions. A processor can be reachable but lack graph access; authenticated but lack a grant; receive context without an active reasoning loop; heartbeat without progress; produce work without permission or ability to publish. Evidence licenses only the path actually tested.

### 8.3 A safeguarding transfer example

The national source's concrete duty is: transfer required; verify receiving authority; prepare minimum secure package; attempt delivery; obtain receipt from an authorised person; resolve questions/gaps; close with evidence. The same pattern may serve correction/deletion requests, appeals, SEND reviews, assessment adjustments, support referrals, incident remedies and research approvals. A sent email is an attempted delivery, not completion.

A recipient often needs an actionable abstraction—use a communication adjustment; follow a current supervision plan; contact the designated lead through a verified channel—rather than the underlying narrative. Record issuer, purpose, audience, validity/review and escalation. An emergency path is separately justified, actor-identified, field-limited, time-limited, receipted and reviewed; it must not create a universal administrator override. [05](05-inclusion-and-human-service.md) supplies human-service requirements; [09](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md) the national context.

### 8.4 Selective lifelong portability

School, resit, FE/HE, vocational, workplace, professional, community, informal and later-life settings have distinct goals, assessment, authorities, expectations and risks. Prefer portability of learner-selected goals/access choices, work/notes/reflections, source- and context-bearing evidence and issuer-attested achievements with criteria and status. Keep most inferred mastery, misconception and strategy state local to its time, subject and purpose. Any receiving use needs revalidation.

[Kay & Kummerfeld (2019)](https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/bjet.12878) offer a scrutable learner-centred lifelong model as a conceptual direction, not lifetime-outcome proof. [Open Badges](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/open-badges) and [W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) offer issuer/holder/verifier, criteria, evidence, status and validity mechanisms. They do not make a claim true; persistent identifiers and aggregation can enable correlation. A transferable hidden psychological dossier is outside this service's direction.

### 8.5 Provider-independent continuity as a wider opportunity

The personal-graph source proposes user-governed continuity across changing AI tools: canonical events, claims, provenance, conflicts and policy remain attributable; models and applications are replaceable processors; storage, exchange, interaction and governance stay distinct; compartments are default-deny; agents propose candidate writes.

Its stronger product idea is preserving both accumulated context and the person's control over how that context continues to influence them. This is a broader research direction, with additional relational-rights problems for children and institutions. It is not a prerequisite that tuition adopt a personal graph.

A capability boundary distinguishes provenance, version, trust, management authority, availability, activation, capability, permission, execution location, health, revocation and evidence. Installed does not mean granted; granted read access does not permit inference or recurring action; trusted deterministic export does not imply trusted interpretation. MCP, A2A, OAuth/OIDC, RDF/JSON-LD, property graphs, Neo4j or Solid are potential replaceable implementation choices, not sovereignty by themselves.

A real substitution test removes the provider, context compiler, identity broker, bridge, graph/vector store or coordinator and checks that authoritative evidence, grants, prohibitions, disputes, obligations, revocations and receipts remain intelligible and usable. Export alone is insufficient. Governance should offer outcome-oriented operations—request scoped context, correct a claim, resume a duty, stop a generator, revoke a grant, explain context, reconcile descendants—without making users administer an ontology.

The personal-graph proposal's sequence is a bounded option: reconcile existing specification; define only the ontology needed for one journey; add candidate transitions; test governed context; test waiting/correction/cancellation/provider switch; reconcile derivatives; diagnose failure loci; test exchange; evaluate longitudinally. It explicitly declines wholesale import of OCE branches, queues, claim files, role fleets and repository ceremony. Transfer the semantics and tested behaviour.

For every case record initial versions and authority, event order, expected boundary decision, observed effect, time to controlled convergence, exceptions, decision owner and evidence. Passing covers only the declared implementation and scope.

## 10. Practical rights, safety and controlled service change

### 10.1 Rights must change what happens

When a material hypothesis is used, learners need age- and context-appropriate ways to see it exists and matters; distinguish declaration, observation, inference and attestation; inspect uncertainty/date/scope/expiry; add context or contrary evidence; correct source data; challenge the inference; restrict disputed use where appropriate; reset; request applicable deletion; use the no-derived-state route; and obtain meaningful human review.

Test an actual journey: identify the object and its allowed effect, dispute or change it, then establish what changed. Include differing ages, languages, access modes, unfamiliarity and power relationships, and people unable to complete the journey. Measure comprehension, wrongful expectations, completion/help, time/effort/repeated explanation, false suppression of legitimate access, time to stop use and finish repair, human queue/workload, recovery and fallback quality. A probability dashboard, data dump or trained-administrator demonstration is not usable control.

Human authority requires competence, time, access to evidence, communication access and the power to stop or alter the decision. Educators cannot review every micro-decision after delivery or become the liability sink for opaque automation.

### 10.2 Privacy and logging tensions

The specialist sources ground minimisation and child protection in the [ICO Children's Code](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/), [special-category guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-is-special-category-data/), [accuracy](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/accuracy/), [rectification](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-rectification/), [storage limitation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/) and the [DUAA factsheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/data-use-and-access-act-2025-factsheets/data-use-and-access-act-factsheet-uk-gdpr-and-dpa). Their applicability, commencement, controller roles, lawful bases and changed guidance need current specialist assessment before an implementation decision. Uncertainty in an inferred health/disability label is not permission to treat it as harmless ordinary data.

The sources identify a real tension with [DfE generative-AI product safety standards](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/generative-ai-product-safety-standards/generative-ai-product-safety-standards): safety observation can create intimate behavioural records, false inferences and chilling effects. The design response is minimum necessary logs; separate safeguarding from tuition, discipline, commercial and improvement uses; local/enclave processing where suitable; privileged access; accessible notice; explicit short retention; observable interaction measures; evaluated false positives/negatives and subgroup effects; and separately justified secondary use. Neither monitoring nor a protected influence receipt should create a shadow dossier.

### 10.3 Privacy-enhancing methods have bounded guarantees

[Yacobson et al. (2021)](https://learning-analytics.info/index.php/JLA/article/view/7353) linked de-identified K–12 learning data with public school information, showing why de-identification alone is insufficient. Federated Averaging ([McMahan et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v54/mcmahan17a.html)) reduces raw example movement; [Zhu, Liu & Han (2019)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/60a6c4002cc7b29142def8871531281a-Abstract.html) recovered training information from gradients in tested settings. Federation needs a threat model and, where justified, secure aggregation, access controls and differential privacy. [NIST SP 800-226](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/226/final) explains quantifiable privacy loss and implementation hazards: privacy unit, budget and composition matter. These methods cannot prove necessity, teaching validity or exact individual state without trade-off.

Scientific grounds also matter: [Barrett et al. (2019)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31313636/) reject simple facial-movement-to-emotion mapping; [Ocumpaugh et al. (2014)](https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/bjet.12156) show population-generalisation problems in educational affect detection. [Kosinski, Stillwell & Graepel (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1218772110) illustrate how digital traces can predict sensitive traits: predictive possibility increases the case for restraint.

### 10.4 Feedback loops and accountable change

| Possible loop | Mechanism and warning |
|---|---|
| Self-confirming misconception | Initial label selects only confirming tasks; narrow exposure and no discriminating counterexample |
| Inflated mastery | Assisted success updates as independent evidence; weak unaided anchors despite high internal state |
| Suppressed opportunity | Low estimate repeatedly removes challenge; persistent content/group exposure differences |
| Thrashing | Noise repeatedly flips state and support; repeated explanations and confusion |
| Dependency | More help optimises immediate correctness; fewer genuine attempts and poor withdrawal performance |
| Engagement capture | Duration/clicks/conversation replace learning; longer use without retention/transfer |
| Safety distortion | Incorrect signals restrict ordinary learning or miss support; unequal blocks/escalations |
| Version drift | Changed model/item/curriculum invalidates calibration after release |

The general dependence of later observations on prior predictions is formalised by [Perdomo et al. (2020)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/perdomo20a.html). [Ensign et al. (2018)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v81/ensign18a.html) and [Chaney, Stewart & Engelhardt (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11214) supply cross-domain analogies for preferential observation and recommendation-conditioned narrowing; they are not measured tuition effects. [Amodei et al. (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565) discuss reward hacking, side effects, unsafe exploration and shift.

A live policy selects only profile-approved moves. Candidate change has a named mechanism, version, affected coverage and rival explanation; fixtures, simulation, adversarial/accessibility checks, shadow and appropriate offline analysis precede authorised causal testing. A staged release has owner, feature controls, monitoring, bypass, rollback and withdrawal conditions. No participant interaction directly rewrites objectives, state semantics, prompts, routing, rewards, safeguards or weights; no live bandit/RL allocation invents new teaching experiments.

Offline evaluation needs logging-policy support and, where applicable, known propensities. Doubly robust methods ([Dudík, Langford & Li, 2011](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.4601)) and replay ([Li et al., 2011](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.5956)) can screen under assumptions, not establish unsupported-action value or replace independent learner outcomes. Baseline bootstrapping ([Laroche et al., 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/laroche19a.html)) supports the idea of fallback only within its formal assumptions. [NIST AI RMF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf) and its [Manage playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/manage/) provide lifecycle guidance, not proof that residual harm is absent.

Bind runs and claims to model/provider, estimator/features, curriculum/profile, content/tasks, pedagogical policy/prompt, safeguards/moderation, interface/access functions, instrumentation/outcomes and permissions. [04](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md) owns the detailed release and rollback contract. Retain null, adverse, mixed and missing evidence and a state-free fallback.

### 10.5 Several clocks and kinds of correction

The historical *OCE Graph Tools: End State and Roadmap* (20 August 2026, §11) adds useful precision. Event time, observation/extraction time, validity interval, record/transaction time, decision/ratification time, review/due time, expiry/retention, activation/last successful probe, correction/retraction, effective supersession/withdrawal and propagation completion are separate clocks. A consequential “current” query needs the relevant clock and source epoch.

If an assertion was false when recorded, link a correction or retraction. If it was true then changed, close its validity and add the later state. If interpretation changes, preserve observations and supersede the interpretation. If a commitment is retired, withdraw operational eligibility. If a projection is stale, invalidate and rebuild it. Physical erasure has separate authority and derivative duties. These distinctions prevent an apparent clean present from falsifying history while preserving applicable erasure obligations.

The same roadmap separates semantic role, epistemic kind, purpose/time authority, lifecycle, home/stratum, sensitivity, clocks and retention policy. A file may contain several assertion kinds; file-wide status is insufficient. Useful record families include working packets, episodic observations, outcomes (including negative, non-use and reversal), ignorance/coverage, protected dissent, intentions, correction lineage, activation/probe evidence and calibration. They need not become separate stores. Measure withdrawal latency, descendant coverage, false suppression, protected-dissent survival, historical recovery and stale-active-context burden. [08](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md) owns the engineering homes and query implications.

## 11. Governed forgetting: the wider research model

This section preserves the broader intellectual contribution of *Governed Forgetting* (version 1.0, 2 August 2026). It is a critical integrative literature review and research launch, not a completed systematic review or meta-analysis. Searches were not preregistered or exhaustive, screening/extraction was not independently duplicated and effect sizes were not pooled. Its own empirical results are proposals. Cross-disciplinary analogies must earn target-domain evidence and do not authorise psychological inference or live self-modification in tuition.

### 11.1 The object is temporally displaced state

The initial hypothesis—forgetting regulates the causal authority of the past—expands to state acquired, inherited or committed at other times, including obsolete future-directed goals. A completed intention can still trigger action. The design question is how availability, interpretation, authority, transmission, persistence and erasure should change, in which carrier and boundary, under whose authority and with what evidence.

For an item or disposition, the source proposes a descriptive vector:

\[
M_x(t)=\langle P,A,F,C,S,E,T,R\rangle
\]

Here \(P\) is physical/representational persistence; \(A\) accessibility under a specified retrieval policy; \(F\) fidelity/reconstructive accuracy; \(C\) confidence or epistemic weight; \(S\) salience/attentional priority (including affect in the human research domain); \(E\) behavioural efficacy; \(T\) transmissibility; and \(R\) recoverability/reversibility. This is an analytical vocabulary, not a learner-schema recommendation or licence to infer affect.

A transition need not decrease every dimension. Compression can reduce episodic fidelity while increasing a summary's frequency and influence. Archiving can lower access while retaining evidence and recovery. Extinction can reduce expressed response while an older association survives. De-indexing changes discovery without necessarily removing a source.

A precise forgetting claim uses:

\[
\mathcal F=\langle x,c,l,b,m,d,g,r,p,e\rangle
\]

The terms are object/disposition; carrier; system level; boundary; mechanism; changed dimensions; governing actor/objective/incidental cause; reversibility; provenance/propagation; and identifying evidence. “The model forgot the user” is incomplete without these conditions.

### 11.2 Mechanisms, carriers and non-transfer

| Mechanism | Distinction worth preserving | Cross-domain limit |
|---|---|---|
| Non-encoding | No durable representation formed, or preservation prevented | Absence is not decay; record why a source was unobserved, unmeasured or excluded |
| Retrieval failure/context gating | Cues, context, role or environment prevent current access | No demonstrated trace destruction |
| Inhibition/suppression | Access or expression is actively reduced | Old influence may return under other cues |
| Interference/competition | Other learning or resource competition changes access/fidelity | Not necessarily purposeful or beneficial |
| Decay/turnover | Strength, carrier or population share changes | May be incidental; retention at another level can survive |
| Compression/semanticisation | Detailed episodes become selected structure | Qualifiers and exceptions may disappear while the aggregate gains authority |
| Reconsolidation/revision | Reactivation may open modification under conditions | Human results have boundary and replication problems; no reliable erase button |
| Extinction/counterlearning | New learning suppresses old response | Context-dependent renewal or reinstatement remains possible |
| Supersession/retirement | Old content loses current belief or action status | Evidence can remain under justified historical access |
| Dormancy/archive | Reduced active use with protected recoverability | Ongoing exposure and custody costs remain |
| Quarantine | Isolate contested rights, truth or security state | Review/expiry and false-suppression costs matter |
| De-indexing | Reduce discovery paths | Underlying identifying content and use may remain |
| Erasure | Remove a representation from a named carrier | Copies, derivatives and worldly effects may remain |
| Machine unlearning | Satisfy a declared relation to training without specified data | Output suppression, model editing and exact removal are different |
| Structural erasure | Institutions prevent recording, recognition or transmission | Power must be analysed; there may never have been a public trace |
| Intention deactivation | Completed, cancelled, expired or invalidated goal stops generating effects | History and trigger eligibility are different |
| Offloading | Information or expertise moves to another person, tool or archive | Local loss can be broader persistence; access dependency increases |
| Anti-resurrection | Minimal non-permission prevents restoration | Metadata can itself disclose; indefinite identifying markers are not a default |

A common structure does not establish a common substrate or optimum. Neural inhibition is not a database row, fear extinction is not deletion, epigenetic reset is not an organisational wipe, immune imprinting does not make every prior pathological, ecological legacy does not make every causal consequence “memory”, cache hit rate is not epistemic quality, turnover is not guaranteed innovation, and low public attention is not cultural disappearance.

### 11.4 Time, costs and stability

The source's bookkeeping objective is:

\[
J(\pi)=V_{decision}+V_{learning}+V_{accountability}+V_{identity}
-C_{carry}-C_{retrieval}-C_{staleness}-C_{exposure}-C_{lock-in}-C_{loss}.
\]

Each term depends on stakeholder, time and level. This is not a claim that all values are commensurable: rights, safety and constitutional conditions constrain choices independently of a weighted sum.

Costs include storage/index/maintenance/energy versus deletion/migration/verification; retrieval interference versus reconstruction; stale influence versus lost useful guidance; rigidity versus noise chasing; exposure versus lost redress evidence; reacquisition/repeated incidents; coordination lock-in versus lost shared expectations; imposed identity versus broken recognition; buried anomalies versus erased provenance; and unequal allocation of benefits and harms.

Conditional decisions therefore differ:

| Condition | Candidate disposition and rationale |
|---|---|
| Stable regime, high recurrence | Active retention often useful |
| Volatile regime with credible change evidence | Down-weight or make dormant; retain recovery option where legitimate |
| Rare catastrophic consequence | Protect applicable exception and evidential standing; also measure false activation |
| Cheap reacquisition, low unique value | Retention has less option value; release may be easier |
| High privacy/security exposure | Restriction may be insufficient; erasure may dominate |
| Public-interest evidential duty | Protected archive with accountable access, not universal retrieval |
| Suspected poisoning or contested authority | Quarantine; preserve review evidence while blocking risky influence |
| Superseded policy with useful rationale | Historical retention, no present activation |
| Completed intention | Stop trigger; retain only justified account |
| High uncertainty about future aims | Dormancy can preserve options unless continued existence creates greater harm |
| Credible anomaly or minority testimony | Protect against frequency-based deletion |
| Exact copies covered by valid erasure decision | Delete except a separately justified exception |

Stability is itself a profile:

\[
S=\langle resistance,recovery,persistence,robustness,plasticity,transformability\rangle.
\]

A controller with \(\dot z=e\), \(u=Kz\) accumulates past error; leakage \(\dot z=e-\lambda z\) can reduce windup while sacrificing exact long-term adaptation under relevant conditions. The equation applies where a state actually integrates error. Archives are not integrators.

Compare four configurations: homogeneous persistence; fast surface with slow core; protected archive with revisable operation; and plural populations with different histories. A hybrid may work better, but slow cores can entrench errors and plural histories complicate coordination and erasure. Candidate “governed adaptive continuity” predicts advantage only when timescales, consequences, carriers, authority, exposure and lineage align. It is weakened if one global retention policy matches typed policies; ordinary recall tests adequately measure latent influence; lineage adds no useful correction; plural models add no adaptive value; turnover fails despite retained invariants; or governance adds burden without legitimacy/outcome gains.

Finite active context requires selection/compression, not necessarily destruction. Non-stationarity may require changed weighting; completed goals require deactivation; rights or exposure can require actual erasure; saturated representations may require renewal. None makes wholesale forgetting universally necessary.

### 11.5 Unlearning, editing and security

Exact unlearning normally concerns equality or bounded indistinguishability of output distributions under declared training algorithms/randomness, not identical weights. Certified approximate, empirical and behavioural guarantees are weaker and different. Record target, threat model, reference distribution, tolerance and verification; removal of a datum's contribution does not require suppressing a proposition supported independently elsewhere.

### 11.6 Protected alternatives, silence and ordinary operation

Keep a current operational view distinct from protected evidence/alternatives, diagnostic access and explicit reopening conditions. Otherwise either history dominates every new decision or a polished consensus hides how confidence was earned. Selective independent first passes can receive raw evidence and governing constraints before inherited synthesis; reconcile later. This is an experiment, not permanent adversarial ceremony or a claim of independent assurance from agent count.

Absence needs meaning: `unobserved`, `unmeasured`, `unrecorded`, `unfunded`, `classified`, `suppressed`, `destroyed`, `inaccessible`, `de-indexed`, `disbelieved`, `overwritten`, `compressed` and `superseded` describe different failures or dispositions. A missing event or intentionally withheld source must not be silently reconstructed as a complete history.

A reactivatable preservation bundle needs content/checksums, provenance/rights, schemas/vocabularies, dependency/lineage information, usable formats or emulation, discovery, examples, periodic retrieval tests, custody and access/expiry/destruction conditions. Research additionally needs code, environments, protocols, instruments and often tacit expertise. Neither stored bytes nor an immutable archive proves preservation or justice.

### 12.7 Useful patterns and stopping conditions

Promising patterns are protected archive/revisable operation, typed horizons, provenance-preserving summaries, protected dissent, isolated clean comparisons, explicit completion/non-permission, dependency-aware supersession, reacquisition-aware dormancy, preserved long-horizon baselines and purpose-scoped retrieval. Memory governance belongs primarily in a shared substrate spanning writes, reads, revision, authority, archival, lineage, security and erasure. Bounded skills can assess retirement, execute authorised erasure, invalidate descendants, consolidate with provenance, audit stale/poisoned content, review regime change or prepare reactivatable archives. They call governed capabilities; forgetting is not an autonomous preference of a transient model.

Pause or simplify a proposed programme if its ontology stretches terms beyond usefulness; typed governance adds no robust value over simple baselines; provenance cannot support invalidation; protected archives create unacceptable exposure; removal claims cannot be verified under the required threat model; affected parties reject the authority allocation; task gains harm real impact/autonomy/safety; or analogy substitutes for target-domain evidence. A new layer should identify what procedure or duplicated representation it removes.
