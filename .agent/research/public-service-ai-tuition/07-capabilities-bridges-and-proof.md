# Capabilities, bridges and proof

**Purpose:** make the connections from precise engineering through warranted educational activity to useful public outcomes explicit, testable and usable by OCE. This document owns the connecting model and complete working proof-profile contracts. The seven bands are a useful view, not an adopted implementation architecture.

**Standing, 6 September 2026:** synthesis of the source-based capability model and its revision, informed by the detailed engineering inquiries retained in [08](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md). B01–B24 are proposed bridges; BP-01–BP-10 and PS-01–PS-34 are proposed proof profiles and scenarios. None of these 34 scenarios was executed on a tuition implementation. The eleven separate historical source probes P1–P11 are recorded in 08 and the two original assets.

Start with the [service concept](01-service-concept.md), then use this document to select the claim and responsibilities you need to connect. The [educational profiles](03-learner-experience-and-profiles.md), [evaluation](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md), [human service](05-inclusion-and-human-service.md), [state and rights](06-state-rights-and-context.md), [public infrastructure](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md) and [institutional evidence use](11-evidence-publication-and-decisions.md) own domain detail. Role names below are responsibilities awaiting actual appointment.

## Reading routes

- For architecture or extraction: capability bands, relation obligations, B01–B06 and BP-01–BP-03; then the full C01–C24 register in 08.
- For a learning episode: B07–B19 and BP-06/BP-08; then the subject profile and evaluation contracts.
- For authority, correction or replacement: B12–B16/B21/B23 and BP-04/BP-05/BP-07.
- For evidence, public benefit and transfer: B17–B24 and BP-09/BP-10.

The material counterframe is that a neat stack could conceal changes in meaning and proof. A low/high split is a useful filter but hides intermediates; a rigid stack implies false adjacency and one-way relations; an undifferentiated graph burdens readers. A typed graph with responsibility bands preserves many-to-many connections while exposing independent semantic, educational, operational and institutional obligations. Add a band only when it exposes a distinct responsibility or decision.

## Capability bands, independent axes and placement

A capability is an ability to fulfil an identified responsibility under stated conditions. Its implementation may involve software, knowledge, people, governance and organisational arrangements. An artifact is evidence or a carrier of capability; possessing it does not establish that the capability is operative.

| Band | Responsibility | OCE/tuition example | Appropriate warrant |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 — elementary mechanisms and contracts | Provide precise reusable operations and invariants | Clock/deadline ownership, cancellation, canonical bytes, graph traversal | Contract/property/boundary tests with explicit assumptions |
| A2 — reusable technical capabilities | Compose operations into a usable technical service | Complete artifact loading/publication; schema adapters; durable work; installed package API | Consumer integration, concurrency/recovery and deployment evidence |
| A3 — domain knowledge and semantics | Define what objects mean and when claims apply | Curriculum/PCK profile, source claim, learner observation, assessment construct | Subject review, provenance, applicability and semantic validation |
| A4 — domain behaviour | Apply policy to observations within a task or sequence | Interpret an attempt provisionally; select a permitted move; fade support; record actual exposure | Reviewed cases, policy conformance and observed interaction |
| A5 — service capabilities and journeys | Enable people to achieve a purpose with usable control and support | Accessible tuition episode; educator assignment/intervention; resumability; assessment participation | Complete journey, access, authority, operational and burden evidence |
| A6 — human outcomes | Establish effects on capability, agency and educational opportunity | Durable unaided learning, transfer, educator time and learner confidence within legitimate measurement | Valid outcomes, comparison, follow-up, heterogeneity and implementation evidence |
| A7 — public and institutional impact | Establish sustainable collective value | Equitable provision, public stewardship, affordable scale, independent reuse and exit | Deployment/adoption, distributional, economic and institutional evidence over time |

These bands describe responsibility, not a mandatory implementation stack. A profile has artifacts in A2, semantics in A3 and operational interpretation in A4. An educator intervention crosses A4–A6. Access and authority can constrain every band. A high-level purpose may govern one task; a small timing mechanism may affect an entire estate.

Six coordinates must remain separate: **dimension** (learning, integrity, accessibility, rights, reliability, workload, reuse); **abstraction band**; **scale** (event, task, episode, sequence, delayed revisit, cohort, organisation, sector); **perspective** (learner, educator, access specialist, supporter, evaluator, engineer/operator, public steward); **maturity** (implemented/consumed, dormant, app-local, stub, plan, proposal); and **POC stage** (bench, expert/access validation, participant feasibility, bounded efficacy, effectiveness/reuse). [F20 §3](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md) supplies the immediate source distinction.

**Placement is another view.** A workspace, package, application, managed provider or organisation is a possible carrier/owner. Many capabilities use one carrier; one service capability crosses many carriers. Moving code to `core` does not demonstrate domain neutrality. A useful public outcome cannot be assigned wholly to a package. OCE's wider consumers remain legitimate: tuition is a first or additional consumer, not an ownership claim over every reusable capability.

## First-class relations and proof obligations

Edges are addressable objects, not unlabeled arrows. Each has an ID, source and target object(s), relation type, claimed mechanism, preserved and potentially lost meaning, scope/assumptions, evidence/defeaters, acceptance observation, accountable role, status and reopening condition. A relation can be many-to-many or involve several inputs jointly; pairwise links alone must not conceal composition prerequisites.

Useful relation types include `implements`, `composes-with`, `depends-on`, `represents`, `interprets`, `enables`, `delivers`, `measures`, `causes`, `authorises`, `constrains`, `reuses`, `supersedes` and `contradicts`. A reference can establish `derived-from` without establishing `supports`. An enabling relation does not automatically establish a causal effect. A measurement relation needs a construct-validity argument. An authorisation relation needs a legitimate decision and enforcement evidence. A historical supersession relation does not establish that all earlier knowledge is obsolete.

For every bridge, the minimum record is:

1. **Claim and endpoints:** what lower/source responsibility is said to establish about what higher/target responsibility; versions, context and unit of analysis.
2. **Mechanism and composition:** how the result would arise; other required nodes and interfaces; failure and recovery paths.
3. **Semantic accounting:** invariants, identity, uncertainty and context preserved; compression, defaults, transformations or omissions; whether an inverse mapping is valid.
4. **Evidence accounting:** supporting, challenging and missing evidence separately; provenance and dependence between sources; no promotion from plan to implementation or from test to educational effect.
5. **Acceptance and defeat:** observable criterion, relevant boundary/negative case, stage, evaluator/owner and predeclared thresholds where appropriate. An unset numerical threshold remains an explicit design decision.
6. **Authority and lifecycle:** who may approve or override, current status, applicable versions, monitoring and conditions for reopening after a change or adverse observation.

In the register below, every bridge is **proposed/unproved as an end-to-end tuition claim** unless its evidence column explicitly identifies narrower existing evidence. Role names are required future responsibilities, not appointments. The acceptance columns are required proof obligations; they do not report tests performed in this transfer session. The corpus's source inspections and local probes retain their original dates and pins. This prevents an assurance ladder: lower-level success narrows possible failure, but each upward claim needs its own warrant.

## Worked connections and their limits

**A bounded mathematics episode.** An educator selects an objective and reviewed profile. Oak resources carry curriculum content; source/PCK records state why the chosen diagnostic and representation fit this objective. A complete validated profile is loaded and its identity recorded (B03–B07). The learner's response is an observation compatible with more than one explanation (B08). Policy permits a discriminating question or scaffold; it does not declare a stable deficit (B09). The interface delivers a usable representation and captures the subsequent learner action and degree of help (B10/B11). The learner and educator can correct, pause and obtain support (B12/B15). Durable records and run identity support reconstruction (B13/B14/B16). An unaided check informs the episode; delayed independent outcomes and comparison answer the different question of learning effect (B17/B18). Failure at any bridge qualifies the resulting claim even if upstream tests pass. Year 9 proportional reasoning is a preserved candidate setting, not the mandatory first profile.

**A materially different English episode.** A profile may ask for textual evidence, compare interpretations or support revision. It needs subject-specific quality judgements, epistemic plurality and legitimate feedback limits. Shared resource loading, timing, records and control may transfer. The mathematics diagnostic logic, response schema, correctness model and sequence do not transfer automatically. A fixed attempt–hint–answer sequence is not imposed on every subject or context. Access-mode and assessment review must ask whether the same intended construct survives. This tests B20 and the boundary between reusable machinery and domain judgement.

**An engineering capability with other OCE consumers.** Canonical identity, complete artifact publication or cancellation can support graph ingest, curriculum tooling and evidence bundles as well as tuition. A coherent reusable contract, a recognised standard concept and an embedded instance can justify admission to OCE's broader foundation work; multiple consumers or a tuition-first runtime seam are not universal prerequisites. Actual consumers and proportionate contract evidence then test ownership and productisation claims. A new package is not required for every responsibility. Existing app-local carriers, dormant exports and sketches remain separately classified. This preserves the wider Atlas/foundation charter recorded in08, from F19 and the curated-POC alternative in F20.

**Optional learner-state proof.** E7 follows B08 → B09 → B18 → B23 with B12/B13/B16/B17 as co-conditions. First establish scoped prediction/calibration without confusing it with semantic truth; then show which approved action actually changes; compare that policy with an otherwise equivalent no-derived-state service for worthwhile incremental durable benefit and acceptable harms/rights burden; finally make the authorised retain, simplify, reset or rollback decision. Failure at any step blocks the corresponding persistence claim even if the whole service shows benefit. The full service, state and evidence contracts are in [04](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md), [06](06-state-rights-and-context.md) and [11](11-evidence-publication-and-decisions.md), respectively.

**A public-value claim.** A promising learning effect does not establish equitable national provision. Learners must be able to access and continue the service; educators need workable support and acceptable burden; institutions need legitimate stewardship, viable costs and exit; profiles and evidence must fit new contexts. Standards are useful means when independently implemented and governed. A public repository or open schema alone is inadequate evidence. B20–B23 preserve these additional obligations without pretending the initial POC can prove sector-wide impact.

## What the POC must prove at each stage

| Stage | Reviewable result | Claim it may support | Claim still outside its warrant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bench/synthetic | Bounded reviewed profile, executable actions, failure cases, accepted artifact identities and evaluation trace | Specified technical/domain behaviour under tested conditions | Participant usability, learning effect, public impact |
| Expert and access validation | Subject/PCK judgement, meaningful activities, mode/construct checks, challenging cases and corrections | Fitness for the reviewed contexts and permission question for supervised use | Universal accessibility or efficacy |
| Participant feasibility | Working supported journeys; actual exposure, burden, access, missingness, fidelity and recovery | Feasibility and functioning instruments in the participating context | Durable causal learning benefit without the appropriate study |
| Bounded efficacy | Governed comparison, valid independent delayed unaided outcomes, version/exposure and uncertainty | A bounded effect estimate for the studied service/context | General effectiveness, all-subject validity or national value |
| Later effectiveness/reuse | Additional contexts, implementation/adoption, disaggregated outcomes, costs, independent consumers and exit | Transportability, sustainability and public-value claims within new evidence | Unlimited generalisation |

The quality of curriculum validity, accessibility, authority and meaning does not become optional in early stages. Their implementation depth and evidence scope change. Synthetic files can support bench evidence; participant use needs appropriate minimal durable records, role/resource decisions and staffed support. A reviewed curated sequence can establish useful early capability without first completing every graph or general knowledge platform. Assessment and statistical analysis may be external; OCE must preserve the relevant interface/custody contract.

## Refined coordinates and representational roles

### 9.1 Separate the question answered by each coordinate

The six coordinates in §2 remain useful. Four further questions prevent responsibility, causal explanation, control and time from being conflated. These are independent views to use where relevant, not four additional capability bands or a demand to populate every field on every small operation.

| Coordinate or view | Question answered | Example and prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Capability band | What responsibility is fulfilled? | A profile interprets disciplinary knowledge; storing its bytes does not fulfil that interpretation. |
| Dimension | Which quality or value is at issue? | Access, integrity and learning can have different results; an average score cannot compensate for an authority failure. |
| Scale | For what unit, population and duration is the statement made? | An event, a task, a cohort and an institution have different denominators. |
| Perspective | Whose experience, burden or judgement is represented? | A usable operator console does not establish a usable learner journey. |
| Maturity and POC stage | What exists, and which next claim is being tested? | An owner-ratified plan is stronger planning authority than a proposal, but neither is an implemented capability. |
| Causal distance | Which mediating changes separate an action from the claimed outcome? | Working resource access may enable an activity; learner participation, instructional quality and independent practice still mediate a learning effect. Counted arrows are not an effect estimate. |
| Feedback destination | Which decision could this observation change? | A rendering failure may require an interface correction; a well-delivered activity with no durable benefit may require a different instructional policy or hypothesis. Recurrence alone does not diagnose the responsible level. |
| Authority | Who may make or change this particular decision, for this resource and purpose? | An educator may assign an activity without authority to disclose a learner's whole record. |
| Lifecycle and control moment | When does a statement apply, and by when must control act? | A revoked grant must stop the next controlled disclosure; a later review cannot undo an already completed disclosure. |

This distinction is already grounded in the collection's stage matrix, [state/context contracts](06-state-rights-and-context.md), [public-value requirements](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md), and [F18's capability model](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md). It sharpens the questions those sources already require. It does not assign educational authority to an engineering feedback mechanism.

### 9.2 Preserve the correspondence between sources, judgements and projections

Three representational roles often coexist inside A2–A5. Their identities and authority differ even if the same storage technology carries all three.

| Role | Minimum meaning to retain | Relationship to other roles |
|---|---|---|
| Source occurrence | Identified document/record, revision, exact region or event, issuer/origin, original context and capture limits | Repeated wording in two documents remains two occurrences. A shared upstream origin qualifies independence; equal text does not establish equal authority or correction rights. |
| Qualified assertion or judgement | What is claimed, by whom, about which object, under what conditions; interpretation method, uncertainty, alternatives, validity and permission for use | A source's own claim, a reviewer judgement and a learner hypothesis are different assertions. An observation can be retained without inventing an inferred assertion. |
| Consumer projection | What a named consumer receives or uses, for which purpose/audience, from which eligible versions, after which transformations and exclusions | A projection can omit a teacher-only note for a learner view while the admitted original remains intact. It cannot silently become the source of authority for a stronger claim. |

The required correspondence records source units, qualified meanings and output units where they exist. Mappings may split, combine, reorder or omit; they can be zero-to-many or many-to-one. A generated explanation is an authored output with its own status. A navigational index or cache is a projection, even if it is the easiest surface to read. If a source is corrected, a metadata-only permission change can invalidate a projection without changing the original text's hash. Accordingly, a derivation's identity must include the material policy/authority/context versions on which its eligibility depends.

This refines B03–B06/B10/B13/B16/B23/B24. The semantic basis is [F20 §5.2–5.3](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md), [F07's custody and lifecycle distinctions](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md), and [purpose and correction across derivatives](06-state-rights-and-context.md#7-correction-revocation-retirement-and-erasure). It does not select an ontology, graph database or universal context compiler. See companion profiles BP-02, BP-04 and BP-05.

### 9.3 A relation name does not supply an inference rule

When a relation is queried, composed or relied on by a machine, its contract needs permitted endpoint classes; direction; whether an inverse exists and its meaning; any scoped transitivity or symmetry; multiplicity and parallel occurrence rules; applicability time; supporting and challenging evidence; authority; and aggregation/projection rules. A particular bridge must then warrant its own instance of that relation. A valid relation definition does not prove the claim using it.

For example, `derived-from` can identify ancestry while preserving multiple occurrences. It does not make every ancestor independent supporting evidence. `depends-on` can define an operational prerequisite; a path does not prove educational causation. `supports` and `contradicts` require compatible claim scope and cannot be treated as automatically transitive. `authorises` is bound to a decision, actor, resource, purpose and time, rather than being inherited through arbitrary relationship paths. `represents` must declare which meanings survive and any permitted inverse. BP-01 supplies exact negative scenarios.

### 9.4 Account for cumulative loss without inventing universal equivalence

For each composed path, list the channels material to the final claim: identity, content/qualifiers, uncertainty, audience/disclosure, disciplinary construct, authority, timing and completeness are common examples. Each step states whether it preserves a channel under the named profile, changes it with a justified and authorised disposition, omits it, cannot represent it, or has not established the result. These are per-channel statements; one overall `lossless` flag is insufficient.

An end-to-end exactness claim is bounded by what every required step warrants. A later exact copy cannot restore an upstream omitted qualifier. A lossy mapping followed by a stable round trip is still lossy relative to the original. Recovery from an identified original creates an explicit new derivation with new evidence; it is not retroactive proof that the lossy path preserved the meaning. Where no valid common construct or semantic inverse exists, retain incomparability. A subject or access judgement cannot be replaced by a successful serialization check.

Supported-profile limits distinguish unavailable evidence, unimplemented support, deliberately excluded scope, demonstrated representational inability, and authorised loss. Each has a different next action. An input rejected outside a supported profile may demonstrate correct rejection; it does not prove that every advertised input is supported. The source archive remains unchanged; this successor may synthesise useful meaning under the commissioning brief. This sharpens B04–B06/B10/B11/B16/B21/B24 and [the C21 projection/loss contract](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md#time-effects-graphs-and-algorithms); BP-02/BP-03/BP-10 supply the corresponding checks.

### 9.5 Match evidence receipts to the claim they can establish

| Evidence object | What it may establish | What remains a separate claim |
|---|---|---|
| Source lineage | Origin, source version, transformation and attributed assertion | Truth, independence of corroboration, or use by the service |
| Eligibility decision | Which item could permissibly participate for a purpose at a time | Delivery to a component, use by a policy, or benefit |
| Input membership/consumption receipt | Which identified inputs were actually supplied to, or observably read by, the named boundary | That a model attended to them, complete causal attribution, or what the learner experienced |
| Action/exposure observation | The actual selected action and, separately, delivered representation, help and observed learner interaction | Unaided learning or attribution of a choice to every input |
| Controlled contrast | A bounded policy/action difference or outcome estimate under the declared comparison | Universal causation, transport to another context, or perfect token-level influence |

Record only what can be observed and is necessary for the legitimate purpose. A context list or model-generated explanation must not be promoted into a complete causal account. A projection's explanation may itself disclose sensitive exclusions and needs its own access decision. B16 supports reproducibility; B17 supports measurement interpretation; B18 requires the causal warrant. [Calibration, expiry and correction](06-state-rights-and-context.md#33-calibration-abstention-and-e7) and [the distinct E7 causal comparison](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md#7-e7-optional-learner-state-and-causal-decision-value) continue to govern optional persistent-state proof.

### 9.6 Close the operational loop at the appropriate boundary

For consequential exchange, connect sender authority and selected meaning to the receiver's permitted purpose, explicit acceptance, actual capability, expiry/correction/revocation behaviour, acknowledgement and residual limits. A schema-valid packet does not carry unlimited former authority into a new setting. For correction, record immediate inhibition at controlled use boundaries separately from eventual reconciliation of descendants. For interruption or provider change, preserve the authorised service duty and its effect status where one exists, alongside data and configuration; a resumed process is not necessarily resumed useful work.

These are specific profiles of B02/B12–B15/B21/B23 and existing [derivative correction](06-state-rights-and-context.md#7-correction-revocation-retirement-and-erasure), [selective continuity and current authority](06-state-rights-and-context.md#8-continuity-across-workers-providers-and-life-contexts), and [practical sovereignty](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md#6-national-capability-and-practical-sovereignty) contracts. They apply to actual duties, exchanges and retained state selected for the service. The state-free baseline and a bounded task-local POC remain legitimate. BP-04–BP-07 supply the details. A material observation should identify the affected bridge, alternative explanations, responsible decision owner and cheapest discriminating check. Reopening changes the relevant claim and decision; it does not automatically activate a new architecture or wider programme.

## Proof profiles and scenario contracts

## 1. Use, record shape and result discipline

For a proposed capability or service decision, identify the claim and B01–B24 bridges on which it depends. Select the relevant profiles, bind them to actual versions and contexts, appoint competent owners, and choose scenarios that could defeat the claim. Set any consequential thresholds before observing results. Record the result with its evidence and limits; reopen only the affected claims and legitimate decisions. A scenario with an unset boundary, actor or threshold remains an incomplete protocol.

The common record is a conceptual schema. A future implementation can encode it in an appropriate format. Fields that do not apply carry a reason; omission is not a pass.

| Field | Required meaning and validation |
|---|---|
| `profile_id`, `bridge_ids`, `claim_id`, `revision` | Stable identity within a named collection/run; point to the exact claim being evaluated. The profile revision cannot silently change during a result-producing run. |
| `endpoints` | Source and target objects, object classes, versions, context and unit of analysis; include joint prerequisites where a pairwise edge is insufficient. |
| `relation_contract` | Named semantics and permitted inference, including the profile-specific laws below. A label alone is insufficient. |
| `scope` | Domain, population, task, supported inputs, host/control boundary, time interval, completeness denominator and exclusions relevant to this claim. |
| `preservation_and_loss` | Per-channel invariants, changes, omissions and unknowns; authorised dispositions and the meaning of any equivalence claim. |
| `authority` | Actor/role, resource, purpose, decision right, current permission, enforcement point and escalation. Role names here are responsibilities awaiting appointment. |
| `mechanism_and_dependencies` | The proposed way the result arises, other co-conditions, failure/recovery paths and evidence dependence. |
| `proof_plan` | Fixtures or study contrast, instruments, expected observables, adverse/boundary cases, acceptance and stopping criteria, responsible reviewers and unperformed checks. |
| `result_and_receipts` | Actual observations if performed, identified evidence, result state, interpretation and explicit claim ceiling. Until execution, set `not_run`. |
| `lifecycle` | Validity, expiry, correction, review/reopen triggers and the decision each trigger can change. |

Keep these result dimensions separate:

- **Support disposition:** supported within a named profile; not implemented; intentionally outside advertised scope; unsupported by the chosen representation with a demonstrated reason; available only with authorised loss; or evidence unavailable. A proposal cannot establish an impossibility claim.
- **Evidence result:** `not_run`, `invalid_evidence`, `failed_claim`, `inconclusive`, or `bounded_pass`. A broken instrument gives invalid evidence. A valid counterexample defeats the tested claim. A valid but imprecise study may be inconclusive.
- **Authority standing:** proposed, approved for a stated use, expired, withdrawn or disputed. This neither creates implementation nor proves an outcome.

No arithmetic combines these into one assurance score. Learning, access, rights and meaningful control retain their own acceptance conditions. Source material supports the rationale for a scenario; it is not evidence that the scenario has passed.

## 3. BP-01 — relation semantics

**Responsibility.** Make the implications of an edge explicit before a query, traversal or policy relies on it. The elementary graph structure and the domain relation are different objects. A shortest path can answer a distance question under a declared graph model; it cannot, by that fact, answer why a learner learned or which evidence is most important.

**Profile schema.** Require `endpoint_classes`; `direction`; `inverse` with its own meaning or explicit absence; `symmetry` and `transitivity` as scoped laws or explicit non-assumptions; `cardinality`; `parallel_occurrence_policy`; `valid_time`; `assertion_authority`; `support_and_challenge`; `aggregation`; and `projection_rules`. Endpoint validation belongs to the relation definition; the particular assertion still needs its own warrant. For a contradiction, specify compatible subject, time, conditions and proposition before applying any conflict-resolution rule.

| Scenario | Input and deliberate challenge | Expected observable result | Owner and remaining unsatisfied claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PS-01 parallel evidence** | Two documents repeat a claim from one upstream study; a third independently challenges it. | **PS-02 unlawful inference** | Supply a `supports` path A→B→C, a `depends-on` path and an `authorises` relationship to a different resource. | **PS-03 false conflict** | Two claims concern different populations, time periods or assistance conditions but share a short label. | Preserve qualified statements; identify scope mismatch before calling them contradictory. ## 4. BP-02 — representation and cumulative loss

**Responsibility.** Preserve or explicitly account for the meaning required by the receiving task. Separate an admitted source occurrence, any qualified judgement made about it, a planned representation, and the output actually delivered. Sometimes a direct source-to-output mapping suffices; do not manufacture an inference layer.

**Profile schema.** Identify `source_occurrences` with revision and selectors; `qualified_meaning` if interpreted; `audience_and_purpose`; `source_to_output_correspondence`; `transformations`; `semantic_channels`; `per_step_dispositions`; `cumulative_disposition`; `fallback`; `actual_output_identity`; and `review_authority`. Correspondence must represent zero/one/many mappings, including split, combination, reordering, omission and introduced material. Each transformation states what changed and why. Machine byte equality, disciplinary equivalence and accessibility adequacy are separate channels with different proof methods.

The cumulative rule is conservative: an exactness claim reaches only channels warranted throughout the required path. Authorised loss remains visible after later exact steps. Where a channel is inexpressible or unverified, a downstream claim cannot silently mark it exact. Restoring from an identified original forms a new derivation. An accepted loss disposition for a pupil view does not authorise loss of decision-relevant source meaning from this transfer collection.

| Scenario | Input and deliberate challenge | Expected observable result | Owner and remaining unsatisfied claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PS-04 erased qualifier** | A source says a finding applies to a stated age/task and has substantial uncertainty. The end-to-end claim remains qualified or fails for uses requiring those channels. A successful downstream round trip does not restore the missing meaning. | Knowledge/artifact owners; subject reviewer where used pedagogically. Exact rendering does not warrant individual teaching use. |
| **PS-05 audience and answer disclosure** | A reviewed activity contains a teacher-only note and a concealed worked step. Human review is needed to establish which support changes the target thinking. |
| **PS-06 specialist extension** | An activity includes a representation whose semantics the consumer does not understand. Supply a generic textual fallback and an unsupported extension. | Use a reviewed fallback only if it supports the named disciplinary/access purpose; otherwise report inability and offer an appropriate alternative route. Do not silently discard specialist meaning or declare all fallbacks equivalent. | Subject/access and adapter owners. No universal medium-conversion or round-trip claim follows. |
| **PS-07 derivative reconciliation** | A consumer edits a derived activity; several source units were combined, and one output unit was added. ## 11. BP-09 — public value and feedback

**Responsibility.** Identify the particular value claim and its mechanism, then route contrary evidence to the decision it can actually change. Capability demonstration, useful professional work, repeated reuse, independent adoption, learner benefit and institutional capability are legitimate but different outcomes. None automatically proves the next.

**Profile schema.** Require `beneficiaries_and_other_affected_parties`; `claimed_value`; `mediating_changes`; `counterfactual_or_comparator`; `distribution`; `full_lifecycle_burden_and_cost`; `adoption_and_receiver_conditions`; `dependency_and_exit`; `observation`; `alternative_explanations`; `affected_bridge`; `decision_owner`; `next_discriminator`; and `continue_stop_transform_or_reopen_action`. Record direct outcomes and bounded enabling contributions separately. Do not invent multipliers for downstream benefits merely because a chain can be drawn.

## 13. Acceptance and change control

Before a profile is relied on, its responsible owner must name the actual implementation/claim revision, supported scope, relevant control moment, required reviewers and observable acceptance conditions. A bounded bench case can be valuable without pretending to establish participant suitability or effect. A participant study needs its own instruments, comparator, operational arrangements and authority. Numerical thresholds that are not settled remain explicit decisions in those owning protocols.

A result receipt states the executed scenario/version, fixture or population, actual observations, evidence identity, support/result/authority states, remaining claim ceiling and any decision made. A failed or inconclusive scenario need not invalidate unrelated bridges; record the actual dependency path. Changes to source meaning, eligible use, profile, model, policy, host, assessment, population or critical provider reopen the affected proof. Feedback is useful when it changes a warranted decision or exposes the next discriminating uncertainty, rather than merely increasing the number of review artifacts.

These profiles make the model's existing bridges concrete. The service remains an additive public tuition service using Oak curriculum and OCE capabilities with independently justified composition. Engineering correctness, educational warrant, human authority, practical access, learning effect and public value each retain the proof appropriate to their own claim.
