# Public AI-enabled tuition: service concept

**Reconstruction date:** 6 September 2026. **Status:** current intent and preserved design proposals; educational and operational effects remain to be established. This is the substantive home for public purpose, intended value and service responsibilities. [The evaluation contract](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md) owns the proposed studies, measurements and seven service gates.

## 1. Purpose and authority

Expand access to excellent tuition that helps people build durable knowledge, understanding, fluency, judgement, transfer, metacognition and increasing independence. Use AI to create valuable opportunities to practise, investigate, compare representations, receive feedback, create and revise work, alongside teachers, peers, families and other human support. Build useful public capability whose value can extend across subjects and throughout life.

The service is a genuine public offering, with purposeful activities and dependable functions. Educational quality determines what it should enable. It adds to teaching and to the relationships, institutions, resources and communities through which people learn. Learners should become more capable of acting and learning well, including knowing when to seek help and how to use others' expertise.

Oak's complete, high-quality curriculum is an established project asset, supplied through the Oak API and bulk downloads. OCE is part of the owner's own engineering estate and can be directly composed and extended. Curriculum access, software capability, pedagogical expertise and educational impact are distinct assets or claims. The task is to use these assets to enable valuable activity and dependable services; [08](08-oce-audit-and-engineering-findings.md) describes the inspected engineering estate and [13](13-oce-work-programme-and-integration.md) connects it to useful work.

| Standing | Direction and practical consequence |
|---|---|
| Current user commitment | Build a public AI-enabled tuition service POC and run experiments. Develop through managed Platform as a Service delivery. Preserve all-subject and lifelong direction, pedagogical excellence, learner agency, excellent accessibility/SEND support and public value. |
| Governing design constraints | Use one public kernel with governed subject–phase–context profiles; preserve educational, observational, inferred, action, outcome and authority distinctions. Operate with the smallest necessary current-task state; persistent inferred state must earn its place. Details are in [03](03-learner-experience-and-profiles.md), [06](06-state-rights-and-context.md) and [04](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md). |
| Preserved research proposals | Mathematics and English as first contrasting profiles; England-first delivery with later jurisdictional federation; Year 9 proportional/multiplicative reasoning in high-disadvantage schools as an initial causal study; matched non-generative practice and a four-week delayed unaided near-transfer primary. Their rationale survives; exact population, allocation, instruments, thresholds and sequencing remain to be decided. |
| Future choices | Providers, detailed architecture, profile granularity, operational entitlements and capacity, institutional appointments, funding, later subjects and contexts. Reuse existing OCE capabilities while making these choices against actual consumer needs. |
| Stage-specific authority | Building and investigating are committed. Participant research, operational provision, procurement, wider adoption and scale each require the applicable evidence and accountable decision. Successful technical work informs those decisions. |

Earlier builds can themselves deliver useful capabilities and resolve distinct uncertainties: reviewed task resources, interactive representations, evidence capture, accessible journeys and repeatable evaluation. Select them by beneficiary value and what their operation makes observable. The final bounded POC tests educational mechanisms and additional value. The experiment programme is a means to improve the service and choose later directions, with its results and reusable assets valuable even when a particular learning configuration changes.

## 2. Whose needs and welfare count

Unequal access to timely practice, feedback and educational support is the motivating problem. The historical report records 2024/25 GCSE mathematics grade 4+ attainment of 53% for disadvantaged pupils against 72% nationally, citing the [EEF secondary mathematics research agenda](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/research-agenda-themes-priority-areas/research-agenda-theme-secondary-maths), accessed 4 September 2026. This is dated contextual evidence, not an estimate of the service's potential effect or a reason to equate disadvantage with low attainment.

| Participant or beneficiary | Intended value | Consequential burden or decision right |
|---|---|---|
| Learners in school, including underserved learners | Timely subject-valid practice, exploration, feedback, challenge and increasing unaided capability | Effort, time, privacy, access, dignity and choice; accessible information, correction, help and remedy. Research participation uses the applicable voluntary assent/consent arrangements. |
| Teachers and other educators | Useful formative evidence, purposeful activity and practical professional control | Preparation, configuration, review, correction, incidents, vigilance and professional learning; authority to interpret, change support, override and escalate. |
| Families and chosen supporters | Intelligible purposes, appropriate support and clear routes to challenge or help | Burden, access and privacy remain visible; role and information entitlement depend on context. |
| Schools, colleges and learning institutions | Dependable support integrated with curriculum and local educational practice | They retain their educational, adjustment and safeguarding responsibilities; a product cannot assume or discharge them merely by offering a dashboard. |
| Disabled/SEND learners and specialists | Ambitious, construct-preserving routes through the whole activity, with ordinary assistive technology and effective specialist or human alternatives | Paid affected-user participation, meaningful influence, correction, accessible recovery and protection against concentrated verification or access labour. |
| Adult/community/workplace learners | Goal-directed learning, reskilling, professional and personal development with appropriate continuity | Adults control goals, pace, material inferences, selected evidence export and exit. Their provider/employer does not acquire a hidden psychological dossier. |
| Independent adopters and small organisations | Public knowledge, specifications, fixtures, components and evidence that reduce duplicated work | Implementability, clear rights, preserved meaning, maintainable integration, measurable burden and exercised supplier substitution. |
| Public owner, evaluator and steward | Decision-grade evidence, public accountability, durable options and continuing value | Publish safe methods and null/adverse findings, manage conflicts and costs, preserve independent evaluation, maintain correction and exit obligations. |

The specific school proposal concerns whole Year 9 classes in English state-funded secondary schools meeting a prespecified high-disadvantage threshold. FSM/Pupil Premium and other relevant variables are proposed for distributional analysis. Eligibility must be transparent and accessible; non-participants should receive curriculum-equivalent non-AI provision. Any later entitlement is a separate capacity decision. The source proposes free-at-point-of-use, advertising-free, adjustment-capable provision backed by human help; this is a service design direction, not an entitlement already in force.

Success includes durable learning and fair access, professional capacity, learner dignity and agency, reliability, manageable burden, full-lifecycle affordability and public options. Plausible spillovers—better curriculum data, shared evaluations, lower procurement/adopter burden—remain unbanked until observed. Learner, family, teacher, institution and steward costs count even when absent from the software budget.

## 3. Educational scope and human relationships

The service spans all subjects and lifelong learning. It is subject-specific in pedagogy: a common kernel carries shared responsibilities while profiles state the learning goal, disciplinary practice, PCK, tools/representations, permitted moves, access, assessment crosswalk, evidence, authority and versions. [03](03-learner-experience-and-profiles.md) contains the full contract and examples. Architectural extensibility, suitability for a context and demonstrated effect are separate propositions.

Mathematics and English offer a useful first contrast. Mathematics tests constrained strategies, multi-representation reasoning, errors and transfer. English tests textual evidence, interpretive plurality, discussion, audience and purpose, drafting, revision, voice and authorship. Neither can be reduced to a subject label appended to a generic instruction. Later scientific, historical, linguistic, computational, creative, practical, performative and vocational activities may require materially different tools, evidence and human roles.

School, post-16 continuing study, GCSE resits, FE/HE, vocational/workplace, professional, community, informal and later-life learning are different educational settings. Resitting a qualification and wider post-16 continuing study are also different constructs. Subject/phase/context evidence and authority travel through explicit mappings. Preparation for a laboratory activity or performance may be valuable while practical competence still requires physical activity and expert observation. Welsh/bilingual or other jurisdictional profiles require curriculum, language, assessment and governance work beyond translation.

Good education has a broader outcome profile than any single POC score: durable and organised knowledge; understanding; appropriate use and transfer; epistemic judgement; increasing self-direction; agency and belonging; social, ethical and civic formation; equity and wellbeing. Those aims and the teaching knowledge needed to pursue them are conserved in [02](02-education-and-teaching-knowledge.md). Human educators' rich understanding of people is not a grant of permission for automated sensitive inference. [06](06-state-rights-and-context.md) owns the service's narrower permitted state.

A useful learning episode has the functions of orientation, genuine attempt, cautious interpretation, warranted support, new learner action, unaided checking, fading/revisiting and a return of intelligible evidence to the accountable learner or educator. Their form and ordering need profile validation; they are not an empirically universal linear script. AI can propose a question, contrast, representation, simulation, source, critique or practice opportunity within the approved activity. The learner does the inference, retrieval, composition or practice being developed. Removing an irrelevant access demand can help preserve that work; completing the target reasoning for the learner can displace it. Purpose and stage determine which is occurring.

Teachers establish purpose and sequence, approve relevant activity and support bounds, interpret evidence and choose follow-up. They are instructional partners. Learner-directed adult profiles allocate equivalent goal and support decisions explicitly. Peers, families, community and specialists remain part of learning; independence includes effective interdependence and well-judged help-seeking.

## 4. How the proposed value arises

Three connected claims require separate evidence. First, educationally valid activity may help a learner build durable capability. Second, public knowledge and engineering assets may reduce the burden of other organisations delivering worthwhile services. Third, accountable and sustained institutions may turn those gains into durable public value. None establishes the others by implication.

### 4.1 The learner mechanism and its rivals

| Transition | Proposed mechanism and necessary conditions | Rival, observable test and claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Interaction quality → appropriate use | A valid purpose/task, genuine attempt, uncertainty-aware interpretation and accessible support make productive action possible; educator framing supplies context | Fluent error, inaccessible mode, coercion, novelty and gaming may mimic success. Inspect attempt quality, support level, correction and affected-user experience. Expert agreement establishes covered process quality only. |
| Appropriate use → assisted performance | A warranted question, representation or feedback helps the learner revise the current strategy | Copying, guessing and answer leakage also produce correctness. Inspect revision and explanation as well as score. Assisted improvement is a leading indicator. |
| Assisted performance → immediate unaided attainment | Generation plus useful feedback changes available knowledge/strategy | Recency, memorised surface form or easier test items remain rivals. Use counterbalanced, equivalent no-help tasks. Failure there rejects immediate acquisition despite supported success. |
| Immediate attainment → delayed retention and near transfer | Retrieval, spacing and organised understanding preserve capability and enable structurally related unfamiliar work | Short rehearsal, novelty decay, template learning and selective follow-up may explain gains. The proposed four-week independent near-transfer measure tests the configured package; a separate exploratory 12-week follow-up can examine persistence. |
| Near transfer → bounded farther transfer | Multiple representations, comparison and explanation may support recognising the same relation in more distant contexts | Covert overlap or changed construct/reliability undermines the inference. Independently specify novelty and distance. Near-transfer success is valuable but does not prove farther transfer. |
| Transfer → increasing independence | Fading, self-explanation and metacognitive support shift regulation towards the learner | Easier withdrawal tasks, reduced attempts, dependency or inflated confidence can mislead. Compare matched work after complete withdrawal and examine appropriate help-seeking with context. This is bounded independence, not lifelong self-sufficiency. |

Moderators include subject and goal, age/phase, prior attainment, disadvantage, SEND/access setting, language, lawful demographics, teacher practice, class/school, devices/connectivity, dosage, concurrent teaching and fidelity. Measures and falsifiers belong in [04](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md); profile-specific interpretations belong in [03](03-learner-experience-and-profiles.md).

The empirical reason to test this chain is substantive. The 5 September source report records generally positive but heterogeneous and short-study ITS evidence in Létourneau and colleagues' 28-study K–12 review, while warning that stronger comparators attenuate effects. Bastani and colleagues' study of nearly 1,000 Turkish high-school pupils found a 17% lower subsequent unaided score for an unconstrained GPT condition relative to no AI despite improved practice; the safeguarded condition avoided a detected decrement but did not demonstrate a positive unaided advantage. Oreopoulos and colleagues' working paper covering more than 6,000 US middle-school pupils found conditional-accuracy gains alongside slower progression/attempts, with limited delayed evidence on practised material. These are bounded studies, not a forecast for this service. Their practical implication is to evaluate the actual package against a credible active comparator and observe learning after support is removed. Detailed dossiers, denominator disputes and source limits are in [10](10-comparative-research-and-evidence.md).

### 4.2 Public capability and institutional value

Public specifications and artefacts can support non-author implementation, conformance, deployment and actual use. If meaning and task completion survive, adopters may spend less time and effort and gain genuine substitution options. Sustained upgrades, maintenance and exercised exit can then contribute public value. Each step can fail through unclear rights, hidden semantics, author dependence, unused integrations, subsidy effects, captive operations or unfunded stewardship. [09](09-public-value-standards-and-commons.md) preserves the full ladder, public package, governance zones and operating obligations.

Accountable service ownership should connect accessible, reliable operation with meaningful adoption, educational practice, institutional learning and continuing stewardship. Public owners commission evidence; evaluators and gate specialists challenge; institutions retain applicable authority; stewards maintain profiles and common assets; suppliers provide replaceable capabilities. This is a proposed allocation of responsibilities, not a staffed institution already established. [05](05-inclusion-and-human-service.md) details the human service and [11](11-evidence-publication-and-decisions.md) the force and use of evidence.

## 5. What the service must provide, prove and enable

The following complete responsibility map consolidates the 5 September report's useful service contract. Rows describe responsibilities, not workspaces, deployment units or a staffing plan. “Must” expresses the applicable project constraint, proposed participant/release condition or externally applicable duty; legal force remains separately classified in [11](11-evidence-publication-and-decisions.md). Proposed capabilities are not claims of current implementation. Specialist files own the operational details.

| Capability class | Must provide | Must prove at the relevant gate | Must enable; boundary and owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Learner tuition/guided practice** | Task purpose, attempt, diagnosis, scaffold, feedback/action, fade, uncertainty and human help | Valid moves; no systematic leakage/dependency; delayed benefit after withdrawal | Accessible teacher-directed use; competitive implementation, public behavioural/evidence profile; education owner |
| **Teacher/educator service** | Preview, assign, set bounds/adjustments, inspect provenance/evidence, correct, override, pause, intervene/escalate | Control is understood and used; decision quality; calibrated trust; acceptable total workload | Local pedagogy and policy without supplier dependency; school/teacher owner |
| **Curriculum/domain knowledge** | Authoritative concepts or declared adult goals, progression, prerequisites, content-scope boundaries, identifiers, mappings, versions and provenance | Correct authority, coverage, currency and meaning; mappings independently reviewed | Jurisdiction, subject and context profiles and replacement; publicly governed, no learner data; curriculum/domain steward |
| **Pedagogical content knowledge** | Subject-, topic-, phase- and context-specific representations, explanations, contrasts, examples, characteristic error patterns, candidate misconception hypotheses and permitted moves | Independent educator judgement, declared disagreement and move reliability in covered cases; learner outcomes evaluate the configured package, not pedagogical content knowledge in isolation | Profile revision without changing curriculum authority; pedagogical profile steward with subject educators |
| **Model-of-learning propositions** | Bounded, condition-bearing propositions about processes such as prior knowledge, feedback, retrieval, spacing, explanation, metacognition and fading | Evidence synthesis states population, subject, context and uncertainty; POC tests the configured mechanism | Constrain candidate designs without defining curriculum, diagnosing an individual or becoming a universal teaching recipe; research/evaluation lead |
| **Observation and declaration records** | Direct, minimised learner events with provenance and exposure; separately attributed goals, access settings and relevant context | Record accuracy, correction, minimisation, purpose and retention controls; no inferred mastery | Evidence for bounded decisions without turning declarations into observations or service-selected exposures into neutral data; education owner advised by privacy lead |
| **Learner-state estimation — optional** | If justified, expiring concept/strategy or possible-misconception hypotheses with evidence, confidence, estimator version, inspection, correction and reset | Calibration against policy-independent held-out/common anchors, subgroup/access-mode validity and incremental value over no derived state | Reversible support only; no significant decisions, portable whole-person profile or silent cross-subject inference; education owner advised by privacy, accessibility and evaluation leads |
| **Tuition policy and action** | A versioned approved mapping to a bounded move repertoire, plus the separately logged task, prompt, hint, representation or hand-off actually delivered | Policy/action conformance, expert case performance, exposures and overrides; causal policy value only through a risk-bounded independent contrast | Subject-valid variation without fabrication or live self-modification; an action is not state or outcome evidence; education owner and profile steward |
| **Independent learner-outcome measurement** | Separately governed immediate, delayed, transfer and withdrawal constructs, instruments, scoring and analyses | Construct validity, reliability, contamination control, accommodation validity and evaluator independence | Assessment authority owns instruments, scoring and warranted inferences—not the learner's outcome; institutions retain progression decisions; protected records remain purpose-limited |
| **Content/resource stewardship** | Licensed sources, attribution, quality review, correction, version/restriction/withdrawal | Rights and provenance complete; withdrawn/error content ceases use | Multiple content providers and safe replacement; public metadata, content only under its licence; content steward |
| **Accessibility/language/adjustment** | Inclusive core, assistive-technology compatibility, alternative modes, cognitive accessibility, approved adjustments, low-bandwidth fallback and specialist/human route | WCAG/EN baseline plus access-needs matrix, affected-user completion of authentic core journeys and construct-preserving access within declared coverage; critical barriers block release | Local adjustments and later subject/jurisdiction/language profiles; accessibility owner with paid affected-user governance |
| **Privacy/identity/rights/redress** | Minimum role/authorisation, clear information, access/correction/deletion, objection/challenge, audit and complaint route | Lawful roles/bases, DPIA, minimisation, retention/deletion, transfer and breach controls work | Research with synthetic/controlled data and provider change; personal data protected; controller/service owner, advised and monitored by DPO |
| **Safeguarding/safety/incident** | Prevention, detection, human escalation, case handling, remedy, disclosure and organisational learning | Scenario/operational coverage, severity tolerances, DSL route and incident drills; continuing assurance | Local contacts/policy and cross-provider evidence without publishing exploitable/personal detail; safeguarding owner |
| **Evaluation/benchmark/research** | Versioned constructs, tasks, rubrics, human scoring, fidelity/adverse-event and longitudinal protocol | Reliability, coverage, contamination control, evaluator independence and causal validity | Replication, audit and cumulative learning; public methods/results, protected microdata; independent evaluator |
| **Organisation/ecosystem adoption** | Onboarding, policy/configuration guidance, documentation, support and contribution/correction routes | A small unaffiliated adopter completes workflow with measured burden | Local curriculum/institution adaptation and supplier competition; adoption steward |
| **Interoperability/conformance** | Bounded profiles, stable identifiers, examples, fixtures, validators, implementation reports and capability declarations | Non-author implementation, semantic/task preservation, version migration and representative exit | Independent integration/substitution; publicly governed artefacts, no forced central learner record; standards steward |
| **Public-service operations/governance** | Accountable ownership, support, versioned change/incident control, adaptation evidence register, continuity, cost, rollback and retirement | Service objectives, staged-release/recovery tests, audit, whole-life value, transparent decisions and exercised exit | Challenge, redress, organisational learning, succession and reuse by future services; public owner, competitively provided replaceable operations |


## 6. Additionality, alternatives and the strongest challenges

Additionality asks whether this particular intervention adds worthwhile public value beyond plausible alternatives and opportunity costs. Public provision is a legitimate purpose; private provision is not the default against which its existence must be justified.

| Realistic alternative | Existing value and implication for this service |
|---|---|
| Teacher-led provision and present school practice | Accountable relationships and contextual judgement. Document the baseline and support effective teaching. |
| Human tuition | Valuable relational and instructional support with variable availability, delivery and cost. Use as an economic/educational benchmark; its observed effects do not transfer to AI. |
| Conventional intelligent systems or high-quality non-generative practice | Structured practice, examples, diagnosis and feedback with less generative uncertainty. The proposed primary active comparator matches these capabilities. |
| Simpler curated, state-free activity/resource service | Can deliver reviewed resources, purposeful activities, progression and evidence with current-task context and minimal exposure history. Compare before adding persistent inference or broad autonomous scheduling. |
| Existing commercial and open systems | Useful components, delivery capacity and research. Evaluate fit, effect evidence, rights and exit; open code alone supplies neither local educational authority nor operational assurance. |
| Educator/collaborator copilot | May improve human support with lower direct learner exposure; a serious Transform route if direct activity fails on independence, safety or burden. It needs evidence for the human-supported configuration. |
| Generic AI assistants | Informative capability/comparator context. Open-ended provision to children is not automatically an ethical experimental control. |
| Independent institutional builds/procurements | Can achieve local fit but may repeat assurance and make meanings incompatible. Shared public assets earn their place when non-authors demonstrate lower burden and preserved decisions. |

The strongest service countercase is no worthwhile delayed advantage over matched non-AI practice while adding error, dependency, correction, rights, cost and continuity burdens. Evidence supporting it should change or end the affected learner-facing configuration; it does not determine all subjects' future. The strongest common-kernel countercase is that it obstructs valid subject pedagogy or repeatedly requires rewriting while simpler or human-led implementations work better. That observation should change the shared boundary. A generic, undifferentiated policy erasing subject meaning is not a satisfactory route to breadth.

Later evidence can support **Continue**, **Stop**, **Transform**, or **Focus on supporting other services**, separately for different profiles or capabilities. [04](04-poc-requirements-and-evaluation.md#10-decisions-after-evidence) supplies the full triggers, owners and surviving duties. A stopped learner activity may leave useful public evidence or independently adopted tools; public reuse cannot compensate for a material educational or rights failure in that activity.
