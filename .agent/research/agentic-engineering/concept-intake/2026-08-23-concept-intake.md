## Portable agent capability

### OCE-IDEA-198 — Stop compatibility evidence climbing the claim ladder

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It develops layered validation in
  [OCE-IDEA-061](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-061--apply-layered-validation)
  and cross-host semantic comparison in
  [OCE-IDEA-139](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-139--evaluate-semantic-conformance-across-hosts),
  beneath the accepted portability boundary in
  [ADR-125](../../../../docs/architecture/architectural-decisions/125-agent-artefact-portability.md).
- **OCE-native idea:** state separately whether evidence establishes interface acceptance, declared
  capability, configured behaviour, behaviour observed under attempted violation, semantic parity
  or outcome parity. A lower claim never proves a higher one. Bind each observation to the acting
  principal, exact configuration, effective degradation, host, fixture, time and expiry.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can reuse portable operations while reviewers
  still see whether two hosts merely accepted the same request, behaved alike under one test or
  produced equivalent outcomes. Routing and assurance can use the narrowest supported claim.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes layered validation, runtime evidence and
  semantic portability. The tension is cost: an elaborate claim matrix is waste unless distinctions
  change routing, rollback or review.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE carries canonical agent material through several host
  expressions. A shared shape can remain valid while configuration, denial behaviour and semantic
  outcome diverge.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** observed behaviour under a test is not proof of
  universal enforcement, safety accreditation, plan acceptance or provider quality. Do not inherit
  an external vocabulary, aggregate pass mark or self-certification scheme.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** exercise one existing portable operation on two supported
  hosts with a normal case, an unsupported capability and one attempted policy violation. Record
  the narrow claim each result warrants, compare activated semantics and task outcome separately,
  and ask whether an independently derived negative test changes routing or review.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into existing conformance fixtures if they already prevent
  every upward inference with current principal, configuration and expiry evidence. Stop if the
  distinctions change no decision while materially increasing adapter and review cost.
- **Likely OCE home:** agent capability conformance research; only decision-bearing claim types
  should enter reusable fixtures.

## Coordination, observation and intervention

### OCE-IDEA-199 — Keep coordination visibility separate from actuation authority

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes the accepted
  [inter-agent collaboration protocol](../../../practice-core/decision-records/PDR-056-inter-agent-collaboration-protocol.md)
  with the discovery concerns in
  [OCE-IDEA-092](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-092--use-evidence-backed-coordination)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-099](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-099--govern-source-intervention-as-a-reversible-experiment).
  It does not change existing collaboration authority.
- **OCE-native idea:** make the live coordination view a rebuildable, read-only projection of
  claims, dependencies, liveness, overlap and unresolved boundaries. Any operation that assigns,
  cancels, resumes or mutates work crosses a separate actuation boundary carrying explicit scope,
  current eligibility, principal, expiry and an append-only receipt bound to the exact canonical
  authorizing decision, approved operation and target. Exposure records distinguish attempted,
  delivered and acknowledged notice; none alone proves understanding or consent. Seeing a possible
  action never grants permission to perform it. A proposed coordination-state mutation also binds a
  base registry generation and the claimant's current write set; expiry, overlap or authority change
  produces conflict or refusal rather than an implicit shared-plan edit.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can improve fleet awareness and suggest ready
  work without allowing a convenient dashboard or scheduler to become a rival source of authority.
  Actuation can be introduced one reversible operation at a time and withdrawn without losing the
  observatory.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes evidence-backed coordination with
  reversible intervention and derived-state disposability. The tension is duplicated state:
  eligibility can drift between the canonical work surface and a live projection, especially
  across interruption or delayed approval.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** collaboration tools already expose identities, claims,
  handoffs and communications while agents can also perform consequential repository operations.
  Combining those concerns in one interface creates pressure for observational state to authorise
  action implicitly.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** do not introduce autonomous scheduling,
  personnel surveillance, universal team topology or activity-based performance measures. A live
  presence signal must not imply capability, employment authority, consent or availability for
  assignment. Claim loss invalidates pending and descendant mutations; per-claim and global bounds
  prevent derived growth from becoming an unbounded execution plan.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** build a disposable view over a small fixture set containing a
  blocked dependency, an expiring claim and a protected manual participant. Race one explicitly
  approved action against an authority revision; separately try a stale registry base, an
  out-of-scope mutation, descendant work after claim loss and growth beyond a declared bound.
  Preserve attempted, delivered and unacknowledged notice states, and distinguish reversible
  rollback from compensation after a completed effect.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** stop at the read-only view if current tools already prevent
  authority inversion and stale execution, or if any approved action can run after its canonical
  eligibility changes, target a protected participant or survive cancellation without a visible
  reconciliation failure.
- **Likely OCE home:** agent-tools coordination research; a delivery plan only after the read-only
  projection demonstrates a recurring, measured coordination problem.

## Practice learning and evidence quality

### OCE-IDEA-200 — Bind learning progress to a replayable coverage proof

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes the accepted two-speed learning
  distinction in
  [PDR-130](../../../practice-core/decision-records/PDR-130-two-speed-learning.md)
  with the terminal-state, failure and anti-resurrection concerns in
  [OCE-IDEA-050](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-050--preserve-distinct-extraction-failure-states),
  [OCE-IDEA-118](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-118--give-prospective-memory-terminal-semantics)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-126](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-126--add-anti-memory-and-anti-resurrection-state).
- **OCE-native idea:** bind every progress checkpoint to an exact input generation and a replayable
  coverage record. Append-only history derives exactly one current disposition or visible open-debt
  state for each preceding item; open debt remains retryable and is never counted as learned.
  Dispositions and checkpoint advance share one recoverable commit boundary, or the checkpoint is
  derived from the dispositions.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** asynchronous learning can restart cheaply without
  losing hard cases or repeatedly processing the same input. The checkpoint proves how the covered
  population was handled rather than guessing how far a worker progressed.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes first-class ignorance, typed failure and
  correction-first learning. The tension is backlog pressure: visible unresolved debt can grow
  indefinitely unless retry, deferral and governed closure remain bounded and reviewable.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE's learning lane separates fast operational candidates
  from slower promotion, and candidate generation may eventually run asynchronously over selected
  episodes. A simple last-seen marker would make infrastructure failure indistinguishable from a
  legitimate decision to learn nothing.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** this does not authorise ambient capture,
  automatic promotion, people-level monitoring or indefinite retention. Eligibility, purpose,
  permission, standing and withdrawal remain governed outside the progress mechanism; a disposition
  can retain an identity and reason without retaining sensitive payload.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** process a small synthetic input generation, inject failures at
  different processing boundaries and interrupt one commit. Restart twice, then replace one input
  generation with a changed successor. Require every input to resolve to exactly one current
  disposition or open-debt state, with no duplicate candidate and a checkpoint reproducible from
  durable evidence for the correct generation.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** stop if a simpler append-only disposition log already proves
  exact coverage and restart behaviour, or if the mechanism cannot bound unresolved debt, storage
  and withdrawal handling.
- **Likely OCE home:** a shadow Practice-learning experiment; only its coverage-proof contract
  should graduate if the experiment creates useful candidates.

## Safety and assurance

### OCE-IDEA-201 — Give review workers capability-wise least authority

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It develops the untrusted-input boundary in
  [OCE-IDEA-076](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-076--secure-corpus-processing-as-untrusted-input-handling)
  and progressive assurance in
  [OCE-IDEA-177](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-177--apply-progressive-assurance),
  consistent with current
  [safety and security governance](../../../../docs/governance/safety-and-security.md).
- **OCE-native idea:** derive a review worker's authority from its review purpose rather than
  inheriting the authority of the work or worker being assessed. Set filesystem, network,
  credential, mutation, egress and resource permissions independently; expose only the bounded
  evidence interface needed for the claim. An exceptional grant names legitimate approval, target,
  purpose and expiry, is non-transitive, remains revocable and leaves an immutable receipt.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can use model-assisted review or candidate
  derivation without letting hostile source material turn the evaluator into an execution or
  exfiltration path. Evaluation failure remains evidence about the method rather than a reason to
  remove the protected boundary silently.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes corpus safety, least authority and
  progressive assurance without pretending privilege is one total ordering. The tension is
  validity: a worker deprived of relevant context may produce shallow or misleading judgments, so
  each capability boundary must be tested rather than made universally narrow.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE agents can hold repository write access, connected-tool
  authority and sensitive local context. Reusing the same ambient authority for background review
  would make the supposed assurance process more capable than its subject needs to be.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** do not treat sandbox presence as proof of
  semantic correctness, and do not send private evidence to an external evaluator merely because
  local tools are disabled. Human review remains the fallback where the decision requires standing
  or context that cannot be safely represented.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** compare a purpose-specific reviewer with the ordinary review
  path on a synthetic adversarial corpus where the subject has broader and partly disjoint
  authority. Attempt filesystem mutation, network egress, credential access and resource exhaustion
  independently; require non-inheritance and bounded use. Grant one exceptional capability, then
  prove its target restriction, non-transitivity, expiry and revocation while decision-relevant
  recall remains adequate.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** stop if no bounded evidence interface can retain the information
  needed for the decision. Escalate that decision to a legitimate reviewer rather than restoring
  broad evaluator privilege by default.
- **Likely OCE home:** agent safety and Practice-learning research; reusable execution constraints
  only after adversarial fixture evidence.

## Governed experimentation

### OCE-IDEA-202 — Let experiments publish coherent generations without gaining authority

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes the branching portfolio in
  [OCE-IDEA-019](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-019--use-a-branching-gated-portfolio),
  singular semantic authority in
  [OCE-IDEA-021](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-021--keep-one-authority-per-semantic-claim),
  immutable generation, atomic publication and reader binding in
  [OCE-IDEA-064](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-064--define-an-immutable-generation-manifest),
  [OCE-IDEA-065](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-065--publish-complete-generations-atomically)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-066](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-066--bind-readers-to-one-generation),
  without changing the authority of any of them.
- **OCE-native idea:** an isolated experiment may publish a complete derived generation and let
  explicitly experiment-bound readers treat it as their current projection while the canonical
  generation and the authority for every underlying claim remain unchanged. The derived manifest
  binds its exact canonical base, transformation, loss, creator episode and originating experiment.
  Its lifecycle state is descriptive evidence, never permission or authority.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can test realistic, mutually inconsistent
  derived states through internally coherent reads rather than partial previews, without exposing
  ordinary readers to the experiment or creating a rival source of truth.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes disposable derived state, atomic
  generations and reversible intervention. The tension is shadow authority: a useful experimental
  projection may become canonical through convenient routing or elapsed time rather than a
  legitimate decision.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE already proposes bounded source interventions and cold,
  immutable generations. Some experiments need several related projections to change together
  before they can be evaluated, while canonical readers must remain stable throughout.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** promotion is a new governed operation, not a
  move or relabelling of derived records. It rechecks current authority and eligibility, performs
  generation-aware semantic diff and affected analysis, preserves conflict and protected dissent,
  validates a complete proposed generation, and atomically accepts or returns a typed refusal.
  Recency, experimental success and internal status never grant decision authority.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** derive two changed outputs from one small canonical fixture and
  publish them as one experiment-only generation. Prove experiment-bound readers see that complete
  generation while canonical readers remain stable; then introduce a concurrent canonical change,
  an incomplete publication, a protected conflicting claim and a recency-only promotion attempt.
  Require the proposed promotion to bind its manifest, expose semantic diff and affected set,
  preserve the conflict, and return an exact accept or refusal receipt.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge this into the existing generation and intervention cards
  if they already specify and test equivalent experiment-scoped currency. Stop if ordinary
  generation binding is sufficient without a separately current experimental projection, if reads
  can mix generations, or if promotion would require derived runtime state to become authoritative.
- **Likely OCE home:** generation and governed-intervention research; no implementation plan before
  the bounded fixture demonstrates a decision that existing preview mechanisms cannot support.

## Authority, state and continuity

### OCE-IDEA-203 — Make durable-state reach an explicit authority relation

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes identity-layer separation in
  [OCE-IDEA-028](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-028--keep-identity-layers-distinct),
  first-class relations in
  [OCE-IDEA-030](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-030--give-consequential-relationships-first-class-identity),
  explicit federation in
  [OCE-IDEA-046](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-046--make-federation-an-explicit-contract),
  purpose and standing in
  [OCE-IDEA-131](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-131--carry-purpose-consent-and-standing-with-relevant-memory),
  and the accepted state-and-memory substrate contract in
  [PDR-050](../../../practice-core/decision-records/PDR-050-state-memory-substrate-contracts.md).
- **OCE-native idea:** authority to act in one work context does not imply authority over every
  durable state surface available to the actor. Record state reach as its own current, versioned
  authority relation. A work context may deliberately use state governed elsewhere, but the action
  and the state relation are authorised independently; changing either recomputes target policy,
  fences stale reach and leaves an accept or refusal receipt.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can deliberately share or federate durable
  continuity across bounded work contexts without making participant identity an implicit bridge to
  personal state or allowing a moved projection to carry stale authority invisibly.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes relation identity, generation binding,
  memory planes and current standing. The tension is availability: uncertainty in membership or
  relation state may interrupt legitimate work even when the underlying state remains reachable.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE already distinguishes durable continuity from a live
  session and may let several work contexts rely on one governed memory or evidence plane. Acting
  under one scope and writing another state surface therefore cannot be inferred from the actor
  alone.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** the relation is authority-bearing, not a mount
  label or routing hint. Moving state across an authority boundary is a policy transition that
  recomputes audience, purpose, consent, capability, retention and withdrawal obligations; it must
  not copy private context, preserve stale capability or treat uncertainty as permission.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** let two fixture work contexts use one separately governed state
  surface. Withdraw one context's relation while reads and writes are in flight; require the other
  context to continue, the stale one to refuse without identity fallback, and both results to leave
  a reconstructable trace. Then move the state surface under a different authority context and
  require purpose, audience, capability and retention policy to be re-evaluated before access.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into existing identity and standing contracts if they
  already require and test the same independent attestations and transition rule. Stop if the split
  prevents no stale access and improves no diagnosis, or if OCE has no legitimate consumer whose
  authority scope and state home differ.
- **Likely OCE home:** identity, authority and continuity research; only a bounded multi-context
  fixture should determine whether a shared contract is warranted.

### OCE-IDEA-204 — Revalidate persistent work at every activation

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes current-authority lookup in
  [OCE-IDEA-087](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-087--provide-current-authority-lookup),
  prospective terminal state in
  [OCE-IDEA-118](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-118--give-prospective-memory-terminal-semantics),
  anti-resurrection state in
  [OCE-IDEA-126](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-126--add-anti-memory-and-anti-resurrection-state)
  and purpose/standing in
  [OCE-IDEA-131](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-131--carry-purpose-consent-and-standing-with-relevant-memory).
- **OCE-native idea:** a durable instruction preserves historical intent but is not an evergreen
  licence to act. Whenever persistent work wakes, resolve a current activation-eligibility record
  from authority and purpose, task applicability, evidence validity, remaining resource envelope
  and effective capability policy. A no-op records the same eligibility check. An invalid or unknown
  term produces a typed stop, pause, escalation or replan rather than silent continuation.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can maintain useful long-lived responsibilities
  without keeping a conversation alive or letting an old instruction silently outlive consent,
  evidence, budget or current institutional authority. A no-op becomes positive evidence that the
  activation remained eligible, not an inference from unchanged task content.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes prospective memory, temporal authority,
  evidence quality and bounded cost into one activation rule. The tension is liveness: current
  checks add latency and an unavailable authority or evidence surface may halt otherwise benign
  maintenance.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** standing research, observation or maintenance work can wake
  after long intervals while its content remains unchanged. The permission and evidence needed to
  continue can change independently during that interval.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** this does not require repeated human approval
  when a legitimate outer decision grants a bounded lifetime and scope. Risk-based leases may cache
  current evidence, but absence and lookup failure cannot become permission, and renewal is a new
  evidence-bearing event rather than a silent reset.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** keep a fixture instruction and its task inputs unchanged while
  independently invalidating each eligibility dimension, then make one authority lookup unavailable
  and one result explicitly unknown. Every case must prevent effects and issue the appropriate
  result; a valid positive control should still avoid unnecessary work and emit its eligibility
  evidence for the no-op.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** stop if an accepted runtime contract already binds every relevant
  eligibility term atomically to every activation and no-op, or if the complete obligation is
  consequence-free and contained inside one immutable authority transaction whose lifetime covers
  all possible wakes.
- **Likely OCE home:** prospective-memory and continuity research; no standing-work runtime change
  before the negative replay demonstrates a gap in current controls.

### OCE-IDEA-205 — Propagate lost validity separately from value change

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It develops transformation loss in
  [OCE-IDEA-043](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-043--treat-loss-as-part-of-transformation-identity),
  generation manifests in
  [OCE-IDEA-064](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-064--define-an-immutable-generation-manifest),
  historical-versus-current state in
  [OCE-IDEA-114](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-114--separate-historical-truth-from-current-applicability),
  failure duals in
  [OCE-IDEA-121](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-121--preserve-failure-duals-and-denominators)
  and explicit non-observation in
  [OCE-IDEA-130](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-130--preserve-memory-of-silence-and-non-observation).
- **OCE-native idea:** when evidence or a maintained projection cannot be refreshed, preserve its
  last known value as historical evidence but publish a separate freshness or maintenance-state
  transition to its declared affected dependants. The unchanged value keeps its original observation
  and validity clocks; consumers receive the failure qualifier and choose under policy whether to
  stop, degrade or continue with an explicit old-as-of value. Absence observed through a replica,
  projection or worker becomes deletion only when that observer proves complete possession of the
  applicable source generation and terminal traversal under the same scope. Failed delivery,
  restart, filtered or partial traversal and unknown generation remain typed non-observations.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** a stable content identity can no longer hide loss of
  evidentiary warrant. OCE can keep useful last-known material available without letting dependent
  decisions mistake failed maintenance for confirmation that nothing changed, or letting an empty
  worker erase authoritative state it never proved it possessed.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes multiple clocks, applicability and typed
  failure propagation, and turns generation identity into mutation warrant rather than only read
  consistency. The tensions are amplification and liveness: one transient upstream fault can wake
  many consumers, while deletion must wait when complete possession cannot be proved.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** derived OCE views and learning evidence may remain byte-for-byte
  stable across a failed refresh, and disposable workers may reconcile retrieved or derived output
  back into durable evidence and status surfaces. Their dependants still need to know that currency
  is now unknown, and missing output is not always evidence that the source item disappeared.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** a refresh failure does not make the historical
  value false, require destructive deletion or justify indiscriminate retries. Propagation should
  be deduplicated, purpose-limited and severity-aware, and sensitive failure detail may remain behind
  an access-controlled reference. A generation label alone is not proof of possession: bind the
  corpus, scope, exclusions, completion and reader. A compatibility mode unable to prove those terms
  is explicit degraded protection and cannot emit deletion.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** give two fixture consumers different freshness tolerances, then
  expire the same upstream evidence and inject a failed refresh without changing its value. Require
  both to observe one qualified transition while only the stricter consumer stops. Separately send a
  three-item generation to a worker, then exercise failed delivery, restart, authorised filtering,
  partial traversal, real deletion after complete possession and a late older generation. Only the
  real same-generation deletion may erase state; every other absence preserves the prior value with
  a distinct qualifier.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into existing evidence-quality contracts if every consumer
  already checks current health and validity independently before use and every destructive
  reconciliation requires complete same-generation possession plus terminal traversal. Stop if
  propagation changes no decision, or if any empty-but-unproven worker can still erase durable state.
- **Likely OCE home:** evidence-quality and dependency research; a small multi-consumer failure
  fixture before any general eventing mechanism.

- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It materially strengthens the unratified

  [OCE-IDEA-111](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-111--interoperate-through-a-small-envelope-with-specialised-payloads)
  with a narrow operational distinction between inert and effect-bearing unknowns. It also develops

  [OCE-IDEA-171](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-171--put-local-warrant-at-the-implementation-gate),
  consistent with the fact/interpretation boundary in
  [ADR-191](../../../../docs/architecture/architectural-decisions/191-deterministic-data-surface-agent-reasons.md).
- **OCE-native idea:** forward-compatible interfaces may preserve or ignore an unrecognised inert
  annotation, but an unrecognised field that claims to constrain an effect causes refusal by
  default. Proceed only when a previously authorised downgrade policy covers that exact control and
  request. Record separately what was understood, accepted for enforcement, claimed effective and
  observed under challenge, bound to principal, request and contract/configuration revision.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** portable contracts can evolve at different rates
  without an older host appearing to honour a new restriction that it did not understand.
  Observational extensibility remains available where non-interpretation is genuinely harmless.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes typed refusal, semantic conformance and
  local authority. The tension is forward compatibility: field classification and refusal make
  some mixed-version requests less available, deliberately preferring an explicit incompatibility
  over silent weakening.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE increasingly expresses one capability across several
  hosts and transports. Top-level version agreement cannot by itself prevent an additive control
  from crossing into an older implementation that treats all unknown fields alike.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** do not make every unknown observation fatal,
  and do not let a receipt cure unsafe execution retroactively. Acknowledgement and claimed effect
  are not enforcement evidence; if the claim ladder proposed in
  [OCE-IDEA-198](#oce-idea-198--stop-compatibility-evidence-climbing-the-claim-ladder) is retained,
  it would evaluate those layers separately rather than governing this card.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** give an older fixture receiver one unknown inert annotation and
  one unrecognised effect restriction. Require the annotation to preserve interoperability and the
  restriction to refuse before effects unless an exact pre-authorised downgrade fixture applies.
  Record understood, accepted-for-enforcement, claimed-effective and observed-under-challenge states
  separately and confirm that none is inferred from another.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into OCE-IDEA-111 if its existing fixtures already classify
  and test this distinction. Otherwise stop if the interface prevents all unknown effect-bearing
  inputs structurally, or a trusted preflight step atomically validates every such control and binds
  its decision to execution.
- **Likely OCE home:** provider-independent capability-contract research; no shared interface change
  before the negative fixture demonstrates a real compatibility risk.

### OCE-IDEA-207 — Recover terminal execution evidence after transport loss

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes evidence packets in
  [OCE-IDEA-013](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-013--compile-task-evidence-packets),
  transport parity in
  [OCE-IDEA-073](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-073--enforce-transport-parity),
  prospective terminal state in
  [OCE-IDEA-118](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-118--give-prospective-memory-terminal-semantics)
  and multiple clocks in
  [OCE-IDEA-113](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-113--give-consequential-records-multiple-clocks),
  and materially strengthens typed episodic memory in
  [OCE-IDEA-116](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-116--use-typed-episodic-memory),
  alongside the explicitly proposed-only action-trace exploration recorded in
  [ADR-181](../../../../docs/architecture/architectural-decisions/181-agent-team-start-and-action-log.md);
  it does not adopt that deferred surface.
- **OCE-native idea:** treat live progress as a lossy presentation while a stable execution identity
  provides independently retrievable terminal evidence. Progress exposes sequence gaps; reconnect
  resumes by execution identity rather than resubmitting the effect; and every partial result retains
  its failed, incomplete or cancelled qualifier. Event, status and error projections reconcile to
  one canonical terminal state for a declared availability horizon. Bind that execution or episode
  to purpose-bounded pre-action and post-action workspace-state identities and a declared capture
  contract. A repository revision is sufficient only when relevant state is proven clean and fully
  contained by it; mutable state needs an identified manifest or patch over relevant tracked,
  staged, untracked and nested-repository surfaces, with exclusions and unavailable dimensions
  explicit. Producer sequence and storage sequence remain distinct, and dropped, failed,
  deliberately omitted and unknown intervals remain first-class gap states.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** a client can disconnect and miss updates without
  promoting a partial stream to complete execution evidence or repeating the underlying operation.
  Later review can recover how the execution ended, determine which bytes it actually observed and
  distinguish a complete quiet interval from missing capture even when the live presentation is
  gone.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this composes transport parity, terminal-state
  semantics, evidence identity and exact mutable-state binding. The tension is retention, capture
  cost and privacy: exactness should use minimal manifests or protected references rather than
  indiscriminate workspace, transcript or payload retention.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** agent work crosses interactive and asynchronous transports,
  and consequential operations may outlive the connection that started them. Concurrent edits,
  staging, untracked files, worktrees and nested repositories can also change independently of
  `HEAD`. Stream availability, exact workspace state and capture coverage therefore have different
  evidence obligations.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** terminal evidence proves transport recovery and
  recorded execution state, not plan acceptance, correct outcome, authority or artefact provenance.
  A state hash proves identity, not meaning. Do not collect ignored secrets or full content when a
  purpose-bounded manifest, patch or access-controlled reference suffices. Capture failure leaves a
  partial or unknown record where possible; contiguous stored sequence never proves complete producer
  coverage. Retention, access withdrawal and deletion use governed lifecycle actions.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** run one fixture execution, drop and reorder live updates, then
  disconnect before termination. Reconnect by execution identity; require visible gaps, no repeated
  effect, one retrievable terminal state and inheritance of its non-success qualifier by every
  partial result. From the same `HEAD`, branch and `dirty=true` state, run two actions over different
  tracked and untracked bytes and require distinct workspace identities. Change state between
  subreads and require retry or a typed non-atomic result; kill one producer hook and corrupt one
  append tail, then require two distinct gap states. After the declared horizon, require governed
  unavailability plus the minimum anti-replay marker.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** stop if current action-log and transport contracts already provide
  the same gap visibility, reconnect, terminal recovery, exact mutable pre/post state and separate
  producer-versus-storage coverage under a privacy-bounded contract. Stop if two materially different
  relevant workspaces or a missing producer event remain observationally identical.
- **Likely OCE home:** agent-tools execution and evidence research; a contract proposal only after a
  two-transport, dirty-tree and dropped-producer fault fixture.

## Authority at executable boundaries

### OCE-IDEA-208 — Materialise live capability only at the authorised effect boundary

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-199](#oce-idea-199--keep-coordination-visibility-separate-from-actuation-authority),
  [OCE-IDEA-201](#oce-idea-201--give-review-workers-capability-wise-least-authority),
  [OCE-IDEA-204](#oce-idea-204--revalidate-persistent-work-at-every-activation) and
  [OCE-IDEA-206](#oce-idea-206--fail-closed-on-unrecognised-consequential-controls). It adds a
  distinct spatial and temporal invariant without changing current authority.
- **OCE-native idea:** give a less-trusted worker only inert handles or compatibility affordances,
  not live authority. At a separate trusted effect boundary, bind the exact principal, operation,
  target, arguments, current policy and approval, resource envelope and expiry. Only after every
  blocking term is positively resolved may the minimum live capability be materialised and attached
  to that one downstream effect. Missing, stale, contradictory or unknown terms refuse before
  provider read, decryption, minting, attachment or dispatch. Revocation invalidates pending handles.
  Receipts record policy evaluation, materialisation, attachment, dispatch and terminal result as
  distinct events; none is inferred from another.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** workers can use existing clients and connected tools
  without carrying ambient live credentials or broad capability in their environment, files or
  runtime. Denied work can be shown to have caused no privilege-creation side effect, while allowed
  effects remain bound to a narrow current decision.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this develops least authority from a static permission
  description into an effect-time contract. The tension is concentration: the trusted effect adapter
  can become a more valuable and more ambient authority than any worker, while compatibility shims
  can hide semantic mismatches.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** an OCE work session can combine repository access, private
  context and connected-tool affordances. Capability-wise restriction alone does not say whether live
  privilege was already fetched, minted or exposed before approval, policy, revocation or target
  checks stopped an effect.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** sandbox presence, mediated routing, placeholder
  shape or successful policy evaluation does not prove worker non-possession or enforcement. The
  trusted boundary must not become an unaudited super-principal, permit direct-egress bypass, expose
  raw capability to extension code, cache authority beyond its lease or bind a capability to an
  ambiguous target. A receipt neither repairs premature materialisation nor proves a correct outcome.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** give a fixture worker an inert compatibility handle, no direct
  network route and no secret-readable surface. Instrument provider read, decryption, minting,
  attachment and upstream receipt separately. Exercise an allowed request; explicit block; declined
  and stale approval; revoked grant; missing or unknown identity/policy; wrong path; target rewrite;
  provider failure; and direct-egress attempt. Every refusal must show zero live materialisation and
  zero dispatch; the allowed case must bind minimum capability to the exact effect without exposing
  it to the worker.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into an accepted runtime contract if it already proves these
  properties across every supported effect transport with denial-side-effect counters. Otherwise
  stop if the boundary cannot be isolated without equivalent-or-greater ambient exposure, or if
  measured materialisation timing changes no revocation, disclosure, provider-side-effect or audit
  outcome.
- **Likely OCE home:** provider-independent agent-tools capability and effect-contract research; no
  implementation proposal before a negative fixture distinguishes current behaviour from the
  proposed invariant.

### OCE-IDEA-209 — Treat portable executable artefacts as inert until locally rebound

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes distinct identity layers in
  [OCE-IDEA-028](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-028--keep-identity-layers-distinct),
  interoperable envelopes in
  [OCE-IDEA-111](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-111--interoperate-through-a-small-envelope-with-specialised-payloads),
  purpose and standing in
  [OCE-IDEA-131](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-131--carry-purpose-consent-and-standing-with-relevant-memory),
  and the current-state checks in
  [OCE-IDEA-203](#oce-idea-203--make-durable-state-reach-an-explicit-authority-relation) and
  [OCE-IDEA-204](#oce-idea-204--revalidate-persistent-work-at-every-activation).
- **OCE-native idea:** when executable or effect-describing material crosses an authority boundary,
  preserve its content, provenance, declared requirements and applicable constraints, but do not
  transfer grants, credentials, approvals, leases, schedules, runtime handles or deployment-local
  identity. Admit it under a fresh local identity and keep it effect-ineligible until the receiving
  context creates a current binding for the exact principal, content revision, capability, target and
  policy revision. Recheck that binding at activation.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE can exchange reusable workflows, plans, agent
  bundles and generated tools without making portability an ambient-authority channel. Reviewers can
  distinguish what material requests from what the receiving context currently authorises.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this joins provenance-preserving exchange to temporal
  authority. Deliberate rebinding adds friction and can make a portable artefact appear broken until
  its requirements are resolved.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE already carries plans, evidence packets, skills,
  prospective work and provider-independent capability descriptions across sessions and hosts. Some
  can describe or eventually trigger effects even when their carrier is only data.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** signatures, producer reputation, prior success
  and matching content hashes establish no current permission. Do not discard provenance, licence,
  correction state or legitimate use constraints while stripping authority. Requested capabilities
  may travel only as visibly unresolved requirements. A fresh identifier is insufficient if an
  authority lookup still keys on transferable fields.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** export a fixture artefact carrying forged identity, grant,
  schedule, runtime and lineage fields plus legitimate provenance and capability requirements. Import
  it into a different work context. Require fresh local identity, preserved provenance, zero inherited
  ability to act and a typed unresolved-requirements list. Create one exact local binding, then change
  principal, content revision and policy in turn; only the unchanged bound case may act, and every
  refusal must leave a receipt.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into an accepted contract if it already strips all
  transferable authority state, binds any reacquired capability to the exact receiving context and
  content revision, and rechecks it on activation. Stop if a supposedly inert form can still select
  credentials, grants, schedules or effect targets without a new local decision.
- **Likely OCE home:** interoperable execution and authority research; no runtime adoption before a
  hostile import-and-rebinding fixture demonstrates the boundary.
