## Durable automation and accountable identity

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-001 — Keep durable automation from inventing a human actor

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes the identity distinctions in
  [OCE-IDEA-028](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-028--keep-identity-layers-distinct)
  with activation-time authority checks in
  [OCE-IDEA-204](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-204--revalidate-persistent-work-at-every-activation),
  but neither referenced entry currently requires those distinctions to survive durable and nested
  execution boundaries.
- **OCE-native idea:** carry a versioned, validated acting-identity record through queueing, retries,
  suspension, resumption and child work. Name what actually acts — person, agent, service or deployment — and record an initiating
  schedule or governed trigger as its own relation rather than as the actor, without replacing a
  non-human kind merely to complete an audit or accounting schema. Record current authorisation independently from the audit claim about the act;
  any represented person or service and any cost owner remain additional relations, never fallback
  actors. Bind non-interactive execution to the exact trigger, deployment, workspace and task scope;
  revalidate that scope before each effect. Unknown or ambiguous identity forms fail closed, and an
  older record restores only distinctions its evidence actually establishes.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** OCE could pause, move and resume long-lived work, or
  delegate a bounded child operation, without laundering authority through a convenient user field
  or falsely attributing an automated effect to a person. Reviewers could reconstruct both who or
  what was authorised to act and who or what the resulting record claims actually acted.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** this closes a gap between temporal authority and
  identity-layer discipline. A fresh eligibility decision does not by itself prove that a restored
  execution retained the right actor, subject and attribution semantics. The tension is migration:
  strict recovery may refuse old work that cannot prove these distinctions.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** prospective work, agent handoffs and recoverable executions can
  cross session, process and host boundaries. Some begin from a person, while others begin from a
  schedule, governed trigger or deployment. A generic user identifier cannot honestly represent all
  of them.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** accounting ownership, notification targets and
  historical initiators are not acting authority. A person who owns a workspace or receives a
  result must not be inserted as the actor merely to satisfy an audit or billing schema. Identity
  continuity does not preserve expired permission: current authority and purpose still require
  revalidation at the effect boundary. Do not retain unnecessary personal identifiers merely to
  make the record look complete.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** run one fixture from a person and one from a governed trigger;
  queue, suspend, resume and delegate a child operation from each. Revoke the deployment or trigger
  scope before one resume, corrupt one stored identity version and offer an accounting owner where
  no human actor exists. Give one fixture a represented subject distinct from its actor and one an audit attribution
  that conflicts with the acting principal. Require the valid child to retain the correct identity
  relations, the stale and malformed cases to refuse before effects, the automated case to remain
  non-human, each represented-subject and audit-attribution relation to survive independently
  rather than collapsing into the actor, and accounting attribution never to alter access.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** merge into the existing identity or persistent-work candidates if
  an accepted execution contract already preserves and negatively tests all four distinctions
  across durable and nested boundaries. Otherwise stop if the distinctions change no authority,
  audit, privacy, recovery or incident-review outcome in representative OCE work.
- **Likely OCE home:** prospective-memory, agent-runtime and execution-evidence research; no runtime
  change before the negative fixture demonstrates a gap in current controls.

## Evidence lifecycle and verdict integrity

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-002 — Separate preparation lifecycle from empirical result

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes the terminal-state discipline in
  [OCE-IDEA-118](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-118--give-prospective-memory-terminal-semantics)
  with the claim-ladder guard in
  [OCE-IDEA-198](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-198--stop-compatibility-evidence-climbing-the-claim-ladder);
  neither separates the lifecycle of getting ready to observe from the observed verdict itself.
- **OCE-native idea:** give any observational effort two independent fields: a preparation state
  (not started, registered, cancelled before fire, fired) and a result status that stays null until
  the observation actually fires. Pre-fire expiry, drift, or missed ratification changes the
  preparation state only; it can never be relabelled as an empirical outcome, and a fresh attempt
  requires a fresh registration rather than a reinterpreted old one.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** abandoned or expired evaluation attempts become
  honestly reportable without contaminating outcome statistics. OCE could distinguish "we never
  ran this" from "we ran this and it failed" mechanically, in every report.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** complements terminal semantics for prospective work.
  The tension is tooling: any surface carrying a single status enumeration must split it, and
  historical single-field records cannot be retrofitted with distinctions they never recorded.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE runs benchmarks, probes and experiments whose attempts are
  sometimes abandoned mid-preparation; a single status field lets a cancellation read as a failure
  or silently vanish.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** preparation states carry no empirical claim in
  either direction. Do not inherit "cancelled means failed" or "registered means likely to pass".
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** one fixture cancelling before fire; assert the result field
  stays null and that reporting distinguishes it from a fired failure.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if a single-state model provably never confuses cancellation
  with failure across representative OCE reporting, merge into the prospective-memory candidate.
- **Likely OCE home:** validation-strategy research; experiment and benchmark tooling.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-003 — Order verdicts by integrity precedence and bound claims to their proved scope

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It extends
  [OCE-IDEA-038](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-038--keep-confidence-separate-from-commitment)
  with a verdict algebra and composes
  [OCE-IDEA-121](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-121--preserve-failure-duals-and-denominators).
- **OCE-native idea:** fix a verdict precedence for any evaluated run: integrity breach outranks
  proved failure, which outranks honest evidence loss, which outranks a bounded pass. Expected
  evidence honestly lost yields inconclusive — never a pass and never a manufactured failure — while
  a tampered record or a broken custody boundary invalidates everything beneath it. A pass names
  exactly the scope and evidence it proved; global success labels are forbidden vocabulary.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** evaluation reports become comparable and honest
  under partial failure. A reader can trust that "pass" is scope-bounded and that integrity
  problems were not averaged away.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** composes with failure duals: the inconclusive tier is
  where lost denominators land instead of quietly inflating pass rates. Tension: existing report
  vocabularies may carry global labels that would need mechanical rejection.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** quality claims from benchmarks and gate runs are quoted
  onward in decisions; without a precedence rule, a partially-lost run is narrated at the
  narrator's optimism.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** the precedence governs verdicts about one
  evaluated scope; it is not a severity model for defects generally.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** a fixture run with one expected artefact deleted; require
  inconclusive rather than pass or failure, plus a vocabulary lint rejecting global labels.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if current reporting doctrine already mechanically rejects
  overclaim labels and orders mixed outcomes, mark this a duplicate.
- **Likely OCE home:** validation-strategy; report-shape conventions for benchmarks and gates.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-004 — Register the rules before the observation; a missed boundary cancels, never narrows

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It extends
  [OCE-IDEA-099](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-099--govern-source-intervention-as-a-reversible-experiment)
  and composes
  [OCE-IDEA-048](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-048--define-a-corpus-contract-before-extraction).
- **OCE-native idea:** every rule an evaluator could otherwise choose retrospectively — scope,
  predicates, denominators, cutoffs, adjudication policy — is committed and anchored before the
  observed period begins. If the anchoring moment is missed, the attempt cancels; deriving a
  narrower window, a substitute rule, or a friendlier denominator after seeing any post-boundary
  material is refused rather than negotiated.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** results become immune to post-hoc scope shopping.
  The cost of a missed boundary is one cancelled attempt, which is cheaper than one untrustworthy
  result.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** the corpus-contract idea applied to time: a
  registration is a corpus contract for an interval. It also strengthens the known-answer-first
  discipline already used for search ground truths by generalising it to arbitrary observations.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE evaluates against live, moving surfaces — search quality,
  upstream drift, agent behaviour — where choosing scope after the fact can flatter any result.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** pre-registration does not make subjective
  terms objective, and it must not be diluted into "we registered an intention" — the anchor binds
  exact rules, not aspirations.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** attempt a post-boundary registration in a fixture and require
  typed refusal rather than acceptance with a warning.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if every live OCE evaluation surface already binds its rules
  pre-observation, this reduces to documentation of existing practice.
- **Likely OCE home:** validation-strategy; search-quality and experiment protocols.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-005 — Only pre-ratified objective predicates prove failure

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-047](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-047--let-algorithms-generate-questions-not-verdicts)
  with
  [OCE-IDEA-023](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-023--separate-deterministic-fact-agent-interpretation-and-human-authority).
- **OCE-native idea:** deterministic aggregation does not make subjective terms deterministic. A
  failure verdict is provable only through objective predicates ratified before observation;
  semantic judgements carry identified adjudication, evidence, rationale and uncertainty; and
  unresolved subjective disagreement returns inconclusive — never a manufactured pass or failure in
  either direction.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** agent judges and panels can score semantic quality
  without their disagreement being laundered into false certainty, and hard failures stay
  attributable to named, testable predicates.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** the questions-not-verdicts stance given a lifecycle:
  algorithms and judges propose; only pre-ratified predicates convict. Tension: inconclusive-heavy
  results frustrate consumers who want a number, and the cure is better predicates, not laxer
  adjudication.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE uses model judges and multi-expert panels whose semantic
  scores feed decisions; the boundary between measured failure and disputed reading is currently
  carried by convention rather than mechanism.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** do not inherit predicate theatre — restating a
  subjective term with objective grammar does not make it objective.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** a fixture where two adjudicators disagree; require an
  inconclusive verdict carrying both rationales rather than a majority-vote failure.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if panel doctrine already separates objective conviction from
  semantic adjudication mechanically, mark this a duplicate.
- **Likely OCE home:** evaluation and judge-panel conventions; validation-strategy.

## Assurance economics and machinery failure modes

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-006 — Name artefact gravity: internal completeness is not external adequacy

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-150](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-150--require-outcome-evidence-outside-the-machinery)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-020](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-020--require-a-value-heartbeat)
  by naming the failure mode they guard against and its detection signature.
- **OCE-native idea:** an assurance or evidence system's internal completeness — more schemas,
  actors, receipts, stores and phases — can grow indefinitely while its external adequacy (can the
  real experiment run; does the value-bearing artefact exist) stays unproved. Treat rising artefact
  mass with an unproved end-to-end path as a stop signal in its own right, and re-derive the bridge
  from the next action to the value-bearing artefact at every boundary.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** programmes get stopped at the moment ceremony
  starts substituting for delivery, instead of at the retrospective. The signature is cheap to
  check and does not require judging anyone's intentions.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** gives the outcome-evidence and value-heartbeat ideas
  a shared adversary with a name. Tension: legitimate foundational work also grows artefacts before
  outcomes; the discriminator is whether the single end-to-end proof advanced, not artefact count.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE builds evidence systems about its own work; those are
  precisely the systems where internal elaboration feels like progress.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** this is a stop-and-check trigger, not
  permission to dismiss rigour as ceremony; the non-override rule stands — the gravity check may
  change what is built, never how well.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** for one live programme, list artefacts added versus the
  end-to-end proof state across recent boundaries; gravity has fired when artefacts grew and the
  proof did not.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if outcome-evidence doctrine as practised already catches every
  instance before material spend, this adds only a label; fold the label into that doctrine.
- **Likely OCE home:** metacognition and planning doctrine; programme review checklists.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-007 — A lifecycle is real only when every artefact has a shipped producer

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It extends
  [OCE-IDEA-179](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-179--implement-real-operations-before-reserved-placeholders)
  and composes
  [OCE-IDEA-008](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-008--make-bounded-completeness-a-product-property).
- **OCE-native idea:** a declared lifecycle is implemented only when every required artefact has a
  real producer path through the shipped tool, exercised end to end. A package check that validates
  the structure of whatever parts are present, and reports OK on a partial package, is not a
  readiness check; completeness of the whole is its own verified property, and structural validity
  never substitutes for it.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** "the lifecycle exists" becomes a testable claim.
  Operators stop discovering at execution time that a phase has no producer.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** bounded completeness applied to toolchains rather
  than query results. Tension: staged delivery legitimately ships partial lifecycles — the cure is
  an honest not-yet-complete report, not a green partial check.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE ships CLIs whose commands accrete over time; a
  structurally green check on a partially producible package is a standing false-green risk.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** do not inherit "every artefact needs a
  producer immediately" — the claim is that readiness reporting must track producer coverage, not
  that coverage must be instantly total.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** from a clean fixture, run the shipped tool end to end and
  assert it produces every required artefact; then delete one required artefact and require the
  package check to fail on completeness rather than pass on structure.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if existing gates already distinguish structural validity from
  lifecycle completeness everywhere it matters, mark this a duplicate.
- **Likely OCE home:** agent-tools validator conventions; CLI acceptance checklists.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-008 — Correlated review is not independent assurance

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-176](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-176--use-targeted-specialist-escalation)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-098](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-098--include-negative-controls-for-intentional-duplication);
  neither states the independence condition.
- **OCE-native idea:** reviewers or agents that share sources, models, prompts or priors constitute
  correlated review: their agreement is one lens repeated, not accumulated assurance. Disjoint
  evidence bases, method diversity and genuinely adversarial briefs reduce correlation but do not
  by themselves establish independence — an independence claim is earned by evidence of distinct
  failure-mode coverage, with the remaining dependence between legs modelled explicitly, and
  assurance is counted by independent failure modes covered, never by reviewer headcount.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** panel design becomes deliberate. Agreement stops
  being over-read, and the marginal reviewer is chosen for the failure mode they uniquely cover.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** composes with negative controls: a panel's
  independence claim is itself testable by planting findings only one lens class should catch.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE runs multi-expert reviews and voter fleets whose legs
  often share a model family and corpus; measured near-zero finding overlap between genuinely
  different lenses is existing local evidence that lens diversity, not count, does the work.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** correlated review still has value — as one
  strong lens; the claim is only that it must not be priced as several.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** run one artefact past the same brief twice with different
  lenses and twice with the same lens; compare novel-finding yield.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if reviewer-routing doctrine already prices independence
  explicitly, fold this into it as the stated condition.
- **Likely OCE home:** reviewer-invocation doctrine; fleet and panel design guidance.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-009 — Synthetic material carries no evidential standing

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-202](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-202--let-experiments-publish-coherent-generations-without-gaining-authority)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-098](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-098--include-negative-controls-for-intentional-duplication).
- **OCE-native idea:** rehearsal and synthetic material is marked non-evidential at creation, and
  the mark is mechanical — a schema field or path class the tooling refuses to promote — so no
  later process can quietly convert synthetic outputs into empirical claims. Test and calibration
  runs still accept marked synthetic material and retain bounded, non-evidential machinery
  evidence; the refusal binds at promotion into empirical outcome results. Rehearsal proves
  machinery; it never proves outcomes.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** fixtures and dry runs can be rich and realistic
  without any risk of laundering into results, because the boundary is enforced where data flows,
  not where people remember.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** the experiments-without-authority idea pushed one
  level down, from generations to individual artefacts.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE test estates include deterministic synthetic fixtures
  that resemble real evidence closely enough to be mistaken for it under context pressure.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** non-evidential does not mean unmaintained —
  synthetic material still earns review; it just never earns standing.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** score a marked synthetic artefact in a calibration run and
  assert its results stay marked non-evidential, then attempt to promote it into an empirical
  outcome result and require typed refusal.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if no OCE scoring path can currently reach synthetic material,
  record this as a guard to add at the first path that can.
- **Likely OCE home:** testing-strategy conventions; evidence tooling schemas.

## Evidence custody and authority transitions

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-010 — Classify committed evidence by disclosure class

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-064](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-064--define-an-immutable-generation-manifest)
  with a disclosure axis the manifest idea lacks.
- **OCE-native idea:** repository-carried evidence declares one of three disclosure classes at
  authoring: readable (public-safe committed files); sealed (exact or losslessly encoded bytes
  committed with digests and reconstruction instructions); and encrypted (committed ciphertext with
  public integrity metadata, the key held outside the repository, its fingerprint committed).
  Encoding, compression and opaque paths are never privacy. Both consequences of the encrypted
  class — losing the external key loses readability; exposing it makes already-public ciphertext
  readable forever — are accepted in writing at classification time.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** evidence that cannot be public can still be
  durable, portable across accounts and machines, and integrity-checkable by anyone, with the
  trade-offs chosen deliberately instead of discovered.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** extends manifest discipline with confidentiality;
  composes naturally with atomic publication, since class is part of each artefact's identity.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** some OCE evidence involves third-party
  confidentiality constraints yet must survive machine and account loss; ad-hoc choices between "keep it local"
  and "publish it" lose either durability or confidentiality.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** the encrypted class is basic confidentiality,
  not a threat-model claim against a malicious platform administrator; stronger properties need a
  separate decision. Personal data is excluded from every repository class, encrypted included —
  committed ciphertext cannot honour erasure once a key is exposed, so the estate's prohibition on
  personal data in the versioned estate governs and access-controlled external stores own such
  material.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** classify one mixed evidence set; verify a fresh clone
  reproduces sealed bytes exactly and validates encrypted integrity without the key.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if no OCE evidence genuinely needs the encrypted class, keep
  only the readable/sealed distinction and record the third class as rejected-for-now.
- **Likely OCE home:** evidence-handling conventions; safety-and-security guidance.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-011 — Make authority transitions atomic and bound to an exact prior candidate

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It extends
  [OCE-IDEA-065](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-065--publish-complete-generations-atomically)
  and composes
  [OCE-IDEA-202](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-202--let-experiments-publish-coherent-generations-without-gaining-authority).
- **OCE-native idea:** a status transition — proposed to accepted, experimental to canonical — is
  one merge tree that atomically replaces every governed surface and reader link, so no pushed
  state ever exposes mixed authority. The acceptance itself is a commit whose parent is the exact
  reviewed candidate and which introduces no implementation changes, binding the decision to
  reviewed content without self-referential digests.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** readers can never land on a half-transitioned
  estate, and an acceptance record provably covers exactly what was reviewed — nothing rides in
  with the approval.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** atomic generations applied to authority itself.
  Tension: large transitions want staging; the cure is staging on non-canonical surfaces with one
  atomic flip, not incremental canonical mutation.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE transitions documents and tools between authority states
  regularly; a transition that leaves one stale reader link creates split-brain authority.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** atomicity applies to the accepted tree, not
  necessarily to one commit of implementation work; behaviour-first pairs may land separately while
  everything remains non-canonical.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** attempt three fixture cutovers and require refusal of each:
  one leaving a reader link on the old surface, one whose acceptance commit has a parent other
  than the reviewed candidate, and one whose acceptance commit carries an implementation change.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if transition tooling with these properties already exists for
  the surfaces that matter, record this as practice documentation rather than a new mechanism.
- **Likely OCE home:** decision-record and canonicalisation conventions; release checklists.

## Execution lineage and history discipline

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-012 — A stored definition is identity, not the effective contract

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-064](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-064--define-an-immutable-generation-manifest)
  with
  [OCE-IDEA-209](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-209--treat-portable-executable-artefacts-as-inert-until-locally-rebound)
  and the activation-time checks in
  [OCE-IDEA-204](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-204--revalidate-persistent-work-at-every-activation).
- **OCE-native idea:** the stored definition of automated or delegated work that dynamically loads
  live instructions, skills or repository state is a necessary identity, never the complete
  effective runtime contract. Evidence about one execution must recover what actually governed it —
  exact revisions, paths and digests of loaded material, tool and permission surface, and
  provider-visible inputs. Inputs that cannot be recovered get a ratified uncertainty consequence;
  they are never silently treated as fixed.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** two runs of an unchanged definition become honestly
  comparable, and behavioural drift becomes attributable to the input that moved rather than
  mysterious.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** the generation-manifest idea applied to executions.
  Composes with inert-until-rebound: the effective contract is only knowable at binding time.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE agents load live canonical instructions and skills at run
  time by design, so definition stability and behaviour stability are deliberately decoupled.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** recovering lineage does not mean freezing it —
  dynamic loading is the feature; the claim is only that evidence must say what was loaded.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** change one dynamically loaded instruction between two runs of
  an unchanged definition and require the two runtime manifests to differ at exactly that input.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if execution evidence already captures effective lineage for the
  automations that matter, this is documentation of practice.
- **Likely OCE home:** execution-evidence research; agent-runtime conventions.

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-013 — Keep history a cursor, never an oracle

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It composes
  [OCE-IDEA-126](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-126--add-anti-memory-and-anti-resurrection-state)
  and
  [OCE-IDEA-038](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-038--keep-confidence-separate-from-commitment),
  and deliberately contrasts with
  [OCE-IDEA-067](./2026-08-22-oce-significant-ideas-catalogue.md#oce-idea-067--treat-cold-generation-as-the-semantic-oracle).
- **OCE-native idea:** prior outputs of a recurring process legitimately serve continuity — cursors,
  idempotency, deduplication — but must never become expected labels, answer keys, tuning inputs,
  denominators or completeness oracles for evaluating later runs. Early outputs are samples of
  behaviour, not definitions of correctness; treating them as ground truth converts every early
  mistake into a standard.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** recurring processes can be evaluated against
  independently captured evidence rather than against their own past, so improvement and regression
  stay measurable instead of self-referential.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** the deliberate contrast with cold-generation-as-oracle
  is the point: an oracle is legitimate for conformance of derived projections to a definition, and
  illegitimate for correctness of behaviour over the world. The two must not be conflated under the
  shared word "oracle".
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE has recurring generations, benchmarks and routines whose
  accumulated history exerts quiet normative pull on later runs.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** do not inherit a ban on using history — the

### OCE-INTAKE-20260828-014 — Prove execution-context lineage; identity freshness proves nothing

- **Status:** discovery input
- **Relationship to current authority:** `candidate`. It extends
  [OCE-IDEA-198](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-198--stop-compatibility-evidence-climbing-the-claim-ladder)
  and composes
  [OCE-IDEA-204](./2026-08-23-concept-intake.md#oce-idea-204--revalidate-persistent-work-at-every-activation).
- **OCE-native idea:** a fresh context identifier does not prove absence of inheritance. A claim
  that an execution context is independent — no inherited turns, memory, files, connectors or prior
  outputs — requires positive lineage evidence: creation provenance, access logs, or a closed
  allowlist proving what could have entered. Contamination discovered after commitment invalidates
  the dependent result rather than downgrading it.
- **What becomes possible and why it matters:** independence claims in multi-context work — fresh
  judges, clean-room comparisons, unbiased baselines — become checkable properties instead of
  assertions, and a broken one fails loudly instead of silently biasing results.
- **OCE fit, tension or latent composition:** the claim-ladder guard applied to context lineage:
  "new ID" sits on the observation rung; "independent" is a model-rung claim needing its own
  evidence.
- **OCE-local motivating condition:** OCE fans out judge and worker contexts whose value depends on
  what they could not see; that property is currently asserted more often than evidenced.
- **Boundary, risk and what must not be inherited:** lineage evidence is bounded by what the
  platform exposes; where positive proof is unavailable, the honest state is "independence
  unproven", never a silent assumption either way.
- **Cheapest discriminating probe:** seed one inherited artefact behind a context claiming
  freshness and require the lineage check to catch it.
- **Falsifier or stop condition:** if the platforms in use expose no lineage evidence at all,
  record the gap and its uncertainty consequence instead of building an unverifiable ritual.
- **Likely OCE home:** fleet and judge-panel design; execution-evidence research.
