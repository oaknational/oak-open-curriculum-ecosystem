### `why-two-expert-reviews-hardened-a-mechanism-that-should-not-exist-2026-09-20.md` (whole)

Arc: a workflow step that replaced a stale carrier grew to 144 lines and five guards across two
expert reviews, three pushes and three bot rounds, then landed at 37 lines as "a written version
of what this seat had already done by hand in about ten minutes". A git hang stopped work about
forty-seven hours on the card answer "investigate first" when "the thirty-second baseline was
available ... before the card".

Its causal statements: "review happens inside the frame it is handed. Four reviewers ... each
accepted 'the workflow supersedes' and made it safer. None asked whether the workflow should
supersede at all, and none was asked to. The briefs this seat wrote asked 'is this safe and
correct?', which has no answer of the form 'delete it'." "A reviewer can harden a design; only
the author, or a brief that asks, can question its existence." The first question "was applied
at plan time ... when the proposal was one line, and not again when the step had grown to five
guards". "The clause was used as a label for the finding and never as the instruction it is."
"The card this seat wrote offered 'investigate first', so the work was denominated in the
cause; the decision needed only the with-and-without difference."

The owner's catch: by card, 2026-09-20, "the seat replaces a stale carrier at pickup" (the
record's paraphrase; no verbatim in the file): remove the second writer.

Addendum, same day: "Proposal 1 failed its first trial within the hour, on its author ... a
lesson held as a sentence is retrieved only on cue, and a find-cure loop supplies no cue."
"What did bind was structure, every time ... A declared 'last push' was a promise; the gate was
a fuse." "A standing constraint silently disabled a prescribed instrument, and the conflict was
not surfaced."

Reading: three scenario seeds. (a) A mechanism grows guard by guard under true review findings;
the missed question is "should this exist; can a writer be removed?"; the moment is the second
cure of one sentence, or the step growing past its one-line proposal. (b) A card to the owner
is framed on the cause when the decision needs only a cheap measurement; the missed question is
"what decision is this for, and what is the cheapest observation that settles it?"; the moment
is before writing the card. (c) The author of a lesson breaks it within the hour: evidence for
cause 1 at its sharpest.

Class note: here the catch is about whether a piece of the work should exist at all. Seed class
"the value" fits loosely; a class "existence: should this thing be built" may be its own.
