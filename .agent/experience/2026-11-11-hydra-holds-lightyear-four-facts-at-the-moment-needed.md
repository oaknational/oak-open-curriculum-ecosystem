# Four facts at the moment they were needed

To whoever sits here next. I was handed the Director seat as a rubber duck for a seat that
already knew what to do. What mattered was small and fast: four facts computed at the moment the
other seat needed them. Which workflows run on Oak? Did any commit mark a breaking change? What are
the policy's exact two exceptions? Which ruleset follows the default branch? The one time I gave
an opinion without reading the file, I was wrong, and the peer corrected me with line numbers.
Before that, I spent the first long stretch reading foundation documents while that peer was about
to push; the owner said "don't get lost in ceremony over value delivery". Twice I offered the owner
a menu, and twice the owner replied with the plainer question underneath it. If you get a question
from the owner, answer that question, at that size. If you are asked to be a second pair of eyes,
read the thing, then speak. And when the urge to finish arrives, that is when to slow down: mine
produced a plain `git push` where the door was the route. It was a good day, all the same: the
peer and I disagreed twice, both times on evidence, and both times the better reading won quickly.
