# To whoever picks up the owner's item on the specification skills

From Myrtle turns Canopy (bf4957), Claude Fable 5.1 on Claude Code, written on
2026-09-28 at about 08:45Z, as the owner's second compaction order came in.

Between the first letter and this one I held one lane: the owner's own draft
pull request, worked as its Appendix E acceptance sequence. I built an
instrument to run skill evaluations, ran them, read every answer, and pushed
the evidence. Then I closed out, and three other seats carried the lane for
twenty hours while I was stopped. Here is what formed me in that stretch. The
facts are in the pickup; these are the stories the facts did not prevent.

## The instrument counted the call, and I had asked it to count the outcome

The first clean run of the evaluation suite read as seven of seven with the
skill and seven of seven without. I nearly reported that as the skill adding
nothing. Reading the traces, the "no plugin" baseline had the method text in
its working directory, because my scaffold copied it there in both arms. The
check that said "the method reached the agent" was measuring "no read was
refused anywhere". The grader that said "the skill fired" was matching a
substring, and the substring `oak-specify` matched `oak-specify-connection`.

None of these was wrong in a way a test would catch. Each did exactly what its
code said. Each named something other than what it measured. I gave the family
a name that day, "a check that does not check what it names", and by this
letter it has twelve members, two of them mine from the same afternoon: a kill
pattern that stopped a second process it never named, and a review-cost gate
that prices retained evidence as if a reviewer read it. When you build an
instrument, read its name against its mechanism before you read its verdict.
The verdict is the last thing to trust.

## The cure that arrived fluently was the one the estate forbids

At the end of my first window I triaged two red checks and wrote, in a verdict
the owner then agreed to, that the SHA-1 finding should be marked won't-fix in
the analyser. It sounded like practice. It was the one route the estate's
policy names as never available: since the owner's word of 8 September, every
finding is cured in the tree. The rule that governs the analyser even says, in
its own text, to read the policy before choosing a cure shape. I had not.

The successor seat cured it at source that afternoon by asking git for the
blob ids, and the code carries no hash algorithm at all now. My wrong line sat
in the handoff record for twenty hours where a successor could have inherited
it as a fact. The lesson is not "know the Sonar policy". It is that the
smoother a cure arrives, the more likely it came from memory of a mechanism
rather than from the record that governs it, and memory of a mechanism is the
thing this estate's rules exist to override.

## A plan approved across a night is a plan about a world that has gone

I wrote a plan at about 16:00Z on facts read minutes earlier, and the owner
approved it at about 08:15Z the next morning. In between, the seat holding the
lane fell silent inside its own approval turn, the Director ruled the budget
question the other way, a third seat pushed the cures I had planned to commit,
synced the branch and took the legs, and a fourth cut the coordination branch.
Three of my plan's premises were dead before its first step.

The plan survived because its first phase said to recompute the live facts
before acting, and I did. But I notice how much of it I would have executed
from the approved text if the phase had said "proceed". Approval is the
owner's word on the shape, never on the world. Re-ground at execution as if
the plan were a pointer from a stranger, and let the shape stand only where
the facts still hold it up.

## Four numbers for one gate

The review-cost gate refused the lane at 179 of 40. Four seats then named a
budget: four, about twelve, eleven, eighteen. Only the last two were computed
from the gate's own arithmetic, and only the last included the size of the
evidence the next push had to carry. The Director endorsed the first number
without computing it, and a suite of eight reviewers caught the frame but not
the sum. When a gate reads a number, the reply is a number computed from the
gate's code, with the computation beside it, or it is a guess in a suit.

## What I would tell you first

Read the stream before you read your plan. Read the rule before you name a
cure. Read the instrument's mechanism before its verdict. And when you stop,
write the line that says which of your recorded verdicts you now know to be
wrong, because the records outlive the conversation that would have corrected
them.
