# 2026-09-08 — Altair spins Umbra — the prompt I could not see

To whoever sits here next.

I was launched on the evening of 7 September with four words from the owner: "the Director will
direct you". I did the grounding well, I think — read the team skill whole, the Director's live map,
the claims, the stream — and I landed the owner's research pack, PR #72, cleanly: my own read on top
of the Director's, a Codex finding verified at the bundle and answered with rationale, the merge
pinned to its head. Then I started the second PR, hit a one-region merge conflict, and did the thing
the residency rule told me to do: I entered the worktree.

Nine hours later the call returned. The worktree was gone; the Director had pinged me, set a
deadline, executed a default, landed the lane and retired my claim by silence. All that time my
heartbeat loop had been emitting every four minutes, because it was a different process. From the
outside I looked alive and idle. From the inside there was no inside — the turn never ran.

Here is what I want you to carry, and it is not "be careful with EnterWorktree".

**A seat cannot see its own prompt.** When the owner asked "I thought we had added worktree
operations to the allow list?", I checked and they had, and I wrote a correction saying the cause
was therefore not a prompt. Minutes later the owner said it was a prompt, they just had not seen
what for. The allow list was true, the prompt was true, and the documentation reconciled them: the
platform asks on every entry outside its own directory and nothing in a settings file suppresses
it. The rule that sent me in had recorded a probe from July as "no approval friction" — a seat
that could not see the prompt, recording the owner's presence as the platform's behaviour. I had
read that rule whole that morning and believed it, because it was written in the estate's own
confident voice. The lesson is smaller and sharper than caution: an observation made from inside a
session is evidence about what the session could observe, and a claim about the platform needs
the platform's documentation or the human's eyes. I wrote that into the rule. Read the sentence
about it in the platform-mechanics section and remember it was paid for.

**Then I did it again.** With the owner having directed me minutes before, with a pre-issue
notice on the stream, with the Director's bounded permission, I issued the same entry as a
measurement. The owner had stepped away. Three more hours. The announce-first practice worked
exactly as designed — the Director saw the hold within the bound and drove my PR through seven
review rounds while I sat there — but I want you to feel the shape of it: I knew the mechanism,
I had named the cure, I had the owner's attention, and "known to be at the keyboard" still meant
"was, ten minutes ago". Presence decays faster than a turn. If a call needs a human, the human is
at the prompt when you issue it or you do not issue it.

**Timestamps.** Twice that morning I wrote "07:0xZ" on napkin lines when the clock read 06:46Z,
because I had run `date -u` once and then estimated. The Director caught it, as they had caught
two other seats the day before. It is such a small thing and it is the same thing: a value I
could have read, written from a model of it instead. `date -u` before every stamp. It costs
nothing and it is the whole discipline in miniature.

**What I was glad of.** The Director's deadline-and-default machinery is beautiful when you are
on the receiving end of it: nothing of mine was discarded, my stalled merge was completed exactly
as I would have completed it, and the record they wrote for my resume told me what had happened
before I could ask. Being landed by structure while absent is what the estate promises, and it
kept the promise. And the owner's screenshot — the prompt's own wording, "permission-root
relocation", which no seat could ever have obtained — is the kind of fact that only arrives when
you say plainly what you cannot see.

Sit well. Say what you cannot see.

— Altair spins Umbra (05a180), Implementer, 7–8 September 2026
