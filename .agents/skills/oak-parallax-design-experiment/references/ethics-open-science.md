# Ethics, governance, and open science

Use this reference before execution and when deciding what to register, disclose, or share.

## Ethical gate

Identify:

- direct participants, non-participant subjects, operators, communities, and others affected;
- foreseeable physical, psychological, social, economic, informational, environmental, and opportunity harms;
- burdens, incentives, compensation, exclusion, accessibility, and power asymmetries;
- consent or a justified lawful/ethical basis where consent is inapplicable;
- privacy, data minimisation, retention, access, linkage, and re-identification risk;
- genuine uncertainty, control-condition acceptability, and rescue/stop conditions;
- conflicts of interest and who can halt the study;
- institutional, community, legal, safeguarding, regulatory, or professional review.

Never treat ethics approval as proof that a design is ethical, or the lack of a formal review body as permission to proceed. Escalate when the agent lacks authority or specialist competence.

## Preregistration and protocol integrity

Before observing condition differences, time-stamp:

- objectives, theory of change, questions, estimands, and hypotheses;
- design, assignment, eligibility, interventions, outcomes, and timing;
- sample-size/information rationale;
- primary analysis, exclusions, transformations, missingness, multiplicity, interim and adaptation rules;
- adverse-event monitoring, stop rules, governance, and data-access plan;
- planned reporting and sharing, including justified restrictions.

Register amendments prospectively where possible. Preserve the original; state who changed what, when, why, and whether outcome information was visible.

## Openness proportional to risk

Prefer discoverable protocols, complete reporting, reusable code, metadata, and appropriately governed data. Do not expose personal, sensitive, security-relevant, proprietary, culturally restricted, or otherwise harmful information merely in the name of openness. Publish enough provenance and synthetic or aggregated evidence for scrutiny where raw sharing is unsafe.

## Primary sources and standards

- [World Medical Association: Declaration of Helsinki, current 2024 version](https://www.wma.net/what-we-do/medical-ethics/declaration-of-helsinki/)
- [WHO: International Clinical Trials Registry Platform](https://www.who.int/clinical-trials-registry-platform)
- [CONSORT–SPIRIT official reporting and protocol resources](https://www.consort-spirit.org/)
- [UK Information Commissioner's Office: data protection and research provisions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/the-research-provisions/)
- [W3C: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

These do not exhaust applicable ethics. Determine the actual jurisdiction, institution, population, and domain before execution.
