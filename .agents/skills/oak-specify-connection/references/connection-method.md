# The connection method

What a relation between two specified things must carry, what a consequential seam records, and
the composition test that decides whether reliance survives interaction. Owned with
`specify-connection`; `specify` names seams by these definitions and `assess-specification` reads
them. Source: the specification framework note, §7.

## Relations carry contracts

A collection of complete local specifications is not an account of their composition. A relation
says what may legitimately travel across it: data, meaning, effects, authority or warrant.

| Relation | Necessary contract | Forbidden inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motivates | Which need or value justifies which obligation, with the rationale | Popularity proves benefit |
| Refines | Which higher claim is made more precise, the preserved assumptions and the added constraints | Any detail is a valid refinement |
| Allocates | Which party or component takes which obligation, and what stays shared | Allocation proves delivery or removes the service owner's responsibility |
| Depends on, composes with | Required and supplied guarantees, compatible environments and interaction obligations | Locally correct parts imply globally correct behaviour |
| Transforms, represents | Preserved meaning, identity and units; permitted loss; rejection conditions; source and target versions | Schema-valid output is semantically equivalent |
| Authorises, delegates | The grantor's authority, the subject, action, purpose, scope, expiry, revocation and enforcement point | A reference, a recommendation or an access right confers other powers |
| Supports, challenges | The exact claim, the evidence, the inference rule, independence and validity limits | An evidence link proves the claim |
| Contributes to an outcome | The causal mechanism, alternatives, exposure, conditions and evaluation | A completed interaction establishes causal impact |
| Supersedes, corrects | Effective scope and time, history, affected consumers and migration or correction duties | A new publication means every consumer has changed |

A consequential relation assertion has a stable identity of its own, distinct from its type and its
endpoints; who asserted it, when and on what basis is preserved. Repeated occurrences in sources are
not independent evidence. A small uncontested structural link needs no record of its own unless
attribution, correction or lifecycle requires one.

## A consequential seam record

> Relation type; source and target identities and revisions; purpose; producer and consumer
> obligations; semantic preservation or permitted effects; authority; failure and recovery;
> evidence; change triggers.

A claim that depends jointly on several premises is one claim with several required premises,
never several individually insufficient links presented as independent support; a small table
suffices.

## The composition test

For a technical consumer, the provider's guarantees must satisfy the consumer's assumptions in the
shared operating context. Check satisfiability and reachability as well as implication: a guarantee
that holds only under impossible assumptions is vacuous. Cyclic assumptions need a justified initial
condition or a joint argument; "A trusts B and B trusts A" is not a basis.

Then examine the behaviour the composition introduces: retries and duplicate effects; shared
capacity and contention; latency budgets; concurrency and ordering; partial failure; cancellation;
inconsistent versions; permission propagation; correlated failures; recovery. Pairwise compatibility
misses a three-party cycle or a shared bottleneck.

For a human service the analogous check is a concrete responsibility and delivery argument, not a
proof that people behave as intended: a support route needs a receiving organisation, capacity and a
usable contact mechanism, not a button.

## Three records that are not one

An operational seam preserves units, meaning or effects across a connection at one scale. A Parallax
bridge claim justifies an inference between scales (component evidence to workflow reliability). A
crosswalk claim translates between conceptual bases. One consequential connection may need all
three; their questions stay distinct and their shared facts are referenced, never copied.
